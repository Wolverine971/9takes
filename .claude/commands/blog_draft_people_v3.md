# Draft the supported psychological portrait

Read the request, canonical standard, evidenceSchema and the supplied evidence and baseline. Do not invoke legacy creation, grading, furniture or prep commands. Evidence must be ready before drafting.

Choose the most compelling supported psychological tension. Build a varied narrative around consequential choices, revealing contradictions, specific testimony and a committed Enneagram argument. Write in the house voice from the standard: commit to the type in the answer, open the type argument with the triad and its core emotion, state the type's mechanism in plain steps, make the case, and answer the strongest rival in its own "Why not Type N?" section. Keep sentences plain and conversational, and vary paragraph length: short standalone lines, a quote block for the strongest line in a section, a few solid paragraphs. Avoid mandatory furniture and invented interiority. Source the opening as carefully as the type argument. Let the subject be larger than the type. Follow the standard's uncertainty and five-year tests. Committing to the type does not loosen any evidence rule: each claim about motive stays attributed and sized to its source.

Quote from the saved source text in the request's `source_cache_dir`, not from the excerpts in the evidence file, which may already be trimmed. Keep the source's own breaks: `"A," he said. "B."` stays two quotations, and two sentences that sit apart in the source are not joined. Save the text of any new source you open to that folder as `<source id>.txt`. Aim for 2,000-3,500 words; the edit stage tends to add length, so do not start at the ceiling.

Write draft.md with complete site-compatible frontmatter and evidence-draft.json, a continuation of the supplied evidence. Assign exact passage strings to all important claims, including direct quotes and factual metadata. Remove unused research claims from the draft inventory while retaining useful sources. If the type hypothesis references a removed claim, retain its relevant supporting passage or revise the hypothesis honestly. New evidence must be opened and added to the record before use. Preserve current dates, slug and publication state from the baseline. New profiles must have published: false. Include one cluster tag in `type` for the field the person is filed under, chosen from the standard's cluster tag table (for example `['musician', 'celebrity', 'pop-star']`). Set production_pretext to a draft state; carry inherited blockers forward for explicit editorial resolution. Leave content_quality/editorial_workflow to the runner.

Use existing draft/component conventions only as format references. Do not copy another subject's psychological explanation. Do not modify files outside the two requested outputs. Do not grade or publish.

Open cases (`profile_format: "open_case"` in the evidence): follow the standard's open-case shape. Lead with who they are and why people are searching, show what the young record does show, set the leading read beside each live alternative with evidence for and against, and include a required H2 starting `## What would settle it`. The 60-word type answer and the titles/FAQs say "leading read", never "is a Type X". Roughly 1,200–2,500 words; do not pad.
