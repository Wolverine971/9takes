# Research an evidence-first people profile

Read the stage request, its standard_file, and the evidenceSchema in contract_file. Follow only the v3 people-profile workflow; do not invoke legacy creator/prep/review commands.

Read the supplied baseline, research_notes and prior_evidence when present as leads. Existing v3 evidence is a durable source record: preserve useful IDs, recheck relevant sources, and extend it rather than rediscovering everything. Gather neutral evidence before assigning or confirming a type. Search for substantial primary sources and named testimony, open them, inspect surrounding context, and record locators and source independence. Read enough long-form material to test the motivation across contexts and time. Search snippets alone do not establish access or verification.

Then build and challenge the type hypothesis: supporting claims, counterevidence, strongest alternative, discriminator, unexplained behavior. Consider circumstance, work incentives and public performance. Check current accuracy without requiring a news paragraph. Flag sensitive allegations, contested typing, difficult historical sources and changes to a previous thesis honestly.

Write only evidence.json to the requested output path. Populate every schema field. During research, claim.passage may be empty. Use insufficient_evidence and specific research_tasks if the leading type lacks a defensible discriminator or essential evidence cannot be verified. A hold is a successful research outcome. Never invent evidence, quote locators, source access, confidence, or specificity to satisfy the schema. Do not write the blog in this stage.
