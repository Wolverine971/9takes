# Interview Question Bank

Choose three to five questions per round. Use the smallest set that deepens the current riff.

## Concrete receipts

- What is the first moment that made you think this about them?
- What do they repeatedly do, not merely say?
- Which interview, decision, conflict, or relationship best shows the pattern?
- What exact wording, tone, or reaction stuck with you?
- Is this a one-off moment or a recurring pattern? What are two other examples?
- Which source would you want a skeptical reader to watch for themselves?

## Interpretation

- What do you think they were protecting, pursuing, or avoiding in that moment?
- What is the emotional logic underneath the visible behavior?
- Which part is strategy and which part feels automatic?
- What contradiction makes the person more legible to you?
- What do most observers notice, and what do you think they miss?
- If you removed their job, fame, and public role, what pattern would remain?

## Type connection

- Why does this point to the proposed type rather than merely resemble its stereotype?
- Which core fear, desire, defense, attention pattern, or emotional habit is operating?
- What does the person notice first in a situation?
- What do they appear to do under pressure?
- Where do you see the proposed wing or subtype, if anywhere?
- Which behavior looks inconsistent with the proposed type, and how do you understand it?

## Vibe and holistic read

- When you watch them for a while, what is the overall energy you feel?
- What does your intuition notice before you can fully explain it?
- Does their presence feel withdrawn, assertive, compliant, image-aware, threat-aware, controlled, expansive, or something else?
- What phrase or image captures the person for you?
- Which part of this read is hard to prove but important to state honestly as your impression?

## Counter-case

- What is the strongest alternate type?
- What evidence supports that alternative?
- Could the behavior be explained by role, culture, status, neurotype, trauma, media training, or the situation itself?
- What does your preferred typing fail to explain?
- Are you over-weighting a dramatic public moment?
- What would someone who disagrees with you say?

## Confidence and falsifiability

- How confident are you from 0 to 100, and what earns that number?
- What evidence would move the number up?
- What would make you change the type entirely?
- Which claim should be removed if no source can support it?
- What are you certain about even if the exact type is wrong?

## Meaning and usefulness

- What becomes easier to understand if your read is right?
- Which behavior stops looking random?
- How would this person likely misread someone different from them?
- What is the useful human insight here beyond assigning a number?
- What should the reader notice in their own life after reading this?

## Founder media extraction

- Which sentence from your riff would make the strongest opening hook?
- What is the one surprising claim you could defend in under 60 seconds?
- Which receipt can be shown or cited on screen?
- What is the honest hesitation that makes the short video more credible?
- What question should viewers answer with their own take?
