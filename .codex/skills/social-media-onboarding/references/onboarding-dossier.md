# Social Media Onboarding Dossier

Use this contract to create the approved handoff from onboarding to ongoing social-media operation. Adapt the representation to an existing project convention; the semantic sections matter more than the file type.

## Status vocabulary

Use these labels on consequential claims and choices:

- `owner-stated`: the owner described this as identity, intent, experience, or constraint;
- `observed`: supported directly by account data, content, or a cited source;
- `inferred`: reasoned interpretation of supplied evidence;
- `committed`: explicitly approved as a current strategic choice;
- `provisional`: a working choice or model that needs testing;
- `open`: a choice the owner has not made;
- `unknown`: missing information rather than an undecided preference.

Time-sensitive observations need an `as_of` time or evidence window. Add confidence when uncertainty can change the operating decision.

## 1. Dossier identity

| Field                        | Purpose                                                                  |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `account_owner`              | Person or team whose intent governs the account                          |
| `platform_accounts`          | Exact accounts and the intended role of each platform                    |
| `onboarding_status`          | Draft, owner-reviewed, or approved                                       |
| `created_at` / `approved_at` | Lifecycle timestamps                                                     |
| `evidence_window`            | Period represented by account data and terrain research                  |
| `sources`                    | Account URLs, analytics, interviews, sites, examples, and research links |
| `refresh_triggers`           | Changes that should reopen part of onboarding                            |

## 2. Identity and brand truth

Capture:

- identity and reason for existing;
- owner-stated purpose and values;
- worldview, convictions, useful disagreements, and non-negotiables;
- experience, access, proof, or perspective that supports credibility;
- a credibility map connecting important claims to supporting experience, evidence, access, or demonstrated judgment, plus claims the account has not yet earned;
- audience promise and intended reputation;
- voice, energy, recurring language, stories, and phrases worth preserving;
- boundaries, anti-topics, and unacceptable growth tactics;
- present identity versus the identity the account is growing toward;
- conflicts between the owner's intended identity and the account's observed presentation.

Avoid generic adjective lists unless they are anchored to examples of actual expression or behavior.

## 3. Destination

| Field                      | Purpose                                                           |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `primary_outcome`          | Prioritized business, creative, reputational, or community result |
| `social_contribution`      | How social media is expected to advance it                        |
| `priority_audience`        | Who must be reached or moved                                      |
| `baseline`                 | Starting condition                                                |
| `target_direction`         | Desired measurable or qualitative movement                        |
| `horizon`                  | Time window                                                       |
| `leading_signals`          | Early evidence that the route is working                          |
| `outcome_signals`          | Evidence of real destination progress                             |
| `secondary_goals`          | Ranked rather than blended with the primary goal                  |
| `non_goals`                | Outcomes or metrics not being optimized                           |
| `tradeoffs` / `guardrails` | What can be sacrificed and what must be protected                 |

## 4. Audiences, communities, and value path

For each meaningful audience or community, record:

- priority and role;
- problem, desire, tension, or job to be done;
- activating situations or moments when that tension becomes live;
- awareness level and current alternatives;
- recognizable vocabulary, questions, and objections;
- current beliefs and the recognition, reframe, or cognitive/emotional shift that could help;
- proof or source conditions needed for credibility;
- current relationship to the account;
- desired relationship or next behavior;
- reasons to save, share, reply, participate, follow, return, or take the next step; do not treat these behaviors as interchangeable;
- where that community gathers and which accounts connect it;
- evidence of relevance rather than assumed fit.

Describe the value or conversion path from discovery through trust to the intended next step. Mark missing offers, weak profile promises, broken links, and attribution gaps.

## 5. Starting coordinates

Record the baseline needed by the operating skill:

- scale and recent trajectory;
- current follower, following, mutual, and relationship patterns when public or authorized;
- cadence, consistency, content history, topics, formats, hooks, series, and calls to action;
- goal-relevant performance and measurement gaps;
- pipeline, reusable assets, launches, events, and blockers;
- current profile and conversion path;
- active strategy, experiments, assumptions, and prior failed approaches;
- production capacity, strengths, dependencies, budget, and sustainable pace;
- production environment: how evidence, ideas, drafting, approval, publishing, reuse, feedback, and learning move, including bottlenecks.

Do not blend incompatible platforms or time windows. Preserve the evidence source and date.

## 6. Compounding edges

Record the starting strength, evidence, and gaps for:

| Edge                    | What to preserve                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `audience_intelligence` | First-party language, recurring audience states, objections, trusted sources, and behavior patterns                                         |
| `creative_judgment`     | Demonstrated ability to select, frame, package, compare, and improve ideas rather than a list of preferred formats                          |
| `relationship_access`   | Trusted peers, curators, collaborators, communities, customers, and connectors that affect access or interpretation                         |
| `operating_memory`      | Prior hypotheses, experiments, causal lessons, production knowledge, and known failed approaches that can be reused                         |
| `retained_demand`       | Repeat audience behavior, direct relationships, branded language, search demand, subscriptions, community participation, or portable assets |

These are capabilities and relationships that can compound or transfer. Keep them distinct from temporary platform mechanics, formats, or trends.

## 7. Starting terrain

Bound the niche and adjacent territory that matters. Record:

- peer, aspirational, adjacent, collaborator, curator, customer, substitute, and source accounts or clusters;
- why each actor or cluster is strategically relevant;
- audience discourse, repeated problems, vocabulary, narratives, and tensions;
- common and emerging topics, formats, templates, hooks, calls to action, and interaction patterns;
- platform norms or mechanics that affect the strategy;
- exact surfaces and relationship paths through which discovery happens, rather than only platform names;
- saturation, sameness, threats, gaps, and credible whitespace;
- trend stage, brand fit, usable window, evidence, and confidence.

Separate trends, durable patterns, templates, topics, and isolated outliers.

## 8. Strategic posture

Record the current operating choices:

| Field                  | Purpose                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `positioning`          | The space the account intends to occupy                                                                                                                            |
| `audience_promise`     | What following or returning reliably provides                                                                                                                      |
| `return_promise`       | The stable expectation the next several artifacts should continue to fulfill                                                                                       |
| `proposed_advantage`   | Depth, access, speed, synthesis, utility, taste, personality, community, or another credible edge                                                                  |
| `content_territories`  | Recurring questions, tensions, stories, or subject areas                                                                                                           |
| `platform_roles`       | What each platform does in the system                                                                                                                              |
| `formats_and_cadence`  | Sustainable production choices, not aspirational volume                                                                                                            |
| `interaction_posture`  | Replies, conversation, community participation, and relationship building                                                                                          |
| `distribution_posture` | Collaborations, curation, partnerships, reuse, and amplification                                                                                                   |
| `conversion_posture`   | Offer, next step, profile promise, and call-to-action policy                                                                                                       |
| `trend_policy`         | When to participate, adapt, ignore, or create a recurring format                                                                                                   |
| `measurement_policy`   | Primary signals, review window, and attribution limits                                                                                                             |
| `experiment_policy`    | Appetite, budget, reversibility, and stopping conditions                                                                                                           |
| `initial_channel_edge` | Why this account may have a situated advantage on an exact surface and tactic now, its enabling assets, timing conditions, dependencies, and invalidation evidence |

## 9. Decision register

Use separate lists for:

- `committed_decisions`: approved choices the operating loop should respect;
- `provisional_decisions`: choices to test with a checkpoint;
- `open_decisions`: unresolved tradeoffs, owner, evidence needed, and decision deadline;
- `unknowns`: missing observations, source needed, and why they matter.

For important rejected alternatives, preserve the reason and the signal that would justify reconsidering them.

## 10. Initial strategic hypotheses

Each hypothesis should connect the account, terrain, and destination:

- `belief`: what is expected to be true;
- `because`: current evidence and owner knowledge;
- `audience_state`: the activating situation, live tension, current belief, and intended shift;
- `mechanism`: expected recognition or reframe, credibility basis, and personal or social utility;
- `distribution_bet`: exact surface, tactic or format, message, timing condition, sharing or participation job, and next step;
- `proposed_move`: the smallest useful way to act on it;
- `prediction`: what should happen if it is right;
- `signal`: leading and outcome measurements;
- `checkpoint`: review date, event, or sample size;
- `failure_interpretations`: plausible explanations if it does not work;
- `confidence`: confidence and the assumption dominating it.

End with the single decision or action that should initialize the faster `$social-media-slam` operating loop.

## 11. Watchlist and refresh triggers

Track only signals capable of changing the route:

- account metrics or audience behavior;
- actors, topics, formats, templates, or platform shifts;
- assumptions behind the proposed advantage;
- the return promise, credibility basis, compounding edges, channel dependencies, and early decay indicators;
- changes in identity, offer, capacity, goals, or audience;
- explicit thresholds that trigger a strategy update or partial re-onboarding.

Full onboarding should be rare. Refresh the smallest invalidated section and preserve prior versions so strategic drift remains visible.
