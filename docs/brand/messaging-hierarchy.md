# 9takes Messaging Hierarchy

**Status:** Canonical and frozen
**Locked:** 2026-08-13
**Owner:** DJ Wayne
**Purpose:** Keep the main thing the main thing. This is the source of truth for public positioning, product copy, founder communications, social content, outreach, and agent-generated drafts.

## The hierarchy

| Level                   | Canonical language                                                  | Job                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Movement belief**     | **The internet collapses nine perspectives into one winning take.** | Names the problem in the world and the change 9takes is fighting for. |
| **Brand promise**       | **See the emotions behind every take.**                             | States what a person gains from 9takes.                               |
| **Product ritual**      | **Answer before the crowd.**                                        | Names the behavior that makes 9takes different.                       |
| **Product explanation** | **One question, nine perspectives.**                                | Explains the product in the fewest possible words.                    |
| **Personal payoff**     | **Stop mistaking someone else’s alarm for a defect.**               | Makes the emotional benefit personal and concrete.                    |

These lines are not five competing slogans. They are five levels of one message:

1. The internet rewards one interpretation and hides the rest.
2. 9takes helps you see the emotional logic beneath those interpretations.
3. It protects your honest perspective by asking you to answer before seeing the room.
4. It then places your answer beside eight other ways of reading the same situation.
5. The result is greater understanding of yourself and less judgment of other people.

## The main narrative

> We each experience our own emotional alarm as reality. Another person’s alarm can look irrational, obstructive, weak, or aggressive from the outside. Online, the crowd hardens that mistake: one interpretation wins the room and the rest disappear. 9takes opens the room again. You answer before the crowd can shape you, then compare nine perspectives on the same question and see the emotions underneath each one.

## Usage rules

### Lead with the human situation

Start with a recognizable conflict, fear, contradiction, or blind spot. The Enneagram is the map that explains the situation; it is rarely the protagonist of the opening.

Good:

> I wanted her to trust my judgment. She wanted me to slow down enough to address her fear.

Weak:

> Here is how an Enneagram 8 communicates with an Enneagram 7.

**Scoped exemption (DJ, 2026-08-13):** Instagram type-pond captions may open type-first (self-ID
hook in the type's vernacular) — that is the deliberate `/carousel` type-pond strategy, not drift.
The exemption covers IG captions only; the carousel's story content itself still leads with the
human tension.

### Use one level for one job

- Use the **movement belief** in manifestos, founder posts, podcast conversations, and category-defining essays.
- Use the **brand promise** in bios, page furniture, social profiles, and closing language.
- Use the **product ritual** in calls to action and product demonstrations.
- Use the **product explanation** when someone needs a fast description of 9takes.
- Use the **personal payoff** in stories about conflict, relationships, blind spots, and self-recognition.

### Do not improvise new master concepts

New metaphors, campaign names, or format labels may help a specific piece. They do not join this hierarchy unless DJ explicitly replaces one of the five levels above.

Before proposing a new public concept, ask:

1. Can the idea be expressed using one of the five canonical lines?
2. Is it a production label or campaign device rather than a brand belief?
3. Will it clarify the hierarchy or compete with it?

Default decision: treat it as supporting vocabulary.

## Message acceptance test

A piece is on-message when a reader can answer all four questions:

1. **What is wrong with the current environment?** One interpretation wins and hides the rest.
2. **What does 9takes do differently?** It asks you to answer before seeing the crowd, then reveals nine perspectives.
3. **Why does that matter personally?** It exposes blind spots and the emotional logic other people are using.
4. **What should I do next?** Answer one real question.

If the piece cannot answer those questions, simplify it before adding another concept.
