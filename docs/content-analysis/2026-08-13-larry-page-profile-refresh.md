## Outcome

The profile now treats Larry Page as a founder who stepped away from day-to-day management while retaining formal governance and voting control. It no longer turns low public visibility, property ownership, or a medical disclosure into evidence of an emotional motive.

The Type 5 case rests on observable mechanisms: technical depth, attention directed toward high-leverage problems, information architecture, governance design, and selective operating involvement. Type 7, Type 1, Type 9, and movement toward Type 8 are included as live counterreadings.

The title exception is deliberate. The prior “disappeared” and “vanished” framing became materially misleading after the current-role audit. The replacement preserves the persona-led strategy without adding an Enneagram keyword to the title.

## Current role finding

As of the current official record:

- Page is an Alphabet co-founder and director.
- He chairs the board's Executive Committee.
- He is not an executive officer.
- He held 27.4 percent of total voting power as of April 6, 2026.
- Page and Brin together held 52.7 percent of total voting power.
- He was elected to another director term on June 5, 2026.
- Alphabet says every director attended at least 75 percent of applicable 2025 board and committee meetings.
- Public material does not disclose Page's exact attendance, advice to management, press strategy, or day-to-day workload.

## Type 5 evidence and counterevidence

### Evidence that supports Type 5

- Page's first-person childhood account shows sustained interest in mechanisms, electronics, reverse engineering, and building.
- The PageRank story shows a research question becoming a scalable information system.
- His stated management philosophy favors technical depth and breakthrough architecture.
- Dual-class governance and later holding-company design preserve decision space and delegate repeated operating decisions.
- His 2019 transition reduced executive visibility while retaining formal strategic rights.

### Evidence that complicates Type 5

- Page led Google directly and reorganized it forcefully, behavior compatible with Type 8 energy or a highly active Type 1.
- His wide, speculative project portfolio resembles Type 7 possibility-seeking.
- The IPO letter's mission, public-good, and principled long-term language supports a Type 1 reading.
- Long partnership, delegated authority, and the orderly Pichai handoff can support a Type 9 reading.
- Page told Wired in 2013 that he liked talking to everyone, which directly complicates a blanket social-withdrawal story.
- Property ownership and low press visibility carry little typing value without evidence of motive.

### Result

Type 5 remains the strongest single interpretation because technical depth, information control, systems leverage, and selective involvement recur across different periods and institutions. The evidence does not justify a confident 5w6, subtype, private feeling, or exclusive verdict.
