1. `OUTRO` and `B34M` move Tara from DJ curation toward releasing music under her own name. They strengthen the primary Type 3 alternative and prevent the article from reducing expansion to a 3 wing by default.
2. The Mindset Tour documentary shows the cost of performance and her own post-set evaluation. The same scene supports Type 2 through audience happiness, Type 3 through competence and public error, and Type 7 through the threat of a fun event becoming heavy.

## Personality counterevidence

### Type 2 remains the lead interpretation

The case now rests on Tara-specific repetition. She defines success as changing a viewer's day, calls the audience her priority, returns to whether a difficult set was fun for everyone, and is described by Jake Webber as teaching closeness. The article labels the inferred motive as editorial interpretation rather than Tara's private truth.

### Type 3 is a serious primary alternative

WME representation, national touring, fashion and media expansion, concern with sustaining growth, control of format, and two original music releases can support an achievement-first reading. The article explains why Type 2 stays slightly ahead: when Tara can answer with status markers, she repeatedly chooses audience effect.

### Type 7 is a serious primary alternative

Parties, motion, new formats, fun as legacy, dislike of restriction, and her direct preference for light content can support a novelty and pain-avoidance reading. The limiting evidence is that Tara often describes fun as something she creates for the room.

### Type 6 is a secondary alternative

Privacy, preparation, fear of cruel reactions, and control of narrative support a vigilance reading. It explains pressure behavior less completely than it explains her repeated audience-centered value test.

Culture is treated as identity context only. It is never used as Enneagram evidence.
