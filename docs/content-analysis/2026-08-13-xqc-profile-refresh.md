## Personality evidence

### For Type 7

- Across esports collapse, format shifts, platform choices, and gambling, the repeated observable move is toward another option or source of stimulation.
- xQc described professional danger as exhilarating.
- The non-exclusive Kick structure preserved optionality alongside the reward.
- Gambling creates repeated anticipation and rapid resets, although the 2026 payment disclosure means motivation cannot be reduced to stimulation.

### Against Type 7

- He sustained a nearly six-hour cooking project in March 2026.
- He returned for a second long HealthyGamerGG conversation four years after the first.
- He accepted his father's intervention during the 2022 gambling discussion.
- He revisited his feud with Kai Cenat, named his own harmful role, and expressed regret over a burned bridge.

### Complicating the verdict

- Type 6 is the strongest alternative because trust, betrayal, threat sensitivity, loyalty, and backup plans recur in the public record.
- The Type 7 verdict would weaken if calm, noncommercial evidence consistently showed that security and loyal bonds organize his choices before novelty does.
- Type 8 explains confrontational style, but the observed choices tend to multiply routes rather than consolidate control.
- Long duration alone does not falsify Type 7 when the activity continues to supply novelty, mastery, or stimulation.
