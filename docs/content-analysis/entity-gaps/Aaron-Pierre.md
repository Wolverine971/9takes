## Ownable thesis

**Driving contradiction:** the actor whose size, voice, and roles command a room learned early to
manage how much of himself he released into it.

**Type hypothesis:** Enneagram Type 1 with a 9 wing, probably self-preservation-led.

**Why it is ownable:** Pierre repeatedly describes the desired outcome in ethical and internal terms:
give every audience the full effort, find truth and authenticity, make a contribution, and leave a
project having earned his own respect. He has passed up the more visible choice when it did not feel
right. This is a better fit for a standards-led Type 1 than a success-led Type 3. His quietness and
careful impact make Type 9 a real alternative, but his response to error is correction and harder
work more often than numbing or accommodation.
