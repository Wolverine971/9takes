### The five questions, answered

1. **How many top-ten results are dedicated, substantial biographies of this exact person?**
   On the core name query, roughly five to six — Wikipedia, Meta official, Forbes, Fortune, The Week,
   Entrepreneur. That is a saturated biography SERP, not a weak one.
2. **Is there a personal Wikipedia page?** Yes. `en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandr_Wang`, a personal
   article, not a company page. This alone triggers the -20 penalty.
3. **Does a strong publisher own the general biography intent?** Several do, simultaneously. Forbes
   owns net-worth intent, Wikipedia owns life-timeline intent, Meta owns role intent, Fortune and
   CNBC own the current-narrative intent.
4. **Would a reader still need multiple searches after opening the current results?** Mostly no for
   facts. Yes for _interpretation_ — no top result explains what kind of operator Wang is or why the
   LeCun critique lands. That interpretive gap is real but it is not a biography gap.
5. **Can 9takes offer the strongest general-interest page?** No. We cannot out-authority Wikipedia and
   Forbes on a subject they have covered since 2021.

The only genuine weakness is the **fact-query tail** (age, parents, girlfriend), where content farms
dominate. That tail is low-value, partly unanswerable, and partly unsafe to chase. It is not worth a
profile on its own.

## Claims to avoid or qualify

- **Do not publish a bare age.** Sources currently in circulation say "28," which was written before
  his January 2026 birthday; born January 1997, he is 29 as of August 2026. Write the birth month and
  year and let the reader do the arithmetic. The content farms are propagating a stale number.
- **Do not name a partner, girlfriend, or spouse.** Kiernan Shipka and Lucy Guo both circulate with
  zero evidentiary basis. Repeating either — even to debunk — risks amplifying it.
- **Do not publish a net-worth figure.** Point to Forbes' real-time profile instead.
- **Do not state the Llama 4 benchmark allegation as fact.** It is LeCun's characterization in an FT
  interview; attribute it to him explicitly, every time.
- **Do not assert Wang is being pushed out.** The "first casualty of the restructuring" framing traces
  to commentary (Medium), not reporting. Treat as speculation or omit.
- **Do not treat LeCun's critique as neutral.** LeCun departed to found a competing company and holds
  a prior architectural commitment against LLMs. His criticism is real, on the record, and worth
  building on — but he is an interested party and the page must say so.
- **Do not diagnose.** Standard rule; especially load-bearing on a living, litigious-adjacent subject
  at the center of an active corporate conflict.
- Verify the exact Scale AI valuation and Meta stake figures against a primary source at draft time;
  reporting varies between ~$29B valuation / $14.3B for 49%.
