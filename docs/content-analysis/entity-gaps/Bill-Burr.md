## Rubric divergence — read before drafting

A different question — **is Bill Burr a good 9takes personality-analysis subject?** — comes back yes,

  typology databases and forums (boo.world, So Syncd, sakinorva, Personality Database comments,

- **9takes has proof this surface works.** In the 2026-08-13 GSC window,
  `/personality-analysis/joe-rogan` drew 947 impressions at avg position 9.9, and the query
  `joe rogan personality type` sat at **position 7.7 with 1.39% CTR**. That is 9takes competing on
  page one for exactly this query shape against exactly these competitors.
- **The comedian cluster exists but is thin:** Rogan (947 imps), Dave Chappelle (20), Andrew Schulz
  (12), Kevin Hart (no window data). Nate Bargatze sits in the backlog queue. Burr slots in and gives
  the cluster a second genuinely high-demand anchor.
- **The existing typing is contestable, which is the whole opportunity.** The databases split — Boo
  and So Syncd say **6w7**, SunSigns and personalitylist say **ISTP**, PDB commenters argue **ESTP**.
  None engages the evidence. The counter-phobic reading is available and defensible: _"I don't give a
  f*** what these phony f***ing people are saying"_ is not detachment, it is a Six under pressure
  performing not-caring — and the therapy record underneath it is the tell.

**Conclusion:** proceed, but on a narrowed target. Do not write "the definitive Bill Burr biography."
Write the page that argues, from his own nineteen years of tape, why the angriest man in comedy is
better read as the most frightened.

## Content requirements

- **H1 stays `Bill Burr`.** No cleverness in the visible headline.
- **SEO title = name + falsifiable thesis.** The thesis must be arguable, e.g. the gap between the
  rage persona and a documented, self-reported fear-management program. Avoid pure-typology titles
  that read as database bait.
- **Open with who he is and why he matters now** — comedian, _Breaking Bad_, _F Is for Family_,
  _Monday Morning Podcast_, and _The Social Reckoning_ in October — **before** assuming fandom.
- **One organizing contradiction**, not a list of traits: the anger that made him famous is the thing
  he has spent the most private effort trying to regulate, and he has narrated that effort in public
  for nineteen years.
- **Argue the type against the databases.** Name that the consensus says 6w7 or ISTP, then show the
  evidence. A page that merely asserts a number is indistinguishable from boo.world.
- **Use the Riyadh episode as the live character test**, held in tension rather than resolved.
  Present the HRW condemnation, the named peer criticism, and his own "People are cool. Governments
  are the problem" defense. Do not adjudicate his morality; read the *pattern* — the immediate
  counterattack, the preemptive dismissal of critics as "phony," the refusal to be seen flinching.
- **Answer only the safe fact queries**, in prose or FAQ metadata: age, wife, children (unnamed),
  parents, education. Skip siblings and net worth entirely.
- **Mine the podcast directly.** The differentiator no competitor can match is nineteen years of
  first-person tape. A page built from secondary articles wastes the only real moat.
- Canonical URL, citations, author identity, and real-entity links (Nia Renée Hill, Emerson College,
  _The Social Reckoning_, Human Rights Watch) must be correct.
- **Cross-link** into the comedian cluster: Dave Chappelle, Joe Rogan, Andrew Schulz, Kevin Hart —
  all Riyadh festival co-billers, which makes the link contextual rather than decorative.
- No word count prescribed.

## Claims to avoid or qualify

- **Net worth — do not publish any figure.** Sourced estimates range $8M–$120M with no methodology.
- **Siblings — do not publish a count.** Content farms say three and five; one names his parents as
  "Bill senior and Brenda," which contradicts Wikipedia's Robert and Linda Ann. Wikipedia is silent.
  If a sibling matters to a story beat, source it to a specific podcast episode or drop it.
- **Do not name his children.** Birth months/years only, and only if the biography spine needs them.
- **Do not diagnose.** "Emotional abuse" is *his* framing of *his* childhood — attribute it to him
  every time. Anger is not a disorder, therapy is not pathology, and psilocybin use is a disclosed
  personal detail, not a character claim.
- **Do not launder the Riyadh episode in either direction.** Not "he was brave," not "he was bought."
  Report HRW's position, the named critics, and his own words, and analyze the reflex rather than
  the ethics.
- **"Radio degree"** is a content-farm artifact. Wikipedia says communications, Emerson, 1993.
- **The 2006 Philadelphia rant** is well documented but is a set piece the internet has flattened.
  If used, note that he has since expressed regret — that regret is more diagnostic than the rant.
- **The helicopter pilot detail** is real and beloved but is podcast-sourced. Attribute it to his own
  telling, not to a bio page.
- **Verify at draft time:** the exact release year of the _Drop Dead Years_ streaming album (Aug 1;
  year not confirmed in this audit), and whether Burr was personally an Emmy nominee versus the
  special being nominated in craft categories.
