# Emerging Entity Gap Packet: Cara Delevingne

## Headline: not an entity gap, but a defensible typology-lane create

Cara Delevingne **fails** the Emerging Entity Gap test outright, and it is not close. She scores
**37/100 — PASS**. The exact-name SERP is fully owned: a complete personal Wikipedia biography, her
own official site, IMDb plus a separate IMDb biography page, Instagram, TikTok, Spotify, the BoF 500
profile, TV Guide, Fandango, and live 2026 coverage from WWD and NBC News. Her sister Poppy and her
grandmother Angela have their own Wikipedia articles. 9takes cannot become the biography of record
for Cara Delevingne and should not attempt it.

The `create` recommendation is therefore **re-scoped, not an entity-gap endorsement** — the same
disposition already on record for [Charlize-Theron](Charlize-Theron.md) (score 40, classification
pass, action create). It rests on two things the entity-gap rubric does not score:

1. **Cluster completion.** 18 directly adjacent people are already published, including her entire
   model cohort (`gigi-hadid`, `bella-hadid`, `kendall-jenner`, `kaia-gerber`,
   `emily-ratajkowski`), her _Suicide Squad_ castmates (`margot-robbie`, `will-smith`,
   `jared-leto`), the squad-era social cohort (`taylor-swift`, `selena-gomez`, `katy-perry`),
   and `anna-wintour`, `anne-hathaway`, `zendaya`, `harry-styles`, `rihanna`, `riz-ahmed`,
   `amber-heard`. Delevingne is arguably _the_ defining model of the 2010s Instagram-model era and
   is the conspicuous hole in a cluster 9takes has otherwise finished.
2. **A genuinely weak typology sub-SERP.** `cara delevingne enneagram` / `personality type` is served
   only by crowd-vote databases and thin generated pages — Personality Database (three separate
   competing profiles), Sakinorva, Boo, PersonalityCafe, MBTI Oracle, Level Life Up. No credible
   publisher holds that query family. The crowd consensus is 7w6, which is exactly the reading her
   own account contradicts.

If the pipeline proceeds, it must target the typology sub-SERP and accept that bare-name traffic will
not come. A generic "who is Cara Delevingne" page is wasted effort.

Caveat on demand shape: her name has carried high, roughly persistent search volume for over a
decade. This catalyst re-activates existing demand rather than creating an emerging entity. That is

**Signature contradiction (the ownable thesis):** Delevingne's entire commercial value in the 2010s

catalyst that finally let her make the music that had "always been lurking in the background."
The aristocrat from Belgravia built a global career on being the chaotic un-posh one; the woman
whose product was uninhibitedness found her actual voice only after she stopped being uninhibited.
The crowd databases type her 7w6 on the strength of the persona. The persona is the part she has
publicly disowned. That gap — between the type the internet assigned her from the performance and
the account she gives of the performance's cost — is the page.

## Content requirements

If the pipeline proceeds under the re-scoped rationale:

- **H1 stays the exact person name.** Visible H1 comes from `formatPersonalityDisplayName()`, not
  the `title:` frontmatter field.
- **SEO title = name + a distinctive, falsifiable thesis** aimed at the typology query family, not
  at "who is Cara Delevingne."
- **Open by establishing who she is and why she matters in 2026** — album, tour, campaign — before
  assuming the reader remembers the Burberry years. Do not assume fandom.
- **One contradiction organizes the piece:** performed uninhibitedness as a product versus her own
  account of what it cost. Do not run a parallel second thesis.
- **Sourced life/career spine, not a resume dump.** Belgravia and the Stevens/Sheffield lineage
  matter only because they sharpen the anti-posh brand contradiction; include them for that reason
  or cut them.
- **Answer the legitimate fact queries concisely in prose or FAQ metadata** — age, parents, school,
  current work. Do not expand them into a fact farm; they are table stakes, not the differentiator.
- **The typing must be argued against the crowd reading.** A page that restates 7w6 adds nothing to
  a SERP that already has six pages saying 7w6.
- Canonical URL, citations, author identity and real-entity links correct.
- No word-count target. Note the corpus-wide 4,500-word lint ceiling if a second pass is run.

## Claims to avoid or qualify

- **Never present a psychiatric diagnosis.** Depression, ADHD and substance use are **self-reported**
  and must be attributed to her own on-record statements every time. 9takes does not diagnose.
- **Minke (Leah Mason)** is nameable — she appeared with Delevingne on the GLAAD red carpet on
  March 5, 2026, and Delevingne has credited her on record with supporting her sobriety. Keep it to
  exactly what those sources establish. Do not infer a residence, a timeline, a marriage, or a
  household.
- **The Amber Heard relationship** rests on Delevingne's own 2026 confirmation. Report it as her
  statement and stop there. Do not narrate it, and do not touch the surrounding Depp/Heard
  litigation — that is a defamation-risk zone with no upside for this page.
- **No net worth figure.** Every result offering one is a content farm.
- **Do not chase the 2026 red-carpet layer.** A large share of current coverage is body- and
  outfit-focused ("sideboob," "sheer back"). Those impressions are gossip-shaped; capturing them is
  not a win. If a published page later shows low CTR on that query family, that is the _correct_
  outcome — mark it `do_not_optimize: true` rather than optimizing.
- **Unverified, do not use without checking:** an NBC News video result referenced a California home
  destroyed by fire. Date and details were not confirmed in this audit.
- **Verify the podcast citation** (show name and date) before it enters a draft.
- Early outlets sometimes conflate her with sister **Poppy Delevingne**, also a model. Check every
  attribution.
