# Emerging Entity Gap Packet: Carl Jung

**Verdict in one line:** Carl Jung is not an Emerging Entity Gap — the exact-name SERP is one of
the strongest biography fortresses on the web — but the backlog's actual rationale
(psychology-hub-cluster) is a different, still-defensible play that targets the thin typology
long-tail, not general biography intent. This packet scores the entity-gap model honestly (PASS)
and then specifies what the page must and must not attempt if the pipeline proceeds under the hub
rationale.

## Why now

- Backlog status: `carl-jung` is the current in-progress queue item (priority 59, added
  2026-07-18, started 2026-08-13), rationale "psychology-hub-cluster: evergreen search, natural
  internal-link hub between people blogs and the Enneagram corpus."
- Catalyst: there is no dated, rising catalyst. Demand is **saturated-evergreen**, not emerging.
  The nearest thing to a current catalyst is the continued mainstreaming of "shadow work"
  (2026 coverage shows it moving into clinical practice and self-help mass culture), which keeps
  Jung-adjacent queries durable. This is sustained demand, not a window.
- Demand evidence is **directional only**: no trend tool was used; evidence is the density and
  commercial maturity of the SERP ecosystem (dedicated institutes, publishers, book editions,
  active 2026 content targeting Jung topics). Local GSC (2026-05-01 → 2026-07-30) shows
  essentially zero Jung demand for 9takes — one impression for "cillian murphy jung" — which is
  expected with no page and says nothing about market demand.

## Biography-intent map

Intent breadth is real and large, but every family is already answered authoritatively. Recorded
so the pipeline knows what to _reference_ (for reader satisfaction) versus what to _target_ (for
search).

- **Core identity (reference, don't target):** founder of analytical psychology; coined
  introvert/extravert, archetypes, collective unconscious; the 1913 break with Freud.
- **Life and career (reference, don't target):** Kesswil 1875 → pastor's son → Burghölzli →
  Freud collaboration and split → self-experimentation years (Red Book, 1914–1930) →
  Bollingen → died Küsnacht 1961.
- **Fact queries (reference, don't target):** wife Emma Jung (m. 1903, five children, analyst in
  her own right, d. 1955); died June 6, 1961, age 85; _The Red Book_ published 2009.
- **Typology queries (the only targetable family):** "carl jung enneagram type," "carl jung
  personality type," "carl jung mbti/INFJ vs INTP," "what enneagram type was carl jung." Live
  disagreement exists: popular sources type him 5w4; Riso–Hudson typed him 9w1; MBTI community
  splits INFJ/INTP/INTJ. Contested typing of the father of typology is an honest, ownable angle.

## Content requirements

The entity-gap play (own general biography intent) is **not viable** — do not attempt it. If the
pipeline proceeds under the psychology-hub-cluster rationale, the requirements change from the
standard template:

- H1 remains "Carl Jung"; the SEO title must carry the typology thesis, not "biography" — the
  falsifiable-thesis pattern should sit on the contested-type angle (e.g., the father of
  personality typing is the type system's unsolved case). Never put "biography" or "bio" in the
  title; that aims the page at the fortress.
- Target queries: the `carl jung enneagram type` / `personality type` family only. General
  biography facts appear as a compact, sourced spine for reader satisfaction — never as the
  page's search thesis.
- Present his Enneagram type as **contested and argued**, not declared: lay out 5w4 vs 9w1 with
  the reasoning behind each, then make 9takes' own case. This fits the site's
  clustering-hypothesis positioning and is more credible than another hobbyist verdict.
- Hub duty is load-bearing: this page exists to interlink the Enneagram corpus (type pillars,
  shadow/masking content) with the people corpus (typology-adjacent figures). Plan explicit
  internal links both directions; that is most of the strategic value.
- FAQ metadata should answer the typology questions plus at most 2–3 fact queries (wife, death,
  Red Book) with tightly sourced one-paragraph answers.

## Claims to avoid or qualify

- **Jung is not part of the Enneagram's lineage.** The Enneagram of personality comes through
  Ichazo and Naranjo. Do not imply Jung created, used, or endorsed it. The honest frame is
  retrospective typing of Jung, and conceptual kinship (types, shadow, individuation).
- **Do not state a definitive type as fact** — his typing is genuinely contested (5w4 vs 9w1;
  INFJ vs INTP). The contest is the content.
- **Quote hygiene is critical.** Jung is one of the most fake-quoted figures on the internet.
  Every quote must be traced to a primary text or the collected works; when in doubt, cut.
- **Affairs (Toni Wolff, Sabina Spielrein):** documented in mainstream scholarship, but handle
  as sourced biography, not insinuation; National Geographic and Bair are adequate anchors.
- **1930s Nazi-era conduct and antisemitism accusations:** genuinely contested scholarly
  territory. Either treat with careful named sourcing or omit; never a drive-by mention.
- **His mother's mental illness and his childhood:** use only what MDR and biographies support;
  no diagnostic language.
- No etiology claims anywhere (standing do-not-write list applies).
- Standard rule: no invented birthdays, net worth, or medical/diagnostic claims.
