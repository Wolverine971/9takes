## Why now

**Catalyst: _The Odyssey_, released 2026-07-17. Rising, not peaked.**

- Crossed **$1.008 billion worldwide in three weeks** — Nolan's first film since _The Dark Knight Rises_ (2012) to clear $1B ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/box-office/the-odyssey-surpasses-1-billion-christopher-nolan-1236830121/), 2026-08).

- $429.65M domestic / $578.8M international; **$140M of it in IMAX**. First feature ever shot entirely on IMAX cameras.

Two independent attention signals: sustained trade box-office coverage across Variety and Deadline
through August, and the IMAX-70mm presale sellout a full year before release (reported first in film
history). Demand is **directional-strong** — no Trends/volume tool was available; this is inferred from
dated trade coverage and release performance, not measured.

Timing note: the audit lands ~4 weeks into the wave with **no indexed 9takes URL** (`published = false`).
The window is open but 9takes has zero incumbency in it.

## Biography-intent map

**Core identity** — who he is, why he matters now, _The Odyssey_. Fully owned by Wikipedia/Britannica.

**Life and career** — Anglo-American childhood split between Illinois and London, Haileybury, UCL,
_Following_ on $6,000, the Warner Bros. rupture, the Universal move. Owned, but thinly _interpreted_.

**Fact queries** — age (55, b. 1970-07-30), wife (**Emma Thomas**, m. 1997, four children, produced
every one of his features), brother (Jonathan Nolan), knighthood (2024), net worth. All answered
authoritatively elsewhere.

**Recommendation: do not chase the fact-query bucket.** The existing draft's FAQ set correctly skips
age/wife/net-worth and instead answers _why no phone_, _why the dialogue is inaudible_, _why the
secrecy_, _why The Odyssey_. Those are the questions with real search need and **no authoritative
answer on page one**. Adding an age/spouse/net-worth FAQ block here would be exactly the
"personality profile → SEO fact farm" conversion this command prohibits, and would lose to Wikipedia anyway.

### Rubric divergence: why the action is CREATE at a WATCHLIST score

The rubric gates CREATE at ≥60 and this scores 47, so **this recommendation overrides the stated gate.**
Flagging that explicitly rather than reverse-engineering the score upward.

The gate exists to ration _creation effort_ toward winnable SERPs. That effort is already spent: an
8,061-word draft graded 8.8 (B+, rubric v2, high confidence) has been sitting unpublished since
2026-07-22. The marginal decision is publish-or-shelve, and the marginal cost is a figures refresh plus
the quote verifications. Withholding a finished, evidence-dense page during the largest name-demand
spike of Nolan's career because it cannot outrank Britannica on `christopher nolan biography` would be
optimizing the wrong variable.

What the 47 _should_ change: **expectations, not the ship decision.** Do not brief this page as a
biography play, do not measure it on exact-name position, and do not spend a second round of effort
trying to broaden it toward general-interest coverage. Ship it as a personality-cluster page riding a
catalyst, and judge it on that cluster alone.
