## Claims to avoid or qualify

- **Parents' careers. This one is a warning, not an opening.** Wikipedia's parent sentence cites two
  sources:
  (a) the Smithsonian NASM Wall of Honor entry for "Capt David McLain Desbordes". It is real, but it
  **never mentions Druski or any family**, and nobody is named as its submitter.
  (b) _Earn The Necklace_, a content farm. That page sources the mother's details to a LinkedIn
  profile. The farm lists her employers as the **State of Georgia** Department of Public Health,
  Georgia DHR, the CDC, the DeKalb County Board of Health, and Georgia Vital Records. So Wikipedia's
  "worker at the Department of State" is **most likely a garble of "state departments,"** and the
  draft copies it. "Decorated" appears in **no** source. The master's degree rests only on the farm's
  reading of a private LinkedIn profile. **Verdict:** the parents' names are established; their
  mother's on-record podcast appearance and Wikipedia both support them. Cut the career and degree
  details unless a primary source (Druski or a parent on record) ties them to him.
- **Never let these farm claims in:** a minor younger sister named with her birth year (Earn The
  Necklace); "Black Jamaican and White German" ethnicity (gazettedirect); a wife or children
  (houseandwhips); height; phone number (a real query in GSC).
- **Net worth:** Forbes publishes earnings. Use "creator earnings" with the year.
- **Forbes rank:** Wikipedia prints "#9 in 2025" and aggregators disagree for 2026 (6th, 7th, or 9th).
  Print dollar figures only.
- **Relationship status:** only the Rubi Rose 2024 on-record statements. No current partner claims.
- **Parham suit:** "dismissed for failure to prosecute" (December 2025). Never write "cleared" or
  "exonerated."
- **BET saxophone play-off:** keep the production source's account (producers made the call; Druski
  was backstage) next to Punch's criticism. Quote only "weird for that Sax stunt", because the full post
  contains a slur.
- **Depression:** use his words about one college semester. No clinical labels, and no implied
  ongoing condition.
