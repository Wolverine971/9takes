## Biography-intent map

- **Core identity:** Queen frontman, born Farrokh Bulsara (Sept 5, 1946, Stone Town, Zanzibar),
  died Nov 24, 1991 (age 45); four-octave range; "Bohemian Rhapsody." Fully answered by
  incumbents — cover in one grounding paragraph, do not target.
- **Life and career:** Parsi-Indian parents (Bomi and Jer Bulsara); British boarding school in
  India; family fled the 1964 Zanzibar Revolution to Middlesex; Smile → Queen (1970–71); Live Aid
  1985; solo Mr. Bad Guy / Barcelona; final years in Garden Lodge. Fully answered — use as spine,
  not as target queries.
- **Fact queries (answerable, sourced):** never married; called Mary Austin his common-law "wife,"
  she held his hand at his death and scattered his ashes secretly; Jim Hutton was his partner
  1985–1991 (they wore matching rings); AIDS announced publicly Nov 23, 1991, died of
  bronchopneumonia resulting from AIDS the next day. Good FAQ-metadata material, not page thesis.
- **The unowned intent:** "what was Freddie Mercury actually like / personality type / was he shy."
  The famous Mercury line — "All my lovers asked me why they couldn't replace Mary… I couldn't fall
  in love with a man the same way as I did with Mary" — is quoted everywhere but analyzed nowhere.

## Claims to avoid or qualify

- **Sexuality labels:** he told Mary Austin he was bisexual and never publicly adopted a label.
  Report his statements and relationships; do not assign a definitive label.
- **Diagnosis date:** the 1987 HIV diagnosis is reported (Hutton dates it to spring 1987) but was
  never publicly confirmed by Mercury; only the Nov 23, 1991 statement and Nov 24 death are hard
  dates. Cause of death: bronchopneumonia resulting from AIDS.
- **The 2024 "secret daughter" claim** (Lesley-Ann Jones, _Love, Freddie_): contested and
  unverified; not established biography. Omit, or attribute strictly to the author with the
  dispute noted — verify current status before any use.
- **No posthumous diagnosis:** personality analysis is interpretive pattern-reading from sourced
  behavior, never clinical claims (mental health, HIV-era conduct, or otherwise).
- **Estate/net-worth figures:** frequently garbled; use only well-sourced facts (Austin inherited
  Garden Lodge and the largest estate share) and no invented numbers.
- **Hutton's memoir tone:** _Mercury and Me_ details his final days intimately and fans dispute
  parts of its framing; quote it for facts and lived testimony, not for sensational deathbed color.
