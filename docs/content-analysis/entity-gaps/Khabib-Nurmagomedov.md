## Content requirements

The entity-gap play is dead on arrival; do not build a general-interest biography page. If the
nightly pipeline continues with Khabib as a standard personality-analysis profile (consistent with
the demand-drives-traffic finding), constrain it as follows:

- Target the niche intent only: personality type, enneagram, the psychology of the retirement, the
  father relationship, discipline/faith queries. Do not chase `bio`, `age`, `wife`, `net worth`.
- H1 stays the exact name; SEO title = name + falsifiable personality thesis (the
  control-as-surrender contradiction), not "Biography".
- Open by locating him for non-MMA readers in two sentences, then go straight to the thesis. The
  life spine (Sildi → Makhachkala → UFC → UFC 254) serves the analysis; it must not read as a
  resume, because Wikipedia already owns the resume.
- Evidence-quote from the first-person record (PBD, Lex Fridman, retirement speech) and named
  third parties (Cormier, Mendez), per the quote-pinning transcript workflow.
- FAQ metadata: answer only what can be sourced and what the niche reader actually wants — "what
  enneagram type is Khabib?", "why did Khabib really retire?", "why does Khabib never show his
  wife?" (answered as the sourced privacy stance, not an identity reveal).

- **Father's death:** phrase as reported — heart condition with COVID-19 complications, July 2020.
- **Personality typing:** present 8w9 as 9takes' argued analysis; never as fact or diagnosis. The
  typing-site consensus is unsourced and should not be cited as evidence.
- **Folklore calibration:** the age-9 bear-wrestling footage is real but heavily mythologized; the
  "wrestled a bear to build a champion" framing is embellishment. Use the footage, skip the legend.
- **Post-2023 timeline:** he announced a full exit from MMA in Jan 2023 "to focus on family," then
  returned to cornering Islam later; verify exact dates during drafting before asserting either.
