He came fourth" closes it, with the record placed against the self-account in between: "He was the youngest self-made billionaire in the world at 24... Nobody sneaks in on those terms. Knowing why a man built a machine tells you nothing about how it treated the people inside it." Most drafts let the type explanation quietly absolve the subject. This one names the boundary.

- Rabbit hole is exemplary on honesty about its own limits: the 3w2 call names its own scope problem, the subtype section acknowledges the 9takes subtypes guide takes the other reading, the stress arrow refuses to invent a disintegration instance, and the counter-typing section names two findings that would overturn the call.
