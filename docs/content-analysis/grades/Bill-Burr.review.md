- The Riyadh two-tellings table is the best structural invention in the recent batch. Setting the
  October 2025 Conan appearance ("all of these sanctimonious c***s") against the unprompted August 10,
  2026 Monday Morning Podcast ("I had like fucking dry mouth for like the first 20 minutes") in a dated

  and it collapses on anyone who doesn't self-document. It is also original research: nobody else has

  twelve minutes, 10,000 people, no retreat — is turned by his own Bertcast explanation into a rescue of

  "The most feared act of aggression in his career started as a rescue" is earned by the evidence rather
  than asserted over it.

  there, running, readable to a half-point." That is the small specific moment that makes the type click,
