FEEDBACK (2026-09-01):

- The hook is the best in the recent batch and it is not decoration. "A cast is a sentence the body can say out loud" opens the piece, returns as the tie-breaking evidence against the Seven read ("what she wanted from the hospital was the cast: a token a stranger could read across a room"), and returns a third time in the close as the first item in the list of evidence other people found first. Three loads on one image, and it earns all three.
- "What Cara Delevingne's body kept saying for her" is a genuinely non-transferable form. A dated three-column ledger of moments where the pain became visible, each paired with her own account, would collapse on a subject without that record. It is the strongest structural invention in the batch alongside Naval's aphorism autopsy.
- The final act refuses the redemption arc, which is rare here. The album section turns the 4w3 shadow on _Not Normal_ itself — a record sold on rawness released inside a Rag & Bone campaign shot backstage (WWD) and a Topshop capsule (Retail Gazette) — and leaves it standing: "The album is not the test. The test is what she makes after the raw thing stops selling." Both anchors resolve clean; this one does not.
- The evidence work is above corpus norm in kind, not just degree. The editor pass transcribed the Call Her Daddy audio (YouTube, 1h52m) instead of trusting the Singju Post transcript, and that caught a real misdate — the fractures are childhood, not the modelling years — which killed a manufactured causal claim against the fashion industry. The crowd-vote claim is now verified and dated (Boo 1, Sakinorva 2, both 2018, plus a dissenting 3w4) rather than the usual hand-wave.

- NEEDS WORK — source concentration is the Evidence ceiling, and the draft knows it. All six load-bearing quotes in the source audit come from one podcast episode: Call Her Daddy, June 2026, three months ahead of an album release. Every sentence doing the typing work — the fractures, the cast, "I just wanted to feel pain," "I don't believe what I'm selling," "I didn't feel like I belonged in my body," "I loved not having to think about my mum" — is from a single promotional appearance, and the piece never says so. The counterargument section comes within one clause of admitting it ("from a better-sourced interview than most of what argues the other way"), and the repair written to address exactly this (P2-01, the sourcing-asymmetry paragraph) was skipped in both the editor and revision passes. That is the single reason Evidence is 8 and not 9.
- NEEDS WORK — the section-closer rhythm is metronomic. Six consecutive sections end on a short declarative in the same cadence: "None of it was a complaint about the hours." / "It waits." / "…which outlasts the drug." / "…refused three times and repeated anyway." / "The test is what she makes after the raw thing stops selling." / "This one she wrote, named, and declined to gloss." Fresh eyes flagged this at the paragraph level and the revision cut three one-line tics, but the pattern survived one level up. Individually each line is good; serially it is the house fingerprint, and it is the same tic the Alex-Warren C anchor was marked down for.
- NEEDS WORK — the speculative disclaimer is missing. Naval-Ravikant, Laura-Loomer and Zach-Bryan all close with one; this draft has zero hits for "speculative" or "disclaimer." That is a brand-risk gap, not a style nit, on a page carrying self-reported suicidality, an addiction history, and a named third-party allegation (Weinstein, "You can't be gay"). Add the standard block before publish.
- MINOR — the somatic section's title over-claims relative to its table, and the seam shows. "What Cara Delevingne's body kept saying for her" has to hedge in its own first sentence ("whether she put it there or it came on its own") because two of five rows are things she did deliberately, one is an illness, and one is what a photographer did. The close repeats the hedge as a triple ("by her body, or by her hands, or by strangers with cameras"). The P0-01 repair made the claim honest, which was right, but the metaphor now carries three different agents and the heading only names one.
- MINOR — "The brand was no filter. The note was filter." is a reversal-shape construction the contrast-pair regex does not catch (0 strong / 0 comparative is a clean tool result, not a clean page). One instance, not an engine, so no cap — but it is house grammar in a piece that otherwise runs on her vocabulary.
