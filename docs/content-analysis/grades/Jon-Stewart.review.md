FEEDBACK (2026-08-14):

- **The Chappelle / Hinchcliffe / Rogan section is the best argumentative writing in the people corpus.** Most Stewart coverage collapses those three into one "he defends his friends" story; this draft refuses to. It grades each defense separately and names what each one cost: Chappelle — "Knowing a friend's heart is evidence about intent, not a rebuttal about effect"; Hinchcliffe — "This looked less like personal loyalty than guild solidarity — a comedian protecting comedy's permission structure"; Rogan — "the most persuasive of the three defenses… The limit is scale." Then it lands the synthesis Cenac's own quote could not reach on its own: "he often evaluates a speaker's intention and right to continue before evaluating the concrete objection from the people on the receiving end." That paragraph is the reason this piece is a C and not a D.

- **"Childhood history may help explain Stewart's defensiveness; it cannot acquit it… Calling that a 'loyalty wound' and stopping there would turn psychology into defense counsel."** This is the anti-defense-counsel move the rubric has been asking for across the whole corpus, and it is executed in two sentences. Keep it verbatim through any revision.

- **The Larry Leibowitz thread is the signature detail.** One brother becomes COO of NYSE Euronext, the other becomes America's most prominent critic of Wall Street. "Same wound, opposite conversions" is the cleanest sibling-divergence frame in the corpus and it passes the swap test outright.

- **The modern half is sourced to a genuinely high standard.** The Apple TV+ section is a model: NYT reporting flagged as reporting ("That is reporting from people familiar with the decision, not an admission by Apple"), then Stewart's own on-record narrowing via the Lina Khan interview, then his refusal of the martyr story to Belloni ("They didn't censor me, it wasn't free speech"), then the harsher May 2026 characterization flagged as his and not Apple's. Four inline links, escalating specificity, honest about what each source can and cannot establish.

- **NEEDS WORK — the sourcing is split down the middle of the piece, and the wrong half is bare.** Everything from 2015 forward is inline-linked with outlet and date. Everything before roughly 1990 — which is where the thesis actually lives — has no sources at all. The draft asserts that one quote "contains the entire architecture of Jon Stewart's personality" and then does not tell the reader where the quote came from. A skeptical reader who checks the strong half will trust the piece; one who checks the childhood half finds nothing to check. This single split is what caps Evidence at 6 and costs the draft roughly 1.3 points of overall.

- **NEEDS WORK — the diagnosis section talks itself out of its own argument.** Six epistemic disclaimers cluster in and around the type analysis: "It does not prove the cause." / "That is a typology-based reading of Stewart's public story, not a research finding about him." / "it remains an interpretation, not a measured psychological state." / "These are first-person observations… not proof of a diagnosis." / "It is a refinement of the speculative typing, not a separate fact about Stewart." / "It cannot tell us, by itself, whether that pattern began with his father." Each is individually defensible. Together they are seven of the ten contrast pairs the deterministic report found, they read as legal review rather than prose, and they leave the reader with a type that has been proposed and then withdrawn. The frontmatter disclaimer and the standing disclaimer block already do this job once; the body should not do it six more times.

- **NEEDS WORK — the piece ends on a disclaimer.** Final line: "It cannot tell us, by itself, whether that pattern began with his father." That is a hedge, not a cut-to-black, and it fails the swap test — it could close any hedged profile in the corpus with the noun swapped. The real ending is sitting three paragraphs earlier: "He was describing other people. He was describing himself. He was describing the exact opposite of his father."

- **NEEDS WORK — no rabbit hole exists.** The 7w6 wing argument (the Colbert/Oliver/Bee/Noah lineage paragraph) and the stress-movement-to-Type-1 reading both sit in the main body, which is exactly the insider-typology bleed v2 penalizes.
