FEEDBACK (2026-08-27):

- **The Epictetus split is the best analytical move on the page.** "The version Epictetus drilled into students, at Discourses 3.10 in George Long's 1877 translation, ends 'blame what is wrong, in what is right rejoice.' Marcus ran the blame half for thirty years. The rejoicing half he wrote out as Book 1, and assigned every item in it to somebody else." That single paragraph does three jobs at once: it answers the objection the typing has to survive (every Stoic marked himself in an empty room), it produces the signature detail, and it sets up the empathy turn two sections later. This is what a novel thesis looks like on a subject with two thousand years of prior coverage.

- **Evidence handling is the strongest in the corpus for a pre-modern subject.** Every unreliable source is graded on the page rather than laundered: the Historia Augusta is introduced as "a late and famously unreliable set of imperial biographies," the Avidius Cassius letter is explicitly framed as "a charge someone found plausible," the death location is left disputed, and the Lyon 177 martyrdoms get a full four-sentence apparatus of what supports and undercuts the claim before landing on "It does not resolve, and it should not be smoothed over." The corroboration structure is also real, not decorative — the circus-games ridicule is carried by the HA _and_ by Fronto complaining about the same behavior decades earlier "with no reason to invent it."

- **The two-archive structure passes the swap test structurally, not just rhetorically.** "Two Archives, One Emperor" braids the Fronto correspondence against Meditations across thirty-three years, and the form genuinely collapses if transplanted — almost no other subject has two independent first-person archives three decades apart. The `<div class="timeline">` at 143 / c. 146 / c. 175 is the load-bearing element and it earns the component.

- **The empathy turn is built from his vocabulary, not house grammar.** No "armor," no "scar tissue," no "not X, it was Y." It runs on the debt language the subject himself supplies — penance, indebted, falling short — and it refuses to excuse: "That still made him hard to be around, and Fronto was right to push. Rome spent nineteen years reading the ledger as arrogance." That last clause is what keeps the turn from becoming an apology.

- **Rabbit hole is a model of the form.** The 1w2 call concedes its own weakness plainly ("On that axis Marcus reads 1w9, and the concession has to be made plainly"), the Type 5 rival gets a real discriminator rather than a dismissal ("A Five builds. This rehearses."), and the strongest objection is correctly identified as not being a rival type at all. The falsifier — "What would change our minds: evidence that the self-criticism was situational" — is answered by the structure of the piece itself. The growth-line-to-Seven read via the bread/figs/olives passage is a genuinely lovely catch.

- **Ledgers spot-check clean, which is unusual.** Verified three claims against the body: the HEADING MIX LEDGER's nine H2s match exactly; the interior beat is where the FORMULA FINGERPRINT LEDGER says it is; the Fronto "austere and unreasonable" testimony is in the body and attributed. - NEEDS WORK: **The aphoristic section-closer is the one visible house rhythm.** Seven of nine sections land on a compressed declarative fragment: "Two sources, a generation apart, watching him work through the show." / "Rome spent nineteen years reading the ledger as arrogance." / "At roughly fifty, running an empire, he was still marking his work against a dead man." / "Whatever he felt went into institutions." / "Everything you can buy at an airport with his face on it descends from that accident." Each one is good in isolation and they vary in function, which is why this did not trigger a cap. But read serially the cadence becomes predictable, and this is the pattern that reads as house-template across a batch. Vary two or three of them — let one section end mid-scene on a quote, let another end on an unresolved question.

- NEEDS WORK: **The Lyon paragraph is the only dense block on the page.** Seven sentences of qualification stacked into one paragraph ("Against that: no other contemporary source records the episode, the letter names no emperor, Marcus was weeks' march away, and Tertullian, alive then, called him a protector of Christians, on the strength of a document widely judged spurious"). The epistemics are correct and should not be cut, but the visual rhythm dies for the length of it while the rest of the piece breathes. Break it into two paragraphs at "Against that:" so the counter-evidence gets its own beat.

## Grader's note on the score

This is the highest grade issued in this corpus under v2, so the justification should be explicit against the inflation warning. Both required re-checks for an A-band score were run independently rather than taken from the draft's own ledgers: the sameness scan reports clear with zero shared phrases, and the citability audit reports 0 untagged load-bearing quotes. The A-band criteria are met item by item — novel thesis (the Epictetus blame/rejoice split), signature detail (Book 1 credits him with nothing, therefore rest is unearned spending), exceptional sourcing (page-pinned Loeb, graded reliability on every disputed source, a documented misattribution catch in the testimony ledger), and real tonal range (the polecat letter is funny, the seven buried children are devastating, the Forum auction is wry, Lyon is grave).

The one frame that is _not_ original to this draft is "the serene Stoic was actually anxious" — that is an established scholarly reading, and Yale UP's piece on Marcus and anxiety sits in the citations. What is original is the mechanism: the Type 1 machinery, the Epictetus split, and the debt-ledger read of Book 1. That distinction is why Originality is 9 and not 10.
