FEEDBACK (2026-10-07):

- The evidence base is the real strength. Nearly every quote carries an outlet and year, and roughly 40 of the subject's own statements span 2010 to October 2026. The ones I traced hold up almost word for word, which is rare in this corpus.
- "The Question of the Book Itself" faces critics squarely and with intelligence. Reading the PEN report past its headline ("prisons also ban medical textbooks, foreign-language dictionaries and works by celebrated Black authors... landing on the list proves little either way") is genuinely original critic handling. "True by count and disingenuous by emphasis" is the best sentence on the page.
- The signature details are earned and specific to Greene: about a hundred handwritten notebooks with the last pass "on the computer with one hand"; the funeral seen from above at the cemetery where his father is buried; "It takes me like 20 minutes to get dressed in the morning." The busing passage's "It's hard not to hear his anxious mother in that 'so worried'" is a connection no other analysis of him makes.
- NEEDS WORK (Evidence): the critic section's key move rests on an unsourced paraphrase ("His defense, that only a handful of the forty-eight laws are overtly manipulative, is true by count and disingenuous by emphasis") and then says Greene "rarely answers head-on". But in the same Rubin interview the page cites six times, he does answer it head-on: "it would be very disingenuous of me to act like I'm a saint. I have a dark side as well... So I was playing a double-edged game." Meanwhile that interview's "There is a naughty element in there" is moved into "The Two Faces" as self-knowledge ("Greene knows this about himself"), when "in there" means the book. "In 2026 he still stood by it" stretches a Perell quote about anger as creative fuel into a defense of the manipulation content. Smaller items: "five months" should be about four (his words); credit "48 is a better number" to Tetragrammaton 2023; source "Zen-like calmness" to Greene's 2009 post, or confirm Forbes.
- NEEDS WORK (Originality/Enneagram): the clinching Five evidence is post-stroke ("What I fear is being incapacitated... like I'm in a hospital"), and the page never meets the obvious objection that a 67-year-old who needs 20 minutes to dress would fear incapacity whatever his type. Greene's pre-stroke words are missing too: "I'm sort of an anxious person... it's kind of an anxiety about death" (Tetragrammaton, 2023). That line is a Six signal, and it also gives a real arc against his 2026 "I don't fear death itself". The Type 6 counterargument runs only two sentences. The body also slips into textbook Five language ("Many Type 5s describe a childhood that felt like that: unreadable." / "If I understand everything, nothing can touch me.").
- NEEDS WORK (Writing/Hook): the cold open ("Robert Greene's father sold chemical supplies for the same company for forty years.") is accurate but quiet, with no scene. "The Hustler's Philosopher" is the weakest section: it jumps from 2007 to junior high to Malcolm X to VICE 2014 and closes on an assertion ("that is recognition"). There are aphoristic section-closers ("They also make a wall."; "which is not the same as gone"; "the person who could explain exactly how their game worked") and one stock line ("Before Robert Greene was Robert Greene, he was nobody.").
- TO REACH B (8.0+): (1) Rewrite the critic paragraph around the verbatim Rubin 2023 concession ("disingenuous of me to act like I'm a saint... I was playing a double-edged game"), cut the unsourced "only a handful" paraphrase and "rarely answers head-on", and drop or re-source "In 2026 he still stood by it" (body and FAQ 3). (2) In "The Two Faces", replace "There is a naughty element in there" with "I have a dark side as well" from the same answer, or quote it with the book as its subject. (3) Fix "five months" to "four months" in the body and FAQ 4, and tag the Elffers and "Zen-like calmness" quotes.
- TO REACH B+ (8.5+): (4) Under "That is the Five's fear, stated plainly: helplessness.", add two or three sentences that meet the universality objection, anchoring the fear in pre-stroke behavior: routines vs. "like hell, like a nightmare" in 2019, and the vow "I'm never going to get emotional". Use the 2023 "anxiety about death" vs 2026 "I don't fear death itself" shift as the turn. (5) Replace "Many Type 5s describe a childhood that felt like that: unreadable." and the inner-thought "If I understand everything, nothing can touch me." with Greene's own words. (6) Tighten "The Hustler's Philosopher" to the busing origin plus 50 Cent, and cut the VICE coda's assertion. (7) Consider opening on the skip-tracing desk or the funeral-from-above image instead of the father's job.
