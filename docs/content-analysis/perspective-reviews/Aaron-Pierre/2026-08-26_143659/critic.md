## Bottom-line verdict

The sympathetic reading is earned. The draft admits publicity polish, cultural causes, financial and
gender context, mixed criticism of _Lanterns_, and three alternative types. One small causal claim
should be tightened.

## What landed

**CRITIC-H1:** “Slow speech can be a publicity technique. Privacy can make a rising actor easier to
mythologize.” This is the skeptical case the article needed. It interrupts the temptation to treat
every controlled interview answer as transparent evidence.

**CRITIC-H2:** The positive GQ review and mixed TechRadar review appear together. This keeps the live
release section from becoming franchise publicity.

## What missed

**CRITIC-C1:** In “The comments became so intense that the show closed them,” the word “that” asserts
causation. The packet establishes intense responses and a now-closed comment section. It does not
establish the show's reason for closing them. Reader effect: a small unsupported flourish makes a
careful article look willing to improve a viral story. Minimum repair: state the two facts without a
causal connector, such as “The responses became intense. The comment section was later closed.”
Expected benefit: factual precision. Confidence: high. Acceptance test: the live draft no longer
states or implies an official reason for the closure.

## What I expected

excuse. The roles caveat, cultural caveat, and alternative-type case meet that requirement.
