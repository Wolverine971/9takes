## Identity and scope

Aaron Stone Pierre is a British actor born 7 June 1994, raised in West Croydon. This packet audits a
personality-analysis draft arguing that his public evidence best fits Enneagram Type 1w9. The article
is about the actor, not the footballers who share his name. Knowledge cutoff: 26 August 2026.

The draft's type, wing, and instinct are interpretations. His birth date, career credits, quotations,
and reported behavior can be verified or attributed. His private motive cannot.

## Dated timeline

- **1994:** Born in Brixton, London; raised in West Croydon. Biography fact, supported by Wikipedia,
  GQ, and the Guardian.
- **About 2007-08:** Joins youth theater at 13 or 14 and performs in _Moby-Dick_. First-person
  recollection in GQ/AP.
- **2016:** Graduates from LAMDA. Biography fact.
- **2017:** First television work in _Prime Suspect: Tennison_. Biography fact.
- **2018:** Plays Cassio in _Othello_ at Shakespeare's Globe. Barry Jenkins sees him and later casts
  him in _The Underground Railroad_. Pierre says he chose stage over advice to seek more TV
  visibility.
- **2018-20 period:** Endures debt and unemployment, lives largely on potatoes, considers courier
  work. First-person account published by Men's Health in 2025; precise calendar dates unresolved.
- **2021:** Appears in _The Underground Railroad_ and _Old_.
- **2022:** Leads _Brother_.
- **2023:** Wins a Brazilian jiu-jitsu tournament medal, according to Men's Health.
- **2024:** Leads _Rebel Ridge_, voices Mufasa, plays Malcolm X, and is named an AP breakthrough
  entertainer. GQ later reports more than 30 million _Rebel Ridge_ streams in three days.
- **2025:** Appears on _The Morning Show_; Spirit Tunnel clip spreads widely; long-form Men's Health
  profile published.
- **3 August 2026:** GQ cover profile published.
- **16 August 2026:** _Lanterns_ premieres on HBO and HBO Max, confirmed by WBD.
- **2027:** _Star Wars: Starfighter_ and _Man of Tomorrow_ are announced. These dates are provisional.

## First-person evidence

- **Backstage, 12 June 2025:** Pierre says he wants to leave a project knowing he did his best and
  “earned my own respect,” adding that anything short does not sit well. This supports an internal
  work standard. It cannot by itself distinguish Type 1 from Types 3, 6, or other conscientious
  patterns.
- **Square Mile, 2021:** Pierre says an audience of one deserves the energy of an audience of one
  thousand, calls himself hypercritical about writing, and recounts correcting a first-set protocol
  error permanently. These statements support audience obligation, exactness, and correction.
- **Men's Health, 2025:** Pierre recounts debt, potatoes prepared seven ways, reluctance to burden
  other struggling people, and later learning to share the load. This supports the hardship and
  growth narrative. It does not establish a type or a clinical condition.
- **GQ, 3 August 2026:** Pierre says his belief in the power of words leads him to speak at “one mile
  per hour,” describes asking his sister for help after fame, and identifies himself through family
  roles before actor. These support deliberate speech, changed help-seeking, and family priority.
- **Men's Health video, 2025:** Pierre jokes about reaching for self-discipline at 2 a.m., then gives
  people grace around eating. This supports the opening's discipline-and-grace tension.

## Third-party testimony

- **Barry Jenkins, AP, 2024:** says he created scenes for Pierre and that Pierre's presence entered
  the “spiritual essence” of _The Underground Railroad_. Named collaborator testimony, Tier 2.
- **Chris Mundy, Men's Health, 2025:** describes Pierre's physically imposing presence alongside
  softness and thoughtfulness, ending, “You can't teach that.” Named showrunner testimony, Tier 2.
- **Don Johnson, Letterboxd video, 2024:** calls Pierre powerful and capable while also kind and
  pleasant off camera. Named co-star testimony, Tier 2.
- **Olive Pometsey, GQ, 2026:** observes long pauses, slow speech, philosophical abstraction, and a
  gentler off-screen manner than Pierre's action roles suggest. Reported observation, Tier 2.

## Public record, accomplishments, failures, and controversies

Pierre's public record is currently dominated by theater and screen work. The draft covers the main
career spine from LAMDA and Shakespeare's Globe through _The Underground Railroad_, _Rebel Ridge_,

No material public controversy was found that would need inclusion to prevent a misleadingly clean

## Enneagram hypothesis evidence

**For Type 1:** an internally judged work standard; equal effort as an obligation to an audience;
truth/authenticity vocabulary; correction after error; close revision; improving weaknesses; choosing
aligned theater work over visible television work.

**Against or limiting:** conscientious acting craft is not unique to Type 1. Body-and-voice
self-management also reflects racialized adultification and social learning. Financial secrecy can
reflect class, masculinity, pride, Type 9 self-minimizing, or Type 6 worry. Roles are casting evidence,
not personality evidence.

**Closest alternative:** Type 9, because of quietness, careful impact, solitude, and aversion to
burdening others. The discriminating evidence favoring Type 1 is the repeated correction-and-refinement
response and Pierre's explicit focus on the quality of his contribution.

**Second alternative:** Type 3, because of polish, physique, modeling, and franchise success. The
stage-over-visibility choice, equal effort for a tiny audience, fame withdrawal, and “own respect”
language weigh against image or recognition as the central motive.

**Theory limits:** wing, instinct, and connecting lines are presented as tentative. Stress responses
have real-world causes. The draft keeps the technical case in one accordion.
