## Identity and scope

**Verified fact:** Adéla Jergová, professionally styled ADÉLA, is a Slovak singer, songwriter, and dancer born November 27, 2003, in Bratislava. She trained in ballet, joined the HYBE x Geffen development program documented in _Pop Star Academy: KATSEYE_, was eliminated from _Dream Academy_ in 2023, and began releasing solo music independently in 2024.

**Scope:** The frozen article proposes Enneagram Type 3w4 as a motivational hypothesis. Public interviews, a long-form creator interview, documentary transcripts, collaborator testimony, and July 2026 album-announcement material form the evidence base. The knowledge cutoff is August 12, 2026. _PRIMA_ remains scheduled for September 4, 2026 at that cutoff.

**Interpretation:** The article's central claim is that Adéla uses defined work, measurable goals, and artistic construction to regain a coherent sense of self after uncertainty or rejection. This is a reading of public evidence, not an established fact about her private psychology.

## Public record, accomplishments, failures, and controversies

**Verified facts:** Adéla sustained international ballet training, entered the HYBE x Geffen program, was eliminated first after finishing last in the opening public vote, built an independent solo launch, then signed a major-label deal. These events support a career-recovery narrative.

**Attributed criticism:** Some viewers interpreted her documentary comments about Manon's attendance and popularity as bitter or mean. The article fairly identifies the strongest objection: Adéla treated visible attendance and effort as if they exhausted the fairness question. Community reactions establish audience expectations, not the truth of either trainee's private conduct.

**Counterevidence:** She has maintained friendships with trainees, publicly supported KATSEYE, and was shown helping others with choreography. These facts complicate a one-note antagonist reading without erasing the impact of the comments.

**Current status:** _PRIMA_ is announced rather than released. Statements about its meaning, collaborators, and planned date are current as of August 12, 2026 and need a post-September refresh.

## Enneagram hypothesis evidence

**Evidence for Type 3:** Early distant goals were translated into learnable tasks. Adéla describes attention and energy arriving through work and doing well. After an identity-disorganizing loss, she rebuilt direction with a professional mission statement, launch deadline, and solo body of work. Her current language foregrounds ambition and an uncertain capacity to feel arrival.

**Evidence against Type 3:** She accepts polarization, emphasizes personal taste, foregrounds outsider identity, and uses art for emotionally specific self-expression. These are compatible with Type 4 as a core type. Public ambition and discipline are also career-selected behaviors for a professional performer.

**Closest alternative:** Type 4. The article's discriminating claim is that identity loss was answered through a defined professional product and execution. That inference is plausible, not conclusive.

**Other surface alternative:** Type 8 can describe bluntness and power reclamation. Evidence returns more consistently to proof, reception, rank, and professional achievement.

**Behavior the type does not explain:** Her moral and aesthetic beliefs about sexuality, Eastern European identity, and the media treatment of women have content beyond an achievement strategy.

## Disputes and unresolved questions

- **Unresolved question:** Does family attention function exactly as the draft claims, or does home simply offer privacy and continuity? Only Adéla has described the boundary; relatives have not confirmed its emotional function.
- **Unresolved question:** Is achievement the primary motive or the professional vocabulary available in interviews about a rising career? Public material cannot eliminate selection bias.
- **Disputed interpretation:** The Manon comments can be read as a principled fairness complaint, interpersonal resentment, documentary editing, or some combination. The draft should keep responsibility and uncertainty visible.
- **Unresolved question:** 3w4 and the social/sexual ordering remain lower-confidence extensions of the core hypothesis.
- **Time-sensitive fact:** _PRIMA_ release status, track content, and reception cannot be described beyond the July 2026 announcement at this cutoff.

## Research limitations

The packet has no independent family interview, therapist material beyond Adéla's public comments, or complete unedited _Dream Academy_ footage. The documentary and retrospective interviews are selected narratives. English subtitles were unavailable for one MTV video, and its metadata-only transcript was excluded from evidence. No claim about diagnosis, trauma, or private intent can be verified from this record. The artist's rapid career growth makes all 2026 status claims vulnerable to change.
