An informed fan would recognize the work ethic, Dream Academy rupture, self-authored visual project, polarizing humor, and the meaning of _PRIMA_. The piece offers a shareable connection between ballet correction, the public-vote conflict, and the moving career rank. It understands her process better than it describes the music, leaving the artist's sound and two defining releases underrepresented.

I expected the KATSEYE relationships to be handled without fandom mythology. The draft meets that expectation by showing both criticism and care and by avoiding claims about private feuds.
