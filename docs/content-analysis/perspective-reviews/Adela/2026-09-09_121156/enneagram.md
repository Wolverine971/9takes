## Bottom-line verdict

The Type 3 case is built honestly: it is hedged proportional to the evidence, it names Type 4 as a live alternative rather than a token gesture, and it contains three genuinely sophisticated methodological moves that most Enneagram-typing writing about performers gets wrong (occupation-as-proof, performed sexuality-as-subtype-proof, and letting the article's own central narrative hook settle a typing question it can't settle). What keeps this from being a model piece is that it never tests its Type 3 case, or its Type 4 alternative, against the specific diagnostic markers 9takes' own pillar pages (`enneagram-type-3.md`, `enneagram-type-4.md`) treat as most central — the vice/defense mechanism (deceit — mistaking a performed image for identity) on the Three side, and the envy-of-others'-ease/"ache of incompleteness" marker on the Four side. Neither gap is a factual error or an unfair claim; both are missed opportunities to make an already-good hypothesis sharper and more falsifiable.

## What landed

- "A career in pop makes achievement unusually visible, so the occupation itself must carry little diagnostic weight." (Rabbit Hole) — this explicitly forecloses the single most common Enneagram-typing error for public figures: treating a person's job as proof of their type. Most personality-analysis writing about performers skips this step entirely.
- "The theatrical sexual confidence of ADÉLA is especially weak evidence for a private instinctual subtype." (Rabbit Hole, subtype section) — a precise methodological point: a performed public persona's sexuality is not evidence about the Sexual (Sx) instinct, which per the site's own Type 3 material concerns real one-to-one intimate connection, not stagecraft. This is exactly the kind of discriminating rigor the role calls for.
- "Writing a plan cannot distinguish Three from Four. An artist of either type can be disciplined and strategic." (Rabbit Hole) — the piece's own central narrative device (the Google Doc) is its strongest available hook, and the draft explicitly refuses to let that hook decide the type question. That is real intellectual honesty under pressure to resolve the mystery neatly.
- "Needing a supplied schedule complicates the image of a naturally efficient planner. A Type 3 reading can accommodate that, but it should not absorb every counterexample without consequence." — names a real counterexample and refuses to explain it away, which is the falsifiability the role's test list asks for ("what the proposed type does not explain").

## What missed

The Type 3 case rests entirely on one mechanism — competence as the route to attention/energy/value — and never engages the mechanism 9takes' own Type 3 pillar page treats as most diagnostic: the vice of deceit (mistaking a performed image for one's actual identity, editing personal narrative to remove luck or help, defaulting to action to bypass shame). If anything, the cited evidence tilts against that pattern (she names her collaborators specifically — Julia Michaels, Blake Slatkin — rather than editing them out of the story; she volunteers a genuine weakness, poor time management, rather than packaging it as strategic vulnerability). A reader who has read the site's own Type 3 page will notice this test was available and wasn't run.

The Type 4 alternative has the same shape of gap from the other side. It's argued at the level of authorship, aesthetic control, and outsider status — real Type 4 territory, but not the emotional register the site's own Type 4 page treats as most central: envy not of others' achievements but of their unearned ease of being, and an ache of incompleteness that isn't tied to any specific goal. None of the cited Adéla evidence is tested against that marker, so "Type 4 remains a serious alternative" is asserted with real but generic evidence rather than with the sharpest available discriminator.

## What I expected

Satisfied: certainty proportional to evidence throughout; a real (not token) alternative-type treatment; explicit refusal to let ambition/artistry/occupation stand in as type proof; correct, appropriately hedged use of wing (3w4), connecting lines (stress→9, growth→6 — both directionally correct per the site's own dossier table), and instinctual subtype (all three declined with stated reasons).

Not fully satisfied: a test of the core type against its own defining defense mechanism/vice, not just its surface motivational pattern. The heart-triad line ("The heart-center concern is value") gestures at the right neighborhood but stops well short of the site's own sharper claim (shame, bypassed via action, driving a vice of self-deception about image vs. identity).

## What surprised me

The subtype paragraph's distinction between performed public sexuality and genuine private-instinct evidence is a better methodological move than most published Enneagram writing about celebrities makes — it would have been easy, and defensible-sounding, to read ADÉLA's stage persona as Sexual-subtype evidence, and the draft explicitly declines to do that.

The closing discriminating question — "what feels most intolerable: failing to accomplish something that establishes value, or succeeding through a version of herself she cannot recognize?" — is a genuinely non-obvious, theory-grounded restatement of the Three/Four boundary that stays accessible without jargon. It's the single strongest sentence in the piece from this perspective.
