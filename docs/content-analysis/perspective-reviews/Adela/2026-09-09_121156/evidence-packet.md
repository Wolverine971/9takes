## Identity and scope

The subject is **Adéla Jergová** (born November 27, 2003, Bratislava, Slovakia), a Slovak singer-songwriter and dancer performing under the mononym **ADÉLA**. She is not Adele (Adele Laurie Blue Adkins), the British singer — the draft never states this disambiguation explicitly, which is a gap flagged both in `docs/content-analysis/entity-gaps/Adela.md` and in the draft's own embedded fresh-eyes review comment.

She trained with HYBE x Geffen Records' K-pop-style development program beginning in 2022, appeared on the competition series that became *The Debut: Dream Academy* (2023), was eliminated in the first round, and was later featured in Netflix's documentary *Pop Star Academy: KATSEYE* (2024), which covers the group KATSEYE's formation. She went on to build an independent solo career, releasing the single "Homewrecked" (September 2024), the EP *The Provocateur* (August 22, 2025), and her debut album *PRIMA* (September 4, 2026, Capitol/Universal). Her domain relevance to 9takes is as a public figure whose own accounts of ambition, competence, and identity are being read for an Enneagram Type 3 hypothesis.

**Knowledge cutoff:** This packet reflects sources checked live on September 9, 2026 (the same day *PRIMA*'s reviews were finishing publication and the North American tour leg was scheduled to open in Detroit). No evidence of events after September 9, 2026 is included. A personal Wikipedia article exists (`https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ad%C3%A9la_(singer)`) and was confirmed live on September 9; it was not used as a primary source for psychological claims, only for basic identity confirmation.

## First-person evidence

All quotations below are Adéla's own words as reported by named outlets. Each note states what the quote can and cannot support.

- **"I get mine from work."** (Interview Magazine, 2026-04-29, answering whether she craves attention.) Supports: an explicit, self-reported link between professional competence and a felt sense of receiving attention/energy. Cannot support: a claim that she never wants attention outside work, or that this exhausts her motivational structure — she also describes being "relatively relaxed" about attention privately in the same piece per existing repo research (not independently re-fetched in this pass; see Research limitations).

- **"Maybe it's not going to come and maybe that's what's going to keep me being good."** (PAPER, 2026-05-18, verified live in this packet.) Full context: "I don't know when I'm going to realize that I've achieved what I wanted to achieve. Maybe it's not going to come and maybe that's what's going to keep me being good." Supports: an unresolved, ongoing relationship to "arrival" or completion — directly relevant to a Type 3 achievement-treadmill reading. Cannot support: that she is unable to feel pride — the same interview opens with explicit pride (per existing repo research), and she separately explains rest as valuable ("if I don't have time to just be, then what am I writing about?").

- **"I think bringing collaborators in that are great at the things that you might be weaker at is what a smart person does, and what a smart artist does."** (PAPER, 2026-05-18, verified live.) On Julia Michaels specifically: "I go to Julia and I go, 'This is what I want to say. What are your ideas?' And then obviously if I don't like them, I'm like, 'I don't love that. Let's try something else.'" Supports: a described shift toward delegating creative weaknesses while retaining final say — relevant to the draft's claim that she became "more comfortable learning." Cannot support: a claim about her emotional motive for this shift beyond what she states (contrasted with conformity during the group program).

- **"I'm basically myself, but I do find that something happens before I walk on stage where it's time to turn it on."** (V Magazine, 2026-09-08, verified live — the draft quotes only the first clause.) Supports: her own account of continuity between the ADÉLA stage persona and her private self, i.e., she describes a "switch" rather than a fabricated identity. Cannot support: a claim that performance costs her nothing — Interview Magazine separately has her acknowledging performing charm while exhausted (per existing repo research, not independently re-verified here).

- **Manon remarks, attributed to Adéla:** "She doesn't put a lot of effort, and she doesn't show up for her team a lot," and, of the attention Manon was receiving, "It's not based on anything right now, it's just because she's pretty." (Reported by Sheldon Pearce, NPR/Georgia Public Broadcasting, 2026-04-27, verified live in this packet — wording is very close to but not word-for-word identical to the phrasing in prior repo research, which read "doesn't show up for her team a lot" without the "put a lot of effort" clause.) Supports: that Adéla is the named speaker of both remarks in a reputable third-party report, resolving an attribution gap left by an unlabelled third-party documentary transcript. Cannot support: an assessment of whether the criticism was fair, or what she knew of Manon's actual circumstances.

- Childhood list (English fluency, Los Angeles, Disney headquarters, meeting Miley Cyrus), concealed from her parents: reported via The Line of Best Fit (2025-06-30), not independently re-fetched in this pass. This is an **attributed claim** describing Adéla's own retrospective account of a childhood aspiration list, not an independently documented artifact (e.g., no photograph of an actual written list is cited).

- Feet-stretching ballet anecdote and the "given a schedule" preference: both directly confirmed against the local timestamped transcript of the Coco Mocoe interview (`youtube-transcripts-people/adela-coco-mocoe-interview.md`). ## Public record, accomplishments, failures, and controversies

- **Dream Academy elimination:** Adéla placed last in the competition's first public vote and was part of the first pair of trainees eliminated (2023-09). She has stated (Teen Vogue, 2024-10-14) that the program was originally presented to her as dependent on work ethic and monthly scores, with the fan-vote format introduced later — and that she sensed the program was wrong for her before elimination. This is an **attributed claim** (her account of the selection criteria's evolution); existing repo research notes the actual competition process retained some executive/staff selection alongside audience voting, so "public ranking replaced the familiar evaluations" would overstate a shift to pure voting.

- **The Manon controversy:** In the documentary, Adéla criticized fellow trainee Manon's attendance/effort and separately attributed audience attention Manon was receiving to her appearance rather than substance. This is now attributable directly to Adéla via NPR/GPB reporting (see First-person evidence, above). It is a **disputed/interpretation zone**: the effort criticism and the appearance-based dismissal are analytically distinct (one about a teammate's conduct, one about dismissing audience judgment), and existing repo research explicitly recommends not treating this as evidence that Adéla "possessed every relevant fact" about Manon's circumstances.

- ***PRIMA* critical reception is genuinely split** (disputed claim, see below).

- No evidence was found in this packet or in prior repo research of legal trouble, on-record misconduct allegations, or verified controversies beyond the Dream Academy/Manon episode and ordinary critical reception of her music.

## Enneagram hypothesis evidence

The draft's central hypothesis is Enneagram **Type 3**, with Type 4 presented as a serious alternative. Per this command's governing rule, the type and its supporting mechanics are **interpretation**, not fact, regardless of how confidently the draft states them.

**Evidence consistent with Type 3:**
- Repeated self-reported linkage between competence/work and a feeling of receiving attention, energy, or value ("I get mine from work"; PAPER's collaboration and arrival-uncertainty material).
- A described pattern of recovering a sense of self through a self-authored professional project (the Google Doc / mission-document account, Best Fit 2025-06-30) after a period of externally-defined identity (the group training program).
- An unresolved "moving finish line" self-report (PAPER, "maybe it's not going to come").

**Evidence against, or complicating, a clean Type 3 reading:**
- She explicitly says she is not a naturally organized planner and depends on externally supplied structure (Coco Mocoe transcript, verified above) — this cuts against a stereotyped "efficient achiever" caricature, though the existing research and this packet both note a Type 3 reading can accommodate it as evidence of channeling effort rather than refuting the core hypothesis.
- She defended a specific creative decision ("Ain't in LA" as a single) against team/label skepticism before it was validated by audience reception (V Magazine, Sennott testimony, verified above) — this is **meaningful counterevidence** to a reading in which her choices are driven purely by anticipated approval, since the conviction preceded the validation.
- She states the album has no "overarching manifesto" and describes tolerating "bad songs" along the way (Vogue CS, 2026-08-24, not re-fetched this pass) — in some tension with a highly rank/plan-oriented reading.

**Closest alternative — Type 4:** The competing case rests on her protection of aesthetic authorship, willingness to include personally exposing or unpopular material, defense of Slovak identity/origin material against homogenizing pressure, and resistance to an externally imposed persona during the group program. Existing repo research (`Adela-2026-09-09-independent.md`) frames the discriminating question as: does professional success/recognition function as her preferred remedy when value feels threatened (favors 3), or does protecting a personally truthful mode of expression outrank accomplishment even at a cost (favors 4)? The draft itself acknowledges this is not settled ("Type 4 remains a serious alternative").

**Weaker alternatives noted in prior research but not developed in the draft:** Type 1 (ballet correction/rigor — thin evidence of moral-perfectionism organizing her life independent of achievement) and Type 8 (bluntness, autonomy — competing explanation of cultural directness rather than a controlling core motive). The draft does not present these as serious alternatives, consistent with prior research's assessment that evidence for them is comparatively thin.

**Behavior the Type 3 hypothesis does not fully explain:** her reliance on externally imposed structure/schedules (arguably more typical of other patterns); her account of feeling "basically herself" on stage rather than experiencing a felt gap between persona and private self; and her explicit non-comparison / relative privacy relaxation (Interview Magazine, per existing research, not re-verified live this pass).

**No wing or instinctual subtype claim in this packet is more than tentative.** The draft itself declines to commit to a wing or subtype ("No wing, subtype, or connecting-line certainty" — refresh ledger).
