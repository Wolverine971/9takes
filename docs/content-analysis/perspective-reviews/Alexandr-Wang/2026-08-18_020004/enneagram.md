## Bottom-line verdict

The core call is right and, more importantly, it is argued the way a type call should be argued: from
motivation, against a named alternative, with stated falsifiers. I would reach Type 3 on this
evidence too. The diagnosis section does the hardest thing in Enneagram writing correctly — it
refuses to let ambition be the evidence — and the stress-arrow section refuses to invent a
disintegration instance that the record does not contain. That refusal is rarer than it should be
and it is the best epistemic moment in the piece.

The problem is not the type. It is the apparatus around the type. Three of the four sub-calls in the
Rabbit Hole rest on evidence that does not discriminate, and two of them contradict the 9takes pages
they link to in the same paragraph — a reader who follows the instinctual-subtypes link is told the
opposite of what the draft just told them, and the draft's subtype discriminator collapses on
contact. The draft also tests one alternative type where 86% of this corpus tests two or more, and
the alternative it skips is the one its own hinge sentence keeps describing.

One sentence crosses from reading into totalizing: "He has made every room he has ever been in by
exactly one spot" is asserted as biography, is false of his most famous transitions, and is the
mechanism by which a single childhood datum is made to explain an entire life. That is the specific
failure mode this lens exists to catch, and it sits two paragraphs before the article's emotional
payoff.

Everything I am flagging is repairable inside the Rabbit Hole and one body sentence. None of it
requires new research.

## What landed

**ENNEAGRAM-H1 — the type is defined by motivation in its first three sentences.**

> "The <a href="/enneagram-corner/enneagram-type-3">Type 3</a> is the Achiever, and the shorthand for
> the type is ambition. That shorthand is close enough to be useless. What defines a Three is where
> their sense of existing comes from: the scoreboard, and the standing on it."

Must survive: this is the load-bearing methodological move of the whole genre. Most celebrity type
writing asserts a behavior and calls it a type. This names the behavior, discards it as
non-discriminating, and replaces it with a motivational criterion — before offering any evidence
about Wang. Cutting or softening this makes every subsequent claim un-anchored.

**ENNEAGRAM-H2 — ambition is explicitly refused as proof, with the base rate named.**

> "The tell is not the ambition. Silicon Valley runs on ambition, and the Type 5 investigator who is
> the archetype of this industry has plenty of it. The tell is what survived from the math
> competition. He kept the trip and the placement. The problem set is gone."

Must survive: my contract asks specifically whether ambition is being treated as type proof. Not
only does the draft avoid it, it pre-empts it and supplies a genuine discriminator in its place —
what the subject *retained* from a memory, which is motivational evidence rather than behavioral
evidence. The "problem set is gone" formulation is the single most efficient piece of type reasoning
in the piece.

**ENNEAGRAM-H3 — the hinge sentence, which is correct theory and non-obvious application.**

> "Tell a Three they have not earned their position and you have not insulted them. You have agreed
> with them."

Must survive: this derives cleanly from the type's basic fear (worthlessness) as the 9takes pillar
states it — `src/blog/enneagram/enneagram-type-3.md:91` gives Basic fear: Worthlessness, and
`enneagram-types-in-stress.md:188-192` frames it as "without a winning image, there may be nothing
valuable underneath." It is also the article's whole reason to exist: it explains why *this*
criticism from *this* man landed differently than ordinary criticism would. This is the lens
producing something the reader could not have gotten from the news coverage. It is the delight hit.

**ENNEAGRAM-H4 — the stress arrow refuses to invent evidence.**

> "Honest limit first: the public record does not contain a clean instance of Wang under
> disintegration. The 3 goes to <a href="/enneagram-corner/enneagram-types-in-stress">Type 9</a> in
> stress, and 9-ward collapse in a Three shows up as paralysis, disengagement and numbing. Nothing
> in the available reporting documents that, and asserting it would be inventing evidence."

Must survive verbatim. Arrow sections are where this genre reliably fabricates. Naming the arrow,
naming what it would look like, and then declining to claim it is the correct posture on a living
subject. The follow-on distinction is equally good: "the drive stalled mid-sentence and he restarted
it himself within seconds, which is a Three catching itself rather than a Three going to 9."

**ENNEAGRAM-H5 — stated falsifiers, both genuinely discriminating.**

> "Two findings would overturn the call. One: a documented stretch where pressure made him go quiet
> and stay quiet. Two: a decision where he chose knowing something over being seen to have delivered
> it."

Must survive: these are not decorative. Falsifier one discriminates 3 from 9-ward collapse; falsifier
two discriminates 3 from 5 on the exact axis the draft argues. A reader can hold the article to them.
This is what "certainty proportional to public evidence" looks like in practice, and it is the reason
my trust reads strained rather than broken.

**ENNEAGRAM-H6 — type theory stays out of the reader's way.**

The draft quarantines system language to one bridging paragraph plus the Rabbit Hole. The body
carries Wang, not the Enneagram. My contract warns against rewarding jargon density; this draft earns
the opposite credit. A reader who does not care about the system still gets a coherent psychological
portrait, and the one paragraph that does carry theory (the heart triad) arrives exactly where it
pays off.

**ENNEAGRAM-H7 — house consistency on the spine.** The heart-triad framing matches the 9takes pillar
almost structurally (`enneagram-type-3.md:151-165`: center of the heart triad, furthest from
feelings, shame as the underlying emotion, outrunning it, every win quiets the voice then it
returns). Arrow directions match the pillar's own table (stress 9, integration 6). Whatever else
needs fixing, the spine does not.

## What missed

**The sub-calls do not discriminate.** A wing call, a subtype call, and an arrow call are each
supposed to be answering "why this one and not the neighbor." All three answer with evidence that is
equally consistent with the neighbor:

- The 3w2 case cites Pentagon contracts, an inauguration seat, and meetings with Starmer, Modi and
  Macron. Those are prestige-arena facts. They are not warmth, and warmth is what the 2 wing is.
  Worse, the subtype section two paragraphs later cites *the same facts* ("an inauguration, heads of
  state") to prove the social subtype. Evidence that proves two different sub-calls proves neither.
- The 3w4 counterweight argues that crying at a farewell cuts against a clean 3w2. The wings guide it
  links says the opposite (see ENNEAGRAM-C2).
- The growth arrow to 6 rests on Wang keeping a board seat in a company he still holds equity in.

**The alternative-type test is under-run.** One alternative, where the corpus norm is two. And the
alternative that goes untested is the one the draft's own best sentence describes.

**Nothing is said about what Type 3 fails to explain.** The draft names two findings that would
*overturn* the call, which is excellent, but never names anything in the existing record that the
call handles poorly. There is an obvious candidate sitting in the LeCun section (ENNEAGRAM-C8).

**One generalization escapes the evidence entirely** (ENNEAGRAM-B1).

## What I expected

- **A second alternative type, seriously argued.** Standard for this corpus (99/115 drafts) and
  standard for responsible typing. Type 5 is treated well; it is just not the only live option.
- **The wing call to hinge on interpersonal evidence.** The draft actually has some — the 1-1 calls
  with every hire is real 3w2 material and matches the house tell ("achieve *through* people,
  genuinely care about developing others"). It is buried under a list of rooms.
- **The subtype call to name all three variants.** The draft contrasts social against
  self-preservation and never mentions the one-to-one Three, while linking a page that describes all
  three and writing "how differently the three variants of one type present."
- **An explicit note that the 3-vs-5 tiebreaker is single-sourced.** The draft leans its most
  important discrimination on the all-hands scene and does not tell the reader how thin that scene's
  sourcing is.
- **Some acknowledgment that the "snuck in" self-description is doubt-flavored,** and that doubt has
  a home elsewhere on the diagram.

## What surprised me

**Welcome:** the refusal to manufacture a disintegration instance, and the falsifiers. I expected to
spend this review arguing against invented arrow evidence — that is the default failure of the form —
and instead found the draft policing itself harder than I would have. Also welcome: "The tell is not
the ambition," which pre-empts the single most common objection to typing a founder as a Three.

**Jarring:** the Rabbit Hole contradicting its own hyperlinks, twice, in adjacent paragraphs. The
subtype paragraph tells the reader the self-preservation Three hides accomplishments and then invites
them to click a page that says self-preservation Threes "often highlight tangible evidence of
success, such as promotions or possessions." That is not a nuance gap; it is the article handing the
reader the refutation of its own discriminator.

**Also unexpected:** the draft argues the name-spelling detail correctly in the body ("reported as a
nod to an auspicious number in Chinese culture") and then re-reads the same fact in the Rabbit Hole
as Wang's own self-curation. The body version is right and the Rabbit Hole version contradicts it.

## Red flags

### Blocker

**ENNEAGRAM-B1 — a single childhood datum is generalized into a totalizing life claim, stated as
biography.** Confidence: high.

- **Passage** (H2 5, "What Yann LeCun said…", the paragraph after "Now put it next to the sixth-grader
  who came fourth of four"): "He has made every room he has ever been in by exactly one spot. Ranked,
  seeded, cut lists, top four in the state."
- **Reader effect / trust problem:** This is written as fact about his life, not as his self-perception.
  It is false in the direction the reader already knows: he became the world's youngest self-made
  billionaire at 24, and Meta paid $14.3 billion substantially to acquire him — neither is entry "by
  exactly one spot." A reader who notices concludes the type reading is being retrofitted onto a life
  it does not fit, which is precisely the charge Enneagram writing is most vulnerable to. For this
  lens the specific harm is that it converts a falsifiable type claim into an unfalsifiable life
  narrative: if every threshold was narrow, the type explains everything, and a claim that explains
  everything explains nothing.
- **Evidence:** Packet CLM-27 classes it **unsupported as written** — "Nothing in the record
  establishes narrow-margin entry to MIT, YC, billionaire status, the Pentagon, or Meta — the $14.3B
  deal is the opposite of a narrow admission." One documented instance exists: fourth of four in a
  New Mexico math competition.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Relocate the claim from the record to his own account. The sentence needs
  to describe how he narrates his admissions, not how he obtained them — the draft has already earned
  that framing with "he has been telling us he snuck in since sixth grade." Delete "every room he has
  ever been in" as a factual assertion; keep the qualifying-threshold idea, which the next two
  sentences carry on their own.
- **Expected benefit:** The payoff paragraph stops overclaiming and becomes harder to refute, not
  softer. The closing line ("Top four in New Mexico. He came fourth.") lands on a claim that survives
  a hostile reader.
- **Acceptance test:** No sentence in the piece asserts as fact that Wang narrowly qualified for any
  threshold other than the sixth-grade math competition. Any surviving generalization is explicitly
  scoped to his self-description and attributable to a quoted line.

### Concerns

**ENNEAGRAM-C1 — the subtype discriminator contradicts the 9takes page it links.** Confidence: high.

- **Passage** (Rabbit Hole, "Alexandr Wang's Instinctual Subtype: Social"): "The self-preservation 3
  hides accomplishments and would find that itinerary embarrassing. Wang's itinerary is the
  accomplishment. See <a href="/enneagram-corner/enneagram-instinctual-subtypes">instinctual
  subtypes</a>…"
- **Trust problem:** The entire subtype argument is this one contrast. `enneagram-instinctual-subtypes.md:135-141`
  describes SP3 as tracking measurable accomplishments and says outright: "**Recognize Them**: Often
  highlight tangible evidence of success, such as promotions or possessions." A reader who clicks the
  link the sentence provides is told SP3s display success. The discriminator then discriminates
  nothing, and the reader's takeaway is that 9takes does not agree with itself.
- **Evidence:** Both sides verified directly. The draft's reading is the **mainstream** one — in the
  Naranjo/Chestnut lineage SP3 is the countertype, which "in denying the presence of vanity" avoids
  "the spotlight and self-promotion." So the theory is not the error; the collision is. The house page
  follows a material-security reading instead. Packet also notes no source distinguishes social from
  self-preservation *motivation* for Wang — arena density is behavior.
- **Minimum viable repair:** One clause naming the framework, so the two pages read as different lenses
  rather than a contradiction: identify this as the countertype reading of the self-preservation Three
  (the variant that denies vanity and avoids obvious self-promotion). If that is unwanted, move the
  link off this sentence.
- **Expected benefit:** The subtype call survives a click-through, and the certainty stops exceeding
  what one behavioral pattern can carry.
- **Acceptance test:** Read the paragraph, click the link, return. Nothing in the linked page
  contradicts a sentence in the paragraph.

**ENNEAGRAM-C2 — the 3w4 counterweight contradicts the wings guide it links, and its other leg is a
naming decision Wang did not make.** Confidence: high.

- **Passage** (Rabbit Hole, wing section): "The 3w4 case is not empty. The eight-character spelling of
  his first name is a curated choice about how he lands, and a clean 3w2 does not usually cry at its
  own farewell."
- **Trust problem:** Two separate defects in one sentence. (a) `enneagram-wings-complete-guide.md:234`
  states 3w2 is "**More emotionally available than 3w4**"; line 258 makes 3w4 "more emotionally
  intense." Public tears at a farewell for people he was leaving is availability, not intensity — so
  by the house framework this cuts *toward* 3w2, and the draft claims it cuts away. The guide's actual
  3w4 tell is very different: "moody about whether their achievements actually mean anything." Wang's
  recorded self-correction ("This is so stupid. Why am I doing this?") is embarrassment at display,
  not doubt about meaning. (b) The spelling is reported by Fortune as the eight characters "associated
  with good fortune in Chinese culture" — a naming choice made at birth. You cannot read a subject's
  self-presentation off a decision made before he could speak. The draft's own body handles this
  correctly ("reported as a nod to an auspicious number"); the Rabbit Hole silently upgrades it to
  intentional self-curation.
- **Evidence:** Wings guide lines 232-234 and 256-258, verified. Packet CLM-05 and unresolved question
  6, which notes the numerology sourcing "weakens it as a 4-wing signal in either location."
- **Minimum viable repair:** Drop both legs. Replace with a real 3w4 counterweight or state plainly
  that the 3w4 case is thin. If a counterweight is wanted, the honest one is that the record contains
  little evidence of 2-wing warmth toward anyone outside a hiring context.
- **Expected benefit:** The wing call stops resting on an argument its own linked page reverses, and
  stops attributing a parental decision to the subject's psychology.
- **Acceptance test:** No sentence attributes the name spelling to a choice Wang made. No sentence
  claims an emotional display cuts against the 2 wing.

**ENNEAGRAM-C3 — only one alternative type is tested, and the untested one is the one the draft keeps
describing.** Confidence: medium-high.

- **Passage** (Rabbit Hole, "Counterarguments: Why Alexandr Wang Might Not Be Type 3"): the section
  argues Type 5 and stops.
- **Trust problem:** Type 5 is the field's archetype, which makes it the *expected* foil rather than
  necessarily the *strongest* one. The draft's own hinge — that being told he has not earned his
  position would land as agreement — describes chronic doubt about one's own standing, which is also
  the signature of Type 6. So does "he snuck in." So does building the substrate the whole industry
  depends on, and a career oriented toward national security. And the draft simultaneously assigns 6
  as the growth arrow, which means its two most psychologically loaded passages both point at 6
  material without ever asking whether 6 is the core. A knowledgeable reader will ask; the article
  should ask first. Related: with the corpus statistic removed, "the Type 5 investigator who is the
  archetype of this industry" now stands on assertion alone.
- **Evidence:** Corpus scan of all 115 people drafts carrying a counter-typing section — 76 name two
  alternatives, 23 name three or more, 16 name one or zero. This draft is in the 14% minority. Two
  alternatives is the house norm by a wide margin.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Add a short Type 6 counter-case in the Rabbit Hole only (not the body):
  the doubt structure, the security orientation, the loyalty to Scale — then the discriminator that
  settles it, which is available: a 6 seeks certainty and alliance to quiet doubt, and Wang seeks
  standing. Keep it to the length of the Type 5 case.
- **Expected benefit:** The call goes from asserted-against-one-foil to tested, and the article
  pre-empts the objection most likely to come from readers who know the system.
- **Acceptance test:** The counter-typing section names two non-core types, each with its own
  discriminating evidence, and neither reads as a straw alternative.

**ENNEAGRAM-C4 — the 3-vs-5 tiebreaker rests on the thinnest-sourced material in the piece, and the
draft does not say so.** Confidence: high.

- **Passage** (Rabbit Hole): "And the tiebreaker is the all-hands: the feeling surfaced in public,
  during a story he was telling about himself, and he corrected it out loud. A Five leaves the room to
  feel something."
- **Trust problem:** The reasoning is sound, but the scene fuses three evidential classes the reader
  cannot see apart: Wang's own "I literally teared up" (first-person, to Forbes); "This is so stupid.
  Why am I doing this?" (one unnamed attendee's recollection, one outlet, no recording); and the
  `<p class="inner-thought">` monologue, which is authored and has no evidentiary basis at all. The
  single most discriminating claim in the type argument therefore rests on the weakest evidence in the
  article, presented with the most confidence.
- **Evidence:** Packet CLM-23 and unresolved question 8: "It is the emotional load-bearing point of the
  entire piece and it rests on the thinnest evidence in it."
- **Minimum viable repair:** In the Rabbit Hole sentence only, mark the sourcing — attribute the
  self-directed line to the attendee account rather than letting it read as recorded. The body scene
  can keep its shape; it is the *tiebreaker* claim that needs the hedge, because that is where it does
  diagnostic work.
- **Expected benefit:** Certainty comes back into proportion at the exact point where the draft is
  currently most exposed, without weakening the scene's emotional effect.
- **Acceptance test:** A reader can tell, from the Rabbit Hole sentence alone, that the overheard line
  is a witness recollection rather than a recording, and that the interior monologue is a device.

**ENNEAGRAM-C5 — wing and subtype sections spend the same evidence twice.** Confidence: high.

- **Passages:** Wing — "He built the relationships that got him a pool house from an investor, a Y
  Combinator batch, Pentagon contracts, a seat at a presidential inauguration in January 2025, and
  meetings with Starmer, Modi and Macron." Subtype — "Forbes lists, a TED stage, Congress, an
  inauguration, heads of state, a Zuckerberg memo naming him the most impressive founder of his
  generation."
- **Trust problem:** The inauguration and the heads of state appear in both, as proof of two different
  things. Prestige-arena access is subtype evidence; it says nothing about a 2 wing, because the 2
  wing is warmth and attention to individual people, not proximity to important ones. A reader tracking
  the argument sees the same facts doing double duty and correctly downgrades both conclusions.
- **Evidence:** `enneagram-wings-complete-guide.md:232` gives the 3w2 tell as achieving *through*
  people and genuinely caring about developing them — which the draft actually has better evidence
  for and underuses.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Move the arena list out of the wing paragraph entirely and let the wing
  case stand on the interpersonal evidence already present: the 1-1 call with every hire past the point
  where headcount made it reasonable, and the recruiting record. Leave the arenas to the subtype
  section, where they belong.
- **Expected benefit:** Both sub-calls become discriminating instead of overlapping, and the wing
  argument gets stronger by getting shorter.
- **Acceptance test:** No fact appears as evidence in both the wing and subtype paragraphs.

**ENNEAGRAM-C6 — the growth-arrow evidence is economically self-interested, which ordinary Type 3
behavior explains at least as well as integration.** Confidence: medium-high.

- **Passage** (Rabbit Hole): "Loyalty to a thing after it stops burnishing you, and after you are the
  reason it is bleeding, is the 6 direction."
- **Trust problem:** Integration to 6 means commitment that costs something and outlasts what it
  returns. Wang retained a board seat in a company where he holds equity, and publicly defended its
  value. Defending the value of an asset you own is image and asset management — the thing Type 3 does
  natively. The draft has not established that the loyalty cost him anything, which is the one thing
  that would make it arrow evidence rather than type-typical behavior.
- **Evidence:** Packet's "Evidence against, or unexplained by, Type 3" table raises the same act as
  potential counterevidence, not as arrow support. It also notes an omitted fact that weakens the
  premise: Forbes reports Google "resumed its work a few months later," so the company he defended was
  not in uninterrupted decline.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Either establish a cost (something he gave up or risked by staying), or
  downgrade the claim to what the record supports — the arrow is suggested, not demonstrated, in the
  same register the draft already uses so well for the stress arrow.
- **Expected benefit:** The growth arrow gets the same honesty as the stress arrow, which is the
  section's own standard three paragraphs earlier.
- **Acceptance test:** The growth-arrow paragraph either cites a cost Wang bore, or states its
  confidence as provisional.

**ENNEAGRAM-C7 — the diagnostic contrast is partly host-elicited, and the body does not say so.**
Confidence: medium.

- **Passage** (¶2): "He did not describe the math. He described the prize, and then his own margin."
- **Trust problem:** Per the transcript order, Wang volunteered the prize framing and the "most
  motivating thing I could possibly imagine" line — that part is genuinely his. But the placement came
  as an answer to Von's direct question about whether he won. The sentence's rhetorical structure
  implies free recall of both. Since this contrast is the primary evidence for the type in the first
  screen of the article, an inflated version of it inflates the type call.
- **Evidence:** Packet CLM-03, classed **interpretation**, and the transcript-order finding: "What this
  cannot support: that Wang volunteered his rank unprompted at that moment." Note FAQ 1 already handles
  this correctly with "Asked about the sixth-grade math competition…" — the body is the outlier.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Bring the body in line with the FAQ. The volunteered part is strong enough
  on its own: what he supplied unprompted was the prize and how much he wanted it. Let the placement
  read as the answer it was.
- **Expected benefit:** The spine gets more durable, not less — the surviving claim is fully supported
  and no longer depends on a conversational sequence the reader could check.
- **Acceptance test:** The body's account of the exchange is consistent with FAQ 1's "Asked about."

**ENNEAGRAM-C8 — the draft never says what Type 3 does not explain, and the clearest candidate is
used as irony instead.** Confidence: medium.

- **Passage** (H2 5): "The man who built a business out of pricing human expertise was arguing that
  you cannot read these particular humans off their price."
- **Trust problem:** This is presented as a nice reversal, but it is the strongest piece of
  counterevidence in the record and the draft spends it as a rhetorical flourish. A person whose sense
  of existing comes from the scoreboard would find the price the most legible fact about a hire; Wang
  publicly rejected that reading and gave three non-monetary reasons. Naming one thing the type handles
  poorly is what separates a hypothesis from a lens that explains everything — and this draft, which is
  otherwise disciplined about falsifiability, never does it.
- **Evidence:** Packet's "Evidence against, or unexplained by, Type 3" table lists this explicitly:
  "A pure scoreboard reading of people would find the price the most legible fact." The packet also
  notes the draft quotes one of his three reasons.
- **Minimum viable repair:** One sentence in the Rabbit Hole naming this as a datum the Type 3 read
  handles imperfectly. It does not need resolving — naming it is the whole benefit.
- **Expected benefit:** The article demonstrates that its own lens has limits, which is the strongest
  available answer to the reader who thinks Enneagram writing is unfalsifiable.
- **Acceptance test:** The Rabbit Hole contains at least one explicitly identified fact that the Type 3
  call does not fully account for.

### Preference (recorded, not promotable)

The subtype section contrasts only social against self-preservation while writing "how differently the
three variants of one type present" and linking a page that describes all three. One clause on the
one-to-one Three would close the loop. Taste-level; the call itself is fine.

## Limits of this review

- I audited only `draft-reviewed.md` at sha256 `90b3e932…`, verified against `context.json` and the
  supplied argument. I did not read the live draft, and I did not read any other perspective's review
  or the synthesis.
- The draft carries three embedded pipeline commentaries (a fresh-eyes review, second-pass notes, a
  cohesion pass). I read past them and did not treat their claims, praise, or self-assessments as
  evidence for or against anything here. Several of my findings sit in material those notes describe
  as resolved.
- I am evaluating the *type reasoning*, not the factual record. Where the packet flags a factual
  dispute that does not bear on the type call — the Thunderforge $500M attribution, the revenue
  figure, the "two never formally started" table row, the Muse Code contributor-tier wording — I have
  left it entirely to the critic and future perspectives.
- No Enneagram self-report by Wang exists, and no reputable third party has typed him. Every type
  claim in the draft and in this review is a hypothesis about a living person from public behavior.
  My agreement with the Type 3 call is agreement about the *quality of the inference*, not a
  verification of the type.
- ENNEAGRAM-C3's Type 6 case is my proposal, not a competing diagnosis. I am not claiming Wang is a
  Six; I am claiming the draft owes the reader the test, and that its own hinge sentence is what
  creates the obligation.
- Two of my three highest-confidence findings (ENNEAGRAM-C1, ENNEAGRAM-C2) turn on 9takes' own pages
  being authoritative for this corpus. If the house intends those pages to be superseded by
  per-article readings, both drop from concern to preference — but the links would still need to move.
- ENNEAGRAM-C4 and ENNEAGRAM-C6 depend on the packet's characterization of sources I did not read
  directly (Forbes, May 14 2026, accessed by the packet compiler via summary rather than full text).
