# Perspective Synthesis: Alexandr Wang

Adjudication of six independent reviews of `draft-reviewed.md` (sha256 verified against
`context.json` before reading). Every cited passage was re-read in the frozen draft; no reviewer's
characterization was accepted without checking it. Three external sources were consulted, logged in
the items they settle.

## Executive verdict

**Revise. The article's argument is sound and its spine is excellent; its evidence handling is not
yet at the standard the article itself advertises.**

All six perspectives independently reached `trust: strained` and `recommendation: revise`, and all
six independently marked `delight: clear_hit`. That combination is the whole finding. Nobody
disputes that the piece works — the sixth-grade placement running all the way to the Muse Code
contributor tier is a genuine original connection, and the informed fan (our delight target) reports
having followed Wang for years without making it. What every reviewer hit instead is a pattern: a
piece that trains the reader to expect outlet-and-date rigor, and then breaks that contract in six
specific places.

The convergence is unusually strong and unusually specific. **All six perspectives flagged the same
sentence** — "He has made every room he has ever been in by exactly one spot" — and the evidence
packet independently classed it `unsupported as written` and named it the highest-exposure line in
the piece. That is the sentence the entire closing empathy turn rests on, and it is refuted by the
article's own biography section three thousand words earlier. It is P0-01 and it is the single most
important repair in this brief.

Beneath that, the defects cluster into three families:

1. **Overclaim at the emotional peak.** The "every room" generalization (all six), the unmarked
   invented interior monologue (four), and the closing assertion of Wang's private self-belief as
   fact. The article is scrupulous about marking inference everywhere except where it most wants
   assent.
2. **Selective evidence in both directions.** LeCun's crediting clause dropped from a section built
   on his criticism; Google's return dropped from a customer-exodus claim; Wang's three-part
   rebuttal cut to one part; Zuckerberg's interest undeclared while LeCun's is declared at length.
   Notably the shaping runs *both* ways — toward Wang in the benchmark table, against him in the
   customer row — which tells me the tilt is arc-driven rather than sympathy-driven, and therefore
   likely to recur anywhere the arc needs a beat.
3. **Checkable small errors in a piece whose credibility is built on checkability.** "Twenty years"
   contradicted by the draft's own dates, a dropped word inside quotation marks, a disputed defense
   contract figure stated flat, an anonymous quote the draft's own ledger forbids.

Two things I want to record against any later pass that tries to "fix" this piece by softening it.
First, the draft does several things most profiles refuse to do — it declares the adverse witness's
interest at length, it refuses to invent a disintegration instance the record does not contain, and
it names two findings that would overturn its own call. Five of six preserve lists protect that
refusal. It is the piece's trust credential and nothing in this brief touches it. Second, the fixes
below almost all make the argument *stronger*, not gentler. LeCun conceding that Wang learns fast
and then saying he has never practiced research is more damaging than LeCun merely calling him
inexperienced. The gap between Wang's record and his self-account is better Type 3 evidence than the
false generalization it replaces. The editor should not read this brief as a request to retreat.

The Enneagram call itself survives review. The Enneagram expert would reach Type 3 on this evidence
too and rates the diagnosis section's method — refusing to let ambition be the evidence — as
correct. What does not survive is the apparatus: two of the four rabbit-hole sub-calls contradict
the 9takes pages they link to in the same paragraph, which I verified directly in the repo.

Seven P0s is more than usual, but every one is a one-to-three-sentence surgical repair, and four of
them land in the same two paragraphs. This is not a rewrite. The Disney World spine, the diagnosis
section, the Alex Ren pairing, the stress-arrow honesty, and the ending all stand.

## P0 — mandatory red-flag repairs

### P0-01 — "Every room he has ever been in" is a life claim the article's own biography refutes

- **Originating reviews.** FAN-R1 (blocker), CRITIC-R3 (blocker), SUBJECT-R2 (blocker), UNFAM-R3
  (blocker), ENNEAGRAM-B1 (blocker), FUTURE (flagged). **All six perspectives.** Packet CLM-27.
- **Location.** `draft-reviewed.md:262`, H2 5.
- **Quoted passage.** "He has made every room he has ever been in by exactly one spot. Ranked,
  seeded, cut lists, top four in the state."
- **Adjudicated problem.** Stated as biography, not as reading. Exactly one narrow-margin entry is
  documented anywhere in the record: fourth of four in a New Mexico math competition in sixth grade.
  Against it, the article's own H2 1 supplies the refutation — "became the youngest self-made
  billionaire in the world at 24" and "Meta paid $14.3 billion for 49% of the company." A $14.3
  billion acquisition undertaken substantially to hire someone is the least marginal admission in
  modern technology. Every reviewer arrived here by a different route: the fan already knew it was
  wrong, the critic saw the exoneration lose its premise, the unfamiliar reader refuted it from
  memory of this same article, the Enneagram expert saw a falsifiable type claim converted into an
  unfalsifiable life narrative, and the subject saw a claim he can disprove with one counterexample.
- **Evidence and confidence.** Packet CLM-27 (`unsupported as written`), CLM-08, CLM-10, CLM-22, all
  verified. Internal contradiction confirmed by direct re-read of `draft-reviewed.md:167` against
  `:262`. **Confidence: high.** This is the most solidly established finding in the entire review
  round.
- **Minimum repair.** Move the claim from his record to his self-account. The defensible and more
  interesting version is that he narrates himself as someone who snuck in — and has since sixth
  grade — while his record shows the opposite. Delete "every room he has ever been in" as a factual
  assertion; the following sentence ("A person who grows up on the qualifying side of a threshold
  learns something specific…") already carries the real work and survives the cut intact.
- **Expected reader benefit.** The empathy turn keeps its full force and stops being falsifiable by
  a fact the reader learned in this article. The gap between record and self-account is *stronger*
  Type 3 evidence than the generalization was, because it is exactly what the type predicts.
- **Protected hit at risk.** PROTECT-02 and the payoff line "The man who industrialized the ranking
  of expertise got ranked, publicly, by the highest authority in his field, and came up short"
  (`:264`) must both survive untouched. The repair is a scoping edit to one sentence, not a rework
  of the paragraph. PROTECT-01 (the spine) also passes through this paragraph — do not lose "Ranked,
  seeded, cut lists, top four in the state," which is accurate and is the spine's callback.
- **Acceptance test.** No sentence in the piece asserts as fact that Wang narrowly qualified for any
  threshold other than the sixth-grade math competition, and any surviving generalization is
  explicitly scoped to his self-description. A reader who knows the $14.3B deal terms can read the
  paragraph without encountering a claim they know to be false.

### P0-04 — Thunderforge "$500 million" is a disputed figure stated as flat fact
