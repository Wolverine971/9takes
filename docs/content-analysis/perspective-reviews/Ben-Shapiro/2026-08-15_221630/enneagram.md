## Bottom-line verdict

Type 1 is a coherent best-fit reading because the evidence spans self-correction, rule orientation, anger translated into principled language, institution-building, costly adherence, and a recurring need to classify error. Type 6 is the strongest alternative; 1w2 and social-first should remain less certain than the core type.

## What landed

The public mistake list and Andrew Neil response point the corrective pattern inward. The Sabbath supplies a non-political example of rule adherence, while Breitbart supplies a costly public one.

## What missed

The Type Four stress reading is plausible resonance rather than demonstrated movement. Religious duty, loyalty, and threat-scanning could reflect Type 6, culture, conviction, or professional role without Type 1 being the sole cause.

## What I expected

I expected motive-level reasoning, countertyping, confounds, wing/subtype separation, arrows, and a falsifier. The Rabbit Hole contains each without crowding the main body.

## What surprised me

The TwoSetViolin response is a compact discriminator: external criticism is accepted immediately when Shapiro judges the standard itself to be correct.

## Red flags

No typology blocker. Political ideology, debate speed, Orthodox observance, and anger are not used alone to determine type.

## Specific improvements

At the next refresh, keep arrow language explicitly theoretical and seek first-person evidence distinguishing fear of error from fear of insecurity or betrayal.

## Follow-on questions

When a principle, an alliance, and a security concern conflict, which one does Shapiro describe as decisive?
