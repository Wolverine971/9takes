## Bottom-line verdict

Type Four is a serious and non-obvious call here. The draft types motivation through identity reconstruction, archetype, witnessed meaning, and embodied performance rather than through sadness or eccentricity alone. It names Type One as the strongest alternative and states what evidence would change the call. The subtype case is plausible; the stress-to-Two paragraph and one childhood inference are less discriminating than the central argument.

## What landed

- **ENNEAGRAM-H1:** "Perfectionism describes the schedule. Type Four explains why the schedule became myth, public theater, and a bid for witnesses." This is the decisive behavioral-versus-motivational distinction and must survive.
- **ENNEAGRAM-H2:** The Type One counterargument states a real falsifier: private preoccupation with error, goodness, resentment, or responsibility outweighing identity and significance. That is stronger than a list of superficial type matches.
- **ENNEAGRAM-H3:** "The last job is the Type Four clue" grounds the theory in a bespoke body-role table before interpreting archetype, ambiguity, and audience.

## What missed

- **ENNEAGRAM-C1:** The stress-to-Two paragraph uses absent-father pursuit, usefulness to humanity, and close collaborators as possible evidence. Those behaviors are weakly discriminating and could reflect attachment, mission, or ordinary relationship needs. Reader effect: the connecting-line section becomes the most speculative part of an otherwise disciplined typing.
- **ENNEAGRAM-C2:** "The adult has designed a life in which every cell receives attention" risks treating a poignant childhood analogy as motive evidence. Childhood content can contextualize a pattern, but it should not function as proof of Four or subtype.

## What I expected

I expected the draft to confront Type One, Three, and Five; distinguish core motive from visible discipline; name uncertainty; and avoid typing from trauma alone. It meets those expectations. I also looked for envy or deficiency evidence and found little, which the countercase should continue to acknowledge implicitly rather than invent.

## What surprised me

Johnson's willingness to accept Dracula, Dorian Gray, and Prometheus together is stronger evidence than his unusual appearance. It suggests attachment to a symbol that can hold beauty and monstrosity, not merely image optimization.
