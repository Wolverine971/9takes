- **Evidence and confidence:** The childhood transcript supports waiting, sadness, and carrying on; it does not state the cell-attention mechanism. High confidence.

- **Expected reader benefit:** Readers can separate reported memory from psychological inference while retaining the empathy turn.
- **Protected hit or tradeoff at risk:** PROTECT-03, the window motif's emotional force.
- **Acceptance test:** Pass if the sentence reads unmistakably as the article's interpretation and the paragraph still ends on attention/data; fail if it states causation as Johnson's fact or adds a generic disclaimer.
