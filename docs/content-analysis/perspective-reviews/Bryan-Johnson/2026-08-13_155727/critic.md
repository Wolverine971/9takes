## Bottom-line verdict

The revision closes the original article's biggest credibility gap. Independent scientists now
challenge the evidence, and the workplace-control theme reaches beyond Douthat's philosophical frame
into attributed reporting about actual organizational power.

## What landed

"Radical transparency about personal data is not the same thing as independent validation" is the
necessary scientific distinction. The confidentiality paragraph gives the allegations, their status,
their relevance, Johnson's response, and the Times's defense without pretending to adjudicate them.

## What missed

correctly limits its claims instead of offering false completeness.

receive pressure. Each appears in proportion to a personality profile.

a generic skeptic's dismissal.
