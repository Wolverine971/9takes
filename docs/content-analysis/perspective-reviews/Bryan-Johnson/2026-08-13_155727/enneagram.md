## Bottom-line verdict

Type 3w4 is better supported than the draft's former 4w3 call. The argument types the response to
identity collapse: Johnson replaces a vanished achievement-role with an executable, measured,
publicly distributed, historically positioned role. Four remains a substantial wing and alternative.

## What landed

The diagnosis uses a real discriminator instead of surface behavior. Type One explains the regimen;
Four explains archetype and singularity; Three best explains why identity is rebuilt as demonstrable
value, persona, audience, product, and historical first. The article names evidence that could
reverse the conclusion.

## What missed

Instinctual stacking is necessarily the least certain claim. Social-first is plausible from the
species mission and public exemplary role, but those are also curated brand evidence. The article's
best-guess language and medium confidence contain the problem.

## What I expected

I expected direct treatment of Three, Four, One, Five, wings, instincts, lines, falsifiers, and the
difference between performance and private motive. All are covered.

## What surprised me

Tolo's account that Johnson challenged righteous right-and-wrong boxes is useful negative evidence
against One and prevents discipline from deciding the type by itself.
