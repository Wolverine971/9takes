case and does not weaponize controversy, childhood, or illness as typing evidence. Current science
and family counterevidence materially improve trust.

truths: transparent N-of-1 work can generate signals, and public reach can outpace evidence. The Rich
Roll material prevents Talmage from representing all three children. The workplace paragraph states
claims, basis, response, and diagnostic irrelevance.

more defensible identity discriminator and medium confidence, but future editors must keep the gap
visible.
