## Bottom-line verdict

Type 4w3 is a defensible, discriminated call. The article now makes its strongest case through
self-authorship, embodied meaning, mythic ambiguity, and the conversion of painful material into a
singular witnessed identity. It does not type him Four because he is unusual, deep, artistic, or
uninterested in bragging.

## What landed

The Diary statement that he chose a game outside others' expectations supplies unusually direct
motive evidence. Accepting Dorian Gray, Dracula, and Prometheus together shows that the character
can preserve disturbing meanings instead of cleaning itself into an admirable success image. The
body as philosophy and performance art explains why scientific measurement becomes identity.

## What missed

Envy and felt-deficiency language remains limited, and self-preservation/social is less certain than
the core call. Medium confidence is therefore the maximum warranted by public evidence.

## What I expected

I expected a real 3w4 countercase, One and Five alternatives, 4w3 versus 4w5, subtype rationale,
growth and stress lines, and a falsifier. Each appears.

## What surprised me

The Brooklyn dance is not evidence of type by itself, but it makes the later choice of the body as
identity-bearing medium psychologically coherent.

## Red flags

No blocker. Endurance does not prove self-preservation Four, and identity collapse does not by itself
distinguish Four from Three.

## Specific improvements

No revision required. Any future evidence that shows habitual adaptation to valued audiences and a
need to remain effective should be allowed to reverse the call.

## Follow-on questions

## Preserve list

Preserve the self-selected-game evidence, mythic discriminator, self-preservation caution, Three
falsifier, and medium confidence.
