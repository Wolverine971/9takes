## Bottom-line verdict

An informed follower will recognize a detailed Bryan Johnson portrait rather than a stock
"biohacker billionaire" story. It takes _Don't Die_ seriously as philosophy while preserving the
best skeptical arguments.

## What landed

The childhood window, six-hour dance, clothing tags, five-job body table, Dracula and Prometheus,
rapamycin reversal, DMT coffee run, and incurable-diagnosis gauntlet are distinctively Bryan. The
Four call now rests on chosen identity and meaning, not a claim that quiet intensity automatically
means Four.

## What missed

Nothing material. More supplement or biomarker detail would turn a personality portrait into a
protocol summary.

## What I expected

I expected Blueprint's philosophical ambition, family testimony, the recent Douthat interview, the
best Three countercase, and serious scientific critics. All appear.

## What surprised me

The body first appears as freedom through dancing, then later as discipline and symbol. That reversal
adds emotional depth without romanticizing the protocol.
