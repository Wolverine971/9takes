# Perspective Synthesis — Caitlin Clark

Snapshot verified: `shasum -a 256 draft-reviewed.md` → `4573f054…f9b186a2925`, matching `context.json`
and the supplied `--draft-sha`. Reader-visible body (lines 96–364, tags stripped): **4,494 words**
against a 4,500 ceiling — six words of headroom, which is the constraint governing every decision
below.

## Executive verdict

All six perspectives recorded `delight: clear_hit` and all six recommended `revise`. Nobody wants
this piece killed and nobody wants it rebuilt. The article's best ideas — the turnover resolution,
the box-score table, the character-versus-output filter, the unresolved commercial seam — were named
independently by four to six reviewers each, and the enneagram evaluator called the filter paragraph
"the best type work I have read in this corpus." That is the article to protect.

What has to be repaired is not the argument. It is the evidence handling, and the reason it rises to
mandatory is that the errors are **directional**. Four independent perspectives, working from
different contracts, each arrived at the same table: every time Clark pairs self-criticism with
self-assertion, this draft keeps the criticism and drops the assertion. I checked all four instances
against the frozen text and the packet, and the pattern is real:

| Where | What she said | What the draft carries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ESPN 2026, obsession quote (H2 1, line 135) | "…because I want to be the best player in the world and I want to help this team win" | Excised mid-quote, no ellipsis — and the same sentence is truncated at the identical comma again in the Rabbit Hole (line 333) |
| ESPN 2026, the six (cold open) | "We'll have to see," with a wry smile, seconds later | Absent |
| TIME 2024, Olympic snub (H2 3, line 190) | She also maintained she was good enough; called it a "blessing" that "woke a monster" | Only "I gave them a lot of reasons to keep me off the team" |
| ESPN 2026, her fire (H2 7, line 302) | "The fire and the passion, that's what makes me, me" | Only "I can always say allow myself grace, but…" |

Individually each is arguable compression. Together they manufacture a more self-flagellating person
than the record contains, in exactly the direction the thesis needs, at exactly the three points the
pre-write memo names as its Type 1 versus Type 3 tiebreakers. That is the single finding this
synthesis is built around.

Three defects sit outside that pattern and are equally mandatory: the practice scene is specified far
beyond its sourcing and reverses the only named account of it (the packet's own largest gap); the
Rabbit Hole's falsification list cites an interior monologue the article invented four sections
earlier; and the Rabbit Hole defines the sexual instinct in a way this site's own Type 1 pillar
contradicts, one paragraph from a link to that pillar.

**The good news, and it is load-bearing for the editor: all ten P0 repairs are approximately
word-neutral.** Five of them are deletions or de-specifications that free words (the unsourced scene
detail, the Cooper corroboration sentence, the "no mechanism for pretending otherwise" flourish);
five cost a clause each. My arithmetic puts the P0-only revision at roughly 4,490–4,500. The P1s are
where the ceiling actually bites — see **Conflicts and editorial tradeoffs**.

Recommendation: **revise**. Do not restructure. Every P0 is a clause-to-sentence repair, and in five
of the ten the accurate version is a *better* argument for Type 1 than the trimmed version — a One
who believes she is good enough and is still auditing is a more specific and more defensible claim
than a One who only indicts herself.

### P0-01 — The Olympic tiebreaker omits the half of its own source that contradicts it

- **Originating reviews.** CRITIC-B1 (blocker), FAN-R2 (blocker), SUBJ-R2 (blocker), ENN-R3b
  (blocker). Four perspectives, independently, all blockers.
- **Location.** H2 3, lines 190 and 195.
- **Quoted passage.** "Her own position, given to TIME later that year, was that the people who cut

  verdict, and **she has no mechanism for pretending otherwise.**"
- **Adjudicated problem.** The article asserts as a psychological fact that Clark is constitutionally
  incapable of self-defense, and the same interview it cites shows her defending herself. The
  pre-write memo designates this paragraph as the Three-versus-One tiebreaker, so the reader who
  opens the citation finds the load-bearing beam falsified by its own footnote.
- **Evidence and confidence.** Packet CLM-13 and Disputes item 2. The subject reviewer fetched TIME

  believed she was good enough, and called the snub a blessing that woke a monster. Then rewrite the
  conclusion to survive it — she conceded the committee's arithmetic without conceding her own
  merit; the audit and the self-belief run simultaneously. Delete "she has no mechanism for
  pretending otherwise." Paraphrase her assertion rather than block-quoting it until RQ-05 resolves.
- **Reader benefit.** The informed fan who followed the TIME cycle remembers both halves. Restoring
  the second half removes the strongest "you cherry-picked" objection available and upgrades the
  tiebreaker from selective to genuinely discriminating: a Three takes the flattering half only.
- **At risk.** "That is not grace" is a good sentence three reviewers liked; it survives the repair
  and should be kept. Do not let the restoration soften the paragraph into admiration.
- **Acceptance test.** A reader of the revised H2 3 can state both halves of her TIME position from
  the paragraph alone. No sentence asserts she is incapable of defending herself.
