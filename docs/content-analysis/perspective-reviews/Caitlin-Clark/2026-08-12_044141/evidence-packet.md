## Identity and scope

**Subject.** Caitlin Elizabeth Clark, born January 22, 2002, in Des Moines, Iowa. Professional
basketball guard for the Indiana Fever (WNBA). Middle child of Brent Clark and Anne Nizzi-Clark,
between an older brother Blake and a younger brother Colin. Played college basketball at the
University of Iowa (2020–2024) under head coach Lisa Bluder. Drafted first overall by Indiana on
2024-04-15, eight days after the 2024 national title game. Age 24 at the draft's publication date.

**Aliases and handles.** "CC" and "Caty" (childhood, per family home video). Twitter/X
`@CaitlinClark22`, Instagram `@caitlinclark22`. No TikTok listed in draft frontmatter.

**Domain.** Basketball; also a large commercial and cultural figure in US sports media,
endorsement, and — not by her choosing — in a running national argument about race, media
attention, and the WNBA's history.

**Why the article covers her.** 9takes personality-analysis blog on a high-search-demand living
subject. Draft thesis: Clark is an Enneagram Type 1 (1w2, self-preservation) whose standard is
aimed at correctness of effort rather than at outcomes, which the draft uses to reconcile her
perfectionist reputation with her league-record turnover season.
