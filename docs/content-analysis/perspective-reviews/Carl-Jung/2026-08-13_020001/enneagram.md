## Bottom-line verdict

The argued-not-declared 5w4 verdict is theory-sound, proportionate to the public evidence, and produces genuinely non-obvious insight from the lens — the fortress-object chain and the controlled-disclosure mechanism are readings a surface-behavior typing could not have generated. The Riso-Hudson 9w1 alternative gets a real steelman, the arrows and subtypes are mechanically correct, and the Rabbit Hole states an explicit falsifier, which is rarer and more valuable than the verdict itself. Trust intact, zero blockers. Five concerns, all sentence-level: the most urgent is a checkable book-title attribution in the FAQ; the most theoretically important is the diagnosis section's third pillar, which quietly treats Jung's own function-stack self-report as Enneagram-center evidence — a category slip precisely the audience of this page will catch. Recommendation: revise (small scope), then this is one of the strongest Enneagram treatments in the corpus.

## What landed

**ENNEAGRAM-H1 — The one-sentence motivational engine.** "That sentence is a working definition of the Five's relationship to safety: what you know is yours; what you merely believe can be taken." This is the best accessible rendering of Five avarice/retention in the piece — motivation, not behavior, zero jargon — and it is consistent with the site's own Type 5 material ("understanding equals safety and competence equals freedom," the fortress-mind frame in `enneagram-type-5.md`). It must survive revision because the entire 5-vs-9 tiebreak rests on it: it converts the BBC belief quote from a famous soundbite into type evidence.

**ENNEAGRAM-H2 — The fortress-object chain as discriminator.** "At ten, he carved a secret figure and hid it in a forbidden attic. At thirty-eight, mid-crisis, he poured his inner life into notebooks he showed almost no one. At forty-eight, world-famous, he began raising a stone tower... when life pressed hardest, he made a smaller, more sealed world, and studied his way back out of it." This is discriminating evidence, not decoration: a Nine under pressure merges and smooths; this man serially built sealed enclosures across eight decades. It is the piece's real answer to Riso-Hudson and it is built entirely from verified biography.

**ENNEAGRAM-H3 — The stated falsifier.** "If new first-person evidence surfaced showing harmony, rather than knowledge, as the felt goal, the verdict here would have to move." Proportional certainty made operational. Almost no published celebrity typing anywhere commits to the evidence that would change its mind. This sentence is the page's integrity in miniature and must survive verbatim.

**ENNEAGRAM-H4 — Type as mechanism, never excuse.** "What the type lens adds is a mechanism, and only that... His entire safety system, from boyhood on, ran on controlled disclosure: decide what is known about you, and by whom. Confession hands someone else the file." The shadow section does the hardest thing in this genre correctly: it explains the Wolff concealment through Five withholding while explicitly refusing to let the explanation exonerate ("Nothing in the pages above excuses that"). Controlled disclosure is also a non-obvious Five insight — most treatments stop at "Fives are private."

**ENNEAGRAM-H5 — Mechanics are right, and person-first.** Stress to Seven and growth to Eight run in the correct directions; the 5w4-vs-5w6 wing contrast (aesthetic-personal vs systems-institutional knowledge) is legitimate and matches the site's own wing framing; "Bollingen is the purest sp-Five artifact in this corpus, a hand-built stone shell with the comforts deliberately removed" is Naranjo's self-preservation-Five "Castle" rendered without a word of jargon. The Riso-Hudson steelman even concedes the opponent's best image: Bollingen "read like a Nine's homecoming."

## What missed

**The third pillar leans on the wrong construct.** "The self-portrait he gave Freeman reads like a Five's chart drawn from the inside: thinking dominant, intuition strong, feeling the declared weak point." Jungian function typology and the Enneagram do not map onto each other: thinking-dominance is not evidence of a head-center type (plenty of Nines and Ones are Jungian thinking types), and the packet itself flags the BBC self-typing as unable to "support any Enneagram claim directly." The genuinely discriminating version of this evidence is subtler and already in the working notes: what Jung _named_ as the deficit (feeling) and what he treated as identity-ground (knowing) — the felt-goal test, not the stack. The published pillar reaches for the weaker, more familiar inference. Detailed as ENNEAGRAM-C1.

**"Textbook case" overclaims the stress arrow.** The 1913–18 material was involuntary visionary influx, not Seven's option-chasing scatter; what is textbook is the _Five response_ (record it, systematize it, treat the flood as data — which the Red Book section itself nails). Detailed as ENNEAGRAM-C3.

**Type 4 never gets its dismissal.** Drenth's published field — which the draft itself cites — is 4, 5, or 9. The 9 case gets a full hearing; the 4 case is silently folded into the wing. The Rabbit Hole audience will notice the missing third chair, especially given the epigraph ("I know things and must hint at things which others apparently know nothing of") reads as Four-flavored alienation on first pass.

## Red flags

**ENNEAGRAM-C1 — Function-stack self-report treated as Enneagram evidence (concern, high confidence).**

- Passage: diagnosis section, third pillar ("The self-portrait he gave Freeman reads like a Five's chart drawn from the inside: thinking dominant, intuition strong, feeling the declared weak point"); echoed as the lead evidence in FAQ 1 ("he described himself as 'characterized by thinking' with 'a definite difficulty with feeling'").
- Reader effect: the exact audience this page targets (typology-literate searchers) knows the two systems don't map; seeing the site's flagship argument lean on stack→type inference reads as the most common amateur typing error and discounts the two genuinely strong pillars around it.
- Evidence: Jungian functions describe cognition style, not core motivation; the evidence packet itself rules the BBC self-typing "cannot support any Enneagram claim directly." Thinking-dominant Nines and Ones are unremarkable.
- Minimum viable repair: one clause in pillar 3 separating the systems, then anchoring on the felt-goal reading already present in the draft's own logic — what he _named_ as painful and deficient (feeling) versus what he treated as his one solid ground (knowing). FAQ 1 should lead with the withdrawal-to-know pattern, keeping the quote as self-description rather than proof.
- Expected benefit: closes the one theory hole an expert reader can put a finger through; strengthens rather than weakens the verdict.
- Acceptance test: no sentence in the diagnosis section or FAQ treats his function stack as direct evidence of an Enneagram type; the "difficulty with feeling" quote survives as self-description feeding the felt-goal argument.

**ENNEAGRAM-C2 — FAQ attributes the 9w1 typing to a book no pass has confirmed (concern, high confidence that it is exposed; medium that it is wrong).**

- Passage: FAQ 1 — "Don Riso and Russ Hudson typed him 9w1 in Personality Types."
- Reader effect: a named-author, named-book attribution is checkable by any Enneagram-literate reader with the volumes; a wrong title in the page's very first FAQ answer breaks trust in exactly the audience the entity-gap strategy courts.
- Evidence: the packet's page-confirmed placement is _The Wisdom of the Enneagram_ 9w1 examples list (Jung alongside Lincoln, Disney, Elizabeth II); my two independent searches this run converge on _Wisdom_ and surface no page-level _Personality Types_ confirmation; only the repo research file asserts _Personality Types_, unchecked.
- Minimum viable repair: change the FAQ to "in The Wisdom of the Enneagram" (the confirmed placement, and body prose is already book-agnostic), or page-check _Personality Types_ before publish.
- Expected benefit: removes the single most falsifiable sentence on the page.
- Acceptance test: every book named in a Riso-Hudson attribution has page-level or convergent multi-source confirmation.

**ENNEAGRAM-C3 — "Textbook case" overclaims the 5→7 stress arrow (concern, medium-high confidence).**

- Passage: Rabbit Hole, Stress and Growth Arrows — "Under stress a Five scatters toward Seven's frantic multiplicity, and 1913 to 1918 is the textbook case."
- Reader effect: readers who know the arrows recognize that involuntary visionary influx is not Seven's voluntary option-chasing; calling it "textbook" retrofits a psychiatric-adjacent crisis into the schema and invites the suspicion that the arrows are being used as explain-everything machinery — the exact failure the rest of the draft avoids.
- Evidence: the Seven disintegration pattern is scattered, distraction-seeking hyperactivity; Jung's floods arrived unbidden (his own account: "menaced by a psychosis"). What is type-consistent is the response — recording, systematizing — which the Red Book section renders perfectly.
- Minimum viable repair: soften the claim to resemblance and relocate the Seven-quality to what was voluntary (the proliferating notebooks, the inability to stop: "stopping felt more dangerous than continuing" already carries this).
- Expected benefit: keeps the arrow paragraph's real insight while dropping the overreach.
- Acceptance test: the stress-arrow paragraph claims pattern-resemblance, not proof, and does not label an involuntary crisis "textbook" disintegration.

**ENNEAGRAM-C4 — A general etiology law the framework doesn't claim (concern, medium-high confidence).**

- Passage: childhood section — "A boy who cannot predict the people in his own house starts building rooms they cannot enter."
- Reader effect: stated as a universal causal law, it teaches readers that type strategies are childhood-caused — a claim the Enneagram cannot support and that the site's own positioning explicitly purged from new content (the 2026-07-15 etiology purge; Enneagram as clustering hypothesis, not origin story).
- Evidence: the packet classifies this bridge as interpretation (CLM-07); as written it is not Jung-interpretation but a generalization over all boys.
- Minimum viable repair: particularize it — "Jung's answer to a house he could not predict was rooms no one else could enter" — same image, no law.
- Expected benefit: keeps the section's best transition while staying inside both the evidence and the site's stated epistemics.
- Acceptance test: no sentence in the draft asserts a general childhood-cause mechanism for a type strategy.

**ENNEAGRAM-C5 — The social-last subtype claim is contradicted by the draft's own record (concern, medium confidence).**

- Passage: Rabbit Hole, Instinctual Subtype — "Social last: he resigned from movements more readily than he joined them."
- Reader effect: two sections earlier the draft says he resigned "the movement's journal and its presidency" — meaning he had accepted the presidency of the international psychoanalytic movement. A reader who connects the two discounts the whole subtype stack. (The broader biography — the 1916 Psychological Club, the 1948 Institute, a 1930s society presidency that is out of scope by governed decision — cuts the same way.)
- Evidence: IPA presidency 1910–1914 and Jahrbuch editorship are in the draft and the packet timeline.
- Minimum viable repair: reframe social-last as how he _held_ roles, not whether he took them — he accepted institutional positions and exited them the moment they conflicted with his inner work, and built dyads rather than networks for his real thinking.
- Expected benefit: the sp/sx reading survives contact with the draft's own facts.
- Acceptance test: the subtype entry is consistent with the presidencies and editorships named elsewhere on the page.

Benefit: precision; removes internal contradiction. Confidence: high. Acceptance test: the sentence claims conceptual kinship, not direct descent.

Confidence: medium. Acceptance test: no reader can mistake the stat for person-specific evidence.
