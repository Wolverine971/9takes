# Shared Evidence Packet: Carl Jung

This packet gives all six evaluators the same checkable evidence base for the frozen draft
(`draft-reviewed.md`, SHA verified against `context.json`). It is evidence, not consensus. The
draft's thesis (Enneagram 5w4, argued not declared) is a hypothesis throughout this packet.

## Identity and scope

Carl Gustav Jung (July 26, 1875, Kesswil, Thurgau, Switzerland – June 6, 1961, Küsnacht),
Swiss psychiatrist, founder of analytical psychology. Coined introversion/extraversion as
psychological terms; author of _Psychological Types_ (1921), the base text of the later
Myers-Briggs system; central concepts include the collective unconscious, archetypes, the
shadow, and individuation. **Verified fact** throughout this paragraph (Wikipedia/Britannica-level
biography; uncontested).

Why the article covers him: the entity-gap packet (S19) rules the general-biography play
unviable (fortress SERP, score 34/100 PASS) and directs the page at the typology long-tail
under a psychology-hub-cluster rationale. The packet requires the Enneagram type to be
**contested and argued (5w4 vs 9w1), never declared** — evaluators should judge the draft
against that requirement, not against a declare-the-type template.

Scope decisions inherited from the entity-gap packet: 1930s Nazi-era conduct and antisemitism
accusations are **deliberately omitted** (contested scholarly territory; no drive-by mentions
allowed); affairs are handled as sourced biography, not insinuation. Knowledge cutoff of this
packet: 2026-08-13. Jung is one of the most fake-quoted figures online; a fake-quote ban list
exists in the repo research file (S3) and the draft deliberately excludes unverifiable
favorites (e.g., "Thank God I am Jung and not a Jungian").

## Dated timeline

Event dates separated from publication dates. All **verified fact** unless labeled.

- 1875-07-26 — born Kesswil, Switzerland; father a rural Reformed pastor
- ~1878 (age 3) — mother hospitalized several months in Basel; Jung connected it to marital
  trouble (MDR ch. 1; event date is Jung's own retrospective account)
- 1884 (age 9) — sister Gertrud born; only child until then
- ~1885 (age ~10) — carves the manikin, hides it in the attic; secret kept ~25 years (MDR;
  self-reported dating)
- ~1887 (age 12) — schoolyard shove; months of fainting spells; self-cure over Latin grammar (MDR)
- 1900 — Burghölzli psychiatric hospital under Bleuler; word-association experiments
- 1903-02-14 — marries Emma Rauschenbach; five children (b. 1904, 1906, 1908, 1910, 1914)
- 1907-03 — first meeting with Freud in Vienna; "thirteen hours" is Jung's own MDR figure
- 1909-01 — Freud's "Moses/Joshua" letter (dated Jan 17 in most sources, Jan 19 in some — draft
  says "January 1909," which is safe)
- 1909-04-16 — Freud's "eldest son... crown prince" letter
- autumn 1911 — Emma Jung writes privately to Freud (letters of late Oct–Nov 1911)
- 1912 — Jung publishes the libido rework (_Psychology of the Unconscious_); Dec 18, 1912 "sniffing
  out... sitting pretty" letter to Freud
- 1913-01-03 — Freud: "I propose that we abandon our personal relations entirely"
- 1913-10 — resigns editorship of the Jahrbuch; 1914-04-20 — resigns IPA presidency (draft's
  "within eighteen months" of Jan 1913 holds)
- 1913-10 — the drowning-Europe vision, twice; self-diagnosis fear "menaced by a psychosis" (MDR)
- 1913–1930 — self-experimentation and Red Book years. **Disputed detail:** start of the red
  leather transcription is 1915 per Wikipedia's sourced account (Black Books begin Nov 1913);
  the draft's FAQ says "beginning in 1914." See CLM-10.
- 1921 — _Psychological Types_ published
- 1923 — begins Bollingen tower "two months after the death of my mother" (MDR); built in stages
  to 1955
- 1955-11-27 — Emma Jung dies; Grail study unfinished (completed by von Franz)
- 1957-08 — University of Houston filmed interviews with Richard Evans (publication of record:
  _C.G. Jung Speaking_, 1977)
- 1959-03 — BBC _Face to Face_ filmed at Küsnacht (Jung is 83) — event date
- 1959-10-22 — broadcast (Jung is 84 by then; the BBC narration says "still working at 84") —
  publication date
- 1960-01-21 — Jung's correction letter in _The Listener_ (age 84)
- 1961-06-06 — dies at Küsnacht, age 85
- 1984 — family moves the Red Book to a Zurich bank vault (Corbett, NYT Magazine)
- 1989 — Stephen Martin asks Franz Jung about the Red Book (event); Corbett's account published
  Sept 2009
- 2009-10 — _The Red Book_ facsimile published (W.W. Norton)
- 2020-10 — _The Black Books_ published (Norton, 7 vols., ed. Shamdasani)
- 2024 — _Dedicated to the Soul: The Writings and Drawings of Emma Jung_ (Princeton UP; a 2025
  journal review cites it as 2025 — see Disputes)

## First-person evidence

All quotes below are pinned to local transcripts (S1, S2) or verified against the full MDR text
(S4). Auto-captions garble some words ("pure intruder," "the lunatic" without "asylum");
_C.G. Jung Speaking_ (S6) is the citable wording of record if page-level cites are wanted.

**BBC Face to Face (S1, timestamps):**

- [28:05] "Naturally I have devoted a great deal of attention to that painful question... the
  type is nothing static. It changes in the course of life... [I] was characterized by
  thinking... a great deal of intuition too... a definite difficulty with feeling... my relation
  to reality was not particularly brilliant." Supports: his self-typing testimony; the draft's
  head-type argument. Cannot support: any Enneagram claim directly (he is describing his own
  function typology, not the Enneagram).
- [08:16] "Difficult to answer. I know. I don't need to believe. I know." Supports: the
  knowledge-over-belief epistemology beat. Cannot support: any claim about what he believed God
  to be (he denied that reading himself in _The Listener_, S7).
- [34:04] "The word belief is a difficult thing for me... Either I know a thing, and when I know
  it, I don't need to believe it."
- [05:31], [06:41] Fear of mother at night; "I have not the slightest idea" why. [07:11] "I was
  used to be alone, but I missed it, I missed company." [09:33] "had no money at all" (context:
  why medicine, not archaeology). [13:26] the seven-boys fight. [17:55] Freud "was a complicated
  nature... when he had thought something, then it was settled, while I was doubting all along
  the line." [27:37] Psychological Types motive: "a very personal reason... to find my own
  bearings."

**Houston 1957 (S2, timestamps):**

- [23:03] "The world in general, particularly America, is extroverted like hell. The introvert
  has no place."
- [26:17] "You know how people are. They catch a word, and then everything is schematized. There

**Memories, Dreams, Reflections (S4; Winston & Winston trans., cited by chapter):**
The manikin passage ("This possession of a secret... the essential factor of my boyhood"); the

## Public record, accomplishments, failures, and controversies

- Accomplishments (**verified fact**): founder of analytical psychology; _Collected Works_ in 20
  volumes (Bollingen Series XX, Princeton, 1953–1979); word-association research at the
  Burghölzli; _Psychological Types_ (1921) as the ancestor of the MBTI (Briggs and Myers built
  on it — uncontested lineage); the Red Book's 2009 publication as a major cultural event.
- The Freud rupture (**verified fact** in outline): collaboration 1907–1912, break formalized
  Jan 3, 1913; Jung resigned Jahrbuch editorship (Oct 1913) and IPA presidency (Apr 1914);
  his account of the aftermath ("state of disorientation") is first-person testimony.
- Failures/controversies **in scope**: the Toni Wolff triangle (Bair-documented; Emma's
  accommodation); the Spielrein question (genuinely contested; diaries surfaced 1977, published
  by Carotenuto; consummation not established — any evaluator pushing the draft to go further
  than "intimate" is pushing past the evidence). The draft's fairness move — "the empathy his
  framework extends to everyone else is owed to the two women who subsidized it" — is
  **interpretation**, clearly signaled as such.
- Controversy **out of scope by design**: Nazi-era conduct and antisemitism accusations
  (1930s presidency of the General Medical Society for Psychotherapy, Zentralblatt editorials).
  This is a real, seriously contested area of Jung scholarship. The entity-gap packet ordered
  omission rather than drive-by treatment. Evaluators may legitimately flag the omission as a
  cost; they should know it was a governed decision, not ignorance.
- Reception nuance: the "torture by television" tag attached to _Face to Face_'s close-up
  style generally, and the program made Jung's "I know" world-famous; his _Listener_ correction
  shows he considered the broadcast's most-quoted moment misread. **Verified fact.**

## Enneagram hypothesis evidence

The draft's governing frame (required by S19): the type is **contested**, and the contest is
the content. Jung died in 1961; the personality Enneagram post-dates him (Ichazo/Naranjo), so
every typing is retrospective — the draft states this correctly.

**Evidence FOR Type 5 (the draft's argued verdict):**

- Withdrawal-to-know as lifelong pattern: manikin (~age 10, secret ~25 years) → private
  notebooks in the 1913–18 crisis → Bollingen tower (begun 1923, no electricity) → Red Book
  locked away ~50 years. All individually **verified**; the chain reading is **interpretation**.
- Knowledge-over-belief epistemology: [34:04] and [08:16] BBC quotes. **Verified** as quotes;
  their mapping to Five's "what you know is yours" is **interpretation**.
- Self-reported function stack: thinking dominant, feeling deficient ([28:05]). **Verified**
  as testimony; note it is self-report at 83 about a construct he invented.
- Exit-not-takeover after the Freud break (resigned rather than fought for the movement).
  **Verified fact**, interpretive weight contestable.

**Evidence AGAINST Type 5 / FOR the 9w1 alternative (Riso-Hudson):**

- Riso-Hudson's 9w1 typing is real and **verified**: _The Wisdom of the Enneagram_'s 9w1
  examples list includes Carl Jung alongside Abraham Lincoln, Walt Disney, Queen Elizabeth II,
  Audrey Hepburn, Norman Rockwell. (The FAQ names _Personality Types_ as the placement; the
  independently confirmed placement this run is _Wisdom_; the research file asserts both. See
  CLM-02.)
- Reconciling-of-opposites as signature method; six years of deference to Freud; buried anger
  surfacing explosively (the seven-boys fight, [13:26]); nature-merger language at Bollingen.
  All **verified** as behavior/quotes; the Nine reading of them is a serious rival
  **interpretation**.
- Anti-5 surface facts: large clinical practice, public lecturing, marriage plus affairs,
  physical vigor (stone carving, sailing). **Verified.**
- Closest alternative per the draft and per Drenth's published analysis: the field is 4, 5, 9.
  Drenth (S11) weighed all three and landed 5w4 — **verified** that his article exists and
  concludes 5.
- Community priors: personality-database votes INFJ + 5w4 (**verified** as what the votes say;
  Tier 4 — establishes expectations only).
- Discriminating evidence the draft names (the "fortress-object chain" and self-report
  centering cognition vs harmony): **interpretation**, and fairly labeled as the tiebreaker
  rather than proof.
- Behavior the type does not explain, per the draft's own working notes: eruptive gut
  physicality (the fight, stone carving, commanding late-life presence) — handled as
  integration-to-8. Evaluators should test whether that handling is principled or convenient.

**Falsifier the draft itself states:** "If new first-person evidence surfaced showing harmony,
rather than knowledge, as the felt goal, the verdict here would have to move." This is a
testable commitment; no such first-person evidence was found in this research pass.

## Disputes and unresolved questions

   May 6, 1856). The one clean factual error found. Any conclusion resting on the scene's
   precision should note it.
2. **Red Book work start-year: 1914 (draft FAQ) vs 1915 (Wikipedia's sourced account of the
   folio transcription).** "Some 16 years" survives either way; the FAQ's specific "beginning
   in 1914" should stay qualified or be re-anchored to the Black Books (Nov 1913) vs the folio
   (1915).
3. **Which Riso-Hudson book carries the 9w1 typing.** Confirmed this run: _The Wisdom of the
   Enneagram_ 9w1 examples list. The FAQ's naming of _Personality Types_ rests on the repo
   research file only. Until page-checked, FAQ wording is exposed.
4. **The broadcast-age optics:** the film's own narration says 84; the draft's "83-year-old" is
   correct for the March filming. Not an error, but a predictable reader challenge.
5. **Red Book sales figures** (sold out pre-publication, #4 Amazon, 50k+ copies): jungpage.org
   report only, unaudited; the draft's "as reported by the Jung Page" is the honest ceiling.
6. **"Sometimes at three"** (study habit): plausibly in MDR per the second-pass verification,
   but no page-level pin exists in the repo.
7. **Dedicated to the Soul: 2024 vs 2025** in citations (PUP materials vs the Wiley review).
   The draft's 2024 is the better-supported year.
8. **Spielrein**: permanently qualified — the evidence supports "intimate" and no more; both
   harsher and softer readings outrun the sources.
9. **The Enneagram typing itself** is unresolvable in principle (retrospective, contested at
   the top of the field). The draft's argued-not-declared structure is the required handling,
   and the falsifier it states (harmony vs knowledge as felt goal) is the honest test.
10. **Nazi-era conduct** is omitted by governed decision, so this packet contains no evidence
    on it; evaluators who weigh the omission should treat it as a scope question, not an
    evidence question.

## Research limitations

- The NYT Corbett article could not be fetched directly (domain blocked); its load-bearing
  details (1984 vault, the Franz Jung/Martin quote, physicality) were confirmed through search
  excerpts reproducing its text and through secondary reprints. Page-level re-reading was not
  possible this run.
- No physical books were consulted. _C.G. Jung Speaking_, _The Wisdom of the Enneagram_,
  _Personality Types_, and the Bair biography are cited through the repo research file and
  convergent secondary reproductions; page-level citations would require the volumes. The one
  claim exposed by this is the FAQ's naming of _Personality Types_ (CLM-02, dispute 3).
- MDR quotes are verified against the Winston & Winston full text (prior pass + this run's
  spot-checks) but cited by chapter, not page, except the murderess passage (pp. 115–117 per
  S22).
- The BBC and Houston wordings rest on auto-captions checked against _C.G. Jung Speaking_
  excerpts; caption garble means micro-wording (articles, contractions) cannot be guaranteed.
- personality-database vote _totals_ were not extracted (the profile requires interaction);
  only the headline typings (INFJ, 5w4) are confirmed.
- The #shadowwork 2.3B figure is a 2023-era snapshot and was not re-measured; TikTok's own
  current counter was not checked.
- Nazi-era conduct was not researched at all, by scope decision (S19); nothing here should be
  read as evidence in either direction on that question.
