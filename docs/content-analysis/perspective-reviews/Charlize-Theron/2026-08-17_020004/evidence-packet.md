## Identity and scope

**Verified fact.** Charlize Theron, born 7 August 1975 in Benoni, Transvaal, South Africa; the only
child of Gerda (née Maritz) and Charles Theron, road builders who lived on a smallholding outside
Johannesburg. Dual South African and American citizenship. Actor, film producer (Denver & Delilah
Productions), and humanitarian (founder, Charlize Theron Africa Outreach Project; UN Messenger of
Peace). Academy Award for Best Actress, _Monster_ (76th Academy Awards, 29 February 2004), plus Best
Actress nominations for _North Country_ (78th, 2006) and _Bombshell_ (92nd, 2020). [S-01, S-02, S-16]

**Verified fact.** She turned 51 on 7 August 2026. [S-01]

## First-person evidence

All quotations below are Theron's own words. Where the original was not directly fetched, the entry is
marked **reported**. The repository research file
(`docs/content-analysis/research/Charlize-Theron.md`) carries approximate transcript locators for the
_Call Her Daddy_ material; those locators are reproduced here but were **not independently re-verified
against the audio** by this packet.

### On the childhood persona (_Call Her Daddy_, 2 July 2025) [S-22]

Answering "what would your classmates say you were like in high school":

- "there was a lot of um alcoholism in my family on both sides and a lot of shame" (~06:06)
- "my dad … he'd show up in states that you know was just very inconsistent" (~06:21)
- "there was a part of me that was very uh like closed off and not necessarily sharing the truth about
  my who I was in my life. like kind of I think I created somewhat of **an alter ego that came from
  like a happy house** and like my family were good happy people" (~06:33)
- "I think they think of me in a way that was not really authentic me. It was a put forth version of
  me." (~09:47)
- On two girls she admired, **Charlene** and **Marliss**: "I think it was their confidence and that
  they were authentically truthfully being who not only they are…" (~07:26)

**What this can support:** that Theron describes constructing a concealing persona in childhood, and
that she anchors that description to her **school years**.
**What this cannot support:** any specific age at which the persona began. The question Cooper asked
was about **high school**. See CLM-01.

### On what the damage actually was

- "**learning how to placate or hide or defend and shield** and then go to school and pretend like
  this isn't ruining my entire life and **smiling and pretending I'm this like cute girl that is
  fine. Everything's fine.**" (_Call Her Daddy_, ~13:05) [S-22]
- "years and years of this consistent kind of like walking into a house or **waking up in the morning
  and not knowing what I was walking into** … you're kind of **living at their whim**. like whatever
  they're feeling that day … that's what is going to be the vibe and the energy of the house. And that
  inconsistency, I think, was what affected me way more than just that one night." (~10:39) [S-22]
- **NPR, Dec 2019 (reported):** "The day-to-day unpredictability of living with an addict is the thing
  that you sit with and have kind of embedded in your body for the rest of your life, more than just
  this one event of what happened one night." [S-05]
- **Dec 2019 (reported):** "My father was so drunk that he shouldn't have been able to walk when he
  came into the house with a gun. So both of us were leaning against the door from the inside to have
  him not be able to push through. He took a step back and just shot through the door three times."
  [S-05]
- **Dec 2019 (reported):** "Once I got out of the shock of it, I realized that she saved my life."
  [S-05]

**What these can support:** that Theron herself ranks chronic unpredictability above the single
violent night as the formative damage, and that she uses control/whim vocabulary to describe it.
**What they cannot support:** a causal claim that this produced any particular personality structure.
That inference is the draft's, not hers.

## Enneagram hypothesis evidence

The draft proposes **Type 8, self-preservation dominant, 9 wing**. This is a hypothesis. Enneagram type
is not a diagnosis and no source in this packet establishes a type as fact.

**Crowd baseline (Tier 4 — expectation only, never a correction).** Boo, Personality Database,
Sakinorva and similar crowd-vote sites converge on **8w9**. Boo carries _two competing profiles_ for
Theron. This establishes what a reader arrives expecting; it carries no evidential weight. [S-29]

**Evidence consistent with 8:**

- Her own framing of the core damage as **loss of agency**, not violence: "living at their whim,"
  "not knowing what I was walking into." _(First-person, verified.)_
- A repeated seize-the-controls pattern: fittings by an unnamed director → she takes a producer credit
  on _Monster_ and builds Denver & Delilah; no viable partnership → a family built without one.
  _(Interpretation built on verified facts. The causal arrow is the draft's inference; Theron states
  the producing motive more gently as "I love the nuance of storytelling.")_
- Refusal of pity: "people feel sorry for you… wait guys I'm good"; "I don't want to have shame about
  it anymore." _(First-person, verified.)_
- Protection of the vulnerable expressed institutionally: CTAOP's explicit gender-based-violence
  mandate. _(Verified fact; the motivational link to her own childhood is interpretation.)_

**Evidence against 8 / for the alternatives:**

- **Type 3 (the draft's own named alternative).** She built an image-management persona as a child,
  by her own description, and sustained it. Add modeling, three physical metamorphoses, and three
  Best Actress nominations. _(The draft's tiebreaker — that she detonates images rather than
  protecting them — leans on the "irks me" quote, which is ~2008 and predates Fury Road and Tully.
  See CLM-11.)_
- **Type 6.** Sustained hypervigilance: reading the house before entering it, decades of concealment.
  The draft's counter is that sixes seek a trustworthy authority. _(Interpretation on both sides.)_
- **Type 9 / the wing question.** The draft argues the 9 wing from _stillness_ — Miller's "first one
  on set," her non-crusade about her mother. Note this evidence is also compatible with sp-dominant
  8 generally, so it does not cleanly discriminate wing from subtype.

**Discriminating evidence the draft itself surfaces:** her self-description as a lifelong avoider —
"I practiced avoidance, right, my entire life. I became an expert." An avoidance-expert self-report is
not the textbook 8 move and cuts against the type as much as anything in the file. The draft reads it
through the 8→5 stress line.

## Research limitations

1. **The _Call Her Daddy_ transcript was not archived in the repository.** The research file states it
   was pulled via `youtube-transcript-api` for video `zL6N55FJuYI`, but
   `docs/content-analysis/youtube-transcripts-people/` contains no Theron file. Every timestamped
   quotation in this packet is therefore **reproduced from the prior-stage research file, not
   re-verified against audio**. Since that episode is the source of most of the draft's psychological
   evidence, this is the single largest structural gap in the packet.
2. **The auto-transcript has no speaker labels** and it is a two-person show. The research file flags
   one passage as permanently unusable (ambiguous between Cooper and Theron) and one as Cooper's, not
   Theron's. That attribution trap is live for any new quotation pulled from the same source.
3. **Several originals could not be fetched directly.** The BBC page 404s. Variety redirects to a
   tollbit paywall. Harper's Bazaar, Elle, NPR, NYT and The Telegraph were reached only through
   syndication or search summaries. Where that is true, the ledger says so.
4. **No source was found for age ten** (CLM-01) — across the draft's own research file, the entity-gap
   packet, and targeted web searching. Absence of evidence here is close to evidence of absence, since
   the originating interview was about high school.
5. **The "hot lesbian movie with me and Ricci" quotation has no traceable origin** (CLM-13).
6. **_Apex_ reshoot dates are unknown**, which is what keeps CLM-04 at "likely wrong" rather than
   "contradicted."
7. **The Kormákur vulnerability quote is confirmed as said but not pinned to a fetched page.**
8. **Deliberately not researched**, per the entity-gap packet's instructions: net worth, height, body
   measurements, and any current romantic partner. Their absence from the draft is correct, not a gap.
9. **Nothing beyond her own public statements was gathered about her children.** The correction in
   CLM-03 rests entirely on Theron's own words and standard reference sources, which is the only
   appropriate basis for it.
10. **This packet takes no position on whether the draft is good.** It records what is checkable, what
    conflicts, and what is missing.
