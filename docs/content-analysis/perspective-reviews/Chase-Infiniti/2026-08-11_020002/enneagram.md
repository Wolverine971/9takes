## Bottom-line verdict

The Type 3 call is probably right. The argument the reader is given for it is not sound.

The draft has genuinely discriminating, motivation-level, first-person evidence for an Achiever read — "convincing on camera," the conversion of a contractless six-month limbo into a self-graded curriculum, and an inability to sign off on her own work. Any one of those isolates _motivation_ rather than behavior, which is the hard part of this job, and the draft renders at least one of them better than most of the corpus does.

But every place the piece does formal type mechanics, the mechanics fail. The paragraph that eliminates the runner-up type uses two discriminators, and neither discriminates. The paragraph that assigns an instinctual stack contradicts 9takes' own definitions on the page it links to, on all three instincts. The passage the piece offers as its act of intellectual honesty rests on a quote that means the opposite in context. And the falsifier — the piece's best structural instinct — describes the behavior the same section predicts a Three will show under stress, so it cannot falsify anything.

None of this is fabrication and none of it is unfair to her. It is all correctable, and correctable from evidence already sitting in the packet and in the draft. The repairs are additive: the strongest discriminating evidence is already written, in better prose than the discriminators it would replace. The recommendation is revise, not hold, because no new research is required to fix any of the four blockers.

What the type does not explain is the more interesting problem, and the draft gets it backwards: it manufactures an anomaly it does not have (Miller) while omitting the one it does (the concealed credential).

## What landed

**ENN-H1. "Ambition is the boring part. Every type has a version of it. The tell is what she converts pressure into."**

This is the single most important sentence in the piece from this standpoint, and it is the move most people-blog Enneagram writing gets wrong. Ambition is not Type 3 evidence; it is the folk stereotype that makes Threes the default celebrity type. The draft names that trap and steps around it in three sentences, then replaces it with a real criterion — the conversion, not the drive. It must survive revision untouched, and it should be the model for the repairs below.

**ENN-H2. "Convincing. She set the bar inside somebody else's eye."**

The best piece of Enneagram work in the draft, and it never says the word "Enneagram." A competence standard located in the observer rather than in the work is heart-triad material — this is what image-identification looks like from inside, expressed with zero jargon and zero system vocabulary. It is also, as ENN-R1 argues, the discriminator the piece should be using and is not.

**ENN-H3. "Potential is a compliment about a person you have not become yet, and a Three lives in the ledger of what she has already produced and whether it cleared the bar."**

Non-obvious, motivation-level, and person-first. It explains why a season of breakthrough prizes lands as an injury rather than a triumph, which is a genuinely counterintuitive claim, and it derives that from the type's worth-in-output equation rather than asserting it. This is the lens producing an insight the reader could not have reached without it — exactly the test this perspective applies.

**ENN-H4. "Underneath sits a suspicion that with the output stripped away, there would be nothing left there to love."**

Accurate rendering of the Three's core fear (9takes' own pillar gives it as "worthlessness"), stated as a felt suspicion rather than a diagnostic label. Keep.

**ENN-H5. "The public record is too short and too kind to show this clearly, which is worth saying plainly."**

The right instinct, in the right place. It is undercut by the certainty elsewhere (ENN-C5), but the sentence itself should survive and should be echoed earlier.

**ENN-H6. The existence of a falsifier tied to a dated, scheduled real-world event (_The Julia Set_).**

The _practice_ is exemplary and rare — a type claim that names what would overturn it and points at a specific unreleased film is doing epistemics most of this genre does not attempt. The content of the falsifier is broken (ENN-R4). Fix the content; preserve the practice.

## What missed

**The pressure test does not test anything.** The paragraph beginning "It breaks on the razor" is the only place in the main body where Type 3 is chosen over Type 6, and both of its discriminators are non-discriminating. Detail at ENN-R1.

**The rabbit hole's technical apparatus contradicts the pages it links to.** A reader who follows the draft's own `/enneagram-corner/enneagram-instinctual-subtypes` link finds definitions that do not support — and in two of three cases actively oppose — the stack the draft assigns. Detail at ENN-R3.

**The lens is switched off for the piece's best material.** "What 'Convincing on Camera' Means to Chase Infiniti" describes a girl raised entirely as a role someone else assigned, worn so well that it passes, who grades her own competence by whether it reads to a viewer. That is a textbook dramatization of the Three's characteristic defense — 9takes' own pillar calls it deceit, "mistaking image for identity," and devotes a section to it. The draft never names it. The Agnes/Hannah section is the strongest illustration of the Type 3 defense mechanism in the whole people corpus and it is presented as biography. Detail at ENN-C3.

**The counterevidence inventory is inverted.** The one behavior the draft nominates as unexplained by the type is craft praise from a casting director; the behavior that genuinely strains an image-management thesis — hiding a major credential from a new peer group — is absent. Details at ENN-R2 and ENN-C6.

## What I expected

- **A named center or triad.** For a Three, the heart/image triad is the most illuminating and most accessible frame available, and the site publishes a whole section on it. Zero occurrences of "triad," "center," "shame," "image," "identification," or "deceit" in the draft. This is not a demand for jargon — ENN-H2 proves the concept can be carried without vocabulary — but the _mechanism_ should be somewhere.
- **A discriminator that survives contact with the alternative.** Standard practice for a 3-vs-6 call is to isolate what the person reaches for when self-steadying, and what standard they apply to their own competence. The draft has both and deploys neither for that job.
- **An instinct call traceable to the definitions on the linked page**, or an explicit statement that the record cannot settle the stack.
- **Certainty scaled to a three-year, two-role, zero-failure record.** The rabbit hole says this once; the H3, the TL;DR and the diagnosis section do not.
- **The concealed-credential fact somewhere.** It is the most type-relevant behavioral datapoint in the packet and it cuts against the thesis, which is precisely why it belongs.

## What surprised me

**Welcome:** the anti-ambition move (ENN-H1). Almost every celebrity Type 3 analysis rests on the exact inference this draft explicitly refuses. Also welcome: the 3→6 growth-arrow reading of the mentor behavior. It is directionally correct theory (9takes' pillar gives 3's growth point as 6), it is applied to a real behavioral cluster, and "Most Threes have to be dragged toward that. She got there early" is a real observation. It creates a structural problem (ENN-C1), but the insight is sound and salvageable.

**Jarring:** that the draft treats Anderson's "extremely agile and quick on her feet" as evidence _for_ the type — "an adaptive instinct as a professional asset" — and then, two sections earlier, treats Miller's description of the same adaptive quality as the one thing the type cannot explain. Same trait, two directors, opposite evidentiary roles. A reader tracking the argument will catch this.

**Also jarring:** "I try to remember to breathe" is offered inside the paragraph that rules _out_ Type 6. An explicit self-administered anxiety-regulation instruction is not the sentence to reach for when the case you are dismantling is the anxiety type. It may still be a Three's move; it is not evidence against a Six.

## Red flags

### ENN-R1 — BLOCKER — Both discriminators in the pressure test fail to discriminate, and one is an artifact of an unmarked splice

**Passage.** Diagnosis section, the paragraph beginning "It breaks on the razor. Under acute pressure, a Six's anxiety becomes the material; they reach for the authority in the room. She produced a surface instead. And the sentence she reaches for when she steadies herself is the capability claim from the top of this piece, the one about not being here by accident."

**Reader effect.** This is the only place in the main body where the runner-up type is eliminated. A reader who knows the system reads it, checks both legs, and finds that neither holds — which retroactively puts the whole call in question, including the parts that are well-evidenced.

**Evidence and reasoning.**

_Leg one, the razor._ She was handed an instruction by the ranking authority in the room and executed it flawlessly. That is not Six-disconfirming; for a counterphobic or 6w7 presentation it is close to the signature move, and "a Six's anxiety becomes the material" describes one Six presentation, not the type. The incident cannot separate the two candidates because both types predict the observed behavior. Compounding it, packet CLM-04 establishes there is no first-person account of her interior state during the screen test at all — Kulukundis said "I'm sure she was shaking," explicitly flagging it as her own inference. The tiebreaker is a third party's guess about someone else's inside.

_Leg two, the mantra._ Packet CLM-01 shows the two sentences are not contiguous. The published passage runs: "...I'm capable of doing everything. **I've been given these opportunities. I'm so grateful for them — I can handle it, and the people around me know I can handle it. I ground myself and remind myself that I'm here for a reason.** I'm not here by accident." (_Elle_, Nerisha Penrose, October 1, 2025, S-06.) The draft removes roughly thirty words with no ellipsis. The omitted middle contains "**the people around me know I can handle it**" — an external-reassurance register. Borrowing certainty from trusted others is the Six's self-steadying move. The draft's claim that her self-steadying sentence is a pure capability claim is true only of the compression the draft created; the actual utterance is a composite, and the part removed is the part that favors the alternative.

**Minimum viable repair.** Cut both legs and substitute two discriminators already in the piece: (a) her competence standard is legibility to an observer — "I'm at a point now where it's convincing on camera" — where a Six's competence standard is sufficiency of preparation against risk; (b) under six months of contractless limbo she produced a self-graded curriculum, where a Six under the same conditions typically produces vigilance and reassurance-seeking. Restore an ellipsis to the mantra wherever it appears and stop using it as the discriminator. Two to four sentences, no new research.

**Expected benefit.** The 3-over-6 call becomes supported by motivation-level evidence instead of a coincidence of editing, and the piece's best writing gets promoted into its most load-bearing slot.

**Confidence.** High on leg two (documented at CLM-01 against the published source). High on leg one as a matter of theory; a reader from a different school may weigh the razor differently, but no school treats "obeyed the authority competently" as Six-disconfirming.

**Acceptance test.** The pressure-test paragraph contains no claim that depends on the razor incident or on the spliced mantra, and every discriminator in it names a _standard_ or a _conversion_ rather than a performance under stress. Independently: search the revised draft for "not here by accident" — every instance is either ellipsed or not used as evidence about what she reaches for.

### ENN-R2 — BLOCKER — The "one thing stays unexplained" anomaly is manufactured, and contradicts the draft's own reading of the same trait

**Passage.** Diagnosis section: "Bruce Miller, who cast her in _The Testaments_, saw the same thing from the other side of the camera. He told The Hollywood Reporter in 2026 that he cast her because he could not predict her: 'I just didn't have any idea what she was going to say, if she was going to go left or right.' Threes are legible on purpose. Something in her working process is not, and three years of public record is not enough to say what."

**Reader effect.** This is the draft's showcase act of intellectual humility, positioned deliberately in the main body rather than a footnote. If it rests on a misreading, the humility is decorative — and a reader who checks the source will conclude the piece manufactures doubt where it is safe and omits it where it is costly.

**Evidence and reasoning.** Packet CLM-09 supplies the surrounding sentences (S-16, THR). Miller says "not in a scary way," and immediately compares her to Elisabeth Moss: "It was the same thing with Elisabeth Moss; she's not volatile, but she is unpredictable." In context this is a showrunner praising a desirable audition-room craft quality and naming it as his _reason for hiring her_. It is not a report of something uncontrolled in her working process. Separately, the draft itself reads Anderson's "extremely agile and quick on her feet" as evidence _for_ the type — "an adaptive instinct as a professional asset." Unpredictability in a scene and agility in a scene are the same quality described by two directors. The draft cannot have it both ways.

**Minimum viable repair.** Drop the Miller thread from the diagnosis section and from the rabbit hole's second counterargument. Replace it with the anomaly the record actually contains: she concealed her _One Battle After Another_ casting from her _Testaments_ castmates until the trailer played publicly before _Sinners_ (_Variety_, Jennifer Maas, June 11, 2026, S-11). That is a declined opportunity to convert a major credential into standing with a new group, and it is genuinely unaccounted for by an image-management thesis. One paragraph, swap in place.

**Expected benefit.** The piece's central act of honesty becomes load-bearing instead of decorative, and it stops contradicting its own reading of Anderson.

**Confidence.** High. The disclaiming context is quoted directly in the packet from the originating source.

**Acceptance test.** No passage in the revised draft cites Miller's "left or right" as behavior the type does not explain. The unexplained-behavior slot is occupied by a datapoint whose surrounding context does not reverse its meaning, and a reader who opens the cited source finds nothing that undercuts the use.

### ENN-R3 — BLOCKER — The instinctual subtype call contradicts 9takes' own definitions on all three instincts

**Passage.** Rabbit hole, "Chase Infiniti's Instinctual Subtype: Social (so/sx)," in full — including "The social Three wants to be recognized as valuable by a group, and the whole record points there," and "Sexual instinct second, on the strength of the physical commitment: four months of MMA, an award from the Alliance of Women Film Journalists for stunt work on her first film, a background training people in kickboxing. Self-preservation looks last."

**Reader effect.** This section exists to serve readers who know the system, and it links to the page that refutes it. A reader who clicks through — which is exactly what this section invites — finds three mismatches in a row and stops trusting the technical apparatus entirely.

**Evidence and reasoning.** Checked against `src/blog/enneagram/enneagram-instinctual-subtypes.md` and `src/blog/enneagram/enneagram-type-3.md`, both linked from this draft.

- **Social, assigned dominant.** The site's Type 3 pillar defines Social 3 ("Prestige") as "The classic 3... fixate on image, status, and influence... These are the **most competitive, most aggressive** 3s. They **enjoy the stage, the spotlight, leading the charge**." The subtypes page adds "Skilled at networking and public relations... **Social chameleons**." Nothing in the record shows this. The draft's stated basis — "wants to be recognized as valuable by a group" — is a softened gloss that fits almost any Three; it is not the definition on the page it links to.
- **Sexual, assigned second, on athleticism.** The subtypes page defines the sexual instinct as "focused on **intense connections, chemistry, attraction**," and the pillar defines SX-3 ("Charisma") as winning "through personal presence, attraction, one-on-one connection." Martial-arts training, stunt work and kickboxing instruction are not the sexual instinct under this or any rendering I am aware of; if anything, physical training and bodily capacity read toward self-preservation, which the site defines as "physical safety, comfort, and personal well-being." The inference is a category error, not a contestable weighting.
- **Self-preservation, assigned last.** The pillar's SP-3 countertype: "the **least image-conscious** 3s... They want to _be_ good, not just look good... They have 'vanity for having no vanity.' They **hate being seen as show-offs and actively hide accomplishments**." She hid a Paul Thomas Anderson lead role from an entire new cast until a trailer outed her (S-11). The draft ranks this instinct last and omits the fact.
- **And the behavior the draft documents best belongs to the instinct it ranks second.** The pillar's SX-3: "They **often promote others rather than themselves**." That is the entire content of "Why Chase Infiniti Thanks Everyone but Herself," the draft's strongest section.

**Minimum viable repair.** Rewrite the subtype paragraph. Given this record the defensible options are sx/sp, sp/sx, or an explicit statement that the stack cannot be settled from an evidence base drawn almost entirely from two awards campaigns. Whichever is chosen, every claim must be traceable to the definition on the linked page, and the concealed-credential fact must appear. Three to five sentences.

**Expected benefit.** The rabbit hole stops refuting itself one click away, and the credit-deflection pattern — the draft's best-observed behavior — gets a mechanism instead of floating free.

**Confidence.** High, and verifiable in under a minute against two files in this repository.

**Acceptance test.** Open `/enneagram-corner/enneagram-instinctual-subtypes` and `/enneagram-corner/enneagram-type-3` side by side with the revised paragraph. Every instinct claim matches a sentence on one of those pages. The word "physical" does not appear as the basis for a sexual-instinct call. The concealed-credential fact is addressed rather than absent.

### ENN-R4 — BLOCKER — The falsifier describes what the same section predicts a Three does under stress

**Passage.** Rabbit hole, counterarguments: "What would change our mind: a documented episode where she took the safe, group-approved option under pressure rather than the vivid one." Two subsections earlier, in "Stress and Growth Arrows": "Under pressure a Three moves toward Nine and goes flat, disengaged, quietly unable to start."

**Reader effect.** These sit inside the same accordion, roughly ten lines apart. Going flat, disengaged and passive under pressure _is_ taking the safe option. The stated disconfirmation test is satisfied by the draft's own prediction for the type it is defending, which means the piece's one falsifiable commitment cannot be falsified. For a reader who values the practice — and this perspective values it highly — that is worse than not offering one.

**Evidence and reasoning.** Both passages are verbatim from the snapshot (lines 243 and 253). The confound is the same one that breaks ENN-R1: the test is specified in terms of _behavior under stress_, where the two candidate types converge, rather than in terms of _motivation_ or _standard_, where they separate. 9takes' own pillar confirms the 3→9 stress direction, so the internal theory is right; the falsifier is simply pointed at the wrong observable.

**Minimum viable repair.** Respecify the falsifier at the level of motivation. Two workable versions, both testable against _The Julia Set_'s reception: (a) a documented instance where, facing a setback, she seeks reassurance and backup from trusted authorities rather than producing a new output — that separates 6 from 3, since the Three's move is to re-perform; (b) a documented instance where she declines a role or a campaign because she could not be certain it was safe, rather than because it did not read as valuable. One sentence, replacing one sentence.

**Expected benefit.** The piece's best structural instinct starts working. The Julia Set pointer survives intact and becomes a real scheduled test rather than a rhetorical one.

**Confidence.** High. This is an internal contradiction inside a single section, not a theory dispute.

**Acceptance test.** Read the stress-arrow paragraph and the falsifier sentence consecutively. The behavior named in the falsifier is not one the stress-arrow paragraph attributes to a Three. A skeptical reader can state, in one sentence, an observation that would be inconsistent with Type 3 and consistent with Type 6.

## Specific improvements

### ENN-C1 — CONCERN — The growth-arrow argument absorbs Six evidence rather than weighing it

**Passage.** Rabbit hole, "Stress and Growth Arrows": "In growth a Three moves toward Six, and this is where she is unusually legible. The mentor-mapping, the crew-thanking, the sponge posture, the loyalty to the ensemble, the passing-down of Ruth Negga's advice: those are Six virtues, and she reaches for them constantly."

**Effect.** Combined with the diagnosis section, every Six-flavored observation in the record has now been assigned a Three-preserving explanation: either it "breaks on the razor" or it is a healthy Three integrating to Six. Nothing the record could contain would count against the call. That is the explains-everything failure, and it is the one thing a non-dogmatic Enneagram reader is most alert to.

**Reasoning.** The insight itself is good (see ENN-H-notes above) and the arrow direction matches 9takes' pillar. The problem is that it is deployed without a limiting condition, immediately after the same evidence was dismissed by a different mechanism.

**Minimum viable repair.** One sentence naming the limit: integration to Six should show up as _earned trust in specific people over time_, not as a general deference to whoever holds authority in the room — and note which of the two her record actually shows, or that it cannot yet distinguish them. Cross-reference the repaired falsifier at ENN-R4.

**Expected benefit.** The 3→6 reading stays, and stops being unfalsifiable.

**Confidence.** Medium-high. **Acceptance test.** The rabbit hole contains at least one sentence specifying an observation that the growth-arrow explanation would _not_ cover.

### ENN-C2 — CONCERN — The 3w2 wing rests on speech gathered in a context that manufactures it

**Passage.** Rabbit hole, wing section: "Almost everything she says in public is relational."

**Effect.** The wing call is built on press-interview behavior collected during two consecutive awards campaigns — a setting that structurally rewards warmth, ensemble credit and gratitude from every actor regardless of type. The packet flags this directly ("The evidence base cannot cleanly separate a 2 wing from campaign convention," Disputes item 7). Treating role-constrained behavior as type evidence is the same error class as treating ambition as Type 3 evidence, which the draft elsewhere refuses beautifully.

**Minimum viable repair.** One clause acknowledging the confound, and if possible one non-campaign datapoint. The concealed credential (S-11) is a rare piece of non-performative behavior and it cuts against a warmth-forward 2 wing being the operative motive. The existing hedge ("3w2 with a live four shadow") can stay.

**Expected benefit.** The wing call becomes proportionate, and the draft applies its own best methodological standard consistently.

**Confidence.** High that the confound is real; medium on which wing survives it. **Acceptance test.** The wing paragraph names the campaign-context confound in at least one clause, and cites at least one behavior observed outside a promotional setting.

### ENN-C3 — CONCERN — The defense mechanism and the heart triad are never named, and the piece's best section is their ideal illustration

**Passage.** "What 'Convincing on Camera' Means to Chase Infiniti," entire section — the Agnes MacKenzie / Hannah Bankole material through "The bar sits in the viewer."

**Effect.** A girl raised entirely as a role someone else assigned, "worn so well that it passes," who is "starting to feel the seam," played by an actor whose competence standard sits in the observer's eye. 9takes' own Type 3 pillar has a section called "The Deceit Problem" whose thesis is "mistaking image for identity... The titles, achievements, personal brand? Not things you have. They're who you think you _are_," and a "Heart Triad Paradox" section on shame. The draft has written the perfect dramatization of both and identified neither. This is the single largest missed insight in the piece — free, internally sourced, and the reason the Agnes casting is more than a nice coincidence.

**Minimum viable repair.** Two sentences at the end of that section naming the mechanism in plain language — that the type's characteristic defense is fusing the self with the presentable version of it, which is why a part about a girl who _is_ her assigned role reads less like casting and more like recognition. No system vocabulary required; ENN-H2 proves the register works.

**Expected benefit.** The lens produces its second genuinely non-obvious insight, at the piece's structural climax, and the Testaments section stops being biography with a type label attached.

**Confidence.** High. **Acceptance test.** A reader who does not know the Enneagram can state, after that section, why an actor of this type would find that specific role uncanny — without the passage using the words "deceit," "triad," or "defense mechanism."

### ENN-C4 — CONCERN — The stress arrow is illustrated with core-type material

**Passage.** Rabbit hole: "The closest visible trace is her difficulty landing on her own work, which is what a stalled Three sounds like from inside: the assessment never completes." Related: the main-body link "the pattern is closer to [how a Type 9 stalls out]."

**Effect.** Inability to say "I'm happy with what I did" is most parsimoniously the Three's core worth-in-output shame — the pillar's own "every win briefly quiets that shame voice, then the voice returns" — or a live 4 wing, or ordinary actor's convention. Attributing it to the 3→9 arrow stretches a connecting line to cover material the core type already explains, and the draft has no evidence of her actually going flat, disengaged or unable to start. The section admits the record is too short and then supplies a trace anyway.

**Minimum viable repair.** Either say plainly that the stress arrow has no visible trace in a three-year record with no documented setback, or move the self-assessment difficulty to where it belongs, as core-type shame. Deleting the sentence is a valid repair.

**Expected benefit.** The arrows stop absorbing evidence that belongs to the core type, which is what makes an Enneagram read look unfalsifiable to a skeptical reader.

**Confidence.** Medium-high. **Acceptance test.** Every behavior attributed to a connecting line is one the core type does not already predict.

### ENN-C5 — CONCERN — Certainty is not proportioned to a three-year, two-role, zero-failure record

**Passages.** H3: "Chase Infiniti is an Enneagram Type 3, the Achiever, with a 2 wing." TL;DR: six flat assertions. The only proportionality signal sits in the rabbit hole, which most readers will not open.

**Effect.** The evidence base is two lead roles, no documented professional failure, no adversarial press, and quotation drawn almost entirely from two awards campaigns filtered through interviewers and edits — with no long-form unscripted material at all (`research_limitation: no_transcripts` stands, and the NPR interview was not retrieved). That is a thin and uniformly favorable base. The main body reads more settled than the evidence is.

**Minimum viable repair.** One clause in the diagnosis section — not the H3, which needs to stay clean for search — carrying the rabbit hole's own admission forward: three years, two roles, no setback on record. The draft already writes this sentence well; it just needs to appear where the reader is.

**Expected benefit.** Certainty matches evidence, and the Julia Set falsifier reads as a live commitment rather than a flourish.

**Confidence.** High. **Acceptance test.** A reader who never opens the accordion encounters at least one explicit signal about the width of the evidence base.

### ENN-C6 — CONCERN — The strongest disconfirming datapoint in the record is absent

**Passage.** Its absence — nothing in the draft covers it.

**Effect.** She concealed her _One Battle After Another_ casting from her _Testaments_ castmates until the trailer ran publicly (_Variety_, June 11, 2026, S-11). A Three's thesis in this piece is that worth is established by making the achievement legible to the room. Declining, for months, to make the largest available achievement legible to a brand-new room is real counterevidence at the core-type level, not only at the instinct level (ENN-R3). Omitting it while including a manufactured anomaly (ENN-R2) is the pattern a critical reader will notice.

**Minimum viable repair.** Two or three sentences, in "Why Chase Infiniti Thanks Everyone but Herself" or in the diagnosis. It does not need to be resolved — it needs to be on the page. The most honest handling is to state it as unresolved, which also serves ENN-C5.

**Expected benefit.** The piece's counterevidence inventory becomes real, and the empathy section — already the strongest in the draft — gains its hardest case.

**Confidence.** High that it belongs; medium on which reading wins. **Acceptance test.** The draft states at least one on-record behavior that an image-management thesis does not comfortably explain, and does not resolve it by assertion.

## Follow-on questions

**ENN-Q1. What does she name, in her own words, as the thing that frightens her?**
This is the highest-value unresolved question for the 3-vs-6 call, and it would settle ENN-R1 better than any repair. If she names exposure, being found out, or not being good enough, that is shame and points to 3. If she names getting it wrong, letting people down, or not being prepared enough, that is anxiety and points to 6. **Best source:** THR, "Chase Infiniti on What Terrifies Her Most About 'The Testaments'" — still unread behind the tollbit 402. Try a syndicated copy via the documented Yahoo/AOL workaround.

**ENN-Q2. Does the credit-deflection reflex hold outside a promotional setting?**
Answers ENN-C2 and materially affects ENN-R3. If it holds in unscripted long-form, it is character; if it evaporates, it is campaign convention and the wing and instinct calls both weaken. **Best source:** NPR, April 7, 2026 (S-13, fetch timed out) — Tier 1, long-form, unscripted, and the one source that would partially lift the standing `no_transcripts` limitation.

**ENN-Q3. Was the red-carpet line self-generated or given to her?**
Packet CLM-14 records the dispute and flags that the packet's own reading came through a summarizer. It matters because it sits inside the adjudicating section: a self-authored frame is a Three constructing its own permission structure; a borrowed one is closer to a Six importing certainty from a trusted source. **Best source:** manual read of the _Dazed_ article body (Simran Hans, December 3, 2025).

**ENN-Q4. Is there any documented instance of her converting a credential into standing?**
The concealed credential is one datapoint; a second in either direction would move the instinct call from unsettled to callable. **Best source:** the _Interview_ magazine conversation with Teyana Taylor (S-15, currently headline-only behind a 403), plus the NPR interview.
