## Bottom-line verdict

The Type 3 call is sound and, unusually for this genre, honestly held. The draft names its own counterevidence, refuses to convert a self-described mental-health condition into type proof, and explicitly excludes the one behaviour it cannot explain. That is better epistemic hygiene than most Enneagram writing about a living public figure, and the diagnosis section argues motivation (worth purchased by delivery) rather than surface behaviour (he works hard). The core call should stand.

Three things break trust anyway, and all three sit in the parts written for a reader who knows the system. The instinctual-subtype call is inverted against 9takes' own material and against the draft's own sentence calling Beckham "the most image-conscious footballer in England." The showcase exhibit for deliberate image-construction — the 2000 beanie reveal — is contradicted by Beckham's own account and self-contradicted one paragraph later, and the true version reverses the motive it is cited to prove. And the hinge sentence of the origin section asserts that a parenting method *made* a Three, which the framework does not claim and which 9takes' own positioning research forbids.

Underneath those, the deeper missed opportunity is thematic: the piece has a full H2 on image as a second career and never uses the Heart Triad's image-for-identity mechanism to explain it. It reaches instead for "insurance," a rational-hedge reading that explains the behaviour without touching the motive. The lens is doing 70 percent of the work it could do.

## What landed

**ENN-H1. The exclusion of the 2am routine from the case for type.**

> "No clean typing explains the audience-free cleaning at 2am. It is excluded from the case for type." (Rabbit Hole, line 317)

This is the single most important sentence in the draft for my perspective. A type claim that absorbs every available behaviour is unfalsifiable, and the draft's most cinematic image is the one it declines to spend. It also has the rare structural courage to open on that image and then refuse to cash it. Must survive revision verbatim; if the cold open stays, this sentence is what licenses it.

**ENN-H2. The refusal to convert self-described OCD into type evidence.**

> "Those are Beckham's words. A television interview cannot establish a clinical diagnosis, and an Enneagram profile should not absorb a mental-health condition into type theory." (Father section)

Compulsive precision is the most commonly abused Type 1 "evidence" in celebrity typing, and self-reported OCD is the most commonly abused evidence of all. The draft names the temptation and declines it in-line, then declines it a second time in the Rabbit Hole. Must survive.

**ENN-H3. Counterevidence left standing as counterevidence.**

> "His reserve remains genuine counterevidence rather than something the Type 3 case gets to explain away." (Rabbit Hole, line 315)

Most typings metabolise the inconvenient trait ("the quiet is just the sp-3 counter-type"). This one leaves it on the table. Must survive; it is what makes the confidence claim credible.

**ENN-H4. Ted's mechanism in Ted's own voice.**

> "No. If I told him how good he was, then he's got nothing to work at." (Father section)

The strongest single item of evidence in the piece, because it is the engine described by the person who built it rather than inferred by the writer. Its placement immediately before the interpretive paragraph is correct. Must survive.

**ENN-H5. The growth line used as a hypothesis with actual evidence.**

> "The growth line toward Six is a more useful hypothesis: retirement has emphasized loyalty, service and long commitments, from the club he spent a decade building to two decades with UNICEF." (Rabbit Hole, line 309)

Arrow directions are correct against 9takes' own Type 3 page (stress 9, security 6; `src/blog/enneagram/enneagram-type-3.md:89`, restated at :211 and :569). More than correct, the Six line is populated with real long-horizon evidence instead of being listed as decoration, and it does real explanatory work — the decade on Miami and the twenty years with UNICEF are exactly what a Three-integrating-to-Six looks like, and they are otherwise the piece's most Six-and-Nine-shaped facts. Must survive.

**ENN-H6. The counter-thread: what the type does not explain.**

> "A hive rewards attention, then makes him wait." (Beekeeping section, line 228)

> "It marks the one place where the method has nothing to offer, because the man who has answered every crisis of his life by producing something cannot produce this." (Brooklyn passage, line 292)

This is the delight. My contract asks whether the draft states what the proposed type *does not* explain; this one does it twice, as narrative rather than caveat, and the second instance is the best type-thinking in the piece — it identifies the precise boundary of the Three strategy (it cannot manufacture another person's consent) without claiming to know anything private. Preserve both, and preserve the ordering that lets the bees pay off the Brooklyn beat.

**ENN-H7. The identity-level ambition discriminator, rendered concretely.**

> "Sixteen years from clause to Messi: the same boy in the park, on a timeline he could not compress." (Brand section, line 278)

This is the draft's strongest actual discriminator between Three and Nine and it never announces itself as one. Sixteen years of pursuing an arena nobody assigned him is not accommodation, and it is not craft-conscientiousness either. Must survive; see ENN-C1, where it should also be recruited into the Rabbit Hole.

## What missed

**The image sections never meet the image type.** The brand H2 is the best-reported material in the piece and the diagnosis section never returns to it. The reader is shown a man who staged haircuts, framed a facial wound for the cameras, sold his wedding as a merger, and guest-edited a magazine to place his own honey in it — and the interpretive frame offered is "insurance," a cost-benefit hedge. Type 3 has a specific, non-obvious account of exactly this behaviour, and it is not risk management. See ENN-C3.

**The Nine rebuttal answers the weak Nine.** Calm and a soft voice are the *surface* Nine case. The draft's own line at 190 — Ted "decided, more or less at David's birth, what his son was going to be" — is the strong one, and it is never routed to the alternative it favours. See ENN-C1.

**The piece never says what would change its mind.** It states what its confidence rests on. It never states the observation that would break it. For a profile whose whole rhetorical posture is proportional certainty, that omission is felt. See ENN-C2.

## What surprised me

- **Welcome:** the draft anticipated the standard abuse of trauma-as-type-proof before I could raise it, and then anticipated it again in the Rabbit Hole. I came in expecting to write that finding and instead got to quote it as a hit.
- **Welcome:** the beekeeping thread is a genuinely novel structural device for this genre. Most celebrity typings prove the type; this one builds a second thread specifically to mark where the type stops working, and lands the piece there. That is the "what it does not explain" test answered as craft.
- **Jarring:** the subtype paragraph reads as though written from a different reading of the man than the 4,400 words preceding it. Line 274 calls him "the most image-conscious footballer in England"; line 305 leads his instinct stack with the least image-conscious variant of his type. Encountering those thirty lines apart is the moment my confidence in the Rabbit Hole dropped.
- **Jarring, smaller:** "shame converted into output" (line 332) arrives in the closing movement of the Qatar section as though shame had been established. It has not been mentioned anywhere in the piece. It reads as a phrase imported from Type 3 theory the draft otherwise declined to use.

## Red flags

### ENN-B1 — BLOCKER — Instinctual subtype is inverted against both the house material and the draft's own text

- **Location.** Rabbit Hole, "David Beckham's Instinctual Subtype: Self-Preservation (sp), with a strong Social (so) second" (heading at line 303, paragraph at 305).
- **Passage.** "A self-preservation-first reading accounts for the understatement, discipline and emphasis on competence and security."
- **Trust problem.** This section exists for the reader who knows the system. That reader has just read, at line 274, "the most image-conscious footballer in England went to Real Madrid, the most image-conscious club in the world" — and is now told the dominant instinct is the one 9takes defines as *least* image-conscious. The document contradicts itself in the one place it is talking to the people most able to detect it. Second problem: the justification given is "understatement," which is manner. Manner-based inference is exactly what the draft correctly refuses to accept for core type at line 315; accepting it for instinct is inconsistent within the same collapsible.
- **Evidence.** `src/blog/enneagram/enneagram-type-3.md:99` defines SP-3 as "the counter-type. SP-3s are the least image-conscious 3s... They want to _be_ good, not just look good... They hate being seen as show-offs and actively hide accomplishments." Line 101 defines Social 3 as "the classic 3... fixate on image, status, and influence... presentation shifts to match each group, adopting whatever characteristics that audience values." The subtypes page the draft actually links to (`enneagram-instinctual-subtypes.md:143`) gives SO-3 as "Prioritizes community recognition, status, and fitting in / Adapts persona to different social contexts / Skilled at networking and public relations. **Recognize Them**: Social chameleons, excelling in group environments and image management." That is a paraphrase of the draft's own brand section, including "He learned enough of each place to belong to it, then carried the whole apparatus somewhere new."
- **Fair counter, which the repair should preserve.** There is a real SP case and I do not want it erased. The same linked page (`:135`) frames SP-3 around "financial security and personal comfort" and "tangible evidence of success, such as promotions or possessions" — which fits the £50,000 M3, the watches, the working-class-boy-with-money material, and the domestic/countryside/2am-house thread. The two internal pages are not fully consistent with each other. The defect is not that SP is present; it is that SP leads, and that it leads on the wrong grounds.
- **Minimum viable repair.** Invert the stack to social-first with a strong self-preservation second, and rebuild the justification on evidence rather than manner: SO for the audience-calibrated reinvention across United, Madrid, LA and Miami plus the deliberate reveals; SP for the security-and-provision material. If the writer wants to keep SP first, the paragraph must name that SP-3 is the counter-type, state that it is the least image-conscious variant, and explain why the most image-managed footballer of his generation reads that way anyway — one sentence, not a defence.
- **Expected benefit.** Removes a self-contradiction visible to the exact reader the Rabbit Hole is written for; the subtype claim starts carrying evidence instead of impressions.
- **Confidence.** High on the inconsistency and on the manner-based-reasoning defect; medium on which stack is correct, since the internal pages disagree and subtype is genuinely low-resolution from public evidence.
- **Acceptance test.** The subtype paragraph can be read immediately after line 274 without contradiction, and no clause in it rests on Beckham's vocal manner or reserve.

### ENN-B2 — BLOCKER — The showcase exhibit for deliberate image-construction is contradicted, self-contradicted, and reverses the motive it is cited to prove

- **Location.** Brand section, line 264 (first telling) against line 266 (second telling).
- **Passage.** "When he shaved his head in 2000, he kept it hidden under a beanie through the entire warm-up, so it stayed a secret from his teammates and the cameras alike until he walked out onto the pitch with the team. The reveal was the whole point."
- **Trust problem.** This sentence is the opening exhibit of the section that establishes deliberate image-staging, which is in turn a load-bearing column of the Type 3 case (the packet lists "the concealed mohawk" among evidence consistent with Type 3). Per Beckham's own account, there was no reveal at that fixture: the concealed style was a mohawk, Ferguson saw it in the changing room, ordered it shaved, and Beckham shaved it at Wembley before going out — which is what the draft itself narrates one paragraph later at line 266. So the draft tells one event twice and gets the first telling wrong. For my perspective the fabrication is worse than a date error, because it inverts the inference: the verified version shows concealment driven by fear of the manager ("I was too scared to even show him"), which reads deferential and conflict-avoidant — Nine- or Six-flavoured — while the draft's version shows calculated theatre, which reads Three. The single piece of evidence has been flipped to the opposite pole of the very question the article is adjudicating.
- **Evidence.** Packet CLM-06, status **Contradicted**, sourced to S-06 (Sky Sports, 2021, Beckham's own account: mohawk under the beanie, Ferguson saw it in the changing room, shaved at Wembley before going out, mohawk revealed later that season on England duty).
- **Minimum viable repair.** Delete the first telling entirely. Keep the line 266 account, which is accurate. If the section needs an opening exhibit of staged image-making, use one that is verified and already in the piece: the June 2002 *Attitude* cover, or the Alice band framing the boot wound. Do not attempt to salvage "the reveal was the whole point" by moving it to the later England-duty reveal without sourcing that separately.
- **Expected benefit.** Removes a factual error, removes an internal self-contradiction two paragraphs wide, and stops the Three case from resting on an exhibit whose true version argues the other way.
- **Confidence.** High. The contradiction is internal to the draft as well as external.
- **Acceptance test.** The Charity Shield incident appears exactly once in the piece, and no sentence claims a public reveal occurred at that match.

### ENN-B3 — BLOCKER — "The making of a Three" asserts type causation the framework does not claim and 9takes has ruled out

- **Location.** Father section, line 200 (final sentence of the section's interpretive paragraph).
- **Passage.** "That is the making of a Three: a child who works out that being loved and being useful are the same transaction, and sets about being useful for the rest of his life."
- **Trust problem.** This is the hinge of the article's origin spine, and it is the one unhedged causal claim in a piece that hedges everything else — including, three sections later, "It does not prove David's motive or his Enneagram type." A reader who knows the system reads "the making of a Three" as: this parenting method produced this type. Mainstream Enneagram theory does not teach that parents cause type; childhood material describes how an already-present orientation got reinforced, not what manufactured it. Asserting otherwise is the specific overclaim that gets the framework dismissed as folk psychology, and it is being made about a living man's father on the strength of a documentary.
- **Evidence.** 9takes' own positioning research is explicit that "The Enneagram's defensible contribution here is not etiology" (`docs/content-research/2026-07-15-enneagram-demand-gap-research.md:15`), and its do-not-write list rejects causal claims of exactly this shape — item 4 is struck for asserting "a causal division we cannot support," and the surviving house claim is defined by the fact that "It makes no causal claim." I did not find this sentence verbatim on the numbered list; the governing principle covers it, and the standard is described as active for all new content.
- **Minimum viable repair.** One sentence, converted from causal to descriptive. The observation the draft actually earns is that the worth-for-delivery transaction is visible in his childhood and never subsequently loosens — not that Ted built a Three. The editor writes the line; the test is that no sentence in the section claims a cause for the type.
- **Expected benefit.** Removes the article's most attackable sentence at near-zero cost to the narrative, and brings the origin section into line with the epistemic discipline the rest of the piece is praised for. Also protects the piece from a critic who notices that the draft claims to know what made a man's personality while refusing, four sections later, to claim what ended his parents' marriage.
- **Confidence.** High on the theory point and the house standard; medium on whether "making of" was intended causally, but the reader has only the words.
- **Acceptance test.** Grep the reader-visible body for sentences asserting that a childhood event, parent, or upbringing produced the type. Zero hits. The TL;DR bullet "He learned that love is a thing you earn by delivering" is fine and should stay — it describes a lesson, not a type origin.

## Specific improvements

### ENN-C1 — Steelman the Nine on motivation, using the draft's own best facts

- **Location.** Rabbit Hole, "Counterarguments" (line 315): "The Nine case leans on his calm, deference and soft voice."
- **Problem.** That is the weak Nine. The strong Nine reading is available at line 190 of the same document: Ted "decided, more or less at David's birth, what his son was going to be." A life spent executing, superbly, an agenda authored by someone else, with the executor rarely asserting a want in his own voice, is the textbook self-forgetting-and-merging pattern. The packet names this as the discriminating question — whether the ambition is identity-level or "accommodation and going along with what was built around him" — and the draft does not engage it. As written, the Rabbit Hole rebuts a Nine case no informed reader would bring.
- **Repair.** Two to four sentences in the Counterarguments paragraph: state the merged-agenda Nine reading in its strongest form, then answer it with the arenas nobody assigned him — the sixteen-year Miami pursuit (already at line 278), the fashion house, the *Attitude* cover, the ambassadorships, the post-retirement expansion into roles no father, manager or club ever asked for. That is the answer, and it is already in the piece; it just never gets pointed at the objection.
- **Expected benefit.** Converts the strongest alternative from a manner objection into a motive objection, and gives the Three call its only real win over the Nine. This is the highest-value single edit available.
- **Confidence.** High.
- **Acceptance test.** The Counterarguments section contains at least one sentence naming ambition-authored-by-another as the Nine case, and at least one naming a self-initiated arena as the answer.

### ENN-C2 — State the falsifier

- **Location.** Rabbit Hole, line 317, after "Confidence in Type 3 instead rests on the achievement-as-worth pattern, serial reinvention and repeated use of results as a reply to rejection."
- **Problem.** The draft says what the call rests on and never what would knock it over. My central question as this reviewer is "what evidence would change the call?", and the piece has no answer in it. A confidence claim with no stated failure condition is a posture, not a position.
- **Repair.** One sentence naming what would break it. Candidates that follow from the draft's own logic: sustained documented evidence that Beckham was pushed into each new arena rather than initiating it (Nine); or first-person evidence that the drive is about getting things *right* rather than being valued for results (One); or a documented pattern of hiding accomplishments and refusing visibility (which would also settle ENN-B1).
- **Expected benefit.** Converts the article's hedging from defensive to scientific, and is the sentence most likely to be quoted approvingly by readers hostile to celebrity typing.
- **Confidence.** High.
- **Acceptance test.** The Rabbit Hole contains a sentence beginning in substance "the call would not survive if..." with at least one concrete, observable condition.

### ENN-C3 — Use the Heart Triad to explain the brand section, or drop "shame"

- **Location.** Two places: the brand section's interpretive close (line 258, "It worked like insurance...") and the Qatar close (line 332, "shame converted into output, output into standing").
- **Problem.** "Insurance" is a rational-hedge explanation: a sensible man diversifies after a shock. It explains the behaviour and evades the motive, which is what my perspective is here to check. The type-native reading is not risk management — it is image standing in for identity, the Heart Triad's central move, which 9takes' own page states directly: "That's the deceit: mistaking image for identity... The titles, achievements, personal brand? Not things you have. They're who you think you _are_" (`enneagram-type-3.md:119`, with the shame mechanism at :161-167 and the room-scan pattern at :481). Meanwhile "shame converted into output" arrives at line 332 having never been set up, so the piece pays the price of the jargon without collecting the insight. Note also that the house page describes Threes calibrating to whatever each room values — which is precisely the draft's "He learned enough of each place to belong to it" — and the draft never connects them.
- **Repair.** Two to three sentences, plain language, no triad vocabulary required. Somewhere in the diagnosis or brand section, name the mechanism: for a Three the built image is not protection around the self, it is doing the self's job. Then either keep "insurance" as the rationalisation Beckham himself would offer and mark it as such, or let the two readings sit against each other. Once that exists, "shame converted into output" is earned; if the writer prefers not to add it, cut the shame clause instead.
- **Expected benefit.** This is the largest available upgrade in insight-per-word. The draft's best-reported section currently produces no type insight at all, and the fix is where the "I knew those moments but never connected them that way" payoff lives.
- **Confidence.** High on the gap; medium on placement, which is the editor's call.
- **Acceptance test.** A reader can state, after the brand section, why a Three builds an image, in motivational terms, without using the word "insurance" — and "shame" is either set up before line 332 or absent.

### ENN-C4 — "The most Type 3 thing in the whole story" is not type-discriminating as written

- **Location.** 1998 section, line 246: "What he did next is the most Type 3 thing in the whole story. He did not hide and he did not explain himself. He answered in the only language that had ever bought him safety. He delivered."
- **Problem.** Answering public humiliation with redoubled work is not distinctive to Three. A One would answer by correcting the standard; a Six by proving loyalty; an Eight by escalating; and average Nines are documented as narcotizing through busy routine. As written, the piece's loudest type claim rests on its least discriminating evidence, and an Enneagram-literate reader stalls exactly where the prose peaks. What *is* Three-specific is the next move, which the draft has and does not isolate: he converted the delivery into standing and then into a larger identity — captaincy, Madrid, LA, a knighthood — rather than back into the same job.
- **Repair.** Keep the paragraph and the cadence; add one clause or short sentence naming the conversion, not the effort. The timeline immediately following already demonstrates it, so the fix is a pointer rather than new material.
- **Expected benefit.** The article's most rhetorically confident moment stops being its most analytically loose one.
- **Confidence.** High.
- **Acceptance test.** The paragraph names something a hard-working One, Six or Eight would not have done.

### ENN-C5 — The 1998 episode contains evidence pulling both ways on the stress arrow; the draft uses each half separately without noticing

- **Location.** Rabbit Hole, line 309 ("Those details establish severe distress; they cannot confirm an arrow movement") read against line 246 ("He delivered").
- **Problem.** The refusal to claim an arrow is correct and should stay. But the same episode supplies both a candidate for Nine-ward numbing — not eating, not sleeping, and "I don't know. I honestly, I actually don't know" about how he functioned, which is disengagement-shaped — and a candidate for core-strategy intensification. The draft cites the first as distress in the Rabbit Hole and the second as type proof in the body, and never notes that they are the same three years. An informed reader who reads both sections notices; the piece looks like it is selecting.
- **Repair.** One sentence in the stress paragraph acknowledging that the episode holds both readings and that the public record cannot separate collapse from redoubling. The draft loses nothing — it already declines the arrow claim — and gains the appearance of having looked.
- **Expected benefit.** Closes the only visible seam between the body's confidence and the Rabbit Hole's caution.
- **Confidence.** Medium-high. The "I don't know" line's provenance is disputed in the packet (CLM-05), so the repair should lean on the not-eating/not-sleeping material, which is not disputed.
- **Acceptance test.** The stress paragraph acknowledges the 1998 material cuts both ways.

### ENN-C6 — Beckham's own causal account of his father's method is omitted while the draft asserts a different one

- **Location.** Father section, lines 190-200, and by extension the TL;DR bullet "The father's hours."
- **Problem.** The draft asserts that Ted's withheld praise taught David that love is earned by delivering. Beckham has publicly offered a different reading of the same upbringing: that his father's harshness is what allowed him to withstand the 1998 abuse — resilience, not a worth-transaction. Those are different mechanisms with different type implications, and the draft's is the interpretive one while Beckham's is the first-person one. Presenting only the writer's reading, unmarked, is the same overreach ENN-B3 flags, in softer form.
- **Repair.** One sentence somewhere in the father or 1998 section giving Beckham's own account and, if the writer disagrees with it, saying why in a clause. It also strengthens the piece: a man who reads a worth-transaction as toughness training is a man describing the Three's characteristic conversion of feeling into function, which is the draft's thesis arriving in his own voice.
- **Expected benefit.** Restores the subject's own interpretation of his own childhood to a section built entirely on interpreting it, and buys a thesis beat rather than costing one.
- **Confidence.** Medium. The claim is documented in reporting on the documentary (the tier the packet already accepts at S-08) but I did not reach a primary transcript; verify before use. See ENN-Q3.
- **Acceptance test.** Beckham's own reading of his father's method appears once, attributed, before the draft's reading is asserted.

## Follow-on questions

**ENN-Q1. Is there a primary-source Beckham statement tying work directly to self-worth or low confidence?**
The line "That was my way of getting through difficult times of low confidence — hard work" circulates widely on quotation aggregators (Tier 4; I did not trace it to a primary source and it must not be used as-is). If it verifies, it is the first-person motivational discriminator the piece currently lacks — the difference between a writer inferring worth-through-delivery and Beckham describing it. **What it would change:** ENN-C2's falsifier could be replaced by direct positive evidence, and the diagnosis section could stop leaning so hard on Ferguson and Neville. **Best sources to pursue:** the original interview behind the aggregator listings; the full Netflix *Beckham* episode transcripts (ep. 1 and 4, which the packet notes were never consulted); Beckham's own books, *My Side* and *Both Feet on the Ground*.

**ENN-Q2. Has Beckham ever framed his drive as wanting to be admired versus wanting to get it right?**
This is the cleanest Three/One discriminator and neither the draft nor the packet has anything on it. **What it would change:** a "get it right" framing would materially strengthen the Type 1 alternative the draft currently dismisses in two sentences; an approval framing would close it. **Best sources:** the full June 2002 *Attitude* interview (twelve pages, never retrieved); the complete *Stick to Football* episode with speaker labels; long-form profiles around the 2023 documentary press cycle.

**ENN-Q3. Was the 100th cap the *only* time Ted praised him, or was there also a first-cap moment?**
The draft says approval "arrived only at the big markers, a first England cap, a hundredth cap." Reporting on the documentary emphasises the 100th cap specifically as recognition he did not receive at any other point. **What it would change:** if the 100th cap stands alone, the draft's plural formulation is a softening of a starker fact, and the starker version serves the thesis better. Also bears on ENN-C6. **Best sources:** Netflix *Beckham* ep. 1 and 4; Ted Beckham's autobiography (never consulted per the packet).

**ENN-Q4. Is there any documented instance of Beckham hiding an accomplishment or refusing visibility?**
This is the decisive SP-3 counter-type marker and would settle ENN-B1 in the draft's favour. **What it would change:** a documented pattern of declining the spotlight would rescue the self-preservation-first stack; its absence confirms the inversion. **Best sources:** teammate accounts in the documentary and *Stick to Football*; contemporaneous reporting on declined commercial or media opportunities.
