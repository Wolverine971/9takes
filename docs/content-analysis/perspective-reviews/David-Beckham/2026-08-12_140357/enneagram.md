## Bottom-line verdict

The Type 3 call is plausible, person-specific and unusually productive. The draft does more than point to fame or hard work: it proposes that Beckham converts public judgment into a finished performance, image, institution or account that can answer for him. That is a memorable way to render a Three-like exchange of being for doing, and the draft repeatedly marks the difference between observable behavior and unknowable motive.

The article is not ready to pass unchanged. Its explicit Type 3-versus-Type 9 decision depends in three places on “the fashion house,” which the evidence packet does not establish as a David Beckham venture. That is one blocker because an unsupported premise is being used as decisive typing evidence. Four further concerns remain: the Three/Nine discriminator is stated more confidently than the motivational evidence permits; one FAQ converts an interpretation into “His Type 3 answer”; the wing and instinct stack rest on non-discriminating traits; and the Six-line example treats service and duration as growth evidence without showing a Six-like change in orientation. With those revisions, the type analysis would remain high-value and trust would be intact.

## What landed

- **ENNEAGRAM-H1 — A genuinely discriminating central image.** “His particular strategy is to turn a verdict about who he is into an object he can complete, then give the public something else to judge.” This must survive revision because it connects achievement to identity and external valuation rather than merely cataloguing success. It is accessible to a non-specialist and specific enough that later evidence can test it.

- **ENNEAGRAM-H2 — The draft refuses obvious behavioral shortcuts.** “Success alone is poor evidence of type,” followed later by “The night walk reveals strain. It cannot establish a type.” These sentences must survive because they prevent celebrity, work ethic, perfectionism and self-described compulsions from becoming automatic Type 3 proof.

- **ENNEAGRAM-H3 — The closest alternative is treated as live.** “There is a harder counter-reading. Beckham's first great ambition was selected by his father before he could speak.” This must survive because Type 9 is not a token disclaimer. The inherited assignment, deference and difficulty locating the boundary between obedience and personal desire are relevant counterevidence even though the eventual discriminator needs recalibration.

- **ENNEAGRAM-H4 — The article keeps the person larger than the type.** After the 1998 abuse, it preserves “I don't know” and Beckham's credit to his manager and teammates before interpreting the visible work that followed. This must survive because it does not turn extreme distress into a neat type demonstration.

- **ENNEAGRAM-H5 — The beekeeping thread yields a non-obvious insight.** “It is that he stayed for the part he could not master in one night.” This must survive because waiting, tending and accepting an uncontrollable pace expose the limit of the finished-object strategy without pretending that beekeeping proves integration or psychological growth.

## What missed

- **ENNEAGRAM-M1 — The deciding evidence contains an unsupported premise.** The FAQ, main diagnosis and Rabbit Hole all use “the fashion house” as a self-authored arena. The packet does not establish a David-founded or David-owned fashion house and warns that the phrase may be borrowing Victoria Beckham's venture. Because the example is used to decide Three over Nine, this is more than a stray factual blemish.

- **ENNEAGRAM-M2 — The Three/Nine comparison stops one step short of motivation.** “Nobody assigned” and “multiplying arenas” support self-starting energy, which is relevant to Three, but they do not by themselves establish a heart-center need for recognition or identification with a successful role. The draft's strongest motivational formulation, “achievement as worth,” arrives only near the end and is not tied to discriminating first-person evidence.

- **ENNEAGRAM-M3 — Lower-level typing outruns the evidence.** Warmth, helpfulness and wanting to be liked do not isolate 3w2 from 3w4; an Alice band, a magazine cover and Real Madrid do not by themselves isolate the social instinct from core Three image work or one-to-one attractiveness; “security and provision” is too generic to establish self-preservation second.

- **ENNEAGRAM-M4 — The growth-line example is behavioral, not developmental.** “Retirement emphasizes service and long commitments, from Miami to two decades with UNICEF” names admirable commitments but does not show the cooperative, loyal, group-trusting reorientation the draft means by movement toward Six. UNICEF also began in 2005, well before retirement, which weakens the sentence's temporal frame.

## What I expected

The draft asks where the inherited assignment ends, but it does not state what answer would move the verdict.

That would clarify why the 1998 sequence is relevant while keeping the claim hypothetical.

- **ENNEAGRAM-E3 — Alternative hypotheses at each precision level.** The core-type section seriously considers Nine, but the Rabbit Hole does not compare 3w2 with 3w4, social-first with one-to-one or self-preservation-first, or a Six-line reading with ordinary loyalty, charity and brand stewardship. Precision should decrease as the public evidence thins.

- **ENNEAGRAM-E4 — Clear separation of distress from arrow movement.** The draft delivers this: “Beckham's sleeplessness and lost appetite establish distress rather than an arrow movement.” That is the standard the growth claim should also meet.

## What surprised me

- **ENNEAGRAM-U1 — A celebrity-brand analysis found its best insight in uncontrollability.** The hive, critics and an adult son's consent are not merely three late-life anecdotes. Together they test the boundary of the proposed strategy: some outcomes cannot be completed, released or made to answer on schedule. That makes the Enneagram lens explanatory rather than decorative.

- **ENNEAGRAM-U2 — The draft uses the documentary's form as evidence without claiming editorial control.** The distinction between choosing a finished public form and controlling every frame is careful and conceptually useful. It lets the article examine identification and narrative management without alleging deception.

- **ENNEAGRAM-U3 — Type 9 remains present in the ending.** The conclusion does not pretend that visible achievement can reveal the exact moment obedience became personal ambition. That restraint makes the Type 3 hypothesis more credible, not less.

## Red flags

### ENNEAGRAM-R1 — Blocker: unsupported “fashion house” used as deciding type evidence

- **Exact passage/location:** FAQ “Is David Beckham a Type 3 or a Type 9?”: “the sixteen-year pursuit of a Miami club, the fashion house and the Attitude cover”; main diagnosis: “Nobody assigned the fashion house”; Rabbit Hole, “Counterarguments”: “Yet nobody assigned him the Miami club, fashion house or Attitude cover.”
- **Reader effect/trust problem:** The reader is asked to accept an unverified and possibly misattributed business as evidence that Beckham moved from paternal compliance to self-authored recognition. Because this premise helps decide the core type, discovering that it may refer to Victoria Beckham's company would break trust in the comparison.
- **Evidence/reasoning:** Evidence packet dispute 2 and CLM-06 state that no David-founded fashion house is established and specifically warn against using Victoria Beckham's venture as David's self-authored evidence.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Remove “fashion house” from all three locations unless the intended David-controlled venture can be named and sourced. The argument already has verified alternatives: Los Angeles, the Miami franchise, the _Attitude_ cover and choosing a Studio 99-produced documentary as a public form.
- **Expected benefit:** The central Three/Nine comparison will rest only on attributable evidence while retaining its breadth across sport, business, image and media.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Acceptance test:** The reader-visible draft contains no generic “fashion house” claim. If a specific venture replaces it, the text names the venture, establishes David Beckham's role and cites a source; otherwise each deciding list uses only packet-verified examples.

### ENNEAGRAM-C1 — Concern: self-authorship is supporting evidence, not a complete Three/Nine discriminator

- **Exact passage/location:** TL;DR “They show him authoring new arenas for recognition”; main diagnosis “Those choices mark a shift toward self-authorship” and “the multiplying arenas weigh more heavily for Three”; Rabbit Hole “Type 3 rests instead on achievement as worth, serial reinvention and results used as replies to rejection.”
- **Reader effect/trust problem:** A reader may infer that autonomous ambition itself proves Type 3 and that quiet compliance itself proves Type 9. That leaves the motivational distinction underdeveloped and risks typing by productivity and demeanor.
- **Evidence/reasoning:** The Enneagram Institute's Three/Nine comparison says both types can be adaptable and seek acceptance; self-starting drive and pursuit of attention support Three, while self-discounting and reliance on others' momentum support Nine. The Narrative Enneagram defines Three more deeply through performance-based value and identification with the successful role. The draft has strong behavioral support for self-starting drive but no direct evidence that achievement supplies Beckham's felt worth.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Recast the “deciding evidence” as the strongest public support rather than proof. Add one accessible contrast: the Three hypothesis predicts that recognition and successful roles stabilize value; the Nine hypothesis predicts loss of personal priorities in service of attachment or harmony. State that public evidence cannot settle this motive and name the first-person evidence that would change the balance.
- **Expected benefit:** The article will make a motivational, falsifiable case while preserving its current Type 3 verdict and serious Type 9 countercase.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Acceptance test:** The diagnosis contains (1) one motive-level sentence for Three, (2) one motive-level sentence for Nine, (3) one explicit evidence limit or falsifier, and (4) no claim that self-authored projects alone decide the type.

### ENNEAGRAM-C2 — Concern: the 1998 FAQ converts inference into a known Type 3 mechanism

- **Exact passage/location:** FAQ “Why did an entire nation turn on David Beckham in 1998?” ends, “His Type 3 answer was to deliver.”
- **Reader effect/trust problem:** The unqualified possessive tells the reader that Beckham's recovery behavior is known to arise from Type 3, while the body carefully says only, “What the public could see next was work.” The mismatch makes the most accessible summary more certain than the evidence.
- **Evidence/reasoning:** The packet verifies the work and results but says the record cannot establish that achievement functioned as worth or identify the private motive connecting distress to performance.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Change the FAQ to distinguish observation from interpretation, for example: the public answer was to deliver, and this article reads the repeated pattern as consistent with Type 3.
- **Expected benefit:** Search-facing copy will inherit the article's strongest epistemic safeguard without losing the concise takeaway.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Acceptance test:** The FAQ attributes delivery to the public record and Type 3 to the article's interpretation; it does not state a private type mechanism as fact.

### ENNEAGRAM-C3 — Concern: wing and instinct stack are inferred from non-discriminating traits

- **Exact passage/location:** Rabbit Hole “David Beckham's Wing: 3w2” and “David Beckham's Instinctual Subtype: Social (so), with a strong Self-Preservation (sp) second,” especially “Warmth, helpfulness and a wish to be liked support a Two wing” and “A social-first reading best fits the Alice band, the _Attitude_ cover and the move to Real Madrid. Security and provision give self-preservation a plausible second place.”
- **Reader effect/trust problem:** The reader receives precision that the cited behaviors cannot reliably carry. Warmth and helpfulness are not unique to a Two wing, image display is not automatically social instinct, and nearly anyone can value security and provision.
- **Evidence/reasoning:** The Narrative Enneagram defines social instinct around belonging, group membership and social institutions, social Three around prestige and approval within institutions, self-preservation Three around material security, and one-to-one Three around attractive gendered image. The draft's examples mix these domains and do not compare the live alternatives.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Either mark wing and stack “unresolved from public evidence,” or give each hypothesis at least one discriminating example and one credible alternative reading. Miami's institutional prestige is stronger social evidence than the Alice band; the Alice band and fashion imagery may also fit core Three or one-to-one image. Do not assign self-preservation second without specific evidence of attention priority.
- **Expected benefit:** The Rabbit Hole will reward expert readers with calibrated precision rather than decorative subtype certainty.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Acceptance test:** Each retained wing or instinct claim includes a type-specific discriminator, a live alternative and explicit uncertainty; if that evidence is unavailable, the heading or conclusion says “unresolved” rather than assigning a stack.

### ENNEAGRAM-C4 — Concern: long service is not sufficient evidence of growth to Six

- **Exact passage/location:** Rabbit Hole “Stress and Growth Arrows”: “The growth line toward Six is a more useful hypothesis: retirement emphasizes service and long commitments, from Miami to two decades with UNICEF.”
- **Reader effect/trust problem:** The sentence can teach that charity, loyalty or duration automatically demonstrates a connecting-line movement. It also implies a retirement-era shift while the UNICEF ambassadorship began in 2005.
- **Evidence/reasoning:** The draft correctly identifies Three's traditional line toward Six, but a person-specific integration claim needs evidence of a changed orientation, such as sustained cooperation, trust, shared authority or commitment to a purpose beyond personal recognition. The cited facts are also compatible with values, brand stewardship, ordinary loyalty or core Three achievement.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Keep the theory statement, but describe Miami and UNICEF as possible resonance rather than evidence of growth. Retain a Beckham-specific Six-line hypothesis only if sourced examples show him yielding personal recognition or control to a durable collective purpose.
- **Expected benefit:** The article will preserve correct connecting-line theory without turning admirable behavior into automatic developmental proof.
- **Confidence:** Medium-high.
- **Acceptance test:** The section either supplies a sourced change in orientation that distinguishes Six-like cooperation from ordinary commitment, or explicitly says the public record does not establish a growth-line movement.

## Follow-on questions

- **ENNEAGRAM-Q1 — What does achievement mean to Beckham when no audience is present?** An answer linking performance or recognition to personal worth, love or identity would materially strengthen Type 3; an answer showing that achievement is mainly duty while his own priorities disappear into attachment, routine or avoidance of disturbance would strengthen Type 9. Best source: a long-form first-person interview or memoir passage focused on failure, downtime and desire rather than career highlights.

- **ENNEAGRAM-Q2 — What exact venture does “the fashion house” mean, and what was David Beckham's role?** Proof that he founded, owned or directed a named fashion house would preserve the example; evidence that it is Victoria Beckham's venture would require removal and would slightly weaken, though not overturn, the self-authorship case. Best source: corporate filings or a contemporaneous official company announcement identifying ownership and operating role.

- **ENNEAGRAM-Q3 — Does Beckham describe adapting his image to gain approval, or selecting images because he personally enjoys them?** The former would strengthen Three's identification and recognition mechanism; the latter would make several fashion examples non-discriminating. Best source: the full 2002 _Attitude_ interview or another long-form first-person conversation specifically about image choices and public response.

- **ENNEAGRAM-Q4 — Is the relational style closer to 3w2 or 3w4?** Evidence that helping, charm and accessibility are used to earn personal value would strengthen 3w2; repeated emphasis on expertise, distinction, taste and a more private professional identity would strengthen 3w4. Best source: sustained first-person material plus close collaborators' accounts, not endorsement copy or fan discussion.

- **ENNEAGRAM-Q5 — Is there a documented shift from personal proof toward shared authority?** Examples of Beckham trusting collaborators, accepting a supporting role, sharing credit when no image advantage follows, or subordinating recognition to a collective purpose would strengthen the Six-line hypothesis; continued use of institutions primarily as finished personal proofs would weaken it. Best source: long-form interviews with Beckham and named Miami or UNICEF collaborators about decision-making and credit.
