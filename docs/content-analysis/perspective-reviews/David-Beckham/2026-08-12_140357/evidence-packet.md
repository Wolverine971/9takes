## First-person evidence

**The childhood rule, in Beckham's remembered vocabulary.** In the October 2023 *Stick to Football* conversation, Beckham imagined asking his father for therapy and supplied the expected reply:

> “If I turned around to my dad and said, ‘Dad, I don't feel great today, can I see a therapist,’ he'd be like, ‘Boy, just get on with it.’ That's how I was brought up.”

The local transcript verifies the substance and shows that Beckham immediately added that talking is “a good thing” today. This supports the draft's claim that he remembers a household rule of moving through distress. The article's claim that delivery became his route back to approval is interpretation, not something Beckham says.

**The 1998 aftermath.** Netflix episode 2, “Seeing Red,” released 4 October 2023, contains the three fragments used in the draft: “The whole country hated me”; “I felt very vulnerable and alone”; and “I wasn't eating, I wasn't sleeping. I was a mess.” In the archival clip replayed on *Stick to Football*, when asked how he functioned, he says, “I don't know. I honestly, I actually don't know.” In the live discussion immediately after, he credits the people around him and calls the red card his own mistake. These are direct accounts of distress, support and responsibility. They do not establish a clinical diagnosis or prove a Type 3 mechanism.

**The halfway-line goal.** Beckham's retrospective “It changed my life” supports his own view of the 17 August 1996 goal as a turning point. It does not independently support the article's language about childhood approval “paying out.”

**Self-described compulsions.** The April 2006 ITV wording reported by *The Independent* is:

> “I've got this obsessive compulsive disorder where I have to have everything in a straight line or everything has to be in pairs... Everything has to be perfect.”

The ellipsis in the draft marks an omitted example about Pepsi cans. This supports only that Beckham publicly described the rituals and called them OCD. It cannot establish a diagnosis, cause, severity or Enneagram type. The opening's 2am candle-wick, dimmer and cleaning details come from the 2023 documentary; the packet does not have an episode-level locator for that scene.

**The mohawk.** Beckham told Sky Sports that Ferguson saw the haircut in the changing room and ordered it shaved, and that he found clippers at Wembley. “Manager always rules” is Beckham's own comic summary. This supports a scene of deference and concealment. The draft no longer falsely stages an on-pitch reveal at that match.

**Money, clothes and cameras.** Archive played in the official *Stick to Football* episode verifies Beckham saying that he liked watches, shopping and cars; that he had a £50,000 Adidas contract and bought a £50,000 BMW M3; and “You've got to look good for the paps.” It supports a youthful, openly image-conscious appetite. It does not by itself establish an identity strategy.

**Marriage-as-brand denial.** On *Desert Island Discs* in 2017, Beckham answered the proposition that the couple stayed together because they were a brand: “Of course not. We stay together because we love each other.” This verifies his rejection of the commercial interpretation; it cannot settle the private history of the marriage.

**Attitude and Qatar.** In June 2002 Beckham told *Attitude*: “It shouldn't matter what people are and it never has done to me. It's just not an issue.” On 4 October 2023 he told Sky Sports that LGBTQ community members he spoke with had felt safe at the World Cup and that he was proud to have taken part. Both statements are verifiable. The second does not answer HRW's documented legal and labour objections, a distinction the article explicitly preserves.

**Beekeeping.** In the October 2025 *Country Life* material Beckham called the process “like a miracle” and used “peace” for what the countryside had given his family. The direct issue source supports the hive count and first phrase; “peace” was corroborated against contemporaneous coverage during the prior final verification but the exact article passage was not retrieved in the original packet. This is sufficient to carry it as attributed first-person language with that limitation disclosed, not as an independently observed interior state.

**The documentary and editorial control.** Beckham publicly said that he did not exercise editorial control over the series. This is an attributed response, not proof of independence. The production credit to Studio 99 is a separate sourced fact. The draft's conclusion that he chose the form of a “finished account” is reasonable at the level of commissioning and production association, but it must not imply that he controlled the final cut.

**Kath Phipps (Manchester United receptionist).** AP's 4 October 2023 report records her account of opening letters containing bullets and calling the police after the 1998 World Cup. This supports the posted-bullets detail as named testimony.

**Brooklyn Beckham (adult son).** AP reported his 19 January 2026 Instagram statements alleging that his parents put “Brand Beckham” and press narratives ahead of family relationships and that he did not want to reconcile. The draft correctly separates them from the parents' later account and says public sources cannot settle the private dispute.

**Rebecca Loos (former assistant).** Loos alleged an affair in 2004. Beckham denied the claims. In October 2023 Loos told CNN that the documentary presented him as a victim and her as a liar. All three propositions are attributed and the underlying allegation remains unresolved.

The draft's sentence that critics were being asked to accept access and optimism as accountability is analysis of Beckham's response, not a fact about every critic's view.

## Disputes and unresolved questions

**1. The salary / “jeans to match” speaker remains unresolved.** The official episode contains the words, and secondary reporting attributes them to Gary Neville. The local auto-transcript is unlabeled and shortly afterward renders “where me and Gary were like,” leaving a speaker boundary somewhere in the passage. This packet cannot independently certify Neville as the speaker. The words may stay if the attribution is removed or a speaker-labelled source / video identification closes the question.

**2. “Fashion house” is not established as one of David Beckham's self-authored ventures.** The snapshot uses “the fashion house” / “fashion house” in the FAQ, diagnosis and Rabbit Hole as deciding evidence against Type 9. The packet verifies his fashion modelling, endorsements, image-building and brand ventures, but not a David-founded fashion house. If the intended referent is Victoria Beckham's fashion house, it is not self-authored evidence for David in the form currently claimed. Name the actual venture and source it, or use a verified example such as the *Attitude* cover, Los Angeles, Miami or Studio 99.

**3. The 2am opening lacks an episode-level locator.** The documentary source and the details are well established in the existing review trail, but the packet cannot identify the episode and timestamp for trimming wicks, resetting dimmers and calling the routine exhausting. Because the scene carries the hook and documentary-as-form thesis, a reviewer may reasonably request that locator even though the factual substance is not currently contradicted.

**4. The “peace” quotation has an asymmetric source trail.** The October 2025 guest-edited issue, hive count and “like a miracle” wording were directly verified. “Peace” was corroborated against contemporaneous coverage during the prior final verification, but the original packet did not retrieve the exact *Country Life* passage. It remains usable as attributed language with this limitation; direct article text would remove the residual.

**5. Documentary authorship must remain precisely scoped.** Studio 99's production role is verified. Beckham's “no editorial control” answer is also on record. The thesis may analyse his selection of a documentary as a public form, but it may not imply that he dictated the edit, authored every narrative choice or made a false confession. The current snapshot states the relevant limits.

**6. Miami's interior remains unknown.** The long delay and pre-completion publicity are public facts. “Recognition arrived before completion” is a defensible structural observation. No source establishes shame, anxiety, frustration or any other private feeling during those years, and the draft correctly declines to supply one.

**7. The Type 9 case cannot be “resolved” by public evidence.** Ted's early assignment and Beckham's reserve support the counter-reading; later ventures support the Type 3 reading. Neither set of facts proves whether Beckham merged with a paternal agenda, converted it into his own desire or pursued recognition as a condition of worth. Reviewers should assess whether the draft argues the balance fairly, not treat the type as source-verifiable.

**8. Current family facts remain contested and time-sensitive.** Brooklyn's allegations and Victoria's response are verified as statements. David and Victoria gave AP no detailed response to the specific allegations at publication. The snapshot appropriately avoids claiming a permanent estrangement, private motive or adjudicated truth.
