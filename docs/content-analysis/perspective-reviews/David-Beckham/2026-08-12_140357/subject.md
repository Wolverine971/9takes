## Bottom-line verdict

Even if David Beckham rejects the Type 3 typing, most of this profile represents him with dignity and unusual evidentiary restraint. It distinguishes his own testimony from Victoria's retrospective clinical language, declines to diagnose his compulsions, gives his Qatar answer without treating it as absolution, and refuses to adjudicate the Brooklyn Beckham dispute. Its best original connection — completed public objects versus living systems that cannot be completed on command — is memorable and substantially person-specific.

The draft should nevertheless be revised before publication. Its deciding Type 3 evidence repeatedly assigns Beckham a self-authored “fashion house” that the packet does not establish and that readers may understand as Victoria Beckham's business. That is a factual and dignity problem at the center of the type argument. The wider “finished answer” thesis also slides at several points from an explicitly framed interpretation into confident claims about Beckham's preferred motives, while omitting his own stated reasons for making the documentary. Trust is strained rather than broken because the profile repeatedly names its epistemic limits and every identified defect has a narrow repair.

Verdict: **revise; one blocker and five concerns.**

## What landed

### SUBJECT-H1 — The Miami limit preserves Beckham's interior privacy

> “The record does not tell us what those delays felt like, and this profile should not invent it.”

This is the clearest subject-fairness hit. It separates a visible structural fact — prolonged recognition before the club played — from a private emotional state the record cannot establish. It must survive revision because it models the evidentiary discipline the more interpretive passages need.

### SUBJECT-H2 — The family dispute is attributed rather than adjudicated

> “Those are competing family accounts. Brooklyn's allegation tests the gap between a durable family image and every relationship inside it, but it proves neither David's motive nor his type.”

The passage neither dismisses Brooklyn nor turns an allegation into settled fact about David. It also acknowledges that Victoria's later statement is not a detailed answer. This must survive revision because fairness to the subject here depends on preserving fairness to the adult son and the unresolved status of the dispute.

### SUBJECT-H3 — Clinical language is kept outside the type case

> “A television interview cannot establish a clinical diagnosis, and an Enneagram profile should not absorb a mental-health condition into type theory.”

This protects Beckham from diagnosis at a distance while honoring the language he used publicly. It must survive revision verbatim or in substance; removing it would make the opening's use of his routines feel invasive and would blur mental health with personality typing.

### SUBJECT-H4 — The Qatar section allows responsibility to remain unresolved

> “None of that rebuts the Qatar criticism. It clarifies the method of response. ... The official honor belongs in the record beside the unresolved objection. It cannot cover it.”

This is criticism without motive laundering. Beckham's response, UNICEF record and honor are included, but none is made to cancel documented legal and labor objections. It must survive revision because it preserves both his record and the impact of his choice.

### SUBJECT-H5 — Beekeeping produces a humane, non-totalizing insight

> “The point is not that he escaped image-making by putting on a beekeeper's suit. It is that he stayed for the part he could not master in one night.”

The October 2025 _Country Life_ interview confirms his beginner question, the 4am assembly, continued care of 28 hives and his word “peace.” The profile turns those particulars into a fresh connection without claiming psychological conversion. This must survive revision because it shows a dimension of Beckham not exhausted by fame, controversy or type.

## What missed

### SUBJECT-M1 — Beckham's own reasons for the documentary do not meaningfully enter the thesis

The profile discloses Studio 99's role and his claim of no editorial control, but it does not include his stated purposes: looking back, experiencing emotions he had not processed, hearing other people's perspectives, and making something for his family, children, future grandchildren and parents. Without those words, “his preferred form of reply” reads less like an interpretation of form and more like a settled account of motive.

### SUBJECT-M2 — “Self-authorship” is sometimes proved by assertion

“Nobody assigned” and “arenas nobody assigned him” are rhetorically decisive but not publicly verifiable. A June 2026 _Variety_ interview offers useful counterevidence in Beckham's own vocabulary: he said that as a child all he wanted was to play for Manchester United and England, that later fame and admiration were not the plan, while also saying he still always wants to achieve more. The article can interpret later expansion as self-authorship, but it should not present the absence of other influences as fact.

### SUBJECT-M3 — The strongest disclaimers are followed by renewed interior certainty

The childhood section first says no public record can tell us what Beckham made of his father's method, then concludes that delivery became his route back to approval. That shift weakens the disclaimer. The same problem recurs when public accomplishments are called “answers” without consistently marking that label as the article's reading.

## What I expected

I expected Beckham's own recurring vocabulary to be used as counterweight as well as evidence. Across the packet and the limited additional research, he repeatedly speaks in terms of work, family, luck, duty, being stronger, talking now when his generation did not, and always wanting to achieve more. Some of that supports the draft's thesis; some complicates it. A fair profile need not accept his self-description, but it should show where its “finished answer” interpretation diverges from the reasons he has actually given.

I also expected every venture used as deciding evidence for self-authorship to be named precisely. “Fashion house” fails that expectation because ownership and authorship matter to the argument, not merely to a caption.

## What surprised me

The draft is more willing than most celebrity personality profiles to leave its central subject partly unknowable. It says “I don't know” with Beckham after 1998, admits the Type 9 case remains alive, refuses to infer feelings during the Miami delays, and does not convert either side of the current family dispute into truth. That restraint makes the more confident approval and documentary-motive claims stand out sharply; they are exceptions to an otherwise fair method.

The Qatar section also surprised me positively. It does not hide the long relationship with gay audiences, Beckham's public response, the undisclosed fee, the documented objections or his charity record, and it does not ask one side of that record to erase the other.

## Red flags

### SUBJECT-R1 — BLOCKER: “fashion house” is unsupported deciding evidence

- **Passage/location:** The FAQ says, “the sixteen-year pursuit of a Miami club, the fashion house and the _Attitude_ cover.” The diagnosis says, “Nobody assigned the fashion house,” and the Rabbit Hole says, “nobody assigned him the Miami club, fashion house or _Attitude_ cover.”
- **Reader effect/trust problem:** The repeated phrase can misattribute Victoria Beckham's fashion house to David, erase her authorship, and make a central “self-authored arena” look factual when the packet does not establish it. Because these passages explicitly call the examples decisive against Type 9, the error reaches the core argument.
- **Evidence/reasoning:** Evidence-packet dispute 2 and CLM-06 verify Beckham's modelling, endorsements, image work and brand ventures, but not a David-founded fashion house. The draft itself identifies Victoria as the person with a fashion brand.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Remove “fashion house” from all three locations, or replace it with a precisely named, David-founded venture supported by a source. Existing verified examples such as Miami, Studio 99, the _Attitude_ cover or the Los Angeles move can carry the contrast without a new claim.
- **Expected benefit:** Restores factual ownership, respects Victoria's work, and keeps the type comparison grounded in traceable acts.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Acceptance test:** A text search finds no unqualified “fashion house” attributed to David, and every venture presented as deciding evidence has a source establishing David's role and the nature of that role.

### SUBJECT-C1 — CONCERN: the documentary is framed as a reputational reply without Beckham's stated purposes

- **Passage/location:** Opening: “So does the form he chose: a finished account of a life built by finishing things.” Qatar section: “The revealing part ... is his preferred form of reply” and “he answered with a finished narrative.”
- **Reader effect/trust problem:** A subject-aligned reader may feel the article has translated a difficult retrospective process into a brand-management tactic, despite claiming not to invent motives. “Preferred” and “answered” imply an intention the public record does not settle.
- **Evidence/reasoning:** In Netflix's 6 October 2023 _Skip Intro_ transcript, Beckham says he wanted to look back, experience the emotions, hear family and teammates' perspectives, and make the documentary for his family, children, future grandchildren and parents. Fisher Stevens says the project could not be “Brand Beckham,” describes correcting Beckham's remembered version off-camera, and says he had to draw out reflection over repeated sessions. This does not disprove the draft's form analysis, but it is strong first-person counterevidence to a single reputational motive.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Keep the finished-object interpretation explicitly as the article's reading, add Beckham's stated family/retrospective purpose and the director's role, and replace “his preferred form of reply” with language about what the public form _can function as_ regardless of private motive.
- **Expected benefit:** Preserves the original insight while visibly separating public effect from claimed intention.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Acceptance test:** The documentary discussion includes at least one specific first-person reason Beckham gave for making it and no sentence presents counter-narrative or reputation repair as his established motive.

### SUBJECT-C2 — CONCERN: the childhood-to-approval mechanism becomes more certain than the evidence

- **Passage/location:** Childhood section: “A hard feeling became something to move through, and delivery remained the available route back to approval.” Later: “The applause he had spent his childhood earning curdled over a single ninety minutes.”
- **Reader effect/trust problem:** These lines convert a sourced paternal rule and a documented ordeal into a causal account of Beckham's inner reward system. That can make the profile feel as if it knows what the child internalized after it has just said it cannot know.
- **Evidence/reasoning:** The packet supports Ted's praise-withholding, Beckham's remembered “get on with it” vocabulary and adult work ethic, but explicitly says no source establishes exactly what David internalized or whether achievement functions as personal worth. A 2016 ESPN long-form interview adds Beckham's own account that his work ethic came from watching both parents work and that he loved practice even when it was work; that broadens the origin story beyond withheld approval.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Mark “route back to approval” and “applause he had spent his childhood earning” as the profile's hypothesis, and add the coexisting first-person explanation that work ethic came from both parents' example and from love of practice.
- **Expected benefit:** Keeps the developmental reading without reducing his discipline to one wound or one parent.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Acceptance test:** The childhood section no longer states an approval mechanism as fact and includes at least one evidenced alternative explanation for his work ethic.

### SUBJECT-C3 — CONCERN: the Brooklyn dispute becomes a metaphor for control that the record does not show

- **Passage/location:** Opening: “an adult son whose consent cannot be produced on schedule.” TL;DR: “it cannot ... compel reconciliation with Brooklyn.” Conclusion: “An adult son controls his own consent.”
- **Reader effect/trust problem:** The repetition can imply that Beckham is trying to manufacture or schedule Brooklyn's consent and reconciliation. The record establishes Brooklyn's allegation and refusal to reconcile, not David's private aim or method of response. It also turns a living person's family boundary into a recurring proof-object for his father's typology.
- **Evidence/reasoning:** The packet says no public source establishes David's motive or current private feeling. In the June 2026 _Variety_ interview, Beckham declined to discuss the family strife because it was private. That boundary does not resolve the dispute, but it reinforces the need not to supply his side as a control strategy.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Keep one restrained reference to the structural fact that adult relationships are not unilateral, remove “on schedule” and “compel” unless a source establishes such an attempt, and avoid using Brooklyn's name in the TL;DR type proof.
- **Expected benefit:** Preserves the living-systems insight while reducing invasive implication and respecting both parties' agency.
- **Confidence:** Medium-high.
- **Acceptance test:** No passage implies Beckham is attempting to manufacture, compel or schedule reconciliation; the dispute remains clearly attributed, unresolved and non-diagnostic.

### SUBJECT-C4 — CONCERN: the salary-and-jeans quotation has unresolved speaker attribution

- **Passage/location:** Brand section: “Neville, who saved his wages, supplies the weekly budget,” followed by the blockquote attributed to Gary Neville.
- **Reader effect/trust problem:** A potentially wrong name beneath a vivid comic quote undermines confidence in the testimony ledger and puts words in a close friend's mouth without speaker-level verification.
- **Evidence/reasoning:** Evidence-packet CLM-18 and dispute 1 say the official episode contains the words, but the local transcript has no speaker labels and the nearby “me and Gary” wording leaves the speaker boundary unresolved. Secondary reporting is not enough to certify the current attribution at the packet's standard.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Remove the speaker name and introduce it as an anecdote in archive shown on _Stick to Football_, or obtain a speaker-labelled transcript/video identification.
- **Expected benefit:** Retains the useful anecdote without overstating quote fidelity.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Acceptance test:** The quote is either supported by a speaker-identifying source recorded in the citation trail or is presented without naming Neville as speaker.

### SUBJECT-C5 — CONCERN: motive-promising headings and metadata exceed the body's own caution

- **Passage/location:** Description: “So why does he clean the house at 2am?” H2s: “Why the Beckham marriage survived its worst year” and “Why David Beckham took the Qatar money.”
- **Reader effect/trust problem:** The description turns a self-described mental-health routine into a personality mystery, while the two H2s promise causal answers about a private marriage and an undisclosed commercial motive. The body responsibly says those causes and motives are not publicly knowable, so the packaging creates a more invasive and certain article than the prose actually delivers.
- **Evidence/reasoning:** The packet classifies Beckham's routines as self-description rather than diagnosis, the affair as unresolved, and Qatar motive as unknown. The body itself says “only they know” and that the available record cannot establish a private motive.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Recast the description around the finished-object/living-system thesis, and change the H2s to promise the analysis actually supplied, such as the public account of marital endurance and what Beckham's Qatar response leaves unanswered.
- **Expected benefit:** Aligns the reader promise with the article's careful epistemic posture and reduces click-driven use of mental health and family privacy.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Acceptance test:** No title, description or heading promises to explain an unknown private cause or uses the 2am routine as an unexplained diagnostic hook.

### SUBJECT-Q1 — What did Beckham say the documentary was for, in the fullest available first-person account?

**What answer would change:** A sourced David-founded fashion house would clear SUBJECT-R1 once named precisely; evidence that the referent is Victoria Beckham's company would require deletion and likely stronger correction. **Best source to pursue:** company filings or an official brand/company history naming founder, owner and launch date, not lifestyle coverage.

**What answer would change:** Speaker-labelled evidence identifying Gary Neville would clear SUBJECT-C4; any other speaker would require immediate correction. **Best source to pursue:** the original labelled archive clip or direct video review of the official _Stick to Football_ episode around the quoted passage.

**What answer would change:** Repeated first-person language about deliberately seeking new arenas of recognition would strengthen the self-authorship case; language centered on opportunity, team proposals, service or family would require a more plural account. **Best source to pursue:** a recent long-form interview or autobiography chapter focused on Inter Miami, Studio 99 and post-football ventures rather than a campaign launch.
