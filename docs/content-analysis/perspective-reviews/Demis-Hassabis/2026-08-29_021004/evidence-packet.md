# Evidence Packet: Demis Hassabis draft (frozen snapshot 2026-08-29)

This packet is evidence, not consensus. Every entry is labeled **verified fact**, **attributed claim**, **interpretation**, **disputed claim**, or **unresolved question**. The draft's thesis (Enneagram Type 5, "the champion who keeps walking away") and its type assignment are hypotheses throughout — the article stating them confidently does not promote them.

## Identity and scope

- **Who**: Demis Hassabis (b. 1976-07-27, London), British AI researcher, neuroscientist, entrepreneur, and former game designer. Co-founder of DeepMind (2010); as of 2026-08-05, chair of Google DeepMind and chief scientist of Alphabet (no longer CEO); CEO of Isomorphic Labs. 2024 Nobel laureate in Chemistry (shared); knighted 2024. No aliases; globally unique name (entity-gap packet, entity clarity 5/5).
- **Why covered**: strategic frontier-builder cluster play (dario-amodei, sam-altman, elon-musk, sundar-pichai pages live), not an entity gap — the entity-gap audit scored him 44/100 PASS and redefined the page's job as winning the personality-analysis long-tail only. The catalyst is the 2026-08-05 role change plus Sebastian Mallaby's March 2026 biography _The Infinity Machine_.
- **Draft's claimed type**: Enneagram 5 (5w6, so/sp) — hypothesis, argued from a "serial post-victory abdication" pattern.
- **Knowledge cutoff of this packet**: 2026-08-29. All 2026 events verified against live web sources on that date; compiler's model knowledge ends January 2026, so every post-January-2026 claim below carries an explicit external source.
- **Verification method**: draft SHA-256 re-hashed and matched against `context.json`. First-person Lex Fridman quotes checked by grep against the local transcript (`docs/content-analysis/research/transcripts/lex-475-demis-hassabis-2025.txt`, 1,168 lines). External claims checked by direct fetch or search-snippet extraction (S-ledger notes which).

Event dates first; publication dates noted where they differ.

- **~1984 (age 8)** — buys ZX Spectrum with chess winnings, teaches himself programming. **Verified fact** (multiply attested).

The draft dates it "around 1987" with Hassabis aged eleven; Althöfer's 2024 chess-history investigation ties the game to a May 1989 German chess-magazine report, implying age ~12.

- **~1992** — completes A-levels two years early; enters a magazine "Win-a-job-at-Bullfrog" competition, **comes in second**, gets a week's work experience, parlays it into a job. **Verified fact** (Time 2023 + Wikipedia; S-05, S-14). Note: draft says "talked his way into Bullfrog" — see CLM-13.

## Public record, accomplishments, failures, and controversies

- **Accomplishments (verified facts)**: Theme Park at 17; five Pentamind world titles; Science Top-10-of-2007 listing as a PhD student; AlphaGo (2016); AlphaFold + free 200M-structure database; Nobel in Chemistry 2024 (shared); knighthood 2024; Isomorphic Labs.
- **Failures (verified facts)**: Elixir Studios closed 2005 after Republic: The Revolution underdelivered relative to ambition (the "underdelivered" framing is the consensus retrospective; the PCGamesN piece itself is gentler). Gemini-era flagship-model delays in 2026 are **attributed** to Aug-2026 press coverage (Time/Fortune/Gizmodo per the draft's own ledger) and were not independently verified this run; Semafor (S-02) does note Hassabis "hadn't been directly focused on Gemini models" and that some insiders thought the change might _accelerate_ development.
- **Controversies, with attribution**:
  - _The demotion reading of 2026-08-05_: real context confirmed — Jeff Dean's same-day exit (S-16), Jumper→Anthropic in June (S-15), Google stock down 4% on the news (Semafor, S-02), Kavukcuoglu carrying SVP rather than CEO title (S-01). The counter-reading is Semafor's sourced reporting: "Hassabis wasn't pushed out against his will, said both people," a transition planned ~a year, duties shifting to Kavukcuoglu for a year prior. Both readings are **attributed claims**; neither is settled fact. Morale-problem reporting is **attributed and unverified this run**.
  - _Weapons-pledge repeal_: **verified fact** that Google removed the pledge 2025-02-04 and that the announcing post was co-authored by Hassabis and Manyika (S-08). The draft's sharp framing ("The man who made a weapons ban the price of his company co-signed its repeal") is **interpretation** built on two verified facts (2014 conditions multiply attested; 2025 co-signature verified).
  - _Techno-solutionism_: Marx's critique, Time 2023 — **attributed**, verified present.
  - _Say-do gap on speed_: "not moving fast and breaking things" (2023) vs. wartime shipping cadence — both halves verified; the tension is **interpretation** (a reasonable one that critics genuinely press).
  - _Suleyman_: 2019 leave amid management-style complaints (**verified**, contemporaneous reporting); "estranged" is the **publisher's description language** for Mallaby's book (S-10) — the LessWrong review itself never uses the word, and in fact the review _raised_ the reviewer's opinion of Suleyman. Evaluators should treat "estranged" as book-marketing/author framing, not Silver-grade testimony.
  - _Not in draft, flagged for awareness_: the 2017 UK ICO ruling that the Royal Free NHS data-sharing deal with DeepMind had an unlawful basis. The research file logged it as not re-verified; the draft omits it entirely. An evaluator judging critic-balance should know this omission exists.

## Enneagram hypothesis evidence

The type is a hypothesis. Everything in this section is **interpretation** built on the evidence above unless marked otherwise.

- **For Type 5**: the repeated post-victory abdications (chess track → games industry → fundraising → CEO title), each trade converting status into thinking time; the decade-long nocturnal second workday (verified); extreme family privacy (Wikipedia's one sentence — verified at research time); knowledge-as-currency behavior in the Thiel pitch (his own telling); the founding slogan's master-key logic; Semafor's "struggled to get satisfaction out of being in the role of a tech executive, rather than a visionary scientist" (verified quote — the single strongest external anchor for the 5-over-3 tiebreaker).
- **Against Type 5 / residue the type doesn't cleanly explain**: world-class competitiveness across five decades (Molyneux quote verified); staging AlphaGo as global televised spectacle; polished media presence, Davos/Nobel/royalty fluency; "Jedi-level charisma" (attributed); the wartime years of decisive operational command. The draft routes this through so-instinct and stress/security arrows — evaluators may reasonably find that circular.
- **Closest alternative**: Type 3 (Achiever). The draft's own pressure test concedes the surface case. **Discriminating evidence** the draft leans on: what happens _after_ wins — privacy and research hours rather than visibility; surrendering the CEO title while holding it securely (Semafor). A committed 3-advocate could counter that chair-of-DeepMind + chief-scientist-of-Alphabet + Isomorphic CEO is not exactly image-shedding, and that the "walk-aways" all landed him in _higher_-status positions eventually. The packet takes no side.
- **Secondary alternative**: Type 6 (alliances, institutions, "cautious optimism" as doctrine, worst-case planning) — the draft assigns this to the wing. Discriminator offered: he keeps exiting authority structures; Sixes tend to seek shelter in them.
- **Behavioral facts both sides must accommodate**: he kept playing and winning games for decades after "walking away" from chess (five Pentamind titles — the walk-away was from the professional chess _track_, not from games); the 2026 move retains substantial institutional power; the 2025 weapons-pledge co-signature is hard to read as withdrawal from worldly compromise.

## Disputes and unresolved questions

1. **The Liechtenstein origin scene (CLM-04)** — the largest unresolved gap. The draft narrates, as scene-fact, details (Danish master, ~1987, age eleven, resignation mechanics) that a specialist investigation (Althöfer 2024) contradicts on opponent nationality/identity and implicitly on date. The Althöfer article could only be read through search snippets (Sage returned 403), so the contradiction itself is snippet-sourced. What is safe: Hassabis has repeatedly told a version of this story himself, and the epiphany is his attested memory. What is not safe: presenting "veteran Danish master," the year, and the exact endgame mechanics as established fact. Conclusions built on the _meaning_ of the scene survive; conclusions built on its _particulars_ must stay qualified.
2. **Mallaby-derived quotes (CLM-11, CLM-12)** — the book itself has never been consulted in this pipeline; both marquee quotes ride on one reviewer's transcription. The draft's working notes acknowledge this. Any evaluator weighing the "wartime" pull quote or the Silver quote heavily should price in a single-transcriber chain.
3. **Voluntary vs. managed exit (CLM-03 vs. CLM-15)** — Semafor's sourced account (not pushed, year-long plan) and the demotion reading (delays, departures, smaller successor title, 4% stock drop) are both live. The draft argues for Semafor-plus-pattern; that is an interpretive choice, not a settled matter. No source available to this packet settles intent.
4. **The £1.4M Thiel figure and the "invited back the next day" beat (CLM-06/07)** — no live source found this run; both rest on the research file.
5. **Type assignment** — unresolved by design; the 3-case and 6-case are documented above and in the draft's own Rabbit Hole.
6. **Omitted controversy** — the 2017 ICO/Royal Free ruling exists in the record and is absent from the draft; whether that omission matters is an evaluator judgment, but its existence should not surprise anyone.
