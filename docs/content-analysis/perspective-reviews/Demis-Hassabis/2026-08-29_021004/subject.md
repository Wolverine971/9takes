## Bottom-line verdict

Even if Hassabis rejected the Type 5 label outright, he would recognize himself in this article, and that is the subject test passing at its core. The piece is built overwhelmingly from his own verified words, takes his stated reasons for the step-down as seriously as the demotion reading, respects the family-privacy boundary he has enforced for decades, and holds him responsible for the weapons-pledge repeal without inventing motives. The strain is concentrated in one place and a few edges: the origin scene narrates his subjective memoir account (age eleven, "veteran Danish master," the resignation mechanics) as established historical fact, when a specialist chess-history investigation contradicts the particulars — the fix is a one-clause attribution that is _also_ fairer to him. Around that, several attribution drifts (a motive gloss on the Facebook rejection, "estranged" sourced only to publisher marketing copy, motive-as-fact in the timeline, a literally-false universal in the meta title) each individually small, collectively put words and certainties near his name that the record does not hold. All are cheaply repairable without touching the piece's spine. Recommendation: revise.

## What landed

- **SUBJECT-H1 — The one-worry-two-directions reframe.** "You can reject his math and still see that the doomsaying and the racing come from the same place. They are one worry, pointed in two directions." This is the single strongest fairness move in the draft: it explains the doomsaying-while-racing charge through his own verified vocabulary (the "I would be really worried for humanity…" quote is verbatim, packet item 6) without excusing him. Must survive revision because it is the difference between an article that understands him and one that scores points off him.
- **SUBJECT-H2 — Responsibility kept on the weapons pledge.** "The man who made a weapons ban the price of his company co-signed its repeal… Concede all of it. The tension is real, and he lives inside it." Two verified facts, sharp interpretation, no motive-laundering and no erasure. A subject-aligned reader can't call this unfair, only unwelcome — which is exactly where criticism should sit.
- **SUBJECT-H3 — Privacy respected as a design fact, not a gap.** "The public record on his family is close to a blank page by design… gossip sites, finding nothing, have simply invented contradictory answers." The draft names no spouse, repeats no invented detail, and converts his boundary into evidence rather than treating it as an obstacle. This is the treatment he has spent decades engineering; preserve it verbatim.
- **SUBJECT-H4 — His own words as the spine.** "Screaming," "Are we wasting our minds?", "I come alive at about 1 a.m.", "wartime," "cautious optimism" — the load-bearing characterization is his own language, and the packet verifies the Lex quotes verbatim against the local transcript. The article lets him narrate himself.
- **SUBJECT-H5 — The step-down given his stated reason first-class standing.** The memo quote and Semafor's "struggled to get satisfaction" reporting are presented as the primary account, with the demotion reading laid out honestly ("the org chart will not stop you") and answered by pattern rather than suppressed. His version of events is neither swallowed credulously nor dismissed.
- **SUBJECT-H6 — Epistemic modesty about the typing.** "The type is just the name for the pattern," the closing disclaimer, and a Rabbit Hole that concedes "The Type 3 case is the serious one" and names what would force a rehearing. The article does not demand he agree with it, which is the correct posture toward a living subject.

## What missed

His documented story (Time 2023, the draft's own cited source, per packet CLM-13) is better _and more him_: he entered a magazine competition, came second, got a week's work experience, and converted it into a job; Molyneux first saw him at the office, and the bus conversation is a separate, later beat. The compression replaces a documented preparation-and-conversion story — the same shape as the Thiel pitch — with a grander gloss he never claimed.

Small, but this is the piece's origin myth and his own rendering is on the record.

## Red flags

- **SUBJECT-R1 (blocker) — The origin scene narrates a contested personal memory as established fact.**
  - _Passage:_ "Sometime around 1987, at a big international tournament in Liechtenstein, an eleven-year-old from North London sat across from a veteran Danish master for roughly eight hours." (opening of "The eight-hour game…")
  - _Effect:_ The article's keystone scene — the one every later section calls back to — presents as checked history details that a specialist investigation contradicts, and it targets the "Demis Hassabis chess" query where chess-literate readers are most likely to know the correction. If the correction circulates, the article looks un-fact-checked and, worse, makes _Hassabis_ look like a fabulist, because the draft never disclosed it was retelling his memory.
  - _Evidence:_ Verified this review: every contested particular (age eleven, "veteran Danish master," eight hours, resignation, the gestured stalemate save) comes faithfully from Hassabis's own first-person introduction to _Game Changer_ (BBC Science Focus extract, fetched directly). Althöfer's 2024 ICGA Journal investigation (packet S-06, snippet-sourced) identifies the opponent as German FIDE-Master Carsten Pieper-Emden, ~25 at the time, at the 1989 Liechtenstein Open — contradicting nationality, "veteran," the year, and the age.
  - _Minimum viable repair:_ Attribute the scene to his telling in the opening clause (e.g. framed as the story he has told in the _Game Changer_ introduction and on Desert Island Discs) and let the particulars live inside that frame; optionally align the year to "the late 1980s."
  - _Expected benefit:_ The scene becomes bulletproof against the Althöfer correction, the meaning survives untouched, and the framing is _more_ honest to him — it is his memory, told his way, which is itself characterizing.
  - _Confidence:_ High that the particulars are contested and that they originate in his own telling; medium on Althöfer's specifics (full text unread, 403-walled).
  - _Acceptance test:_ No contested particular (opponent nationality/status, year, age, endgame mechanics) is narrated outside an explicit attribution to his own telling; the epiphany quote remains attributed as it already is.

- **SUBJECT-R2 (concern) — Invented interiority in the inner-thought panel.**
  - _Passage:_ `<p class="inner-thought">Eight hours. I lost an eight-hour game I could have drawn. And every person in this hall is doing the same thing tomorrow. What else could all of this thinking do?</p>`
  - _Effect:_ First-person thoughts placed in a real person's head. The subject-fairness test flags unsupported interiority even when sympathetic.
  - _Evidence:_ Mitigated: the content closely paraphrases his own attested account (the _Game Changer_ introduction confirms both the could-have-drawn fact and the what-could-this-effort-do question), and the styled device signals dramatization. But his telling places the reflection the next day, and the panel asserts same-moment cognition.
  - _Minimum viable repair:_ Keep the device but ensure every proposition in it appears in his attested tellings, or convert the panel to his actual quoted foreword language.
  - _Expected benefit:_ The one overtly novelistic move in the piece becomes defensible as compression rather than invention.
  - _Confidence:_ High on the facts; medium on whether the device reads as invasive to a general reader.
  - _Acceptance test:_ Every claim inside the panel maps to a sentence in the _Game Changer_ introduction or a Desert Island Discs recap.

  - _Minimum viable repair:_ Align the stated reason with the sourced wording, or drop the motive clause and keep the verified fact of the richer rejected offer.
  - _Expected benefit:_ The attribution becomes accurate; Hassabis is not saddled with a judgment he (per the available trail) never made.
  - _Confidence:_ High.
  - _Acceptance test:_ The rejection's stated ground matches S-09's wording or no ground is given.
