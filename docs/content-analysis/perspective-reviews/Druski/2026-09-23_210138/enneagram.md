## Bottom-line verdict

Type 8 is a defensible call, but the page doesn't earn the certainty it states. The public record does contain real Eight evidence. The page just mostly uses the wrong pieces of it.

- **What holds:** Druski's record includes anti-dependence and creative-control language that fits the Eight's core fear of being controlled.
- **Rejecting Type 7 fails (ENNEAGRAM-B1).** The page's test is consolidation: owning the shows and keeping the crew. That doesn't separate 8w7 from 7w8.
- **The site's own test points the other way.** 9takes' Type 8 page, which the intro links, has an 8-vs-7 test. Applied to this page's central evidence, it leans Seven: the jokes, the silence toward critics, "then the next project".
- **Rejecting Type 3 fails too.** The anti-3 claim ("refuses to smooth them over") is contradicted by a documented 2022 retreat the page leaves out.
- **Missing motive:** the page never names the core fear. The most Eight-specific quotes on record never appear: "kiss up to somebody" and "my team is involved in the creative side".
- **Recommendation: revise.** Every repair below can be made from the existing packet and 9takes' own type pages, and none requires changing the type.
- **Worth keeping:** the lens discipline in the Mafiathon section and the falsifiable *Diggers* test are the best Enneagram writing on the page.

## Red flags

### ENNEAGRAM-B1 · Blocker · The rejections of Types 7 and 3 don't discriminate, and 9takes' own Type 8 page contradicts them

**Passages:**
- Diagnosis, L137: "A Seven keeps doors open and moves on. Druski consolidates. He owns the shows, books the talent, and has kept the same Gwinnett crew in the frame for years. That is someone securing territory."
- FAQ 1 (L78): "The closest alternative is Type 7, but a Seven keeps doors open, and Druski builds rooms he controls and keeps the same Gwinnett crew inside them."
- Rabbit Hole, L306: "Type 7, the strongest rival, is argued above and fails on consolidation."
- Rabbit Hole, L308: "A Three shapes himself to the room's approval. Druski picks fights the room didn't ask for, refuses to smooth them over..."

**Reader effect.** An Enneagram-literate reader, or one arriving from Personality Database with "7w8" in mind, clicks the Type 8 link in the intro and finds 9takes' own 8-vs-7 test contradicting this page. The typing then reads as asserted first and defended afterward. FAQ 1 carries the flaw into search rich results. This is a central-theory failure: this perspective asks what evidence separates the call from its rivals, and the page's answer doesn't separate them.

**Evidence and reasoning:**

(a) **Consolidation doesn't separate the Eight from the 7w8.**
- 9takes' own 7w8 (`enneagram-type-7.md` L118): "You don't just see opportunities—you seize them. Your adventures tend to be bigger, bolder, and more demanding of the world."
- Riso-Hudson's 7w8, "The Realist", as relayed: "determined to get what they want from life... can rapidly organize their internal and external resources", "aggressive, competitive, and materialistic".
- Chestnut's self-preservation Seven builds a surrogate-family "mafia" of trusted allies.

Owning shows and keeping the crew are therefore at least as consistent with a 7w8 or self-preservation Seven as with an Eight. The draft's case for 7 is also built from surface traits (class clown, a "buffet" of formats) rather than the Seven's motive: avoiding pain by reframing it and moving to the next thing.

(b) **9takes' own test points the other way on this page's evidence.**
- The test (`enneagram-type-8.md` L195): "If conflict energizes you, you're an 8. If conflict makes you check for the door and change the subject, you're a 7."
- Nearby: Eights "prefer" direct conflict (L223) and "feel most alive in conflict" (L153).
- How the page itself describes Druski under fire:
  - "AM I CANCELLED?" (L215)
  - the posted-then-deleted "too far" joke (L215)
  - "silence, then volume, then the next project" (L279)
  - "the incident had no oxygen" (L237)
  - "he moved fast enough that nobody had to settle whether the apology was enough" (L239)
- The Enneagram Institute's Seven keeps busy "with projects and positive ideas for the future" to keep "negative feelings out of conscious awareness", "moving from one experience to the next".

On 9takes' own terms, the page's central behavioral pattern leans Seven, and the page never says so.

(c) **The anti-3 claim is contradicted by omitted evidence.**
- In 2022 he took the "OVERLY drink" skit down after criticism because "I didn't want to continue to trigger people", and later explained it on record (Rolling Stone 2024, packet FP-11). That is smoothing it over.
- The whiteface and Kirk sketches were rewarded by the room a Three performs for, his own audience:
  - 100M+ views on the Kirk sketch within about two days (packet, Wikipedia tier 4)
  - Billboard: "just passed the test" (TP-12)
  - praise from Jamie Foxx, Nick Cannon, and Theo Von (TP-13, TP-14)
- "Fights the room didn't ask for" treats the critics as the room. It isn't the room he plays to.

**Not requested:** a retype. Type 8 can stand if the page rests it on motive (ENNEAGRAM-C7) and admits the 7-leaning evidence.

**Minimum viable repair** (keep net words at zero or below; the draft is about 3,894 against a 3,900 band):
1. **L137:** replace the consolidation test with a motive-level one.
   - Give Type 7 its best case: the joke-and-move-on handling of critics, and "I didn't have a love for it".
   - Then say what tips the call to Eight: the fear of being dependent or controlled, backed by the "kiss up to somebody" and creative-control quotes (see C7).
   - Say plainly that his handling of critics is the Seven-leaning evidence.
   - Pay for it by cutting the "buffet" list and "That is someone securing territory."
2. **FAQ 1:** swap "a Seven keeps doors open" for the same fear-level test.
3. **L306:** replace "fails on consolidation" with a one-line honest verdict: 7w8 explains the exits better, and the Eight explains the control and dependence language better.
4. **L308:** delete "refuses to smooth them over", or qualify it with the 2022 takedown in one clause. Then name the real 8-vs-3 test from `enneagram-type-8.md` L191 ("3s... adjust their approach to win approval. 8s would rather be respected than liked") and say where his record is mixed.
5. **Rabbit Hole subtype paragraph (L296):** add one clause saying the crew evidence also fits a self-preservation Seven.

**Expected benefit.** The type claim becomes proportional and survives a click-through to 9takes' own type pages. The page also gains a sharper, shareable insight: an Eight's fear with a Seven's exits.

**Confidence:** high for (a) and (b), which rest on text in 9takes' own type pages; medium-high for (c).

**Acceptance test:**

- (2) No sentence says Type 7 "fails on consolidation" or that a Seven "keeps doors open and moves on" as the discriminator.

### ENNEAGRAM-C1 · Concern · The mechanism offered for the silence is not how Eights handle critics

**Passages:**
- Whiteface, L217: "To an Eight, answering the critic feels like conceding ground, so the silence is consistent."
- FAQ 3 (L84): "To a Type 8, engaging a critic feels like conceding ground..."
- Framing: the meta_title "Enneagram 8 and the Critics He Ignores" and the description "answering critics with more content".

**Reader effect.** Knowledgeable readers know the Eight as the type most at home in confrontation. The claim reads as theory bent to fit, and it appears on the page's most prominent search surfaces.

**Evidence.** 9takes' own Type 8 page:
- "You can handle direct conflict; you *prefer* it... you either confront it head-on or write the person off entirely" (L223).
- "you feel most alive in conflict, challenge, and confrontation" (L153).

Not apologizing is consistent with an Eight. Not *engaging* at all, combined with a joke and a move to the next project, is not Eight-specific. It's the Seven's pattern in both 9takes' own material and Riso-Hudson's.

**Minimum viable repair.** Separate the two claims:
- keep "no apology" as the Eight-consistent part;
- if a type mechanism for the silence stays, reframe it as the Eight's "write the person off" branch;
- add one clause admitting that the same silence is what a Seven's move-on looks like.

In FAQ 3, remove "To a Type 8, engaging a critic feels like conceding ground" or replace it the same way. This edits an existing type paragraph, so it doesn't break the four-paragraph type-theory ceiling.

**Expected benefit:** the whiteface section's type claim becomes accurate, and the page stops presenting a Seven-leaning datum as Eight proof.

**Confidence:** high.

**Acceptance test:** no sentence states, as general theory, that Eights avoid engaging critics. FAQ 3 attributes only the *non-apology* to the type, or explicitly uses the write-off framing.

### ENNEAGRAM-C2 · Concern · "Anger is the Eight's fuel" is asserted, and its three examples aren't type-specific

**Expected benefit:** the diagnosis stops asserting an inner state the evidence contradicts, which is the most likely point for a skeptical reader to leave.

**Confidence:** high.

### ENNEAGRAM-C3 · Concern · "Controlled vulnerability" turns openness into Eight evidence, so the reading can't fail

For example, ask whether he roasts only safe, generic flaws ("piece of s***") while telling the tender material straight in interviews. Weigh the print disclosures briefly. Rephrase the FAQ 4 question neutrally ("Why does Druski roast himself?"); the protected anchor slug can stay.

**Expected benefit:** the section's best line ("Bieber gets to confess...") stops leaning on an argument that can't fail.

**Confidence:** medium-high.

**Acceptance test:** the Roast section contains at least one observation that would count against the control reading. The FAQ 4 question no longer assumes "Type 8 shield".
