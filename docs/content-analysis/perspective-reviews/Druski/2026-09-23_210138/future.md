## Bottom-line verdict

The page should still be accurate a year from now. Its core evidence is old and first-person: the GQ 2020 and Complex 2021 childhood and depression quotes, the Harlow self-roast quote, the Mafiathon stream apology, and the Parham court record. None of it will expire. The controversy sections use absolute dates almost everywhere ("August 31, 2025", "September 4, 2025", "as Essence tallied them in October 2025"). The newest material (the BET Awards, "JOE", the July 4 return, *The Catch*, and Forbes 2026) mostly tests the thesis rather than propping it up. That is the right way to use fresh events.

**What survives if the newest events are removed:** almost everything. Take out June–September 2026 and the Type 8 case, the self-roast analysis, the Mafiathon split between the boy and his mother, the Parham record, and the whiteface/Kirk argument all still stand. Only "What's Next" loses its test case. The thesis does not depend on one passing event.

Six things will decay predictably inside the window, and none is a trust break today:

1. **Erika Kirk is never identified** (FUTURE-C1). This is the highest priority. In twelve months, readers won't carry the context that she is Charlie Kirk's widow and successor at Turning Point USA, or that the sketch came about 6.5 months after his killing. Without it, the critics can't be weighed.
2. **The page's central pattern rests on undated absence claims** (FUTURE-C2). Examples: "hasn't engaged any of it", "the silence was the one thing that held", "never answered". One podcast answer could make them false without anyone noticing. A December 2025 Billboard cover may already make "silence held" too broad (not verified; it is behind a paywall).
3. **Status claims will expire** (FUTURE-C3): *The Catch* "due May 21, 2027"; *The Diggers* "still in development more than a year after it was announced"; "Next is…" in the body and in FAQ 2.
4. **The date readers see is wrong** (FUTURE-C4). The page shows "Updated: April 29, 2026", but the body covers events through July 2026 and statuses as of September 2026.
5. **FAQ 5 drops "without prejudice"** (FUTURE-C5).
6. **"Ten months after the backlash" points at the wrong backlash** (FUTURE-C6).

The recommendation is **revise**: small, mostly clause-level repairs plus the dated refresh list below. No research hold is needed. The one open research question (FUTURE-Q1) narrows a claim but does not decide whether the page ships.
