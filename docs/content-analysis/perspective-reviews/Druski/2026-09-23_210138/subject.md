# Subject-aligned fairness review: Druski

Snapshot SHA-256 re-hashed on disk and matched against `context.json` and the supplied SHA before review. The
snapshot's embedded HTML-comment ledgers (refresh, fresh-eyes, cohesion) were treated as non-evidence and not used

## Bottom-line verdict

Even if Druski rejects the Type 8 call, the article is fair on the parts that could hurt him most. The Parham section
refuses to call a procedural dismissal an exoneration, but it also gives the judge's "almost certainly lack a factual
basis" in full. The Mafiathon section credits his live apology before criticizing it. The Roast section gives his
friend's reading of the self-roast before the type's reading. The quotes are accurate and dated, and his own words
carry most of the biography.

Trust is **strained, not broken**. Two verified omissions make the article's main character verdict look more
consistent than the record allows. That verdict is that he ignores outside critics and shows "little accountability"
outside his circle.

1. **SUBJECT-R1.** In April 2022 critics objected to a skit, and he took it down. Two years later he told Rolling
   Stone he "didn't want to continue to trigger people." The page never mentions this. Its search title, meta
   description, two FAQs and the Rabbit Hole all describe a habit of never backing down.
2. **SUBJECT-R2.** The page judges his silence toward the Mafiathon boy's mother. It never says that within three
   days she was publicly using racial slurs about him, or that her security complaint was aimed at the organizers of
   Kai Cenat's stream.

Each fix takes two to four sentences plus softer search-result copy. Neither fix requires dropping the Type 8
thesis.

Beneath these two, a layer of sentences states motive as fact: anger, defiance, "every laugh," "strategy." They
replace the vocabulary he actually uses on record with the type's vocabulary. His words are hurt, "prove it to
y'all," "the funniest kid," "didn't have a love for it," "coming from a good place," and "make a name for my
friends."
