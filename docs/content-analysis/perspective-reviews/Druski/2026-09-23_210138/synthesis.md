## Executive verdict

This is the most self-critical people page the jury has seen. The Mafiathon frame-limit paragraph and
the Parham close are the page's trust anchors, and every perspective except the future reviewer asked
for them to be kept verbatim. The future reviewer also protected the Parham close. No reviewer asked
for a retype. Five of six rated trust **strained**; the future reviewer rated it intact. The draft is not
publishable as-is. Every repair can be made from packet evidence plus one verified outside source, and
none requires changing the type or the spine.

The trust problems cluster on one claim: **how Druski handles criticism.** The page states that claim
more broadly than the record supports, and it leaves out the record's two best complications:

1. **The 2022 takedown.** A source the page itself cites, Rolling Stone 2024, records him pulling a
   skit after backlash because "I didn't want to continue to trigger people." The meta description
   ("the habit of answering critics with more content") and the Rabbit Hole's main argument against
   Type 3 ("refuses to smooth them over") both contradict it. Four perspectives flagged this (P0-01).
2. **The Mafiathon context.** The page scores his silence toward the mother as character evidence
   without mentioning that the same videos called him a racial slur. Her security complaint was
   aimed at the organizers of Kai Cenat's stream. I opened Atlanta Black Star (2024-11-30) to settle
   this; it confirms both points (P0-03).

The Kirk sketch is judged without the fact that makes it controversial. The page never says Erika
Kirk is Charlie Kirk's widow, and it rules on a "symmetry argument" it never states (P0-02). The
page's one original craft critique, specific people versus categories, rests on a misdescription of
his catalog (P0-04). The absolute silence claims ("The silence was the one thing that held") are
broader than the packet's searches, and a Billboard cover the page cites may contradict them (P0-05).
The type argument's discriminators do not discriminate, and the Type 8 mechanism for silence
contradicts 9takes' own Type 8 page, which I checked line by line (P0-06).

**The narrower thesis is better, not weaker.** He has never apologized for a sketch. He answers
accusations of fact fast, on the record, and through his reps. He does not argue with judgments of
taste or character. Once, in 2022, he retreated without arguing. The fan-level gains land in the same
places:

- The Druski who pulled a skit in 2022 posted "AM I CANCELLED?" in 2025.
- The Kirk sketch put a category title on one identifiable, recently widowed woman.
- The type reads as an Eight's fear of dependence with a Seven wing's exits.

These are the "see a familiar fact differently" moments the delight target calls for.
