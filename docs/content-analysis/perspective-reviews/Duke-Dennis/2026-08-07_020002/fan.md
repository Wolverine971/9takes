## Bottom-line verdict

This writer did the homework a fan respects: both canonical primary sources (the 2018 Army story time and the Feb 2024 360 With Speedy — the only two long-form texts that exist) are clearly absorbed in full, and the piece passes the delight test repeatedly. I knew the rizz standing reply, the Army story, the Deo channel gift, and the "Where is Speed" clip as separate artifacts; the draft connects them in ways the fandom hasn't. But the compression consistently overshoots the tape on exactly the beats a fan knows frame-by-frame: "It stays in the drawer" is contradicted by the tail of the same 360 exchange it quotes (blocker), "he cried" overwrites the canonical shades detail, and "he ruined a generation" is rendered flat when the tape shows him softening it first. Add the total absence of "aura" — the single biggest Duke meme of 2024–2026, and a free extra proof of the refused-labels thesis — and this is a revise: small repairs, all of them, but the kind fans use to sort insiders from tourists.

## What missed

- **The drawer claim reverses on tape (blocker — see FAN-R1).** "The audience would watch it. He knows they would. It stays in the drawer." In the same 360 exchange, Duke adds that he now throws those shelved videos onto his live channel: "I know they want to see it, even though I don't want to drop it" (evidence packet, first-person evidence, S1). Fans who follow Duke Dennis Live know those uploads exist. The draft quotes the first half of the exchange and misses the reversal in the second half — the classic outsider tell — and the claim is load-bearing, since "shelved finished videos" anchors the Type-3 rebuttal in both the diagnosis section and the Rabbit Hole.
- **The Dee shirt beat trades the canonical image for a stronger verb (FAN-R3).** "He opened the package live, in front of everybody, and cried." What fans remember from the December 2024 clip is that he got visibly emotional and put on his shades — sources say "nearly teared up," "a little emotional" (S14, S15); none say cried outright. The shades are the shareable detail and they're on-thesis: even grief gets armor.
- **"He ruined a generation" is flattened (FAN-R2).** The draft presents it as "Duke's assessment." On tape, Speedy quotes Duke's prior statement back at him, Duke first says "I wouldn't say ruined it," and only repeats "he ruined it" when pressed (S1). Anyone who has watched his only interview — which is to say, every informed fan — knows the sequence.
- **"Aura" is absent from a refused-labels thesis (FAN-R6).** The labels inventory (rizz, Unc, 1987, "Duke" itself) skips the biggest Duke-specific meme of 2024–2026. The physique appears only as "meme duty" without the word that powers the meme.
