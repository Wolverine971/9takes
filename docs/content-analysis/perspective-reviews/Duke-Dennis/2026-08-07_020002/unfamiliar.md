## Red flags

**UNFAMILIAR-R1 (BLOCKER) — The ending teaches a false birth year.**

- **Passage:** "It renamed him Unc. It prints his birth year as a punchline." (final section, "The name he never asked about"), read against "the aging jokes, the 'Unc' of it all, the 1987 punchlines" ("Why Duke Dennis hates the word rizz").
- **Reader effect:** the only year the article ever offers for his birth is 1987, and the ending's possessive "his birth year" ratifies it. On my unaided read I concluded he was born in 1987 (age ~39). He was born February 26, 1994 (packet, Identity section, verified [S3]); "1987" is a _fake_ birth year the internet assigns him as an aging joke — the joke's entire comedy is that a man in his early thirties gets treated as elderly. A fresh reader either absorbs a wrong fact or, if they notice "at age 29" in the same section (first interview, Feb 2024), hits an unresolved internal contradiction (29 in 2024 ⇒ born ~1994 ≠ 1987). Both outcomes are trust breaks; the first also inverts the meaning of the "Unc" motif the ending depends on.
- **Evidence:** packet Identity section and note under CLM/Unc verification: "The '1987' is a _fake_ birth year the internet assigns him as an aging joke; his actual birth year is 1994. The draft's line... is ambiguous on this point." My unaided read confirms it is not merely ambiguous in practice — it misleads.
- **Minimum viable repair:** one word-level change in the ending, e.g. mark the year as fake/assigned ("It prints him a fake birth year as a punchline" or equivalent); optionally one clause at "the 1987 punchlines" noting his real year. The editor writes the wording.
- **Expected benefit:** the reader leaves with the correct basic fact, and the "Unc" motif's actual joke (premature aging of a 32-year-old) becomes legible, sharpening the labels-thesis rather than muddying it.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Acceptance test:** a first-time reader, asked "when was Duke Dennis born?" after reading, answers 1994/early-thirties — not 1987 — and can explain why the internet says 1987.
