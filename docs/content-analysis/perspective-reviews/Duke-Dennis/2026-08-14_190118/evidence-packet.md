## Enneagram hypothesis evidence

The draft's Type 8 (8w9, sp/so) reading is a **hypothesis**. Personality Database and mbtilounge both file him as **3w2** [S26], which the draft names and argues against; that disagreement is the falsifiable shape of the piece, and neither side is established fact.

**Evidence for Type 8 (verified behavior and self-report).** The anti-authority statement is unusually direct and repeated: "I don't like being told what to do," stated three-plus ways in one 2018 video, echoed six years later in different vocabulary — "be your own boss," "when you put somebody above you, you going to stay there forever" [S1][S2]. Freedom framed as escape ("felt like I got out of jail"). Instant, unhedged exits: the factory walk-out mid-shift; telling the first sergeant to his face. Protector behavior: mother's house before jewelry; enforcing AMP's sealed-membership rule; spending his own No. 5 moment on IShowSpeed's exclusion [S1][S6].

**Evidence against, and alternatives.**

- **Type 3 (the incumbent internet answer, 3w2 [S26]):** curated physique, a personal-branding professorship, encyclopedic algorithm command, and explicit enjoyment of attention — "my favorite part of the internet right now... seeing compliments about yourself" [S1]. The draft's rebuttal rests on declining attention: shelved videos, interview-free years, credit deflection. Two planks are softer than stated. The shelved videos **go to his live channel** ("I know they want to see it, even though I don't want to drop it") — the draft now renders this correctly, but it weakens "declines attention" as an argument. And the interview drought is qualified by Speedy's own mention of a podcast appearance and *Sneaker Shopping* [S1]. Also relevant: he now runs a heavy vlog cadence and returned to Streamer University in a faculty role two years running [S10].
- **Type 6:** the enlistment pact ("we shook on it"), the sealed loyal circle, the punctuality, and above all **completing four years in a system he hated after openly asking to leave**.
- **Type 9 / 9w8:** the same four-year stay read as inertia or accommodation; the chill affect; the one-line defusal of the RDC friction; the Germany withdrawal.

**Counterevidence to the specific 8w9 wing case that the packet flags for evaluators.** The draft's wing argument says his complaints "arrive as verdicts delivered at low volume, never as rants," and that an 8w7 "would have taken every microphone." Two sourced moments cut against the low-volume characterization:

- His documented response to the "aura farming" accusations is not a wave-off but a slam, reported with direct quotation: "You all nerd a** n***as talking about aura farming. Nobody says that s**t in real life!" [S24].

**Discriminating evidence evaluators can check.** Whether his refusals are anxiety-shaped (6) or dominance-shaped (8) in the primary tape; whether attention is metabolized as fuel (3) or audited for who was excluded (the Rolling Stone reaction); whether the Germany room-24/7 withdrawal is a stress arrow (8→5) or simply baseline introversion in a country where he had no friends, no car and no language — the tape supports either reading.

**Behavior the type does not explain.** Completing the four-year contract after asking, out loud and to the right person, to be sent home. The draft holds this open rather than resolving it. The mother's-sentence reading is interpretation, not evidence.
