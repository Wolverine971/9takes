## Conflicts and tradeoffs recorded

1. **PROTECT-13's number changed.** P0-09's acceptance test requires "fifteen years" to be arithmetically supported by the anchors actually printed. With the corrected dates (1936 Administration Building commission → 1950 tower dedication) the span is fourteen years, not fifteen. I changed the number and kept the sentence, its position, and its function. The alternative — keeping "fifteen" — would have left a protected sentence provably wrong against the dates printed two clauses earlier, which is the exact failure mode this whole revision exists to remove.
2. **P1-10 taken at minimum, not at the better version.** I first applied the attributing form ("which twenty-one painters had told him in print was the whole problem"), then reverted to the plain deletion when the word budget bound. The acceptance test allows either. If RQ-06 is ever closed, the fuller version is the upgrade to make.
3. **P1-09's fifth gloss dropped.** TL;DR bullet 5 still says "The 8 wing" with no gloss at that position. Four of five glosses landed; this one cost ~13 words the budget did not have. The wing is glossed in the body and in the Rabbit Hole.
4. **P2-01 not taken.** The Guggenheim-as-evidence paragraph (~80 words) does not fit. Per Step 4 I checked whether P2-02 (Julian Carlton's fate, <20 words) would fit instead; after P1-01, P1-02, P1-03 and P1-07/08 the body landed at 4,497 of 4,500, so neither fit. Both logged for the next refresh, P2-01 first.
5. **The budget was paid for with cuts beyond the brief.** The accepted P1 additions cost ~250 words against 69 of headroom. Rather than drop P1-01 or P1-03, I cut roughly 180 words of genuine redundancy: a duplicated Kaufmann arrival, a duplicated Catherine divorce beat, a duplicated "family of eight"/"father six times over" pair, a restated "reframe is a refusal to wait," the Guggenheim opening date given twice, a résumé beat ("He was seventy, with two decades of work still ahead"), and Rabbit Hole evidence lists that re-spent body material. One of those cuts — "by a man who decided very young that the only response to catastrophe is another set of blueprints" — was an uncaught second instance of P0-05's exact failure (a decision assigned to young Wright, sourced to nothing). It is gone.
