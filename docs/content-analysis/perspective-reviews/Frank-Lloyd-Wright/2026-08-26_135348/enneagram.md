## Bottom-line verdict

The Type 7w8 self-preservation call is correct, and the case is built the right way round: it argues from motive (reframe, escape, refusal of a fixed position) rather than from surface behavior, and it names the one relationship the theory cannot absorb instead of pretending the theory covers everything. That last move — the Sullivan residue — is rarer in this corpus than any of the evidence.

Two things keep it from a pass. First, the diagnosis section's headline exhibit for the reframe defense ("in love again before the year was out") runs ahead of the record, and the packet grades the companion clause in the Mamah section as unverified. A piece whose entire pitch is _the aggregators don't show their work and we do_ cannot compress a timeline that is doing type-discriminating work. Second, the strongest alternative type is not the one the draft rebuts. The article devotes a body-level H3 to Type 5 and a Rabbit Hole block to Types 5 and 3, while the evidence it showcases hardest — the cold open, an entire H2, the lead FAQ, the 60-ton column, firing his own son over money — is exactly the evidence an Eight-core reader would claim. Eight-as-core is never named. Both are cheap to fix, and 9takes' own Harmonics page supplies the discriminator that fixes the second one for free.

## What landed

**ENN-H1 — The marker read as an address.**

> "A marker is an address, and an address is a place you can be expected to return to and sit in. He would fill the grave with his own hands and then make certain there was nowhere to go back to."

This is the best type insight in the piece and the reason the article earns its lens. It works because the load-bearing element is Wright's own sentence ("Why mark the spot where desolation ended and began?"), not a behavior the writer has interpreted for him, and because the reading is specific to the Seven's core fear as 9takes states it — _being trapped_, including trapped in a location where grief is owed. It is also person-first: the paragraph before it is the shovel and the August light, so the theory arrives after the man, not instead of him. Must survive revision intact, including the preceding burial paragraph that sets it up; severing them would turn a felt observation into an assertion.

**ENN-H2 — Naming what the type does not explain.**

> "The Sullivan story is the only place in the biography where the reframe stalls. Wright reframed his marriage, his career, his murdered lover, his bankruptcies, his children. He did not reframe Sullivan, who stayed _lieber Meister_ until 1959. The thing a Seven cannot reframe is the thing he could not afford to lose."

Typology writing almost never does this. A type claim that explains every data point is not a finding, it is a horoscope, and this paragraph is the draft's own protection against that charge. Preserve the admission even if the closing interpretation is trimmed — the value is in "the reframe stalls," not in the resolution.

**ENN-H3 — The Okura telegram.**

> "Wright handed the telegram to the American press. He did not add the missing words 'considerable damage notwithstanding.' Catastrophe converted to marketing copy in one news cycle."

The strongest single piece of Seven evidence in the article, because it is externally documented behavior rather than Wright's testimony about himself, and because the mechanism is visible in real time: the negative half is suppressed, the positive half is published. Everything else in the Seven case is either Wright's own account or an inference. This is not. Keep it exactly as written.

**ENN-H4 — Proportional certainty on the corroborating source.**

> "The essay is paywalled, so take it as corroboration of a label and nothing further. We have read only the title and the opening lines."

Correct handling, stated out loud, of the one independent expert who reached the same subtype. The temptation to upgrade a paywalled title into an authority is exactly what the aggregators the draft is arguing against would do. This sentence is the article's credibility deposit; do not let a later pass tighten it into "an independent expert agrees."

**ENN-H5 — The arrows, used correctly and concretely.**

> "Under stress, Seven moves to One: rigid, moralizing, certain. That is the Wright of the lecture circuit and Broadacre City, scolding America about its taste."

This matches 9takes' own connecting-lines page almost word for word ("the Seven's open-ended energy can flip into the rigid side of One... insists that everyone follow the correct process"), and it is attached to named, dated work rather than left abstract. The growth-arrow companion — "Wright visited. He never moved in." — is the cleanest six-word statement of arrow-versus-core I have seen in this corpus.

## What missed

**ENN-M1 — The Five rebuttal wins on volume, and the packet predicted it.**

The rebuttal is: Fives conserve and withdraw; Wright founded a school and published a 400-page autobiography; "A Five who lost twelve years of commissions goes quieter. Wright got louder and charged admission." That is an argument from output, and a Five under foreclosure can also monetize expertise — the Fellowship at $650/year was, among other things, a man with two commissions in eight years selling the only asset left. The discriminating evidence is not the volume, it is the _appetite_: the cape, the cane, the porkpie hat, the courting of press, the reframe reflex firing on a telegram within a news cycle. All of that is already in the draft, in other sections. The rebuttal reaches for the school instead. See ENN-C2 for why this compounds.

**ENN-M2 — The Four rebuttal rests on a grief clock.**

> "A Four would have grieved Mamah for years. Wright was writing to a new woman inside four months."

Grief timelines vary enormously between individuals of the same type, and one remarriage interval is not a typology. This is the weakest discriminator in the piece, and it is the one most exposed by ENN-B1: shrink the timeline claim to what the record supports and the rebuttal loses most of its force. The draft has a much stronger Four-discriminator sitting unused two sections away — the Four's identity is organized _around_ deficiency and longing, and Wright's autobiography systematically deletes deficiency ("Twelve fallow years became a chrysalis"). A Four writes the lack; Wright wrote it out.

**ENN-M3 — The passion is never named, so the reframe has no engine.**

The article describes the Seven's _defense_ (reframe) at length and never names the Seven's _passion_. Gluttony is what makes the reframe compulsory rather than merely characteristic — the appetite has to keep finding somewhere to go, which is why the door out of Oak Park, the next wife, the next compound, and the next reframe are the same motion. Without it the draft describes a habit; with one clause it describes a drive. This is not a request for jargon density: one naming, once, in the diagnosis section.

## What I expected

- **A Type 8 core rebuttal.** Given that the draft's most vivid, most repeated material is dominion behavior, this is the alternative a knowledgeable reader arrives holding after the cold open. It is absent. (ENN-C1.)
- **A falsifier.** One sentence saying what evidence would change the call. The draft states what the type explains and, admirably, one thing it does not, but never what would break it.
- **Discriminating first-person evidence for the subtype.** The self-pres call is made from Wright's _constructions_ (two Taliesins, the Fellowship). Nothing from Wright's own mouth is offered for it, and the corroborating essay is explicitly unread.
- **The Harmonics lens, or something doing its job.** A piece whose central claim is that this man's operating system was _reframe under pressure_ is describing the Positive Outlook group. Naming it would have made the 7-versus-8 question answer itself.

- **Welcome:** counter-typing promoted into the body as an H3 rather than quarantined in the Rabbit Hole. "Why Frank Lloyd Wright is not a Type 5" put directly under the diagnosis is the right call for a subject whose search results are wall-to-wall 5w4, and it is a structural choice most of this corpus does not make.

- **Jarring, on reflection:** the growth arrow to Five turns out to be doing three unrelated jobs in three different sections. Individually each reads fine; together they form a claim that cannot be wrong. (ENN-C3.)

## Red flags

### ENN-B1 (blocker) — The replacement timeline is stated faster than the record supports, in the paragraph the whole type case rests on

**Passage.** Diagnosis section, the core reframe paragraph: "When his lover was hatcheted to death in his own dining room, he rebuilt the dining room and **was in love again before the year was out**." Compounded in H2 "The grave Frank Lloyd Wright refused to mark": "**She was living in the rebuilt house within months.**"

**Reader effect / trust problem.** The speed of replacement is one of two headline exhibits for the Seven's reframe defense, and it is the load-bearing discriminator against Type 4. An Enneagram reader who checks it finds that the December 1914 event in the record is _receipt and acknowledgment of a condolence letter_. "In love again" by 31 December 1914 is an interiority claim about a mutual romantic state, asserted as biography. The article's competitive claim is that the free aggregators assert types without showing their work; a reader who catches the piece tightening a timeline to win an argument transfers that suspicion to every other type claim on the page.

**Evidence.** Packet T-08 / timeline row for Dec 1914: Wright receives Noel's condolence letter, acknowledges it, she writes again proposing a meeting, and per Finis Farr they meet "a few days later" (S-14). Packet **CLM-28** grades "inside four months" as accurate but grades "**She was living in the rebuilt house within months**" as **not confirmed**, with a competing account placing them living together **by 1916** (S-36), and notes Murphy dates no move to Taliesin at all. CLM-28's risk note is explicit: "The compressed timeline is doing explicit type-discriminating work in the Type 4 rebuttal, so tightening it beyond the evidence weakens the argument it is meant to win." Note also that "in love again before the year was out" appears in **no** claim-inventory entry — CLM-28 covers three other phrasings of this timeline and does not reach this one, so it has never been graded.

**Minimum viable repair.** Two clause-level edits, no restructuring, net zero words. (1) Diagnosis paragraph: replace "was in love again before the year was out" with what the record carries — he was answering a stranger's condolence letter before the year was out. The Seven point survives fully; arguably it lands harder, because answering a fan letter from the ashes is a stranger act than falling in love. (2) Mamah section: cut "She was living in the rebuilt house within months" or replace with the sourced version (they met within days of the December letter; she was living with him by 1916). Do not delete the beat — the rebuild-and-answer sequence is real and is good Seven evidence.

**Expected benefit.** Removes the one place where an evidence-checking reader can catch the piece overstating, and protects ENN-H1 and ENN-H3, which sit within two paragraphs of it and are the article's best material.

**Confidence.** High on the "living in the rebuilt house" clause — the packet grades it unverified with contrary evidence. High on "in love again" as unsupported-as-written; medium on whether a biography not consulted in this pass (Secrest, Gill, Huxtable, Farr in full) would support it, which is why the repair is to state the sourced version rather than to delete the beat.

**Acceptance test.** Every sentence in the draft describing the Wright–Noel sequence can be mapped to a dated row in the packet timeline or a CLM entry graded "verified," and no sentence asserts a mutual emotional state on a date the packet dates only a letter.

## Specific improvements

### ENN-C1 (concern, highest priority) — Type 8 as core is unaddressed, and it is the reading the draft's own showcase evidence invites

**Location.** Rabbit Hole → "Counterarguments" (currently Type 5 and Type 3 only). The gap is created by the body: the cold open, all of H2 "What Wright meant when he told a client to move his chair," FAQ #2, the 60-ton column, the Harlan House contract violation, and firing his son over unpaid wages.

**Reader effect.** A reader who knows the system spends the first 400 words watching dominion behavior — a man who humiliates a paying client in front of his guests and hangs up — then reads a headline claiming Seven-with-an-Eight-wing and never gets told why the Eight is the wing rather than the core. The draft answers _which wing_ (7w8 vs 7w6) and never answers _which core_. Because the Eight-flavored material is also the most memorable material, the omission is most visible to exactly the reader the Rabbit Hole exists to serve.

**Evidence / reasoning.** Packet, "Evidence against Type 7, and the strongest alternatives": "**Type 8 as core rather than wing.** Not addressed anywhere in the draft... Evaluators may reasonably flag the absence of an 8-core rebuttal as a gap, given that the draft's own 8-wing evidence is its most vivid material." The discriminator is available in-house and is not currently used anywhere in the draft: 9takes' `/enneagram-corner/enneagram-harmonic-approaches` places **7 in Positive Outlook** ("reframe, distract, minimize... negative feelings are the actual problem") and **8 in Reactive** ("Feel it loud and make sure you feel it too"). That lens separates delivery from operating system, which is precisely the confusion here — "move your chair" is _delivered_ as an Eight would deliver it and _contains_ what only a Seven would say, namely that the leak is not a problem. Every catastrophe in the article resolves the same way: the earthquake telegram with the damage clause suppressed, twelve fallow years published as a chrysalis, a grave left without an address. An Eight escalates and makes you feel it. Wright deleted it and kept moving.

**Minimum viable repair.** One paragraph in the existing "Counterarguments" block, beside Type 5 and Type 3, roughly 70 words, linking `/enneagram-corner/enneagram-harmonic-approaches`. No body-level change, no new H2, no word budget from the main narrative.

**Expected benefit.** Closes the piece's largest typing gap, converts its most-quoted anecdote from a liability into thesis evidence, and adds a second internal link to an under-linked Enneagram page.

**Confidence.** High that the gap is real and material; high that the Harmonics discriminator resolves it, since it is Riso–Hudson framework material already published on the site.

**Acceptance test.** A reader who finishes the Rabbit Hole can state, in one sentence, why Wright's dominion behavior is a wing rather than a core, and can name the specific evidence that would have pointed to an Eight core instead.

### ENN-C2 (concern) — The growth line to Five absorbs every disconfirming fact, which makes the call locally unfalsifiable

**Location.** Three places: diagnosis section ("In Enneagram terms that is the Seven's growth line to Five, where the appetite finally narrows to one thing"); H2 "How Fallingwater happened in two hours"; Rabbit Hole ("The Five-looking behavior sits on the growth arrow").

**Reader effect.** Each instance is defensible alone. Together they mean that _all_ Five-looking evidence — the solitary geometric work, the Froebel abstraction, the capacity to disappear into a problem, the two-hour burst, and by extension the sixteen-year Prairie Style and the sixteen-year Guggenheim — is pre-assigned to the arrow. A reader who wanted to argue for Five finds there is no evidence the draft would accept, because the arrow converts counterevidence into confirmation. That is the structural failure the whole piece is positioned against, arriving in a more sophisticated costume than the aggregators'.

**Evidence.** Packet, "Behavior the Seven hypothesis does not explain well": the Prairie Style ran roughly sixteen years and the Guggenheim occupied sixteen years from commission to opening — "Both are hard to square with 'cannot tolerate the in-between.' The draft addresses this only by assigning it to the growth line to Five."

**Minimum viable repair.** One sentence, in the diagnosis section, bounding the arrow: state what Five-looking evidence would _not_ be explainable as the growth arrow — sustained conservation of energy and attention _between_ projects, withdrawal that costs him audience, competence hoarded rather than sold. Wright shows none of it, and saying so converts an unfalsifiable move into an argument.

**Expected benefit.** The Five rebuttal (ENN-M1) stops resting on volume and starts resting on a discriminator, and the article models the epistemics it claims over its competitors.

**Confidence.** High that the triple-use is present; medium-high that one bounding sentence is sufficient rather than a rework.

**Acceptance test.** The draft states at least one observation that would have counted against Type 7 and in favor of Type 5, and confirms the record does not contain it.

### ENN-C3 (concern) — The subtype discriminators do not match the internal page the draft sends readers to, and the canonical mechanism is a better fit than the one used

**Location.** Diagnosis section: "Sexual Sevens chase intensity; **Social Sevens build movements**. Self-Pres Sevens build _compounds_." Rabbit Hole: "Of the three [instinctual subtypes], **self-pres is the one that builds walls around the good life**," linking `/enneagram-corner/enneagram-instinctual-subtypes`.

**Reader effect.** The subtype call is the article's most distinctive typing claim and the one an interested reader is most likely to click through to verify. The linked page's SP 7 reads "Channels enthusiasm into physical comforts, new tastes, or experiences... Always up for trying new cuisines, exploring gadgets, or planning adventures," and its SO 7 is the "class clown," not a movement-builder. Two of the three discriminators the draft states are unsupported by the page it cites — so the reader who checks is undercut by 9takes' own material.

**Evidence.** `src/blog/enneagram/enneagram-instinctual-subtypes.md` lines 247–270 (SP 7, SO 7, SX 7). Against standard theory the draft is the _more_ accurate of the two: Naranjo/Chestnut name SP 7 "Keepers of the Castle," describing a Seven who "establish[es] a partisan network of allies," collecting "a kind of surrogate family of people they value — a family in which they typically occupy a privileged position," and who is "practical, good at networking... opportunistic, self-interested, pragmatic." So the fix is not to conform the draft to the weak page.

**Minimum viable repair.** Two options, either acceptable. (a) Cheapest: in the Rabbit Hole, state the discriminator in the draft's own words rather than outsourcing it to the link, and sharpen "walls around the good life" to the canonical mechanism — the surrogate family of allies in which the Seven holds the privileged position. The Taliesin Fellowship is a textbook instance and the draft already has the evidence on the page ("students paid Wright for the privilege of farming, cooking, drafting, and absorbing his presence"), so this costs no new research and roughly no words. (b) Additionally, log the internal subtypes page for a refresh; that is a separate ticket, not this draft's job.

**Expected benefit.** Turns the subtype call from a place-metaphor into the mechanism standard theory actually specifies, and the strongest evidence for it is already written.

**Confidence.** High on the mismatch (verified directly against the file); high on the canonical SP 7 description.

**Acceptance test.** Every subtype discriminator asserted in the draft is either stated self-containedly in the draft or supported by the page it links.

### ENN-C4 (concern) — A second real person is typed twice, on no evidence, in a piece whose selling point is showing its work

**Location.** Conclusion: "Philip Johnson called him the greatest architect of the nineteenth century, and meant it **the way a Type 3 means most things**, as a critique dressed for dinner." Rabbit Hole: "**Philip Johnson was the Three in that rivalry.**"

It reads as the exact behavior the article criticizes, and it is the moment a skeptical Enneagram reader decides how seriously to take the discipline elsewhere on the page.

**Expected benefit.** Removes the one place where the article does the thing it accuses its competitors of, at zero argumentative cost.

**Confidence.** High.

**Acceptance test.** No person other than the subject is assigned an Enneagram type anywhere in reader-visible text.

### ENN-C5 (concern) — A superlative type claim rides on a disputed event, in an FAQ that will be surfaced standalone

**Location.** FAQ #3: "The procrastination-then-flash pattern is **the cleanest Seven work signature in modern biography**." Body version, H2 "How Fallingwater happened in two hours": "it is one of the purest Type 7 work signatures on record."

**Reader effect.** The body earns its version — it hedges first ("The legend has been picked apart since... the sprint was performance as much as composition") and then claims the pattern, which is fair. The FAQ states the two-hour version flatly, with no hedge, and then stacks a superlative on top of it. FAQs are extracted and shown as standalone answers, so the qualification that makes the body claim defensible does not travel with it.

**Evidence.** Packet D-01 leaves the two-hour design **unresolved**: Toker documents preliminary sketches and compares the story to the Gettysburg-envelope myth. Packet CLM-06 grades the type claim "interpretation," risk **moderate-high**: "if Toker is right... the 'signature' reduces to 'Wright did not show his client drawings until he had to,' which is common professional behavior." D-01 also gives the safe formulation: "conclusions drawn from 'nine months with nothing shown to the client, then a finished scheme in one morning' are safe under either account."

**Minimum viable repair.** Rewrite FAQ #3's final sentence to claim the pattern that survives both accounts — nine months with nothing shown, then a finished scheme in one morning — and drop the superlative, or move it inside a hedge. Body sentence can stand as written.

**Expected benefit.** The article's most-extractable type claim stops depending on a fact historians dispute.

**Confidence.** High.

**Acceptance test.** No FAQ answer asserts a type conclusion that depends on a claim the packet marks disputed, unless the FAQ itself carries the qualification.

### ENN-C6 (concern) — Certainty language outruns the evidence base the draft itself describes

**Location.** Diagnosis section: "The case is **straightforward** once you know where to look." Reinforced by `meta_title`: "Why Frank Lloyd Wright Is an Enneagram Type 7w8, Not a 5w4."

**Reader effect.** "Straightforward" is a claim about the _difficulty_ of the call, and the article then spends two counter-typings, a Rabbit Hole, and an unread paywalled corroboration demonstrating that it is not. The overclaim is unnecessary — everything the sentence is trying to do (this is not a coin flip, we have real evidence) is done by the evidence that follows. It also sits badly beside ENN-C1: a case with an unaddressed strongest alternative should not be called straightforward.

**Minimum viable repair.** One word. Replace "straightforward" with a claim about evidence rather than ease — the case rests on behavior that is documented rather than inferred, which is true and is the actual differentiator against the aggregators.

**Expected benefit.** Certainty becomes proportional, and the sentence starts doing competitive work instead of merely asserting confidence. The `meta_title` can stay as-is; a title stating a position is normal, a body sentence dismissing the difficulty is not.

**Confidence.** Medium-high. This is close to preference, but it is promoted to a concern because the role contract tests certainty proportionality and because the draft's own evidence base contradicts the word.
