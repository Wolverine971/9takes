## Bottom-line verdict

The 4w3 call is defensible and, more importantly, argued the right way: from motivation and a documented response-to-hurt pattern rather than from surface theatricality. The draft's discriminating evidence — transcript-confirmed self-reported withdrawal ("the more I open up the more I get hurt... you do shut yourself off") set against how a wounded 3 or 7 would respond — is exactly the kind of tiebreaker this lens should use, and the base-rate self-check (30 of 80 musicians in the corpus are Fours) is an epistemic move almost no personality content makes. The theory machinery is used correctly: stress arrow to 2 (matching 9takes' own Type 4 page, including its "buying affection" framing of stress-2), growth arrow to 1, wing logic, and a genuinely serious 3w4 steelman fenced into the Rabbit Hole per format.

What strains trust is evidence handling, not theory. The Munich section — the stress-arrow evidence engine — contains a verbatim quote misattributed to the Wigg interview and an embellished verb ("pawned") the transcript does not support, and a SERP-facing FAQ asserts a deathbed detail the packet's dispute record flatly contradicts. Below that, the subtype argument contradicts the draft's own quoted testimony, and two evidence presentations tilt the confessional reading further than the sources allow. Revise; the spine survives all of it.

## What landed

**ENNEAGRAM-H1 — The withdrawal tiebreaker, executed as comparative motivation.** "Wounded Threes perform harder. Wounded Sevens book the next adventure. Mercury retreated to Garden Lodge with his cats and a circle small enough to count on one hand, and turned the wound into 'Somebody to Love' (1976)." This is the whole diagnosis in three sentences, built on a transcript-confirmed first-person quote, and it discriminates between the actual candidate types instead of listing Four traits. It must survive revision because it is the only place the typing rests on behavior under pressure rather than aesthetics — the correct evidentiary foundation.

**ENNEAGRAM-H2 — The Live Aid "untouchable" reading.** "The most extroverted act in rock history was, from the inside, a shy man's safest possible intimacy, conducted from behind a wall of 72,000 witnesses." This is the lens producing a non-obvious insight: it inverts the most famous footage of the man using his own transcript-confirmed words ("they adulate but you're still untouchable... that's my fantasy... I hide behind that") and resolves the stage/offstage paradox instead of just gawking at it. It is person-first, jargon-free, and it is the paragraph a reader quotes to someone else.

**ENNEAGRAM-H3 — The base-rate honesty check.** "Of the 80 musicians profiled on 9takes, 30 are Type 4s, the most over-represented type in the corpus, so a musician defaulting to 4 should be suspected." Verified against `corpus-stats.json` per the packet (CLM-20). Certainty proportional to evidence is a core test of this perspective, and this sentence is the draft policing its own prior. Must survive — cutting it would remove the single strongest signal that the typing was reasoned rather than defaulted.

**ENNEAGRAM-H4 — Munich as the stress-2 arrow, grounded in testimony.** "Under sustained stress, Fours slide into the Two's compulsive over-giving, buying affection they no longer believe they can attract. Munich is that arrow with a street address." Correct arrow direction, consistent with 9takes' internal Type 4 material ("desperate clinging... a frantic search for someone, anyone"), and anchored to Mercury's own transcript-confirmed admission that the gifts came back. Theory-mapping is honestly the weakest class of type evidence, but this is how to do it: one line of theory, then the biography carries it. (The section's sourcing problems are ENNEAGRAM-B1; the arrow logic itself is right.)

**ENNEAGRAM-H5 — The concession of what the type does not explain, placed in the main diagnosis.** "One honest complication: he stayed in the same band from 1970 until he died... That patience sits oddly beside the volatile Four stereotype." Most typings explain everything; this one names its anomaly on the main path, not buried in a footnote. The role contract asks for exactly this. (The wing-patch that follows it is ENNEAGRAM-C4, but the concession itself must survive.)

**ENNEAGRAM-H6 — The 3w4 steelman in the Rabbit Hole.** "The 3w4 case is genuinely strong: relentless output, image control, stagecraft, and Hutton's testimony that he 'never grumbled' and kept the brave face to the end, which reads like a Three's terror of being seen failing." ## Red flags

**ENNEAGRAM-B1 (blocker) — Quote distortion in the stress-arrow evidence section (packet CLM-08).**

- **Passage:** Munich section: "'Every day is Fasching for me,' he told Wigg, borrowing the local word for carnival" and "He bought extravagant gifts... most of them came back a few years later, returned or pawned by people who'd wanted the wallet and moved on"; Rabbit Hole: "an entourage that pawned the gifts."
- **Effect:** The Munich section carries the 4→2 stress-arrow argument. A misattributed verbatim quote ("he told Wigg" — the sentence is absent from the on-file Wigg transcript, existing only in unpinned reported form) plus two embellishments ("pawned" appears nowhere; the transcript says "shitty things... I get them back," not "extravagant gifts") means the type evidence here is partly manufactured wording. Any reader who checks the tape loses trust in the section that anchors the stress arrow.
- **Evidence:** Packet First-person evidence items 6–7 and CLM-08; transcript on file at `youtube-transcripts/1985-freddie-mercury-david-wigg-munich.md`.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Recast Fasching as reported speech unpinned from Wigg ("a line long attributed to him" or quote only what the transcript supports: "they have Fasching over here so I got into that"); delete "or pawned" and the Rabbit Hole's "pawned the gifts"; replace "extravagant" with a transcript-safe rendering of the gifts.
- **Expected benefit:** The stress-arrow argument keeps its full force (the transcript-confirmed gifts-returned admission is sufficient) while becoming check-proof.
- **Confidence:** High — the packet re-verified the transcript this run.
- **Acceptance test:** Every quoted string in the Munich section and Rabbit Hole either appears in the on-file transcript or is attributed in reported form without naming Wigg; grep for "pawned" returns zero hits.

**ENNEAGRAM-B2 (blocker, cross-lane) — Unsupported deathbed claim in a SERP-facing FAQ (packet CLM-07).**

- **Passage:** FAQ "Was Freddie Mercury married? Who was Mary Austin?": "she was holding his hand when he died."
- **Effect:** A checkable factual assertion the packet's dispute record contradicts (accounts place Dave Clark at the bedside, Hutton present, and Mary Austin phoned _after_ death). FAQ answers surface in search results; this is the single most exposed factual error in the draft. Not type evidence, but a trust break for any reader in any lane.
- **Evidence:** Packet Disputes item 1 and CLM-07; S13 (Smooth Radio, Grunge, search sweep found no supporting primary).
- **Minimum viable repair:** Replace with a supported closeness fact, e.g. "she was with him through his final days and was among the first told of his death," or cut the clause.
- **Expected benefit:** Removes the draft's largest documented factual exposure at zero cost to the Mary Austin argument, which never depends on the deathbed detail.
- **Confidence:** High that the claim is unsupported as written (packet ran the checks); the true room occupancy remains unresolved, so the repair must stay qualified.
- **Acceptance test:** No FAQ or body sentence places Mary Austin physically at the moment of death; the ending's ashes/marriage material is untouched.

**ENNEAGRAM-C1 (concern) — The subtype argument contradicts the draft's own evidence.**

- **Passage:** Rabbit Hole subtype section: "The self-preservation Four's quiet endurance is nowhere in his biography; the sx Four's operatic demand is everywhere."
- **Effect:** The sp-4 countertype is defined in the literature precisely by enduring suffering without complaint. Hutton's testimony — "he never grumbled to anyone or sought sympathy of any kind. It was his battle, no one else's, and he always wore a brave face" — quoted in the testimony ledger and The Silence section, is a near-textbook description of it, as is the four-year undramatized illness. An Enneagram-literate reader catches the contradiction in one pass.
- **Evidence:** Draft-internal (The Silence section vs. Rabbit Hole); standard subtype theory (sp-4 as the countertype); 9takes' own subtypes guide frames countertypes as masking typical type patterns.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Qualify the absolute: e.g., the operatic sx pattern dominates the public life and the romances, while the illness years show the enduring-without-complaint capacity — then let the sx call rest on the lifelong pattern rather than on sp's total absence. Either argue the "/so" secondary in one clause or trim the heading to "Sexual 4."
- **Expected benefit:** The subtype section stops being self-refuting and the illness-years stoicism gets absorbed instead of ignored.
- **Confidence:** High on the contradiction; medium on the sx call itself (still plausible).
- **Acceptance test:** No sentence claims sp-4 behavior is absent from the biography while the Hutton brave-face quote remains in the draft; every instinct named in the subtype heading gets at least one supporting clause.

**ENNEAGRAM-C2 (concern) — The pretence quote is presented minus its light-hearted close (packet CLM-04).**

- **Passage:** Intro and "The Great Pretender, Scene by Scene": "The way I see it is that all this is a pretence" — everywhere it appears, the verified continuation "It's fun." is omitted.
- **Effect:** The draft reads the Dolezal lines purely as confession, and the omission tilts the exhibit toward the melancholy the thesis needs. The full quote is more playful than the draft lets it be — which a skeptic can use to argue the 3-or-7 reading the draft dismisses.
- **Evidence:** Packet First-person item 8 and CLM-04 (Louder transcription verified, including "It's fun.").
- **Minimum viable repair:** Include the close once in Scene by Scene and read it — a Four aestheticizing his own pretence _would_ enjoy it as craft; the draft's own "which is the joke and the confession at once" line already knows this. One sentence absorbs the counterevidence instead of hiding it.
- **Expected benefit:** The central exhibit becomes distortion-proof and the confession reading gets stronger, not weaker, for having metabolized the playfulness.
- **Confidence:** High on the omission; medium on how much it changes a general reader's takeaway.
- **Acceptance test:** "It's fun." appears at least once where the pretence quote is analyzed, with a reading that accounts for it.

**ENNEAGRAM-C3 (concern) — The image-fusion discriminator over-claims against Type 3.**

- **Passage:** Diagnosis section: "A Three fuses with the winning image and defends it as the self. Mercury kept insisting, on the record, that the image was a fabrication and that the person underneath was going unloved."
- **Effect:** Presented as a type discriminator, but naming one's persona as an act is common in self-aware 3w4 performers, and grieving that people love the image rather than the person is a classic _Three_ tragedy in the literature. The claim only discriminates against an unreflective Three, which nobody argues Mercury was.
- **Evidence:** Standard theory on Three's fear (worthlessness apart from the image) vs. Four's (no stable identity); the draft's own Rabbit Hole concedes the 3w4 case is "genuinely strong."
- **Minimum viable repair:** Demote the sentence from discriminator to observation ("naming the act doesn't settle it; his response to being hurt does") and let the withdrawal tiebreaker — which immediately follows — carry the weight alone.
- **Expected benefit:** The diagnosis loses a soft argument a literate skeptic can puncture and gains credibility for the hard one.
- **Confidence:** Medium-high.
- **Acceptance test:** The diagnosis section contains exactly one claim doing tiebreaker work (response-to-hurt), with the persona-naming material framed as context, not proof.

**ENNEAGRAM-C4 (concern) — The 3 wing is used as an unfalsifiable patch.**

- **Passage:** Diagnosis section: "His 3 wing carried it" (the 20-year band loyalty anomaly); Rabbit Hole wing section attributes all production discipline to the wing.
- **Effect:** Every behavior the core type fails to explain is absorbed by the wing, which makes the 4w3-vs-3w4 question unanswerable in principle — the packet flags exactly this. The draft never states what would count as evidence _for_ core-3 over wing-3.
- **Evidence:** Packet "Enneagram hypothesis evidence": "The draft assigns it to the 3 wing — unfalsifiable move; evaluators may probe it."
- **Minimum viable repair:** One sentence in the Rabbit Hole wing or counterargument section stating the falsifier, e.g.: if his documented response to career wounds had been image-repair and harder performing rather than retreat, the same facts would read 3w4 — the wing claim stands or falls on the withdrawal pattern, not on the discipline itself.
- **Expected benefit:** Converts the wing from a patch into a testable claim and directly answers this perspective's central question (what evidence would change the call).
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Acceptance test:** The Rabbit Hole names at least one concrete observation that would have flipped the call to 3w4.

**ENNEAGRAM-C5 (concern) — The identity-construction thesis overstates its earliest data point.**

- **Passage:** Diagnosis section: "Farrokh Bulsara manufactured Freddie Mercury... a new first name at eight"; childhood section: "Freestone put it flatly: he became Freddie at school and never went back to Farrokh"; inner-thought device: "Fine. I'll build the boy they'll want to know."
- **Effect:** The nickname was schoolmate-given and adopted (the draft's own childhood section says so correctly); the packet notes the "never went back to Farrokh" phrasing was the interviewer's, which Freestone affirmed — "put it flatly" overstates. The diagnosis compresses a peer-given nickname into deliberate self-manufacture by an eight-year-old, and the invented inner monologue dramatizes intentional identity-construction at an age the evidence can't support. The identity-as-handmade-object argument is real from ~1970 onward (surname, crest, persona); backdating it to eight inflates the pattern's origin.
- **Evidence:** Packet CLM-02 nuance and Third-party testimony item 1 (Farrokh renaming).
- **Minimum viable repair:** In the diagnosis, date the manufacture honestly ("a schoolmates' nickname kept for life, then a new surname and a self-drawn crest at 24"); soften "put it flatly" to "Freestone affirmed it"; keep the inner-thought device but let it express displacement rather than a construction plan.
- **Expected benefit:** The construction thesis keeps its force where the evidence is strong and stops claiming agency the record doesn't show.
- **Confidence:** Medium-high.
- **Acceptance test:** No sentence attributes the age-eight renaming to Mercury's own initiative; the "put it flatly" attribution is corrected.
