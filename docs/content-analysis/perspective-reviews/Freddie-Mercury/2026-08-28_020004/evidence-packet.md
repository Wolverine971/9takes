# Evidence Packet: Freddie Mercury draft (2026-08-28_020004)

This packet is shared evidence for six independent reviews. Every entry is labeled
**verified fact**, **attributed claim**, **interpretation**, **disputed claim**, or
**unresolved question**. The draft's thesis (Enneagram 4w3) is a hypothesis throughout this
packet, never a fact. SHA of `draft-reviewed.md` confirmed against `context.json` before
compilation.

## Identity and scope

**Verified fact.** Freddie Mercury, born Farrokh Bulsara September 5, 1946, Stone Town,
Zanzibar, to Parsi-Indian parents Bomi and Jer Bulsara; frontman of Queen from 1970; died
November 24, 1991, at Garden Lodge, Kensington, London, age 45, of bronchopneumonia resulting
from AIDS. Alias history: schoolmates at St Peter's, Panchgani, India began calling him
"Freddie"; he adopted the surname Mercury around 1970 when Queen formed.

**Why the article covers him:** queued personality-analysis draft (music-icon cluster), with a
timing catalyst — his 80th birthday, September 5, 2026, and the estate's "Great Pretender's
Gala" in Montreux. The entity-gap packet (`docs/content-analysis/entity-gaps/Freddie-Mercury.md`)
scores the general-biography play a hard pass and directs the draft exclusively at the
personality long-tail, where existing type databases contradict each other (3w4 / 4 / 7w6).

**Knowledge cutoff of this packet:** compiled 2026-08-28 from repository research
(`docs/content-analysis/research/Freddie-Mercury.md`), three on-file transcripts, and targeted
web checks the same day. Primary transcript evidence is auto-generated YouTube captions —
wording garbles are possible everywhere below and are flagged where found.

Event dates separated from publication dates where they differ.

  Freestone (2025, transcript on file): saw his parents "maybe once" in that span; holidays

  biography)_

## First-person evidence

All Wigg 1985 lines below were re-verified this run against the on-file auto-transcript
(`youtube-transcripts/1985-freddie-mercury-david-wigg-munich.md`). Auto-captions garble filler
words; the substance matched in every case checked, with divergences flagged.

1. **Scars/withdrawal** (Wigg 1985, transcript-confirmed): "The higher I climb up that ladder,
   the bigger the barrier becomes around me... I find the more I open up the more I get hurt...
   I'm just riddled with scars and I just don't want anymore... you do shut yourself off. It is
   an awful thing." Supports: self-reported withdrawal response to hurt. Cannot support: any
   claim about _why_ (type-level causation is interpretation).
2. **Mirrors** (Wigg 1985, transcript-confirmed): asked who he'd turn to with personal
   problems — "I have a lot of mirrors." Transcript continues "I don't actually... I don't have
   that many [people]." Supports: deflection-then-scarcity of confidants. The draft's framing
   that "the serious answer was one person" (Mary) is supported by the adjacent transcript
   passage about Mary being "about the only person I can think about."
3. **Untouchable fantasy** (Wigg 1985, transcript-confirmed): "they all sort of want you or
   they adulate but you're still untouchable but that to me is about as far as I can go...
   that's my fantasy... and then I come back to reality and I don't have anybody to actually
   give me that... When I have nothing else to do, I hide behind that." Supports: stage
   adulation framed as safe/untouchable intimacy — the draft's Live Aid reading rests here.
4. **"Normal underneath" / "tread all over you"** (Wigg 1985, transcript-confirmed): "when it
   comes to love and a relationship you're equal... you're trying so hard to tell other people
   that look you're normal underneath... then what happens is they tread all over you." Both
   halves present in the transcript (the "tread" clause sits a few sentences after "normal
   underneath"; the draft's ellipsis compresses but does not distort).
5. **Goose/golden egg** (Wigg 1985, transcript-confirmed): "why kill the goose that laid the
   golden egg?" Supports: pragmatic band loyalty — the draft's stated anomaly for the type.
6. **Gifts returned** (Wigg 1985, transcript-confirmed with divergence): transcript reads "I
   buy a lot of shitty things for a lot of people, just most of the times I get them back a few
   years later." The draft renders "extravagant gifts... returned **or pawned**." "Pawned"
   appears nowhere in the transcript, and the transcript's own adjective is "shitty" (possible
   caption garble of another word, but that is what's on file). The Rabbit Hole's "an entourage
   that pawned the gifts" leans on the same unsupported word. See CLM-08.
7. **"Every day is Fasching for me"** (attributed claim, misattribution risk): the sentence
   does NOT appear in the on-file Wigg transcript. The transcript has him discussing Fasching
   at length ("they have Fasching over here so I got into that... I was upside down for days").
   The verbatim sentence exists in reported form ("I live life to the full, every day is
   Fasching for me") on quote aggregators and Munich-history features, unpinned to a specific
   interview. The draft states "he told Wigg," which the on-file evidence does not support.
   See CLM-07.
8. **Dolezal 1987 pretence lines** (attributed claim, verified against Louder's transcription):
   "Most of the stuff I do is like acting. So you go on stage and I pretend to be a macho man
   and all that... So it's a great title for what I do... The meaning in the song is that he is
   pretending about love but I take it a stage further... The way I see it is that all this is a
   pretence. **It's fun.**" Note: the closing "It's fun." is omitted by the draft everywhere the
   quote appears. Evaluators should weigh whether reading the line purely as confession, minus
   its light-hearted close, over-claims. Setting (jukebox, cigarette, Great Pretender promo,
   "final on-camera interview" framing) verified via Louder/secondary coverage.
9. **Mary common-law quote** (attributed claim): "All my lovers asked me why they couldn't
   replace Mary... To me, she was my common-law wife." 1985, widely carried (Biography.com and
   others); NOT in the on-file Wigg transcript. The draft attributes it to "1985" without naming
   Wigg — accurate as far as it goes; first-source remains unpinned.
10. **Kitchen disclosure** (attributed claim, transcript-confirmed as Freestone's account):
    "Look, I have AIDS. This is the last time we will talk about this because I have a life to
    live." Freestone, dopeYEAH 2025, transcript on file. This is testimony about Mercury's
    words, not a recording of Mercury.
11. **"I would understand if you wanted to pack your bags and leave"** (attributed claim):
    Mercury to Hutton, via _Mercury and Me_ (1994). Single-witness memoir; no independent
    corroboration possible.

## Third-party testimony

1. **Peter Freestone** (PA ~1979–1991; present through the final illness). Transcripts on file
   for both cited appearances; all six draft usages re-verified this run:
   - "He left home at 8 years old and went back at 17 and I think he saw them maybe once, his
     parents" (dopeYEAH 2025 — transcript-confirmed; draft reorders "his parents" into the
     clause, faithful).
   - The Farrokh renaming (dopeYEAH 2025 — **nuance**): the transcript shows the interviewer
     asking whether he "totally transferred into that other person and never went back to
     Farrokh," and Freestone answering "yeah, really, basically, because he became Freddie at
     school... that's who he stayed." The draft's "Freestone put it flatly" slightly overstates
     — the flat phrasing is the interviewer's; Freestone affirmed and elaborated. He also adds:
     "it was always Bulsara until he became Freddie Mercury," and that Farrokh was still
     visible "till about 1972–73."
   - "Freddie never wanted anything to take away from the image of Queen... he was 25% of
     Queen, that's what he was proud of" (2021 "Freddie and his privacy" — transcript-confirmed).
   - "He did not know that he would not be around 48 hours later" (2021 — transcript-confirmed).
   - Dark glasses: "the nearest to a disguise was dark glasses and that was when he went
     shopping in the daytime... he was not ashamed of anything" (2021 — transcript-confirmed;
     supports the draft's "dark glasses... extent of his disguise" and "ashamed of nothing").
   - "Freddie loved laughing... my favorite memory... watching him at home with a group of
     friends laughing" (dopeYEAH 2025 — transcript-confirmed). Anti-sad-clown counterweight.
   - Lunch with his mother "once every six weeks or something," always bringing Mary; family
     kept "totally away" from Garden Lodge and shows; Freestone links this to his parents'
     Zoroastrianism and sexuality (dopeYEAH 2025 — transcript-confirmed).
   - Teeth: "on screen he always covered his teeth with his top lip or raised his hand to cover
     them" (attributed claim — carried by Ultimate Classic Rock and others from a 2016
     interview; primary carrier is weak [bymegankelly.co.za per second-pass notes]; his 1998
     memoir would be the upgrade if anyone verifies the line there).
2. **Jim Hutton** (partner 1985–1991; _Mercury and Me_, 1994, with Tim Wapshott): "never
   grumbled... his battle, no one else's... always wore a brave face"; "Don't be stupid. I'm
   not going anywhere"; cats "like his own children," phone calls home to the cats. _(attributed
   claims — memoir; fans dispute parts of its framing per the entity-gap packet; use for facts
   and lived testimony, not deathbed color)_
3. **Mary Austin** (OK! magazine, March 17, 2000 — date verified this run): "When he died I
   felt we'd had a marriage... in sickness and in health." Also relays Mercury: "I've left you
   the house because you would have been the woman I would have married." _(attributed claims)_
4. **Brian May** (Total Guitar/MusicRadar, recounting the 1990 session): "'Fred, I don't know

5. **Roger Taylor** (Rolling Stone, 2014, Alan Light interview — provenance verified this run):

7. **Elton John** (_Love Is the Cure_, 2012): the posthumous Christmas gift account, note

8. **Dave Marsh** (Rolling Stone, 1979, _Jazz_ review): "the first truly fascist rock band."

## Public record, accomplishments, failures, and controversies

- **Accomplishments (verified facts):** "Bohemian Rhapsody" nine weeks UK #1 (1975–76);
  most-streamed 20th-century song certification (1.6B, Dec 2018, UMG/Guinness); Live Aid set
  widely polled/cited since as rock's greatest live performance (attributed superlative —
  polls, not fact); _Barcelona_ with Caballé; ~20 years fronting one band in which all four
  members wrote hit singles (verified fact — Queen is the only band with four members each
  writing multiple top-10 hits per standard reporting; the draft's softer "all wrote hits" is
  safe).
- **Failures (verified facts with attributed framing):** _Hot Space_ (1982) backlash;
  _Mr. Bad Guy_ (1985) sold a fraction of a Queen release (comparative framing is standard
  reporting, no hard figures in draft — good); late-70s critical savaging (Marsh 1979, NME
  1977).
- **Controversies:**
  - **Sun City 1984** (verified fact): Musicians' Union fine, UN cultural blacklist, Taylor's
    later "mistake" admission. The draft carries it in one sentence — present and accurate.
  - **The AIDS silence** (verified fact that criticism existed; balance point): AIDS activists
    and commentators criticized the near-total silence after his death; the draft's defense of
    the silence is **interpretation** in the draft's own voice plus Freestone's attributed
    motives (Queen's image; "he did not know he would not be around 48 hours later").
    Evaluators should note the draft acknowledges the criticism before answering it.
  - **Munich excess** (attributed characterization): standard reporting plus his own Wigg
    testimony.
- **Counterevidence to the sad-recluse frame (attributed, multiple witnesses):** Freestone's
  laughing testimony; the Wigg transcript's pervasive joking; band accounts of his humor. The
  draft carries this pressure in the "Was Freddie Mercury Shy?" section — the packet's research
  file explicitly required it.

## Enneagram hypothesis evidence

The draft's type claim (4w3, sx) is a **hypothesis**. Databases split 3w4 / 4 / 7w6 (Personality
Database, So Syncd, enneagram quiz sites — Tier 4, establishing disagreement only).

**Evidence FOR Type 4** (each item is evidence for a pattern, not proof of a type):

- Self-reported withdrawal when hurt (scars quote, transcript-confirmed) and post-Munich
  retreat to a tiny household circle (Freestone, Hutton accounts).
- Identity as constructed object: renaming at 8 (school-initiated, self-kept), surname change
  ~1970, self-designed crest, and his own naming of the persona as "pretence" (Dolezal 1987,
  verified wording).
- Envy-adjacent longing for ordinary belonging: "normal underneath" (transcript-confirmed).
- Aestheticized loss across the catalog (interpretation — the draft asserts it; the research
  file lists it as hypothesis support, and the fresh-eyes pass noted the draft demonstrates it
  thinly, via one Bohemian Rhapsody reading).

**Evidence AGAINST / for alternatives:**

- **3w4 (closest alternative):** relentless output, image discipline, 20 years of commercial
  pragmatism ("goose that laid the golden egg," transcript-confirmed), Hutton's brave-face
  testimony. The draft's rebuttal — he refused to trade the private self for public love, and
  his stated ache was being unloved "as himself," not worthlessness — is **interpretation**
  built on verified quotes.
- **7w6:** Munich hedonism, "Fasching" appetite. The draft's rebuttal (he marinated in the
  pain rather than reframing it) is **interpretation**; note the Fasching quote itself has the
  attribution problem in CLM-07.
- **Discriminating evidence the draft leans on:** response-to-hurt tiebreaker (withdraw vs
  perform vs reframe). The withdrawal itself is multiply attested; the _tiebreaker logic_ is
  Enneagram theory, not evidence.
- **Behavior the type does not explain (draft concedes):** two decades of calm band loyalty
  and business discipline. The draft assigns it to the 3 wing — unfalsifiable move; evaluators
  may probe it.
- **Base-rate caution (verified fact):** in the current corpus stats
  (`src/lib/data/corpus-stats.json`, regenerated and checked this run), the music domain has
  exactly 80 people and exactly 30 typed as 4 (37.5%) — the draft's "80 musicians... 30 are
  Type 4s" is numerically correct today, and 4 is the modal musician type, so a musician
  defaulting to 4 deserves suspicion. The draft itself raises this.
- **Stress/growth arrows (interpretation):** Munich-as-stress-2 and final-year-discipline-as-
  growth-1 are theory-mappings onto verified behavior, not evidence.

## Disputes and unresolved questions

1. **Who was in the room when he died (CLM-07) — the packet's largest gap.** Dave Clark is
   "reportedly at the bedside" in most accounts; Hutton's memoir places himself present;
   Freestone was in the house as carer; multiple accounts say Mary Austin was phoned first
   _after_ death. No checked source supports the FAQ's "she was holding his hand when he died."
   The claim cannot stand as fact; any conclusion built on it must stay qualified or be
