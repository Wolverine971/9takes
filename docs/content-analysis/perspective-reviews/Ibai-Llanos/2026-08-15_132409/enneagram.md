## Bottom-line verdict

Type 9 is a coherent best-fit reading because the argument is organized around conflict diffusion, belonging, adaptation to discomfort, delayed anger, self-forgetting, and indirect assertion. The article also supplies a serious Type 7 challenge and an observable falsifier.

## What landed

The Xokas response, regulated conflict formats, unchanged friendship circle, CPAP adaptation, Agüero withdrawal, and self-directed transformation create a behavior pattern broader than a single anecdote. The record-escalation mismatch is admitted rather than hidden.

## What missed

The stress-to-Six and growth-to-Three claims are plausible resonance, not demonstrated line movement. The wing and subtype calls are useful but less certain than the core type, and “appetite” should not collapse food behavior into proof.

## What I expected

I expected motive-level reasoning, countertyping, confounds, a wing/subtype distinction, and evidence that could overturn the call. The article contains each, though the arrow section should be read cautiously.

## What surprised me

The best Nine evidence is the ability to make a medical alarm feel routine, paired with anger that surfaces as withdrawal rather than the more obvious public mediator persona.

## Red flags

No typology blocker. The article does not diagnose and does not use anxiety, weight, or generosity alone to determine type.

## Specific improvements
