## Identity and scope

Ibai Llanos is a Spanish streamer, esports caster, event organizer, and co-founder of KOI. The frozen article reads his public record as Enneagram Type 9, likely 9w8 and self-preservation first. The scope is limited to public conduct and attributed testimony: his conflict style, long-term friendships, event formats, health transformation, business behavior, and the Type 7 and Type 2 countercases.

## Dated timeline

- **1995:** Born in Bilbao.
- **2008:** His family experienced financial strain during Spain's economic crisis.
- **2014-2018:** Built his career as a League of Legends caster while earning comparatively modest wages.
- **2020:** Joined G2, organized the LaLiga Santander Challenge during lockdown, and became a national streaming figure.
- **2021:** Interviewed Gerard Piqué, co-founded KOI, and expanded into major live-event formats.
- **2022-2025:** La Velada grew into a record-setting annual event; the article also documents the Xokas response, KOI strain, and the Agüero conflict.
- **2024-2026:** Publicly documented a health transformation from 152 kilos, daily walking, and retirement of a CPAP machine.
- **2026:** La Velada VI peaked at 7.67 million viewers across a multi-platform simulcast.

## First-person evidence

- Ibai described previously unrecognized panic attacks after moving to Barcelona and said he believed he was dying.
- In a January 2026 Wild Project interview, he connected overeating with anger that was not expressed directly.
- He described becoming attached to the CPAP routine even though it treated severe sleep apnea.
- He has said he loved himself before and after losing weight, framing the change as a health decision rather than a moral redemption.
- His public response to the Xokas controversy emphasized therapy and help rather than competitive humiliation.

## Third-party testimony

- Jordi Évole described Ibai as more grounded and capable than his loud on-camera persona suggests.
- Gerard Piqué said he already followed and liked Ibai's work before their first interview.
- Trainer Valentín Colominas attributed the health change to small initial goals followed by sustained motivation.
- Sergio Agüero described Ibai's anger as delayed, personal, and followed by withdrawal.

## Disputes and unresolved questions

- Public evidence cannot establish a private dominant motive with certainty.
- High energy, novelty, appetite, and record escalation sustain a serious Type 7 case.
- Anxiety and loyalty can resemble Type 6; generosity and social gathering can resemble Type 2.
- Stress and growth-arrow language is interpretive and should not be read as clinical causation.
- “Five days before this analysis” is historically bound to the article date and should be refreshed in future maintenance.
- Spanish quotations are translated by the author, and some numerical claims rely on press coverage rather than primary records.
