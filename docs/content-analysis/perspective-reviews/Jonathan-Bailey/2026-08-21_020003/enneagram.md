## Bottom-line verdict

I would land on Type 3 too. That is not the problem.

The problem is that the two exhibits the draft puts under "Confidence on the core type: high" are both
episodes of being closeted, and the draft never once considers that the closet explains them on its own.
A gay thirteen-year-old in turn-of-the-century England who stops ballet the instant a male friend sees him
in a leotard, and a closeted man in his twenties who cannot locate his own feelings, are not describing a
personality structure. They are describing concealment. Any of the nine types produces both accounts under
those conditions. The draft treats them as the diagnostic base anyway, calls the pattern "that mechanism,"
and then asserts high confidence on the strength of it.

What makes this frustrating rather than fatal is that the genuinely Three-discriminating evidence is
already in the piece and is not being used diagnostically. The backflip he will not do because of how a
failed one would look on TikTok. The manufactured effortlessness ("He practised"). "Twinkly," which the
draft correctly reads as the word of a man who has not yet learned to package something. The conversion of
desirability into a grant budget. "Yeah, I've got this," then "a deep spike of fear and self-doubt." Those
are image-calibration under no external threat, which is what actually separates a Three from a person in
a hostile closet. They sit in the prompt book, the sabbatical section and the closer, doing narrative work,
while the diagnosis section leans on the two data points that cannot discriminate.

The second blocker is smaller in footprint and larger in principle: the falsifier is a null set. "Find one
occasion where he abandoned finished work" cannot happen, and the draft's own Open University story is the
counterexample the falsifier was evidently reaching for, excluded by one adjective.

Against that, the Richard II section is the best piece of applied Enneagram reasoning I have read in this
corpus. It takes the image-type mechanism, predicts a specific limitation in his work, and two named
critics independently reported that exact limitation. That is a lens earning its keep rather than
decorating a profile.

Verdict: revise. No new research is required. Everything needed is in the draft or the packet.

## What landed

**ENNEAGRAM-H1. The Richard II mechanism.** "A performer trained from the age of five to read a house and
give it the thing that lands has one blind spot, and it is precisely the register that cannot be calibrated
to a house. Charm is a transaction with a room. Sorrow is not, and neither is love, which is the same border
he polices offstage." This is the piece's whole justification for using typology at all. The lens generates
a prediction about a specific performance register, independent critics (Wolf, Lukowski, Variety) confirm it
on the same axis, and the same mechanism then explains the privacy without needing a second theory. Three-way
lock, and no jargon anywhere in it. Must survive revision untouched.

**ENNEAGRAM-H2. The Type 2 rejection.** "Twos build identity around being needed, and nothing in his record
suggests he needs to be needed. He needs the story about him to be one he wrote." A correct motivational
discriminator, stated in one sentence, no lineup of rejected types. This is how the counterargument section
should work throughout.

**ENNEAGRAM-H3. Naming what the type does not explain, without laundering it.** "Those are Seven appetites,
and the Three read has no use for any of them," plus "What the type misses: Everest Base Camp, gymnastics,
and about nine Open University courses signed up for at once" in the TL;DR. Most typing content
reverse-engineers inconvenient behaviour into the thesis. Refusing to do that is rare and it is the single
biggest reason I read the rest in good faith. (See ENNEAGRAM-C7 for what it should do next.)

**ENNEAGRAM-H4. Correct connecting lines, correctly labelled.** "Threes disintegrate toward 9" and "Threes
integrate toward 6" match the house table in `src/blog/enneagram/enneagram-type-3.md` (stress 9, security 6),
and the arrow content is right: disengagement and numbness for 9, loyalty and courage against advice for 6.
The lines are the thing amateur typing gets backwards most often. These are right.

**ENNEAGRAM-H5. The 3w2 formulation.** "The 3w2 gets where it is going by way of other people, and counts
being liked as part of the score" is a clean, house-consistent paraphrase of the internal page's 3w2
("The Charmer": achieve through people, "Success means being liked as much as being impressive"), rendered
without a single piece of vocabulary a general reader would have to look up. And the 3w4 concession that
follows is genuine rather than performative.

## What missed

The diagnosis section promises to show motivation and then shows situation. Its opening move is exactly
right: "Achievement is the visible symptom of Type 3. What runs it is a nervous system permanently tuned to
how a room is reading you." That sentence commits the piece to motivational evidence. The two paragraphs
that follow deliver two closeting episodes, which are evidence about an environment, and the paragraph after
that ("What settles it is the speed") adjudicates against an alternative it never names. So the section
lands the promise in its first line and spends the next four paragraphs not keeping it.

The Rabbit Hole covers wings, instincts and lines and skips centers, defense mechanism and basic fear. That
would be a defensible accessibility trade if the missing pieces were abstract. They are not. The piece is
about a man who founded a fund called The Shameless Fund and said "The opposite of shame is freedom, right?"
The word shame appears three times in the reader-visible body and every one of them is in the Shameless Fund
section. Type 3 sits at the center of the shame triad. That connection is sitting on the table, fully sourced,
and nobody picks it up.

The instinct tiebreaker is the one place the draft gets the theory itself wrong rather than under-argued.

## What I expected

- The strongest alternative (Type 7) engaged in proportion to its evidence. The draft concedes six or seven
  distinct Seven-flavoured behaviours and rebuts them with one childhood anecdote known only through the
  subject's own retelling.
- Some acknowledgment that a member of a stigmatised group who spent his twenties concealing that membership
  has a non-typological reason to report emotional numbness and self-editing. This is standard care when
  typing anyone whose formative years included concealment, and the draft's own material makes it unavoidable.
- The defense mechanism named somewhere. The internal Type 3 page devotes a full section to it ("That's the
  deceit: mistaking image for identity... You stopped presenting the winning image. You started believing it
  was you"). An actor is the most vivid possible illustration of that mechanism and the draft never reaches
  for it.
- A falsifier that could actually fire.
- Certainty language calibrated to a nine-month source drought. The packet notes no first-person Bailey
  interview exists after November 2025; every present-tense interior claim is extrapolation.

What I did not expect and did not need: a typology debate in the main narrative. The draft's discipline here
is good. Two type-theory paragraphs outside the diagnosis and the Rabbit Hole is the right ratio, and the
Rabbit Hole quarantine is working.

## What surprised me

**Welcome.** The persona framing "Theatre's Rehearsed Natural" and the MARTINI _sprezzatura_ detail
("hired to sell sprezzatura, the Italian art of looking like you never practised" followed by "He practised")
is the Three's core deceit dramatised through a commercial contract, and it happens before the word Enneagram
appears. That is better typology than most of the Rabbit Hole and it never announces itself.

**Jarring.** The section title "Three Times Jonathan Bailey Read the Room and Made a Decision" promises the
Three's signature move. The internal Type 3 page calls it "The room scan... you've mapped power dynamics,
identified what's valued, and calibrated accordingly. Not conscious calculation. Instinct." That is the Three
reading the room. All three beats under that heading are the room reading him: the friend's eyes, casting
directors phoning his agent, strangers on TikTok. The heading says agent, the content says object. See
ENNEAGRAM-C3.

**Unearned.** "Threes reliably report exactly this." No they do not, or at least no one has established that
they do. This is a population-frequency claim with nothing under it, and it arrives at the exact moment the
argument needs to convert one man's interview quote into a type signature.

## Red flags

### ENNEAGRAM-R1 (blocker). The closeting confound is never named, and it is the entire diagnostic base.

**Passage.** Diagnosis section, "What is Jonathan Bailey's personality type?": "Bailey has described that
mechanism twice, unprompted, without any idea he was describing a personality pattern." Followed by the dance
studio paragraph ("The first time was the dance studio") and the Tupperware paragraph ("The second was his
twenties, closeted, working"), closing on "Threes reliably report exactly this, because feelings were replaced
early by a performance that worked better." The same move recurs twice more: in the dating section
("Bailey has one documented, catastrophic experience of it, at thirteen, and a second slower one through his
twenties") and in the Rabbit Hole stress arrow ("His account of his closeted twenties is that arrow in his own
words").

**Reader effect / trust problem.** An Enneagram-literate reader recognises immediately that both exhibits are
minority-stress accounts, not type signatures. The reader then discounts the stated confidence, and by
extension every downstream claim that leans on it: the speed tiebreaker, the 3-to-9 arrow, the dating
section's "for the image type" paragraph. A general reader gets a subtler and worse outcome, which is a piece
that appears to explain a gay man's concealment as a personality trait.

**Evidence / reasoning.** The packet classes the ballet scene as an attributed claim with exactly one witness,
given at 36, in a promotional setting (CLM-02, S-01), and lists the Tupperware cluster as an Attitude
conversation about being closeted (S-06). Neither is type-discriminating: concealment reliably produces
self-editing under observation and impaired emotional access in people of any type. Meanwhile the draft's own
type-discriminating material sits unused in the diagnosis: the backflip calculation under zero threat
(S-02), "Yeah, I've got this" followed by "a deep spike of fear and self-doubt" (S-02), the manufactured
effortlessness composite, "twinkly," and the routing of brand deals into a grant fund (S-16).

**Minimum viable repair.** Two changes, both inside the diagnosis section. (1) Add one sentence before the
two exhibits that names and disposes of the confound, in the piece's own register, roughly: the closet
explains a great deal of what follows, and a Three is not the only type it does this to, which is why the
type case cannot rest here. (2) Promote at least one no-threat calibration datum (the backflip, or "I've got
this" into the fear spike) into the diagnosis section as the third exhibit, since it is the only one of the
three where nothing external was pressing on him. Keep the two closeting episodes as context.

**Expected benefit.** The confidence claim becomes supportable, the piece stops looking like it is
psychologising the closet, and the diagnosis gains the one evidence class it currently lacks.

**Confidence.** High. This is a methodology defect, not a typing disagreement. I still read him as a Three.

**Acceptance test.** Delete the ballet paragraph and the Tupperware paragraph from the diagnosis section. If
what remains still supports "Confidence on the core type: high," the section passes. Today it does not: what
remains is one assertion and one unsourced population claim.

### ENNEAGRAM-R2 (blocker). The falsifier cannot fire, and the draft supplies its own counterexample two paragraphs earlier.

**Passage.** Rabbit Hole, final line: "The falsifier: find one occasion where he abandoned finished work
because something newer looked more fun, or show that the privacy guards his options rather than his story.
Neither is in the public record."

**Reader effect / trust problem.** The falsifier is the piece's explicit offer of accountability, placed in
the section written for readers who care about method. "Abandoned finished work" is a null set: finished work
cannot be abandoned. A reader who notices reads the whole apparatus as pre-immunised, which is the specific
failure mode Enneagram writing is most often accused of. Worse, three paragraphs above it the draft has
already reported nine Open University courses signed up for at once, one module completed, expelled for the
rest, and the closer treats it as charming. That is abandoning started work for novelty. The falsifier
excludes it by one adjective.

**Evidence / reasoning.** Draft text, Rabbit Hole counterarguments: "He signs up for things compulsively and
finishes almost none of them." Closer: "About nine courses. One finished. Expelled from the Open University
for the rest." Source S-02, verified verbatim, including his own framing: "the failure there is obviously
again taking on too much." Separately, "Neither is in the public record" is asserted as a finding, but the
packet records that no such search was run and that a nine-month source drought makes absence weak evidence
anyway.

**Minimum viable repair.** Rewrite the falsifier so it can fire and so the Open University episode is inside
its scope rather than outside it. A falsifier that works on the same evidence: find a pattern of him walking
away from _committed_ work (a contract, a run, a role) when a better option appeared, as distinct from
over-enrolling in optional things and letting them lapse. Then say plainly which way the Open University
episode cuts, rather than excluding it. And soften "Neither is in the public record" to a statement about
what this piece looked for and did not find.

**Expected benefit.** The Rabbit Hole's central methodological promise becomes real, and the strongest
counterevidence in the draft stops being quarantined from the test designed to weigh it.

**Confidence.** High on the null-set defect (it is textual). Medium-high that the Open University episode
belongs inside the falsifier's scope; reasonable people could argue optional courses are not "work."

**Acceptance test.** Hand the revised falsifier and the draft's own Open University paragraph to a reader who
has not seen the thesis and ask: does this datum trip the test, pass it, or fall outside it? Any of the three
answers is acceptable. "The test cannot be applied" is not.

### ENNEAGRAM-C1 (concern). "What settles it is the speed" adjudicates against an alternative the reader never sees.

**Passage.** Diagnosis section: "What settles it is the speed. He did not argue with the reading or wait to
see whether the feeling passed. He took one glance as data and amputated inside a second."

**Reader effect.** "Settles it" claims a decision between candidates, but no candidate has been named in the
reader-visible body at that point, so the reader cannot check the reasoning. The sentence reads as authority
rather than argument.

**Evidence / reasoning.** Speed of self-revision under observation is not a Type 3 discriminator in any source
I checked, including the internal one. Fast image-adjustment is a Heart-center property shared with 2 and 4;
a shame-driven withdrawal from an activity after being seen is at least as available to a Four, and a rapid
threat-read is available to a Six. Whatever speed distinguishes, it is not Three from its neighbours. The
Rabbit Hole does the real discriminating work later (7 by direction-under-threat, 2 by need-to-be-needed) but
the reader who stops at the diagnosis never gets it.

**Minimum viable repair.** Either name what speed is settling it against in half a clause, or drop "settles"
and let the sentence be description rather than adjudication. If it stays as adjudication, the honest
comparison is direction, not speed: he moved _against himself_ rather than sideways into a new option, which
is the discriminator the Rabbit Hole already uses against Seven.

**Expected benefit.** The confidence line that follows stops resting on an unstated comparison.

**Confidence.** High.

**Acceptance test.** Ask a reader who has read only through the diagnosis section: what did speed settle this
against? If they cannot answer from the text, the sentence has not done its job.

### ENNEAGRAM-C2 (concern). The subtype tiebreaker is a category error against the house's own definitions.

**Passage.** Rabbit Hole, instinctual subtype: "The tiebreak is that when he was handed the single largest
one-to-one validation available to a man in his industry, he called it 'completely absurd' and told his dog."

**Reader effect.** For a reader who knows the instincts, this inverts. Sexiest Man Alive is a magazine title
conferred by an institution, announced on network television, that confers standing in a field. That is
social-instinct currency, not one-to-one currency. The draft uses a prestige award as the test case for the
sexual instinct, then counts his deflection of it as evidence against sexual, and reaches the right answer
(social) by an argument that says the opposite of what it means to.

**Evidence / reasoning.** `src/blog/enneagram/enneagram-type-3.md` defines Sexual 3 ("Charisma") as winning
"through personal presence, attraction, one-on-one connection... being captivating in intimate settings,"
and notes SX-3s "often promote others rather than themselves." `enneagram-instinctual-subtypes.md` defines
one-to-one Three as measuring "self-worth by excellence in close relationships" and being "competitive about
being the 'best' partner, friend, or family member." Neither definition is about receiving public
attractiveness accolades. The same page defines Social 3 ("Prestige") as "the classic 3... fixate on image,
status, and influence," which is what a People cover is.

**Minimum viable repair.** Replace the tiebreaker with one that uses the right currency. The available and
much stronger social evidence is already three sentences above it: sector-scale thinking, LGBTQ+ funding as a
category, brand partnerships engineered to spill into a grant fund, institution-building. The cleanest
formulation: when he had maximum one-to-one capital, he spent it on a category rather than on a person or a
pairing.

**Expected benefit.** The least certain call in the piece stops being argued by its weakest sentence, and the
draft stops contradicting the internal page it links to two paragraphs earlier.

**Confidence.** High. Both internal definitions were read this pass.

**Acceptance test.** Show the revised tiebreaker and the house Sexual 3 definition side by side to a reader
familiar with the instincts and ask whether the evidence cited is the kind the definition describes. Today
the answer is no.

### ENNEAGRAM-C3 (concern). The prompt-book section is titled with the Three's active signature and populated entirely with passive episodes.

**Passage.** H2 "Three Times Jonathan Bailey Read the Room and Made a Decision," and its three beats:
"Thirteen: the friend in the doorway" (a friend looks at him), "Thirty: the calls that went to his agent"
(casting directors phone about him), "Thirty-seven: the backflip he didn't do" (strangers might film him).

**Reader effect.** The heading tells the reader they are about to watch a man scan and calibrate. What they
watch is a man being scanned. The section's cumulative impression is a person acted upon, which weakens the
type case rather than building it, because the Three's distinguishing property is that the calibration is
self-initiated and continuous, not triggered.

**Evidence / reasoning.** The internal Type 3 page names the active version as the type's tell: "The room
scan. Within two minutes of entering any environment, you've mapped power dynamics, identified what's valued,
and calibrated accordingly. Not conscious calculation. Instinct. You can't not do it." The third beat (the
backflip) is genuinely this and is the strongest of the three. The first two are not.

**Minimum viable repair.** Cheapest fix is the title: something that names being read rather than reading,
which also makes the section honest about what it contains. Better fix, if there is appetite: swap one of the
first two beats for an active-calibration beat the draft already carries elsewhere. The sprezzatura contract
(hired to sell effortlessness, having practised) and "twinkly" (a man narrating a thing he has not yet learned
to package) both qualify.

**Expected benefit.** The section stops undercutting the type it is built to demonstrate.

**Confidence.** Medium-high. The observation is textual; which repair is better is an editorial call.

**Acceptance test.** For each of the three beats, ask: who is doing the reading? If the answer is "someone
else" for two of three, the heading is wrong or the beats are.

### ENNEAGRAM-C4 (concern). The shame triad is absent from a piece about The Shameless Fund.

**Passage.** Absence, across the Rabbit Hole and the sabbatical section. The word shame appears three times in
the reader-visible body, all in "Why Jonathan Bailey Stopped Acting for a Year": the fund's name, the
pull-quote "The opposite of shame is freedom, right?", and "He has watched shame do its work up close."
None connects to type.

**Reader effect.** The Enneagram reader finishes the piece having been handed the setup and no payoff, and
correctly concludes that the lens was applied less thoroughly than it could have been. This is the piece's
largest unclaimed value, not a defect of accuracy.

**Evidence / reasoning.** `src/blog/enneagram/enneagram-type-3.md` has a section titled "The Shame That
Drives Everything": "The heart triad's underlying emotion is shame. Type 2s cope by becoming indispensable.
Type 4s cope by making their pain beautiful. Type 3s cope by outrunning it entirely. You repress shame, then
channel it into achievement." A Three's characteristic relationship to shame is to outrun it. Bailey did not
outrun it; he built an institution, named it after the thing, and stopped working for a year to staff it.
That is either the type's growth edge or a genuine exception, and either reading is interesting.

**Minimum viable repair.** One or two sentences, in the Rabbit Hole or at the end of the sabbatical section,
naming the heart-triad shame mechanism in plain language and pointing at what he did with it. No jargon
required beyond "the shame the type is built to outrun."

**Expected benefit.** The single highest-value insight available to this lens on this subject, and it also
gives the sabbatical section the interior dimension it currently lacks without inventing any interiority
(the reasoning is about the institution, not about his feelings).

**Confidence.** High that the material supports it. Medium on placement.

**Acceptance test.** After revision, a reader should be able to answer: why is it notable that a Type 3
founded a fund called Shameless? Today the piece does not let them.

### ENNEAGRAM-C5 (concern). Two of the five wing exhibits are not motivational evidence, and one of them is unverified.

**Passage.** Rabbit Hole, 3w2: "He was raised by three older sisters he calls his heroes, he founded a fund
rather than a production company, and his default public register is high-fives and self-deprecation rather
than mystique."

**Reader effect.** A wing is a motivational lean, not a biography. Being raised among women is environment; it
does not produce a Two wing and citing it suggests the argument is padding. "Founded a fund rather than a
production company" is a negative claim about what he has not done.

**Evidence / reasoning.** The packet records the production-company comparison as CLM-24, an unverified
negative: no search established whether Bailey has or does not have a production company. Separately, note a
theory tangle: the promote-others evidence in this paragraph ("cheerleading," "watching someone thrive,"
deferring to hires with "expertise way beyond mine") maps onto the house's _Sexual 3_ description as
readily as onto the 2 wing, since the house page says SX-3s "often promote others rather than themselves"
and "can look like Type 2s with their relationship focus." The draft uses that evidence for the wing while
rejecting the instinct it also fits.

**Minimum viable repair.** Cut the sisters clause and the production-company clause. The paragraph keeps its
three genuinely motivational exhibits ("don't judge your character," cheerleading, the self-deprecating public
register) and gets shorter. If the promote-others evidence stays, add half a clause acknowledging it is
doing double duty.

**Expected benefit.** A tighter wing case and one fewer unverified claim.

**Confidence.** High on the two cuts. Medium on the SX-3 overlap, which is a genuine ambiguity in the
system rather than a draft error.

**Acceptance test.** For each wing exhibit ask: does this describe what he wants, or what happened to him?
Only the first kind belongs.

### ENNEAGRAM-C6 (concern). Certainty language outruns the evidence in two specific spots.

**Passage.** (a) "Threes reliably report exactly this, because feelings were replaced early by a performance
that worked better." (b) "Confidence on the core type: high. The evidence is behavioural, sourced, and
repeated across three decades."

**Reader effect.** (a) asserts a population regularity as if measured. (b) justifies confidence in a
_motivational_ typing by citing _behavioural_ evidence, four paragraphs after the piece told the reader that
behaviour is only the visible symptom. The reader who is paying attention catches the switch.

**Evidence / reasoning.** No source in the packet or the internal corpus establishes reporting frequencies
for any type. On (b), "repeated across three decades" describes the span of three episodes, two of which are
the same closeting confound (see ENNEAGRAM-R1), so the repetition claim is thinner than it sounds. The packet
also records that no first-person Bailey source exists after November 2025, so every present-tense interior
claim is extrapolation from a nine-month-old record.

**Minimum viable repair.** (a) Convert to a claim about the pattern rather than about Threes as a
population: the type's characteristic trade is describable without a frequency claim. (b) Change
"behavioural" to language that matches what the confidence actually rests on once R1 is repaired, and let the
three-decade span be a span rather than a repetition count.

**Expected benefit.** The confidence line survives scrutiny from the reader most likely to apply it.

**Confidence.** High.

**Acceptance test.** Ask of every certainty word in the diagnosis section: what would I cite if challenged?
"Threes reliably report" currently has no answer.

### ENNEAGRAM-C7 (concern). The Seven counterevidence outweighs the Seven rebuttal and is then dropped.

**Passage.** Rabbit Hole counterarguments: six or seven distinct exhibits ("hard to calm down," compulsive
signing up, Everest Base Camp, marathons, triathlons, mountaineering, gymnastics), followed by
"Those are Seven appetites, and the Three read has no use for any of them," rebutted in two sentences by the
ballet episode and the closeted low period.

**Reader effect.** The honesty is admirable (see ENNEAGRAM-H3) and then it stops one move short. The reader is
left holding a large pile of unexplained behaviour and a rebuttal that rests entirely on the piece's single
most fragile datum. The natural conclusion is not "Bailey is a Seven"; it is "this typing has a hole in it and
the author noticed and moved on."

**Evidence / reasoning.** The rebuttal's discriminator (a Seven escapes sideways, Bailey amputated) is sound
in principle, but it rests on CLM-02, an episode with one witness, recalled at 36, in a promotional interview,
with no corroboration anywhere in the record. Weighting one such episode against six documented behaviours is
an asymmetry the reader can see. Note also that "numbness rather than mania" is loose: a stressed Seven moves
toward One (rigidity, criticism), not mania.

**Minimum viable repair.** One sentence after the rebuttal that says what the type genuinely does not
explain and declines to explain it, rather than leaving the pile unaddressed. The draft has already earned
the right to say this. Optionally tighten "mania" to something that matches the 7-to-1 direction.

**Expected benefit.** Converts a visible hole into a stated limit, which reads as confidence rather than
evasion, and reinforces the piece's best epistemic instinct.

**Confidence.** Medium-high.

**Acceptance test.** After the counterargument section, can the reader state one thing about Bailey the Type 3
reading does not account for, and know the author agrees? Today the TL;DR says it and the Rabbit Hole
implies it, but the section that raises the evidence never lands it.

## Specific improvements

These are additive value moves rather than defect repairs. Repairs live inside the red flags above.

**ENNEAGRAM-I1. Name the defense mechanism, once, using the actor as the illustration.**
_Location:_ Rabbit Hole, or the closing paragraph of the diagnosis section.
_Reasoning:_ The internal Type 3 page devotes a full section to it: "That's the deceit: mistaking image for
identity... You stopped presenting the winning image. You started believing it was you. Underneath sits a
quiet terror: nothing real exists beneath the presentation." Bailey's own account of his twenties is that
terror in his own words ("To be able to identify emotion seems to me completely parallel to being honest and
authentic"), and his profession is the mechanism made literal: a man paid to become the character.
_Repair:_ One or two sentences naming the fusion of image and identity, illustrated by the profession.
_Benefit:_ Supplies the one piece of core type theory the piece is missing, and it is the piece that most
directly explains the subject's job.
_Confidence:_ High.
_Acceptance test:_ A reader should be able to say what a Three's characteristic self-deception is after
reading, without the word "deceit" having been used as jargon.

**ENNEAGRAM-I2. Give the basic fear one plain-language appearance.**
_Location:_ Diagnosis section, alongside "The type carries one specific injury."
_Reasoning:_ The house table gives basic fear "Worthlessness" and basic desire "To feel valuable." The draft's
own epigraph is the fear in his voice: "if you long for someone, more often than not you don't think you are
worthy of it." The piece opens on the fear and never says so.
_Repair:_ One clause connecting the epigraph to the type's core fear. No table, no jargon.
_Benefit:_ The epigraph converts from atmosphere into evidence, and the diagnosis gains a motivational anchor
that is not a closeting episode.
_Confidence:_ High. See Limits for a caveat on the epigraph's wording.
_Acceptance test:_ Remove the epigraph. If the diagnosis section loses nothing, this improvement has not
landed.

**ENNEAGRAM-I3. Promote the no-threat calibration evidence into the diagnosis.**
_Location:_ Diagnosis section; sources already in the prompt book and closer.
_Reasoning:_ This is the counterpart to ENNEAGRAM-R1 stated positively. The backflip, "twinkly," "Yeah, I've
got this" into "a deep spike of fear and self-doubt," and the sprezzatura contract are all image work
performed when nothing was threatening him. That is the evidence class that distinguishes a Three from a
person managing stigma, and none of it is currently doing diagnostic duty.
_Repair:_ Move or echo one of them into the diagnosis section as a third exhibit.
_Benefit:_ The type case becomes independently supportable even if a reader rejects the ballet scene entirely.
_Confidence:_ High.
_Acceptance test:_ Cover the two closeting exhibits. Does a Type 3 case remain? It should.

**ENNEAGRAM-I4. Let the Richard II mechanism do one more thing.**
_Location:_ "What Critics Said About Jonathan Bailey's Richard II."
_Reasoning:_ The section establishes that charm calibrates to a room and grief does not. That mechanism also
predicts something about his _best_ work that the piece never claims: Tim Laughlin in _Fellow Travelers_ is a
part where the withheld interior is the role, so the blind spot becomes the instrument. The draft already
notes he "played the Tupperware box and got an Emmy nomination for it" but files it as a career move in a
different section.
_Repair:_ One sentence linking the Richard II limitation to the Fellow Travelers achievement.
_Benefit:_ The lens predicts both a failure and a success from the same mechanism, which is the strongest
