## Identity and scope

Keira Knightley is an English actor whose public career spans child acting, major franchises, period drama, independent film, and television. The frozen article interprets her public record as Enneagram Type 6, probably 6w7 and counterphobic in presentation. The conclusion is an editorial reading of attributed public evidence, not a clinical diagnosis or claim about private motives.

## Dated timeline

- **1991–1993:** Knightley asks for an agent as a young child, is diagnosed with dyslexia, and makes schoolwork the condition for acting.
- **1999:** Appears as Sabé, Queen Amidala's decoy, in _The Phantom Menace_.
- **2002–2003:** _Bend It Like Beckham_, _Love Actually_, and _Pirates of the Caribbean_ rapidly expand her public profile.
- **2005–2008:** _Pride & Prejudice_ brings a first Oscar nomination; severe paparazzi pressure and hostile coverage precede a breakdown, PTSD diagnosis, time away, and hypnotherapy before the 2008 BAFTAs.
- **2007:** Wins a libel action against the _Daily Mail_ over an eating-disorder implication and donates the damages to Beat.
- **2012–2014:** Publicly challenges body retouching, poses under a no-retouching condition, and discusses singing anxiety, wrong-name autographs, and domestic self-mockery on the BBC.
- **2016:** _Begin Again_ director John Carney criticizes her; he later issues an unqualified public apology.
- **2018:** Discusses PTSD and fame on _Awards Chatter_, her Joe Wright collaboration at BAFTA, contemporary scripts and sexual violence, Disney stories, and childbirth in an essay.
- **2024–2026:** Stars in Netflix's _Black Doves_; Netflix confirms season 2 production in and around London but has not announced a release date.

## First-person evidence

- Knightley describes leaving home during the paparazzi years as “a sense of battle every day.”
- She says she happily signs Natalie Portman or Britney Spears autographs but feels embarrassed when asked about herself.
- She describes dyslexia, relentless schoolwork, and acting as contingent on keeping her grades up.
- She says she could not bear possible rejection from _Pride & Prejudice_ and asked not to be submitted.
- She recounts 20 to 30 men surveilling and baiting her, a breakdown at 22, a PTSD diagnosis, three months at home, and hypnotherapy before the BAFTAs.
- She explains body-retouching objections, period-role selection, Disney restrictions, childbirth anger, and her desired _Black Doves_ season 2 confrontation in her own words.

## Third-party testimony

- Joe Wright describes understanding Knightley well, notes their shared dyslexia, and traces her growth across three collaborations.
- John Carney criticized her entourage and guardedness after _Begin Again_, then apologized and called her professional, dedicated, and central to the film's success.
- Public reporting and court coverage corroborate the _Daily Mail_ action and donation.
- Netflix's Tudum listing confirms Knightley's return, London-area production, and the joining cast members; it gives no release date.
