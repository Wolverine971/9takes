# Evidence Packet: Khabib Nurmagomedov draft review

This packet is shared factual evidence for six independent reviews of the frozen draft
(`draft-reviewed.md`, SHA verified against `context.json`). It is evidence, not consensus. Every
entry carries one of five labels: **verified fact**, **attributed claim**, **interpretation**,
**disputed claim**, **unresolved question**. The draft's Enneagram typing (8w9) is a hypothesis
throughout; nothing here promotes it to fact.

Verification method: the two local transcripts were grepped line-by-line for every first-person
quote the draft uses; external claims were re-checked by web search on 2026-08-13 where the risk
justified it; the remainder rest on the repo research pass of 2026-08-12
(`docs/content-analysis/research/Khabib-Nurmagomedov.md`), which is cited where it is the trail.

## Identity and scope

Khabib Abdulmanapovich Nurmagomedov (born September 20, 1988, Sildi, Dagestan, Russia; Avar
family). Retired mixed martial artist: UFC lightweight champion April 2018 until his retirement
announcement October 24, 2020 (officially vacated/closed out March 2021), final record 29-0. Widely
described as the first Muslim UFC champion and, by broad media consensus, the greatest lightweight
in the sport's history — the second half of that is a ranking judgment, not a checkable fact.
Post-retirement he heads the Dagestani coaching tree behind current champion Islam Makhachev,
fronts his cousin Usman Nurmagomedov's contract negotiations, and runs the Eagle FC promotion.
Known aliases: "The Eagle," mononym "Khabib."

The article covers him as a niche 9takes personality-analysis profile (Enneagram Type 8w9
hypothesis, the "control-as-surrender" thesis). The entity-gap packet
(`docs/content-analysis/entity-gaps/Khabib-Nurmagomedov.md`) ruled out any general-biography SEO
play; evaluators should judge the draft as a personality argument, not a biography of record.

Packet knowledge cutoff: 2026-08-13. Most recent events referenced: Miftaah Institute remarks
(August 8, 2026) and Usman Nurmagomedov's free agency (August 2026).

retirement (title formally relinquished by Mar 2021); three successful title defenses (McGregor,
Poirier, Gaethje); UFC single-fight takedown record (21 of 27, UFC 160, 2013 — still standing);

cornered Islam Makhachev to championships. "Greatest lightweight ever" is a consensus _ranking_,

**Failures and controversies (balanced record):**

- **UFC 229 brawl (Oct 2018):** vaulted the cage at Dillon Danis; his teammates attacked McGregor

## Enneagram hypothesis evidence

The draft argues Type 8 (Challenger), wing 9, social subtype, confidence "high." This is and must
remain a hypothesis. The typing-site consensus (mbtilounge, boo.world, personalitylist — Tier 4)
also lands on 8w9 but argues from nothing; it is not evidence and the draft rightly never cites it.

**Evidence consistent with 8:** territory control as fighting identity (takedown record,
two-rounds-lost stat); the testing/warning reflex (supermarket line — "an 8's warning, not a 6's
de-escalation" per the type memo); war framing embraced as compliment; justice eruption when
faith/country/father were mocked (UFC 229); contempt for "it's only business"; flat-declarative
verdict grammar (ring girls; "I can beat anybody"); negotiating with White as an equal; five years
of nine-figure refusals without visible wobble.

**Evidence against 8 / for the closest alternative (Type 6):** lifelong rule-keeping; loyalty to
tradition and tight in-group; piety; promise-keeping as identity; suspicion of "too much freedom";
obedience to father sustained into adulthood. The draft's stated tiebreaker: 6 obedience manages
anxiety, and no anxiety signal appears anywhere on the record; his word-keeping reads as
fullness/strength ("right now I'm full"), not dread-management. Evaluators should test whether
"no anxiety on record" is genuinely established or just absence-of-evidence — public composure is
also consistent with a well-defended 6, and the packet found no footage-level counterexample either way.

**Type 1 alternative:** raised and dismissed in the draft on texture (no visible inner critic or
perfectionism outside fighting position). Thin but stated.

**Behavior the type does not explain (draft flags this honestly):** comfortable, total financial
and decision dependence on his father into his twenties ("okay. Because I don't have choice") —
atypical for 8, which typically claws for autonomy early. The draft's candidate explanations
(9-wing merging, social subtype, Dagestani father-son hierarchy) involve stacked auxiliary
hypotheses; the Rabbit Hole concedes this. Whether the "rarest variant" framing (an 8 who
surrenders upward) is an insight or an unfalsifiable patch is the central interpretive question
evaluators must judge — the draft's thesis survives only if the 8 reading plus wing/culture
explains the obedience better than a straight 6 reading does.

**Discriminating evidence on the table:** (a) the 2018 vs 2020 memory asymmetry (remembers and
defends the rage, cannot remember the weeping) — both halves transcript-verified; the _reading_
of it ("rage is allowed, weeping is not, so the tape got burned") is **interpretation**;
(b) the plan-vs-promise distinction (2023 coaching exit reversed; 2020 promise to mother held) —
both facts verified; the distinction itself is the draft's construct; (c) the mother's
never-performed, never-earned authority vs. the father's earned authority — built from two verified
scenes plus an argument from absence ("that asymmetry is not a gap in the record; it is the
record" is rhetoric, not evidence).

## Disputes and unresolved questions

1. **The "father's age" quotation (CLM-05)** is the packet's largest sourcing gap: a direct quote
   in the draft whose only trail is translated Tier 3 recaps, against an explicit research-file
   instruction not to use quote marks. No primary venue (which interview, which language, what
   date) was pinned.
2. **The "He was not joking" reading (CLM-10)** — the transcript supports either a
   deflection-reveals-truth reading or a joke-that-lands-badly reading; the omitted "we show our
   kids we love you" softening is real. Unresolvable from available evidence; must stay qualified.
3. **8 vs 6 (CLM-01)** — "no anxiety anywhere on record" is an absence claim; public record cannot
   fully establish inner state. The type call is argued, not provable; the draft's confidence
   ("high") is a judgment evaluators may contest.
4. **Submission method at UFC 229** — neck crank (most outlets) vs rear-naked choke (some official
   records). Genuinely **disputed claim** at the record level; immaterial to the thesis.
5. **Children count** — sources conflict (three vs four; a Mendez-adjacent report says a daughter
   and two sons; Khabib said "two sons" on PBD 2023). The draft only ever says "sons" — safe. Any
   evaluator suggesting more specificity should know the record conflicts.
6. **Mother's name and wife's name** — not publicly established / deliberately withheld. The
   draft's structural argument about the mother is built partly on this absence; the absence itself
   is confirmed, the reading of it is rhetoric.
7. **Sponsorship losses (Palestine)** — self-reported only; the Islam/Usman extension is
   single-sourced and was excluded from the draft.
8. **"Biggest fight in UFC history" (UFC 229)** — true by PPV buys (~2.4M, record); "biggest" on
   other measures is arguable. Kept qualified as-is.
9. **"First Muslim UFC champion"** — uniformly repeated across coverage; no counterclaim found; a
   fully rigorous title-by-title audit was not performed. Treated as verified at the
   media-consensus level.

## Research limitations

- **CLM-05 could not be resolved to a primary source.** The "father's age" quote exists only in
  translated Tier 3 recaps; the original interview (venue, language, date) was not identified. It
  is not in either local transcript.
- Every Russian-language quote in the piece (Abdulmanap's RenTV and ToFight.ru remarks, the ring
  girls verdict, the Miftaah remarks, the "100 times" line, Islam's podcast quote) reaches English
  through one outlet's rendering; the draft's stated policy (one rendering, cited) is followed, but
  none were checked against Russian originals.
- Inner-state claims (no anxiety, "he was not joking," "the tape got burned") are interpretations
  of public behavior; no evidence class available to this packet can settle them.
- The local transcripts are YouTube auto-transcriptions with unlabeled speakers; attribution of
  host-side lines (e.g., who raised the weeping on Hotboxin') was inferred from context.
- Attendance, viewership ("a few million people"), and "biggest fight in UFC history" were checked
  only to media-consensus level; the "few million" viewership claim was not verified at all.
- No LexisNexis/court-record pass was done on the NSAC proceedings; figures rest on contemporaneous
  sports coverage.
- The packet did not attempt to verify "first Muslim UFC champion" beyond uniform media usage.
- Combat sambo titles at ages 18–19 (CLM-14) were not established; only the 2009–2010 world titles are.
