## Identity and scope

Laura Loomer is an American political activist, media personality, author, and two-time Republican congressional candidate. The frozen draft analyzes her public personality through the Enneagram and argues for Type 6 with a counterphobic presentation. The type is an interpretation, not a verified fact. The packet covers public evidence available through August 31, 2026, with special attention to her 2025 political influence and July 2026 Ukraine trip.

## Dated timeline

- **1993:** Born in Tucson, Arizona. Verified fact from public biographical records.
- **Ages 12–18:** Attended a remote Arizona boarding school. Her later description of fearing disconnection is first-person retrospective evidence, not proof of a personality cause.
- **2015:** Completed a broadcast-journalism degree at Barry University after a suspension connected to a Project Veritas recording operation. Verified event; some self-promotional academic claims remain unconfirmed.
- **2017:** Interrupted a New York production of _Julius Caesar_ and monitored the online reaction. Verified through contemporaneous New Yorker reporting.
- **2018–2019:** Removed or restricted by several technology and payment platforms under their policies. Verified fact. Her interpretation of coordinated persecution is an attributed claim.
- **2020:** Won the Republican primary in Florida's 21st district with 42.53 percent, then lost the general election with 39.10 percent. Official record.
- **2022:** Lost a Republican primary challenge to Daniel Webster with 44.19 percent. Official record.
- **2024:** Used _Loomer Unleashed_ to advocate loyalty tests and removal of alleged saboteurs. Attributed first-person political position in searchable program transcripts.
- **April 2025:** Met Trump and presented concerns about National Security Council staff; several officials were fired soon after. Sequence verified. Exact causation disputed.
- **2025:** Reported profiles documented her work intensity, home studio, security precautions, government sources, and disputed influence.
- **July 2026:** Traveled to Ukraine and told AP that Russian propaganda had misled her. Verified first-person correction in a reported interview.

## First-person evidence

- **Fear of disconnection:** In her memoir, Loomer wrote that fear of being cut off from the world stayed with her after remote boarding school. Supports a persistent concern with disconnection. It cannot establish childhood causation or an Enneagram type.
- **Fight fire with fire:** In a 2019 Jewish Press interview, she described accepting risk and using confrontation to make political statements. Supports a confrontational self-concept. It does not distinguish Type 6 from Type 8.
- **Loyalty language:** In dated 2024 and 2025 show transcripts, she advocated loyalty tests and said that understanding loyalty is necessary to understand what drives her. Supports loyalty as explicit self-description. The searchable transcripts are automated and should be audio-checked before publication if exact wording becomes contested.
- **Trump attachment:** In public statements and reported interviews, Loomer has presented her fight as connected to Trump's and said she seeks no favor in exchange. Supports self-sacrificial loyalty as public identity. Political incentives remain a competing explanation.
- **Ukraine correction:** In AP's July 2026 interview, she said she had been fooled by Russian propaganda and that rage made her receptive to it. Supports capacity for public belief revision and her own link between anger and information processing. It cannot prove why the correction occurred.

## Third-party testimony

- James O'Keefe described Loomer as apparently immune to fear, shame, and embarrassment. Attributed assessment from a former boss, quoted by The New Yorker in 2025. It supports unusual confrontation tolerance but may reflect a former employer's limited view.
- Shane Cory told The Washington Post in 2025 that Loomer is persistent and obsessive. Attributed assessment from an early colleague. It supports extreme work intensity, not a diagnosis.
- A Trump-connected consultant called her fanatically loyal in The New Yorker. Attributed characterization that matches her public brand but may also serve political positioning.
- Reporters observed a panic button, repeated backward glances, and a work life centered on a home studio. Direct observations support vigilance and security routines. They do not establish irrational fear because threats against her are documented.

## Public record, accomplishments, failures, and controversies

Loomer built national recognition through Project Veritas work, public stunts, online broadcasting, and aggressive opposition research. She won one congressional primary, lost two elections, published a memoir, cultivated a large following, and gained direct access to Trump. Her research and warnings have preceded federal personnel actions, although sources dispute her responsibility for individual outcomes.

AP and other outlets document a record of racist and anti-Muslim posts, misinformation, false accusations, and guilt-by-association campaigns. Platform bans followed some of this conduct. Critics argue that her methods create McCarthy-style personnel purges and harassment. Supporters point to conflicts and archived statements she has surfaced. The draft must retain both the reality of her influence and the human cost of error.

## Enneagram hypothesis evidence

**For Type 6:** explicit loyalty vocabulary; repeated focus on betrayal, infiltration, vetting, and insiders; attachment to a chosen authority; preparation through dossiers and archives; vigilance in public and private settings; aggression organized around identified threats.

**Against Type 6:** stated preference for being feared; appetite for direct conflict; rule breaking; public pride in forcing institutions to respond; branding and influence scorekeeping; professional rewards for threat language.

**Closest alternative:** Type 8w7. The discriminating question is whether autonomy and resistance to control lead, or whether confrontation serves trust, protection, and threat detection. Public material favors the latter by a modest margin.

**What the type does not explain:** ideology, prejudice, factual accuracy, media incentives, factional sources, ambition, real security threats, or the content of any allegation.

## Disputes and unresolved questions

- The public record cannot determine Loomer's private core motivation.
- Exact causation between her interventions and federal firings varies by case.
- Automated show transcripts have not been checked against audio for every word.
- Documented threats make some vigilance rational; the boundary between precaution and generalized threat scanning is not measurable from public reporting.
- The wing call, 6w7, is low confidence.
- The Ukraine reversal may reflect direct evidence, changing alliances, political incentives, or a combination.

## Research limitations

No neutral long-form interview centered on Loomer's ordinary relationships and vulnerability was found. Full audio was not checked for every program transcript. Private testimony about her off-camera decision-making is sparse. Some 2025 profiles rely on anonymous political sources with incentives. The packet therefore supports a medium-confidence public-persona analysis only.
