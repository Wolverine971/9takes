## Bottom-line verdict

The writer understands the transition from banned provocateur to Trump-world personnel force. The piece offers a fresh connection between the familiar loyalty language and the less familiar fear of disconnection.

## What landed

- **FAN-H1:** "A pink-lit spare bedroom has become an unlikely center of Washington influence." It captures the scale mismatch that makes Loomer's current role distinctive.
- **FAN-H2:** The path from two electoral losses to access-based influence is concise and accurate.
- **FAN-H3:** The Ukraine trip complicates the standard caricature and gives the profile a current event that changes the thesis.

## What missed

- **FAN-C1:** The article gives little space to Loomer's claim that conventional conservative media failed at opposition research. That omission does not break the thesis, but supporters may see her work only as anxiety management rather than a response to a real market gap.

## What I expected

Project Veritas, the Julius Caesar stunt, platform bans, congressional campaigns, Trump access, and the 2026 Ukraine change. All appear.

## What surprised me

The article treats public correction as a form of courage rather than using it only to score a contradiction.
