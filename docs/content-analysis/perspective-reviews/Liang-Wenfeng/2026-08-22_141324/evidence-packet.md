### A. 36Kr Waves, 2023-05-24, translated by Zihan Wang for ChinaTalk (S01) — Tier 1

- "People may think there's some hidden business logic behind this, but it's mainly driven by curiosity." Context: the 10,000-GPU buildup, "from a single card in the early days to 100 cards in 2015, 1,000 cards in 2019, and then 10,000 cards." _Supports:_ curiosity as stated motive for compute accumulation. _Cannot support:_ that curiosity was the only motive (he also says "If purely for quant investing, even a small number of GPUs would suffice," which implies he knew the purchase exceeded business need).
- "Verifying whether our hypotheses are correct. If they are, that's immensely satisfying." (answer to "what excites you most"). _Supports:_ experiment-framed motivation. _Cannot support:_ a general personality claim beyond this answer.
- "It's like someone buying a piano for a home — first, they can afford it, and second, such a group of people are eager to play beautiful music on it."
- "NVIDIA GPUs hold their value well, and older cards still find buyers."
- "It might be hard if we must find a commercial justification, because it's not cost-effective."
- "AGI may be one of the next most challenging frontiers, so for us, the question is not 'why' but 'how'."
- "Experienced people will tell you how something should be done without hesitation, while those without experience will explore repeatedly, think carefully, and find a solution that fits the current situation." Core roles go to "recent grads" (translation wording).

## Disputes and unresolved questions

1. **Authenticity and provenance of the May 20, 2026 transcript.** No audio; speaker-unlabeled ASR; one investing institution vouches via NBD; DeepSeek silent; Bloomberg unverified. Every "restraint is a strategy," pricing, team-stability, and "greatest return" claim must stay labeled purported. If the document is later repudiated, the draft's title phrase and its signature section lose their source.
2. **Second-round size.** Bloomberg: at least 10 billion yuan of new money (S07). Chinese media via TechNode/BigGo: a 50-billion-yuan round (S09). The draft attributes "50 billion yuan" inside a Bloomberg-sourced sentence. Unresolved which framing is correct (floor vs target).
3. **Restart date / "eleven days".** Aug 5 (Chinese media via aggregators) vs Aug 6 (Bloomberg's report date). Count is 11 or 12 depending on source.
4. **Meaning of the pause.** The draft reads the freeze as the price of candor ("He paid it"). Bloomberg/S41 describe frustration that private remarks became public, not a refusal to soften them. Whether the pause was principled, reactive, or tactical is interpretation.
5. **Xi symposium seating.** Attendance and the six speakers are verified; "far end of the long table, same end as Jack Ma" is a footage reading, and CNN places Ma "in the front row." Unresolved by text sources.
6. **Open-weights licensing.** Original V3 was not MIT; V3-0324, R1, V4-Pro are. The draft's blanket "all … MIT" is imprecise.
7. **"Never published the number of cards."** Contradicted by his own 2023 count and the Fire-Flyer paper; survives only as "no audited current inventory." The hide-the-engine half of the draft's split is therefore weaker than the publish-the-map half.
8. **Curiosity (2023) vs greatest-return (2026).** The draft leaves this open; the packet cannot close it. The discriminating test the draft proposes (post-IPO price hike) may already be partly answerable: August 2026 trade reports reference price-hike signals (S09 adjacent headlines), **not verified this pass**.
9. **Yan Junjie dinner date.** Source gives none; "early 2023" is inferred.
10. **Gaokao scope.** Primary source says top in Wuchuan; Wikipedia says Zhanjiang and adds a Tsinghua-qualification claim the draft does not use.
11. **Ownership stake.** 78% / 81.7% / 84% / structural 1%-direct all in circulation; "roughly four-fifths" is a hedge, not a number.
12. **Fire-Flyer 1 year and cost**; **High-Flyer 10B-yuan AUM year** (2018 vs 2019) — conflicts noted in S45, not resolved.
13. **Who signed the 2021 apology.** The letter is the firm's; the personal WeChat apology is attributed in Chinese press to High-Flyer's then-CEO. The draft says "the firm," which is safe; any reading of it as Liang's personal act is unsupported.
14. **Negative claims** (no video, no marriage on record, "has not commented on the figures") are supported by absence and by self-disclosures on the uploads, but cannot be proven.
15. **Type.** Five vs Nine vs One vs (unargued) Three/Eight: the same purported document supplies lines for each. Conclusions must stay hypothesis-grade.

## Research limitations

- **No authenticated audio or video of the subject** could be located; the first-person record is three text documents, one of them purported. Evaluators cannot check tone, delivery, or demeanor independently of reporters' descriptions (S29, S13).
- **Purported-transcript dependence.** Roughly a third of the draft's load-bearing quotations (CLM-05, 09, 12, 37, 42) come from a document DeepSeek has neither confirmed nor denied. Two independent translations (S03, S04) agree on substance but differ in wording; the packet cannot adjudicate which wording is "his."
- **Translation layer.** All first-person material is read in English translation; Chinese originals of the 36Kr interviews were not re-read this pass. Named-teacher testimony was verified in Chinese.
- **Chinese regional sources** (S14–S19) are single-outlet, surname-only witnesses in several cases; they corroborate each other on broad strokes (teacher parents, maths talent, low profile) but none is independently cross-checked beyond the research memo.
- **Not re-verified this pass, carried from the research memo (S45):** Fire-Flyer yuan costs and years; "sold directly"; "never once late"; Zhu Xiaohu / Zhou Hongyi / SCMP-employee / LatePost-2026 testimony; the 2013 Jacobi registered-capital split; the distillation-allegation chronology; the Odaily single-source anecdotes (Fiat, internship, flat size).
- **Unread originals:** Bloomberg 2026-07-14/25/08-06 (paywall), WSJ 2025-01-26, Washington Post 2025-01-28, FT 2025-01-31, SCMP 2026-07-26 (paywall). Their content is known through syndication or aggregators.
- **Seating at the Xi symposium** and any other claim that rests on reading CCTV footage could not be checked against a text source.
- **DeepSeek's 2026 API pricing trajectory** (the draft's own falsification test) was not researched; headlines in S09's vicinity suggest price-hike signals in August 2026 and should be checked before any evaluator treats "prices have not risen" as established.
- **Negative claims** (no video, no spouse on record, never commented on net worth) are supported by absence across the sources consulted and by the uploads' own AI-voice disclaimers, not by any positive statement from the subject or DeepSeek.
- The packet does not evaluate prose, grade the article, or rank the type hypotheses; it records what each evaluator can check and where the record gives out.
