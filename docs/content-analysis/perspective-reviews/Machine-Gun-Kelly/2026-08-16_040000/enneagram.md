## Bottom-line verdict

Type 4 is argued through the need to be read correctly, promotion of pain into identity, anticipatory ownership of insult, and destruction or revision of successful selves. The 4w3 conclusion is strong but not certain.

## What landed

The article avoids typing from sadness or occupation alone. The positive-review anger, yearbooks, titles, tattoo, deleted music, public rivalry, and output record distinguish Four from Three and Seven across several domains.

## What missed

The sexual-instinct call relies heavily on relationship intensity and rivalry. The combat reflex remains a genuine tension that wing and battle-rap training explain plausibly but cannot fully settle.

## What I expected

Core motive, childhood adaptation, adult repetition, wing, instinct, arrows, countertypes, and a diagnostic boundary. All are present, with specialized theory contained in the Rabbit Hole.

## What surprised me

Leaving one line visible after erasing the old visual identity is unusually compact evidence for retaining difference through destruction.

## Red flags

None. Zero body-level wing, subtype, or arrow mechanics leak into the main argument, and current same-type similarity is clear.

## Specific improvements

Future first-person evidence about envy, shame, ordinariness, or achievement without crisis could further separate 4w3 from 3w4.

## Follow-on questions

When stability lasts, does Baker experience it as safety, emptiness, loss of identity, or freedom?
