## Identity and scope

Machine Gun Kelly, born Colson Baker, is an American rapper, singer, songwriter, producer, and actor whose work spans battle rap, pop punk, film, and arena touring. The frozen article interprets his public record as Enneagram Type 4, likely 4w3. It relies on attributed public interviews, music, reporting, collaborators, and critics and explicitly treats type as editorial interpretation rather than diagnosis.

## Dated timeline

- **1990s:** Born in Houston, lived abroad, returned to the United States, and describes his mother leaving when he was nine.
- **Teen years:** Moved to Cleveland, sold mixtapes, began battle rapping, and observed a sharp change in peer attention after performing.
- **2012:** Released debut studio album _Lace Up_; a tweet about Eminem's daughter later became part of a public feud.
- **2018:** Answered Eminem's _Kamikaze_ verse within hours with “Rap Devil.”
- **2020:** His father died; _Tickets to My Downfall_ reached number one; he later described a severe crisis involving a shotgun while Megan Fox was abroad.
- **2022:** Released _Mainstream Sellout_ and co-directed _Good Mourning_, which received substantial negative criticism.
- **2023–2024:** Fox disclosed a miscarriage; Baker underwent extensive blackout tattooing and described it as following a breakdown.
- **Late 2024–2025:** Separated from Fox, spent the holiday period in rehab, experienced the Palisades fire, welcomed daughter Saga Blade, and released _Lost Americana_.
- **2026:** Toured _Lost Americana_ internationally while describing sobriety and a less polarizing public posture.

## First-person evidence

- Baker says he ruins his life before album cycles and converts the wreckage into writing.
- A positive “catchy” review of his first number-one album angered him because he believed the critic missed the meaning.
- He describes an unsigned yearbook before battle rap and a signed one afterward.
- He recounts moving to an attic so the stairs would warn him when his father approached.
- He describes the blackout tattoo as following a breakdown while leaving one line visible.
- He describes rehab, asking Jelly Roll for help, the Palisades evacuation, and making _Lost Americana_ after returning home.

## Third-party testimony

- Megan Fox described herself in 2020 as the person Baker used to avoid self-destructive tendencies.
- Travis Barker described upbeat surfaces carrying dark stories in their work together.
- Corey Taylor publicly attacked Baker's genre move, then later acknowledged respect and similarity.
- Mod Sun described Baker's meticulousness and hyperfocus during their collaboration.
- Tattoo artist Roxx described the search for visual order behind the blackout work.
