## Identity and scope

Madelaine Petsch is an American and South African actor, producer, and creator best known for playing Cheryl Blossom in _Riverdale_. The frozen article interprets her public record as Enneagram Type 2, likely 2w3. It uses attributed interviews and career reporting and explicitly treats type as editorial interpretation, not diagnosis.

## Dated timeline

- **Childhood:** Grew up in Washington and describes becoming the family member who tried to defuse her father's moods around age eight.
- **2017:** _Riverdale_ premiered and Petsch launched a YouTube channel that eventually surpassed six million subscribers.
- **2019:** Received a Shorty Award for her YouTube work.
- **2024–2026:** Starred in and produced _The Strangers_ trilogy, shot across a concentrated production period.
- **September 2025:** Discussed childhood, family support, therapy, relationships, and the fixer role on _Call Her Daddy_.
- **February 2026:** Publicly framed the trilogy's completion as a necessary exit for her mental health.

## First-person evidence

- Petsch says nobody assigned her the family helper role; she saw a place to help and repeatedly entered it.
- She describes financially supporting relatives, buying her brother a home, and refusing producer credits she did not earn.
- She calls pride the reason she will not accept honorary credit.
- She describes weekly therapy as the long process of resigning from compulsive fixing.
- She retains compassion for her father while controlling when contact occurs.

## Third-party testimony

- Director Renny Harlin described Petsch's producing contribution during _The Strangers_ production.
- Trade interviews document her dual actor-producer role and the compressed trilogy shoot.
- Collider supplied a substantive negative assessment of the films, which the article includes rather than suppressing.
- Seattle Times reporting records her decision to step away after the trilogy.
