## Bottom-line verdict

The article treats her as a serious worker and a serious artist, lets her make her own arguments, and shows its sourcing more openly than most pages of this kind. A subject-aligned reader would recognize the woman in the discipline section and in most of the aging section.

Trust is strained in one repeated way. The page quotes the wound in her words and then writes the conclusion in its own, and at the three points where she has given a conclusion herself (what the "No" meant, why she works, what approval is worth) her account is cut off one sentence early or left out. Two of the quotations it leans on hardest are also wrong on the page: the thesis blockquote drops a word, and the "take control" line is attached to a moment she did not place it in.

Three blockers, all repairable from material already in the packet. None requires changing the type call. Recommendation: revise.
