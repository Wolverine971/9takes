## Bottom-line verdict

Type 3w4 is coherently argued through earned worth, self-ranking, exhaustion as value, curated difficulty, and collapse under measurement. The self-preservation subtype is plausible but materially less secure than the core call.

## What landed

The article separates performance anxiety from generalized fear and distinguishes artistic taste from the repeated need to deserve a place. The ballet decision, “deserve the room” quotation, director-safety account, and “not losing my mind” achievement form a coherent motivational pattern.

## What missed

Subtype theory can absorb dislike of publicity too easily. The Type 4 case remains close because role choice, identity protection, and resistance to incorrect perception can all be primary rather than wing effects.

## What I expected

Core strategy, wing, instinct, stress and growth, alternative typing, and a statement of what would change the call. Each appears.

## What surprised me

The Kenzo film is discriminating evidence because it shows that dance itself is not the trigger; visible rank and correct execution are.

## Red flags

None requiring repair. The article names the Type 4 countercase and does not present Enneagram theory as diagnosis.

## Specific improvements

Future editions should label the instinct claim as tentative and look for first-person evidence that separates earned goodness from authenticity or fear of exclusion.
