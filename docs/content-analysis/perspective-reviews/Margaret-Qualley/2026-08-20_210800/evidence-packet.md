## Identity and scope

Margaret Qualley is an American actor, former dancer, and model whose public work includes _The Leftovers_, the Kenzo World film, _Fosse/Verdon_, _Maid_, _Kinds of Kindness_, and _The Substance_. The frozen article interprets her public record as Enneagram Type 3 with a 4 wing and a likely self-preservation instinct. That conclusion is an editorial reading of attributed public evidence, not a clinical diagnosis or a claim to know her private motives.

## Dated timeline

- **1994:** Born in Kalispell, Montana.
- **Approximately 2008–2010:** Leaves home at fourteen for ballet school; declines an apprenticeship and stops serious ballet at sixteen.
- **2016:** Stars in Spike Jonze's Kenzo World dance film.
- **2019:** Appears in _Fosse/Verdon_ and _Once Upon a Time in Hollywood_.
- **2020–2021:** Calls executive producer Margot Robbie to pitch Andie MacDowell for _Maid_; the series reaches an estimated 67 million households after its October 2021 release.
- **2022–2024:** Films and releases _The Substance_, including the prosthetic and dance-rehearsal experiences that anchor the profile.
- **2024–2025:** Appears in _Drive-Away Dolls_, _Kinds of Kindness_, and _Honey Don't!_; discusses work, self-criticism, ballet, and safety in long-form interviews.
- **July 2026:** Separation from Jack Antonoff is reported; Qualley's representative disputes infidelity and trust rumors. Qualley is photographed filming Parker Finn's _Possession_ in New Jersey.
- **August 2026:** Variety reports Paramount has dated _Possession_ for June 11, 2027.

## First-person evidence

- Qualley says she left ballet because she could have done it “just barely” and did not love it as much as performing.
- She says she desperately wants to deserve the room, loves exhaustion from hard work, and is harder on herself than anyone else present.
- She describes acting as comforting because it has no technically correct execution.
- She recounts leaving a group dance rehearsal, sobbing in a bathroom, returning for one-on-one work, and rehearsing daily.
- She explains that a highly capable director makes her feel safe and quiets her self-audit.
- She calls “not losing my mind” the achievement she was proudest of on _The Substance_.

## Third-party testimony

- Andie MacDowell calls Qualley brave and says she carved her own road.
- Yorgos Lanthimos describes her as willing to try things and not taking herself too seriously.
- David Fear praises her as the strongest element of _Honey Don't!_.
- David Rooney criticizes her _Drive-Away Dolls_ performance as swallowed by its accent.
- Josh Horowitz challenges her harsh hit-to-miss self-assessment.
- Roger Friedman criticizes the limits of her 2026 Vanity Fair interview.
