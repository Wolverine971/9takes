## Bottom-line verdict

The Type 3 case is the strongest I have seen this pipeline produce, because it is built on the single best kind of evidence a motivational typing can have: a verified first-person admission of the type's vice in action. The invented LaBradford Smith insult is deceit-as-self-story on tape, admitted by the subject himself, and the draft correctly makes it the diagnosis engine rather than decoration. The 3-vs-8 discrimination is done by motivation, not behavior, with two genuinely discriminating tiebreakers. Theory claims (stress→9, growth→6, heart-triad shame, 3w4, so/sp) are consistent with the site's own Type 3 canon, and the "social 3 reads as 8" bridge — which I researched expecting to refute — tracks Chestnut-derived subtype literature.

The verdict is revise, not pass, for one substantiated factual error sitting inside the stress-arrow evidence paragraph (the coin-toss shrug is inverted from the documented scene), plus two calibration problems: the gambling-as-Type-9 arrow claim is stated with more certainty than the evidence supports, and the draft leaves its best first-person corroboration (Jordan's own on-podium freeze-out hedge) on the transcript floor. All three repairs are small and none threatens the typing.

## What landed

**ENNEAGRAM-H1 — The vice, literalized.** "His best game required an enemy, so on nights the league failed to supply one, he handled the sourcing himself." — with the gloss "The word has nothing to do with lying on your taxes. It names the stories you tell yourself to keep the performance running." This is the cleanest public-record instantiation of a type's vice this genre can produce: not an inference from behavior but an admitted fabrication of self-story, verified (CLM-02, packet). It also matches the site's own Type 3 page word-for-word in spirit ("It's the lie you tell yourself"), so the corpus stays internally consistent. Must survive revision untouched — it is the article.

**ENNEAGRAM-H2 — Discrimination by motivation, not behavior.** "An Eight torches the sponsor and keeps the conviction. Jordan kept the sponsor." and, in the Rabbit Hole, "His aggression rented space inside a performance; an 8's aggression owns the building." The 8 mistype is the number-one objection a typing-literate reader brings, and the draft resolves it with discriminating evidence (image-protection over conviction; audience-indexed nerves) instead of trait lists. This is what the role's central question — what evidence would change the call — looks like when answered inside a narrative.

**ENNEAGRAM-H3 — Negative evidence used correctly.** "Praise has no shelf in the archive. The machine files threats, and when inventory runs low, it prints more." The Bird-compliment paragraph turns what the ledger _refuses to store_ into type evidence — heart-triad image dynamics with zero jargon. Non-obvious, accessible, person-first.

**ENNEAGRAM-H4 — The typing tested against its hardest data.** "The one scoreboard he finally owned measured him nightly and refused him the only lever he ever trusted, himself." Most typings in this genre never face an out-of-sample test; this one does.

Wing sections are usually the softest part of these articles; this one is evidenced.

## Red flags

**ENNEAGRAM-B1 (blocker) — The coin-toss shrug is inverted from the documented record.**

- **Passage:** "Security guards roped into pitching coins for twenty-dollar bills, Jordan hitting them with the famous shrug when he won." (section: "What happened when Michael Jordan ran out of scoreboards")
- **Problem:** In the documented Last Dance scene (Ep 6), security guard John Michael Wozniak **won** the $20 quarters bet and did the Jordan shrug **at Jordan** (S-19, NBC Sports 2020; S-25, Action Network 2020). The packet found no source for Jordan shrugging at guards when he won, and lists this as an affirmatively contradicted detail (Disputes #1).
- **Reader effect:** This paragraph carries the stress-arrow argument. Millions of readers have seen the actual scene; catching the inversion mid-paragraph discounts the psychological reading built on top of it, in the one section where the theory is doing its heaviest lifting.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Flip the sentence to match the record — the guard beating him at quarters and hitting him with his own famous shrug — or drop the shrug clause entirely. Note the corrected version is _stronger_ for the thesis: his signature gesture turned back on him over pocket change, and he kept playing. "The stakes were having stakes" survives intact.
- **Expected benefit:** Removes the only affirmatively contradicted fact in the draft; converts an error into a sharper detail.
- **Confidence:** High (contradiction documented in packet with two sources).
- **Acceptance test:** The sentence either matches the documented scene (Wozniak wins, shrugs at Jordan) or contains no claim about who shrugged; no assertion of Jordan shrugging at guards when winning remains anywhere in the draft.

**ENNEAGRAM-C1 (concern) — The stress-arrow claim outruns its evidence and its own paragraph.**

- **Passage:** "The Enneagram maps this exactly. Under stress, the Three slides toward the numbed autopilot of Type 9…" (same section)
- **Problem:** The packet classifies the arrow mapping as interpretation ("the facts support 'he gambled and he withdrew'; the arrow mapping is theory"), but "maps this exactly" asserts certainty. Worse, the paragraph's own best line — "The stakes were never the money. The stakes were having stakes" — describes core-3 measurement-hunger (inventing scoreboards), not 9-style detachment; million-dollar golf debts and pre-game casino runs read as intensity-seeking at least as naturally as numbing. Meanwhile the cleanest 9-arrow evidence the record offers (the 1994 autopilot year on minor-league buses) is relegated to the Rabbit Hole while the weaker exemplar carries the main-body claim.
- **Reader effect:** Theory-literate readers catch the over-claim precisely where the article is most theoretical; the internal tension gives the skeptical reader a loose thread.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Soften the certainty register ("The Enneagram has a name for this slide") and add one clause dividing the labor: the detachment from anything worth measuring is the 9 pull; the inventing of substitute measurements is the 3 refusing to let go. The draft already contains the second half.
- **Expected benefit:** Certainty proportional to evidence; the paragraph stops arguing against itself; consistent with the site's own stress-page framing.
- **Confidence:** Medium-high (facts are sound; register and evidence-assignment are the issue).
- **Acceptance test:** The main-body arrow claim reads as interpretation rather than settled mapping, and a reader can say which behavior the draft assigns to the 9 pull versus the 3 engine.

**ENNEAGRAM-C2 (concern) — The freeze-out row omits Jordan's own hedge, wasting the thesis's best corroboration.**

- **Passage:** Grievance-ledger 1985 row: "Alleged; participants have denied it ever since | Cited it from the Hall of Fame podium 24 years later."
- **Problem:** The transcript on file (S-01) has Jordan hedging on the podium: "no matter what people may have said, if it was a rumor I never took it as truth." The row's phrasing implies he asserted the grievance straight; he actually cited it as fuel _while disclaiming belief in it_. The omission is a small misrepresentation — and a large missed opportunity, because the hedge is the single best first-person corroboration of the table's own thesis: he knowingly burns unverified grievances as fuel. Deceit as curation, not delusion. The packet flags exactly this (Disputes #2).
- **Reader effect:** A reader who checks the speech finds the draft flattened Jordan's most self-aware public moment; a reviser who adds it gets Jordan himself endorsing the middle column.
- **Minimum viable repair:** Add the hedge to the row's record column, or one sentence in the Hall of Fame section.
- **Expected benefit:** Pre-empts the "article claims he believed his inventions" objection; upgrades the ledger from clever device to subject-corroborated argument.
- **Confidence:** High (transcript on file).
- **Acceptance test:** Reader-visible text somewhere acknowledges that Jordan himself disclaimed certainty about the freeze-out while citing it as fuel.

**ENNEAGRAM-C3 (concern) — No reader-visible sentence names what the Type 3 lens does not explain.**

- **Passage:** Absence. The strong-form thesis appears in the TL;DR ("a man who cannot feel his own worth unless a witness is watching him prove it") and diagnosis ("worth exists only while it is being proven in front of witnesses"); even the grief beat is routed through the frame ("Every basket tonight went up on the one scoreboard I can't check"), and the Father's Day section calls the collapse "the only unrehearsed thing he had done in years."
- **Problem:** The working memo concedes behavior the typing doesn't explain — the baseball year as tribute to his father's first dream, the same-day Kerr apology, tenderness with no audience payoff — but no published sentence does. Meanwhile the Rabbit Hole's own w4 evidence (the fadeaway "sculpted, in empty gyms") quietly contradicts the literal witness-required formulation; the type's audience is internalized (the ending gets this exactly right: "still walking past it to check for his name"). A totalizing frame with no stated limit is the "type claim that explains everything" failure mode, and this draft is one sentence away from immune.
- **Reader effect:** The skeptical reader's strongest remaining objection — "everything is the scoreboard, even his dead father?" — goes unanswered on the page.
- **Minimum viable repair:** One to two sentences conceding the limit, most naturally at the end of the Father's Day section or in the Rabbit Hole counterarguments block (e.g., the same-day apology and the baseball year as the places the machine reading runs out).
- **Expected benefit:** Paradoxically increases diagnostic credibility; a typing that names its own remainder reads as diagnosis rather than prosecution.
- **Confidence:** Medium.
- **Acceptance test:** A reader can quote one published sentence in which the article concedes something the Type 3 reading does not explain.
