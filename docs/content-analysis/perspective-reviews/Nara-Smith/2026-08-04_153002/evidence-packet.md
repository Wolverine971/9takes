# Shared evidence packet: Nara Smith

This packet is common evidence for six independent evaluators auditing one frozen draft. It
does not review, grade, or recommend changes to that draft.

Every entry is labeled:

- **[VERIFIED FACT]** directly supported by a checkable source
- **[ATTRIBUTED CLAIM]** a named person or outlet's claim, not independently established
- **[INTERPRETATION]** a reasoned reading evaluators may challenge
- **[DISPUTED]** credible sources conflict

The draft's thesis (a refusal to be categorized rather than a desire to be liked) and its
Enneagram Type 8 call are **hypotheses**. Nothing in this packet promotes them to fact.

## Identity and scope

**[VERIFIED FACT]** Nara Aziza Smith, born Nara Aziza Pellmann, 27 September 2001 in
Bloemfontein, Free State, South Africa. Mother is Mosotho (South African); father is German and
works in finance. The family moved to Frankfurt, Germany while she was an infant, and she was
raised there until 18. She moved to the United States (California) at 18. (S-01, S-14)

**[VERIFIED FACT]** Scouted at 14 (2015) through IMG Models' "We Love Your Genes" campaign.
Model, short-form video creator, and debut cookbook author. (S-01)

**[VERIFIED FACT]** Married Lucky Blue Smith (model; b. 1998) in February 2020 in California.
Four biological children plus one stepdaughter. Resides in Ridgefield, Connecticut. (S-01, S-06,
S-09, S-12)

**[VERIFIED FACT]** As of July 2026 she has 12M+ TikTok followers; Wikipedia's figure ("over 9
million") is stale relative to Forbes. A separate July 2026 report gives 17.2M combined across
platforms. (S-02, S-01, S-11)

**Why the article covers her:** she is the subject of a live, dated public controversy. On 29
July 2026 she appeared on _Call Her Daddy_ to reject the "tradwife" label; named critics
responded within days; Forbes published an explainer on 31 July 2026. Separately, on 1 July 2026
she disclosed that her two-year-old daughter had been in cancer treatment for roughly eight
months, and on 17 July 2026 confirmed remission.

**Packet knowledge cutoff:** 4 August 2026. Sources published after that date are not
represented. Note that several load-bearing events postdate the compiling model's training data,
so every post-May-2026 fact here rests on retrieved sources, not recall.

### On the medical origin (Instagram, December 2024 — the only load-bearing non-Cooper first-person source in the draft)

**[VERIFIED FACT]** that she published this account on ~10 December 2024, reported by multiple
outlets 11-12 December 2024. (S-03, S-04)

- "I got on a flight to Germany seeking help from naturopaths, doctors and other specialists and finally got a diagnosis: Lupus."
- Reported symptom list from the same post: purple hands that felt frozen, extreme fatigue, hair loss, sores.

**What this can support:** that the lupus account predates the July 2026 controversy by roughly
19 months, and was volunteered rather than produced under argumentative pressure.
**What it cannot support:** the specific _Call Her Daddy_ phrasings ("sent home defeated," "a
paper bag full of steroid creams," "paralyzed by the fear of making it worse," "feels the safest
to me"). Those are 2026 recollections of a 2021-2024 experience, not contemporaneous record.

### On the from-scratch origin (_Call Her Daddy_, 2026-07-29)

- "I got diagnosed with an autoimmune disease and my eczema started flaring up really bad to the point where I genuinely felt crippled by it because I couldn't really go out. I couldn't use my hands. I couldn't brush my hair."
- "So then I kind of did so much research to the point where I was like, 'Okay, well, I guess I'll just have to make everything from scratch because that feels the safest to me.'"
- "The bob also came from that because I was like, I can't do my curls anymore because of all these health issues."

**Note:** in this interview she says only "an autoimmune disease." The word **lupus** appears in
her December 2024 Instagram post and in secondary coverage of it, not (per the repo transcript
notes) in the Cooper sitting.

### On the whisper and the work

- "I got to get this video up. I take no days off."
- "It's basically a one-man show, woman show."

### On the tradwife label

### On the eight months (_Call Her Daddy_)

- "I was still posting stories and being like, 'Today my kids were —' while internally I was like crumbling. Truly crumbling. And I was like zipping up my dresses being like, 'Okay, let's just go do it. Let's just take the meeting. Oh, next flight out to here.'"

## Enneagram hypothesis evidence

**The draft's hypothesis: Enneagram Type 8 (Challenger), 8w9, self-preservation dominant.** Treated
here as a hypothesis under test. No evidence below establishes a type; the Enneagram is a
classification framework, not a validated diagnostic instrument, and this packet does not assume
its constructs are real.

### Evidence consistent with Type 8

1. **[ATTRIBUTED CLAIM]** Gut-first certainty under maximum pressure, twice, self-reported: the
   pediatrician's silence and the hospital call. Both self-reported after the fact, so hindsight
   contamination cannot be excluded.
2. **[VERIFIED FACT + INTERPRETATION]** The named injury is categorization, not dislike: "we need to
   put women in very specific very narrow boxes in order to make sense of them." The quote is
   verified; reading it as an Eight's characteristic allergy is interpretation.
3. **[ATTRIBUTED CLAIM]** Non-negotiated autonomy: "it wasn't even a conversation where I was asking
   for permission."
4. **[ATTRIBUTED CLAIM]** Refusal of the servile task, stated publicly: "I don't touch the dishes."
5. **[VERIFIED FACT]** Concealment of vulnerability until resolved, three documented instances: a
   hidden third pregnancy, postpartum depersonalization withheld from her own midwife, and roughly
   eight months of a child's cancer disclosed only after treatment ended. The eight-month
   concealment and its stated reason are verified through her own July 2026 posts, independent of
   the podcast.
6. **[INTERPRETATION]** Self-preservation instinct read: the entire body of work concerns food,
   provision, the body, and what enters it; the origin was her own immune system.
7. **[INTERPRETATION]** 9 wing read: low unhurried register, single sustained rebuttal followed by
   withdrawal rather than escalation, "let them think what they're going to think."

### Evidence against Type 8, stated honestly

1. **[VERIFIED FACT]** The postpartum episode (depersonalization, "did I die?", months of intrusive
   unreality) reads on its face like an anxious-type presentation, not a textbook Eight.
2. **[VERIFIED FACT]** A second concealed collapse: the lupus flare period included stopping eating
   and a self-described depression. Two concealed collapses weaken a "hormonal one-off" defense of
   the Eight call.
3. **[ATTRIBUTED CLAIM]** She accepts a stated household rule in which her husband does all driving,
   on his stated preference that he dislikes seeing "girls driving a guy," and she endorses it
   publicly. A framework that explains her refusal to be filmed washing dishes should not also
   comfortably explain this. **[UNRESOLVED]** the draft itself leaves this unexplained; evaluators
   should decide whether that is intellectual honesty or an unfalsifiability problem.
4. **[VERIFIED FACT]** She works fluently within an aesthetic and an industry that reward
   image management, which is at minimum not evidence _for_ an Eight.

### Closest alternative: Type 3

**Supporting a Three read:** modeling since 14, a personal brand, self-described as "ambitious and
driven," high attunement to how she is read, image-professional skill set.

**Discriminating evidence the draft relies on:** (a) she escalated rather than adjusted under
backlash ("I don't think a trad wife would be cooking in like Schiaparelli"); (b) she declined the
audience's verdict rather than optimizing for it ("I've kind of detached myself from what the
public perception is"); (c) she declined a _flattering_ frame (the admired postpartum photo) rather
than accepting a win; (d) the eight-month tiebreaker, on the argument that a Three does not sit on
an unresolved setback that long.

**[UNRESOLVED]** Counter-consideration evaluators may press: (c) is also consistent with a Three's
image control (correcting an inaccurate image is still image work), and (d) assumes a specific
Three behavioral prediction that is not empirically established. The tiebreaker is an argument, not
