## Executive verdict

All six evaluators returned `trust: strained`, `value: high`, `delight: clear_hit`. That combination is
the accurate read. The piece has four or five genuinely first-rate moves — the lupus origin anchored to
a December 2024 date, the in-frame/out-of-frame table, the Wicker gap paragraph, the refusal to let the
type explain the driving rule, and the oatmeal close — and it fails almost entirely in one direction:
**it is more certain than the record permits, and the certainty always runs toward a cleaner thesis.**

Ten items are mandatory. Nine of them are corrections or subtractions; one is an operational action
that costs no words. The type call itself survives — the Enneagram evaluator independently verified the
three load-bearing motivational quotes through outlets other than the lost transcript — but the
argument the draft nominates as decisive does not survive, and the wing argument's stated evidence is
factually wrong.

Three adjudications went against the reviewers, and they matter more than the agreements:

1. **The medical specifics are mostly fine.** Three evaluators (CRITIC-B5, SUBJ-B2, and FAN's
   "precision mismatch") asked to strip chemotherapy and the five-days-in-sixteen cadence as
   transcript-only claims about a named minor. I checked. Mainstream coverage of the 1 July 2026
   disclosure carries the ER visits, the imaging, the biopsy, cancer confirmed and spread, immediate
   chemotherapy, and "sometimes spending five days in the hospital every 16 days, depending on her
   blood counts." The packet's limitation 3 recorded these as unverified; that was a limit of the
   packet's pass, not a fact about the record. **Only the hospital name is unsupported.** The repair
   shrinks from a blocker-sized excision to one proper noun.

2. **"Right after her birthday" is true and attached to the wrong event.** Four evaluators
   (FAN-R3, CRITIC-C8b, FUTURE-R2, SUBJ-C7) treated it as an unsourceable claim to cut. She actually
   said two separate things: _"Right after her birthday, she was in remission"_ and _"We finished her
   last treatment, we rang the bell."_ The draft welded the birthday to the bell. Cutting would delete
   a verified detail that, correctly attached, **lengthens the documented silence** — she reached
   remission around April 2026 and said nothing publicly until 1 July. The draft is currently
   under-claiming its own thesis because of a misreading.

3. **"She objected once" is wrong, and the fan caught it.** FAN-R1 was a lone blocker; it holds. In
   July 2025, a year before the Cooper sitting, she made the same argument on _On Purpose with Jay
   Shetty_, including the dishes/kids-dressed/hair line the draft treats as a Cooper revelation. The
   packet itself carries her line via Forbes and the draft never uses it: _"It's not even a rebrand
   I've said this for years on here and my other platforms and in press interviews."_ This error makes
   the draft concede more to Levings than the record requires and it falsifies the stated evidence for
   the 8w9 call.

The repair list is long, but the P0 set is roughly nine parts subtraction and correction to one part
addition. The real constraint is arithmetic: the body sits at 4,488 against a 4,500 ceiling, and the
mandatory set is net **word-positive by roughly 30-40**. This pass must cut before it adds. See the
Revision brief for named cut candidates.

### P0-02 — The Whimsy timeline: an age projected backwards, and remission welded to the wrong event

**Origins.** FAN-R2 + FAN-R3 (fan), CRITIC-C8 (critic), SUBJ-C7 (subject), UNFAM-C7 (unfamiliar),
FUTURE-R1 + FUTURE-R2 (future). Five perspectives.

**Location and passage.** L158, the article's first sentence: _"For about eight months, a two-year-old
named Whimsy Lou was in a hospital bed in New Haven…"_ And L407: _"Whimsy rang the bell in mid-2026,
right after her birthday."_

**Adjudicated problem.** Two defects, and the second is not what the reviewers thought.

_(a) The age._ Whimsy was born 8 April 2024. If treatment began around November 2025 she was nineteen
months old and turned two on 8 April 2026 — two years old for roughly the final three and a half months
of an eight-month window. Outlets say "2-year-old" because that was her age at the July 2026
disclosure; the draft's distinct move is projecting it backwards across the whole span. This is the
most-read sentence in the article and the arithmetic is public.

_(b) The bell._ Four evaluators graded this an unsourceable claim and recommended cutting it. **I
reject that repair.** She said two separate things on the record: _"Right after her birthday, she was
in remission,"_ and _"We finished her last treatment, we rang the bell. It was very emotional."_
Remission and end-of-treatment are different events. The draft attached the birthday to the bell, which
is what produces the contradiction with the eight-month window. Reattaching it to remission makes the
whole timeline close: treatment begins ~November 2025; she turns two on 8 April 2026; remission follows
shortly after; treatment continues to the bell; she discloses the diagnosis 1 July and confirms
remission 17 July. **The corrected version is better for the article** — she was in remission for
roughly three months and still said nothing.

**Evidence and confidence.** Birth date verified (packet S-09, S-10, not in dispute). The two-statement
split confirmed by my own check against post-episode coverage (E! News, AOL, Just Jared, IOL, all
29-30 July 2026). Confidence: high on (a); high on (b) as reattribution rather than cut.

**Minimum repair.** (a) _"Whimsy Lou was nineteen months old when it started"_ — three words, more
accurate and more affecting than "a toddler." (b) Split the two events at L407: remission came right
after her second birthday in April; the last treatment and the bell came later; the public heard
nothing until 1 July. Then re-check that "about eight months" still reconciles — it does.

**Expected reader benefit.** Removes a checkable error from the article's first sentence and an
internal contradiction at its emotional climax, and buys a stronger version of the article's own
central claim about release timing.

**At risk.** PROTECT-11 (the cold open) — change only the age clause; do not touch "None of them knew"
or "Both of those are the same move." The bell-ringing must remain the cancer section's closing beat.

**Acceptance test.** State treatment start, remission, bell-ringing, diagnosis disclosure, and remission
confirmation as five anchored points; all five reconcile with an 8 April 2024 birth and an ~8-month
window. No age is attached to a span that crosses a birthday.
