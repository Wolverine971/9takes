## Executive verdict

The draft's argument is sound and its best writing is genuinely distinctive. Six independent
perspectives, working without sight of each other, converged on the same five passages as the
article's value: the persona-gap reframe, the empathy turn, the social-worker refusal, the
Westwood-to-Telluride arc, and Skeete's sentence left broken. That is an unusually strong protected
core and nothing in this brief may damage it.

The failure is narrow and systematic. At roughly a dozen points where the record is thinner than the
sentence needs, the draft supplies the missing piece itself and supplies it in the direction that
flatters the thesis. The pilot's licence is placed before the project it was earned inside. Summit
Ice's documented founding motive is replaced with an implied unspoken one. A critic's quotation is
stripped of its named target and set immediately before a different, friendlier participant. A
congressman's complaint about autism is printed with Fielder's answer withheld. A precise year is
invented for the fandom's most-scrutinised episode. A legal outcome that never occurred is tabulated
as fact. Individually each is a clause. Collectively they form a pattern, and the evidence packet
grades most of them "contradicted," "overstated" or "unsourced" — meaning an editor working from the
packet alone can fix eleven of the twelve without new reporting.

Three findings I am not accepting as filed. The `inner-thought` block that the subject standpoint calls
a blocker is a house device used in 156 people drafts, mandated by the formula ledger as the "interior
beat," rendered with a thought-bubble icon rather than quotation marks, and caption-supported in
substance; the real version of that defect was already repaired in the second pass. The critic's lead
blocker — that the strongest adverse criticism is absent — identifies a real gap but rests on sources
the reviewer could not open, on a show nobody in this pipeline has watched, and on material the packet
does not carry at any entry; it is routed to research, with the one repair that needs no research
(narrowing the completeness framing) shipped as P1. And the fan's 737 finale and the unfamiliar

neither is in the packet.

band** — zero headroom. Every accepted addition must be paid for by a named cut. The P1 list is ranked
