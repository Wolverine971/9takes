## Bottom-line verdict

Type 5 is the right call and the draft argues for it better than anything else on the
open internet about this man. The honesty-as-overhead tiebreaker is real motivational
discrimination — it separates 5 from 8, 3, and 6 on the _justification_ a behavior is
given rather than on the behavior — and the stress-arrow paragraph refuses to resolve
when the evidence does not resolve. That is the two hardest things in typology done
correctly.

The problem is that the page's headline evidence is its weakest evidence. The
90-percent-fear self-report is a **head-triad** datum. It is equally consistent with 5,
6, and 7, and the draft's own Rabbit Hole concedes it is "the single most Six-shaped
datum in the record" in substance. The body handles this correctly two paragraphs
later. The FAQ — the field that gets extracted into structured data and answered
standalone — does not, and calls it "the clearest evidence." Second, the falsification
offer, which is the single most valuable paragraph on the page for a reader like me,
is aimed at the wrong alternative and closes on an absolute negative the research
never ran.

Third, and this is the largest missed opportunity rather than an error: the piece
argues a Five for 4,300 words without ever naming the Five's passion. Type 5's vice is
**avarice** and its virtue is **non-attachment** — both are in this repo's own
`enneagram-concepts.md` table and the Type 5 pillar. Naval's most famous doctrine,
"desire is a contract you make with yourself to be unhappy," _is_ the Five's virtue,
preached publicly by a man the page argues was formed by the passion. And the
avarice/publication tension — Fives are supposed to withhold, Naval gives everything
away — is the thesis's load-bearing weak joint, raised by the packet, never met on the
page. The corpus already has the answer: the sibling draft `Malcolm-Gladwell.md` names
it as "not greed for money, but the hoarding of _self_." Naval publishes his
understanding maximally and withholds himself completely. That resolution is one
paragraph away and it is worth more than any other change on this list.

Revise. The call survives; the argument for it needs its strongest card played and its
weakest card demoted.

## What landed

**ENN-H1 — the honesty tiebreaker.** Diagnosis section:

> "The tiebreaker sits somewhere quieter: how he justifies his own honesty. An Eight
> would defend blunt speech as strength. A Three would defend it as reputation. Naval
> defends it as overhead: when you withhold something, 'you've created a second thread
> in your head that then has to stay active…' He has described a memory leak in the
> register of ethics."

This must survive revision intact. It is the only passage on the page doing what the
Enneagram is actually for: three types produce the same visible behavior (blunt
honesty), and the type is read off the _reason given_, not the behavior. "A memory
leak in the register of ethics" also does the cognitive-conservation argument without
a single piece of system vocabulary. It is the thing I would send to another person
who types people, and it is the specific thing personality-database cannot do.

**ENN-H2 — triad first, then the shape of the fear.**

> "Type 5 sits in the Enneagram's head triad, where the organizing feeling is fear and
> the answer to it is information. What makes the Five specific is the shape of the
> fear: insufficiency… Shrink the demand. Want less, need less, spend less, and you
> can always meet the bill."

Correct theory, correctly sequenced, zero jargon, and the last three clauses are a
better statement of the Five's coping strategy than the house Type 5 pillar manages.
Preserve the sequence — triad, then differentiator — because it is exactly the move the
FAQ is missing.

**ENN-H3 — the stress arrow held open.**

> "The venture churn is the case for that arrow; the AngelList handoff is the case
> against it."

Certainty proportional to evidence, stated in one line, with the disconfirmer given
equal weight. Most typing content resolves this by fiat. Do not let a later pass
"tighten" this into a verdict.

**ENN-H4 — desire read as a budget, not a mood.**

> "That is a budget, written by a man who has run the numbers on himself… Wanting is a
> line item, and he has set a ceiling on it."

Motivation over behavior, argued in Naval's own vocabulary rather than the system's.
This is the need-minimization strategy of the Five without one word of Enneagram
terminology, and it is the reason the page reads as person-first.

**ENN-H5 — the empathy turn.**

> "Most of them are painfully aware of how much they do not know; the flat, condensed
> way they hand over what they do know reads as talking down when it was meant as an
> offering… He is doing the only thing that ever worked on him."

Explains the mocked behavior through motivation without excusing Brock's critique of
the form. The type lens earns its place here rather than decorating.

**ENN-H6 — naming the aggregators' emptiness.**

> "The personality aggregators split their MBTI votes across INFJ, ENFP, INTJ, and
> INTP. Four labels, one subject, zero reasoning published anywhere on those pages."

Correct, and it states the page's actual standard of value. Which is why the findings
below matter: the page has to meet the standard it just set.

## What missed

**The avarice question is never asked.** The single most obvious objection a Five-
literate reader raises against this subject is: _Fives hoard, and this man gave a
million-selling book away for free and has published continuously for twenty years._
The Rabbit Hole's "Counterarguments" section litigates 7, 3, and 6 — and skips the one
objection that comes from inside Type 5 itself. See ENN-C1.

**The FAQ answer publishes reasoning the body has already superseded.** See ENN-R1.

**The 5w6 case is dismissed on grounds the draft's own evidence contradicts.** See
ENN-C2.

**January 2005 is assigned two incompatible meanings.** The body reads it as the
formative injury driving twenty years of compensatory publishing; the Rabbit Hole
reads it as the growth arrow to Eight, "the cleanest example in his life." See ENN-C3.

**The Sovereign Child material is used for irony and never for typing.** It is the
richest type evidence in the piece and the page spends it on a biographical callback.
See ENN-I3.

  fear of helplessness/incapability/incompetence; desire for mastery and understanding.
  The draft substitutes "insufficiency," which is a fine gloss, but a reader who has

  Non-attachment is the whole Naval doctrine. This is free and the page walks past it.

  The draft names Type 8 as "the strongest objection" and then supplies a test that
  Type 8 passes comfortably.
- **The instinctual-subtype call to acknowledge its own evidence gap**, given that the
  packet's binding scope rules removed the entire domain of his intimate life.

## What surprised me

- **The decompression section genuinely earns the type argument structurally.** Taking
  the seventeen-word aphorism apart clause by clause and finding a biography under each
  one is a motivational autopsy, not a literary conceit. "Not a feeling. A document."
  is type reasoning in disguise. I did not expect a bespoke section to carry more
  diagnostic weight than the diagnosis section.
- **The evidence is ranked backwards.** The strongest discriminator (honesty-as-
  overhead) sits mid-paragraph in the body and appears in neither the TL;DR nor the
  FAQ. The weakest (fear self-report, which discriminates nothing below the triad)
  headlines all three surfaces.
- **The word "avarice" appears in this repo's Type 5 pillar, its concepts table, and a
  sibling people-draft, and nowhere on this page** — about a subject whose relationship
  to giving and withholding is the entire thesis.
- **Jarring:** "Fives are heavily overrepresented in tech," asserted flat, immediately
  after the second pass deliberately cut the corpus percentages that were its only
  support.
