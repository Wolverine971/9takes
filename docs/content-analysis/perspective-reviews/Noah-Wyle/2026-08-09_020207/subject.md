## Bottom-line verdict

Even if Noah Wyle rejected the Type 6 call outright, he would recognize himself in this article — and that is the highest bar the subject perspective can set. The psychology is built almost entirely from his own vocabulary ("competency mask," "I volunteer," "divorce with visitation rights," "profoundly sad"), his family is handled with dignity, his humor and career breadth survive the thesis, and the lawsuit section steelmans his accusers before defending him. The portrait is fair.

The trust strain comes from fidelity mechanics, not from the portrait. Two quote/fact handling errors would be the first things Wyle's own team flagged: the article puts "really good" in his mouth about *the arrest itself* when the sourced quote is about the crowd's cheer, and it calls the Crichton suit "a copyright case" when it is pled as breach of contract. Both are one-sentence repairs. Five further concerns — an unverifiable direct quote, two credit misattributions (phone ban, HRW "board"), a dated lawsuit posture, an understated directing nomination, and one flat assertion of his "deepest fear" — keep this at revise rather than pass. Nothing here requires new research; the evidence packet already contains every repair.

The sourced "really good" is about the ADAPT crowd's cheer as he was led away [S-20]; his statements about the arrest itself were "a good fight to fight and I was really glad I did it" [S-19]. As printed, he sounds flippant about handcuffs; the packet classifies this as headline-framing inherited from THR's compression (CLM-05).

- **Minimum viable repair:** Either quote what he actually said about the act — "a good fight to fight" — or re-anchor "really good" to its referent: the cheer from the activists as he was led away.
- **Expected benefit:** The section's cleanest fact-pattern (the two-Capitol-visits case file) no longer carries the piece's most checkable misquote.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Acceptance test:** Every quoted phrase in the 2012 beat can be matched to its sourced referent in S-19/S-20 without a frame shift.
