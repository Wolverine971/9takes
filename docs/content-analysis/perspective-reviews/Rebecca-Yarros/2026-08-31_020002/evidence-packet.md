## Identity and scope

Rebecca Yarros (b. April 13, 1981, Washington, D.C.) is the American author of the Empyrean
romantasy series (Fourth Wing, Iron Flame, Onyx Storm) and roughly twenty earlier contemporary
romances. She is a lifelong military dependent — both parents career Army officers, husband Jason
Yarros a retired Army Chief Warrant Officer 3 Apache pilot — and has publicly disclosed hypermobile
Ehlers-Danlos syndrome (hEDS). The article covers her because of a stacked 2026 catalyst runway
(Threshing Day anthology 2026-09-29, Peculiar Stars 2026-11-17, Prime Video series order May 2026)
and because the entity-gap audit found the interpretive/psychological slot on her SERP empty while
general biography is saturated.

The draft's thesis — that she is an Enneagram Type 6 whose imagination is a threat simulator and
whose romance-with-guaranteed-ending career monetizes certainty — is a **hypothesis**, not a fact,
regardless of how confidently the draft states it.

Packet knowledge cutoff: 2026-08-31. Three full primary transcripts live in this repository and
were used as the verification backbone; external claims were spot-checked via web search on
2026-08-31. Wayback Machine is unreachable from this environment (standing limitation), so
before/after web-state questions were settled by converging secondary sources.

Evaluator note on prior-artifact independence: the research file
(`docs/content-analysis/research/Rebecca-Yarros.md`) was written by the same pipeline that produced
the draft. This packet re-verified its load-bearing entries against transcripts and the live web
rather than trusting it; where the packet says "verified," that means independently re-checked, not
"the research file says so."

## First-person evidence

All quotes below were re-verified against the three repo transcripts unless a different source is

**Threat imagination and anxiety**

- "Can you explain to me why, you know, I constantly have to worry about a doorbell" — LoC
  transcript. Full context matters: Kwong asked how she approaches writing war; Yarros answered "I
  think I've never been to war, but my husband has many times and I live with the consequences of
  that... We've buried our friends... after being a milspouse for, you know, 22 years... It got to
  a point where I'm like, can you explain to me why..." The line is her recounting a question she
  had reached ("it got to a point where I'm like"), delivered on stage — it supports hypervigilance;
  the draft's framing that "her answer came out as a question" is a light dramatization of a
  recounted internal question. _Verified fact + interpretation note._
- Epigraph: "I will never not walk down the street and think, 'What's the worst thing I could make
  happen to you?'" — She Reads, Feb 2025. The published sentence is longer: "I'll always do both
  (contemporary and fantasy) because I will never not walk down the street and look at people and
  think 'What's the worst thing I could make happen to you?' And then how could I pull you through
  that with some romance and some hot sex and some character movement." The full version is about
  why she writes both genres and includes pulling the reader through to a good ending — context
  that can support either a "threat simulator" or a "craft process" reading. Evaluators should see
  the whole sentence. _Verified fact; elision noted._
- GMA fight-or-flight amnesia: "I remember locking my ankle... the only thing I remember out of the
  interview was... lifting my head and thinking my sister moved" (Book Hook transcript); the
  fight-or-flight framing and "where's my sister" variant are in the LoC transcript. _Verified._
- Onyx Storm: "It was the most dangerous time in my entire life" (Book Hook, verbatim). "I really
  thought this book was going to kill me" (She Reads, Feb 2025 — not re-verified verbatim, page
  confirmed to exist and cover this ground). _Verified / attributed._
- "I need to not be their tragedy. I need to be, you know, a source of their joy" — Book Hook
  transcript, verbatim (about her children). _Verified._

## Enneagram hypothesis evidence

The draft claims Type 6 (6w7, sp/so, counterphobic streak), with high confidence. This packet
records the evidence base; it does not endorse the call.

**Evidence FOR 6 (all first-person, transcript-verified unless noted):** doorbell hypervigilance;
threat-simulation description of her own imagination (She Reads — see elision note); stated
safety-first value hierarchy; HEA-as-contract framing of her genre; loyalty extremes (one man since
19; "fall in love over and over" only via romance because "he's the only guy"); institutional
ambivalence (loves the Army community, published a letter questioning its leadership, wrote a
fantasy kingdom whose sin is lying to its soldiers "with the absolute intent" of critique);
preparedness rituals (five pitches, color-coded plot boards, "I can see where my holes are");
documented anxiety (daily anxiety attacks while writing a character's anxiety — Book Hook;
fight-or-flight amnesia on GMA); avoidance of her own image; the teenage government-conspiracy
plague novel.

**Evidence AGAINST 6 / FOR alternatives:** record-shattering output, 20-novel apprenticeship,
five-pitches-when-one-was-asked, empire-building (classic Type 3 pattern); warmth and
audience-reading at signings (Type 2 case: "what are you looking for and how do I give it to you?"
— phrase present in Book Hook transcript). The draft's rebuttals: image-aversion and dissociation
during peak visibility are un-3-like; giving framed as duty/provisioning, not being-needed.
Evaluators should test whether the rebuttals are doing argumentative or evidential work.

**Closest alternative:** Type 3. **Discriminating evidence offered by the draft:** the $105K letter
(attachment-threat beats life-changing reward) — the underlying facts are transcript-verified; the
discriminating force is _interpretation_.

**What the type does not explain (draft concedes):** the velocity. Fourth Wing in ~2.5 months, a
book in 3 weeks, ~800K words in a year — the draft attributes fuel to self-disclosed ADHD
hyperfocus and the 7 wing. ADHD self-disclosure is transcript-verified (multiple venues).

**Theory claims used as bridges (interpretation, not fact):** 6→3 stress arrow ("Sixes under
sustained threat run at the Achiever's tempo"), 6→9 growth arrow ("positively wonderful" — quote
verified in Book Hook transcript, said about being back at her desk in hoodie and compression
socks), wing and subtype assignments. These are Enneagram-system conventions applied to her; no
independent evidence can verify an arrow.

**Note:** Yarros has never publicly stated an Enneagram type for herself. No self-typing exists to
confirm or contradict the hypothesis. _Unresolved by design._

## Disputes and unresolved questions

1. **Deployment count** — her phrasing ("four combat deployments," 22 years) vs. Task & Purpose
   2019 (wounded on deployment one, "agreed to deploy another four times" = five total). Possibly
   "four combat + one other" reconciles them; nothing settles it. Any conclusion must stay
   qualified; the draft's body avoids a number.
2. **"Cash grab" quote** — the pivot backlash is real and documented; this exact sentence is not.
   Conclusions resting on the quote's specific wording must stay qualified or be re-anchored.
3. **Fourth Wing publication date** — May 2, 2023 (book Wikipedia page, her own words) vs. April
   2023 (author Wikipedia bio, entity-gap packet). The draft says May; best evidence agrees.
4. **Erivo quotes** — both reach us secondhand (subject and podcast host); event video was not
   audited. The quotes' gist is corroborated by two independent recounters, but exact wording is
   not established.
5. **Parents' exact ranks / marriage date (2002-05-18) / "read romances at 11"** — carried from
   secondary profiles and the research file; not re-verified to primary sources. Low stakes.
6. **Book 4** — genuinely unannounced. Any characterization of the silence (boundary vs. burnout
   vs. marketing) is interpretation; the draft's "declined to schedule her own dread" reading is
   argument, not record.
7. **Whether the grind predates the fear** — the draft names this as its own falsifier ("What
   would change the call: evidence that the grind predates the fear"). The packet found no
   childhood/pre-marriage evidence of compulsive output (the high-school novel is a single data
   point that cuts both ways: early writing, and early catastrophe-theme). Unresolved.
8. **Self-typing** — no public statement by Yarros about the Enneagram exists in any source
   reviewed. The type call rests entirely on inference.
