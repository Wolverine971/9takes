### Robert Greene, 2018 — the single most load-bearing external quote in the draft

`[VERIFIED FACT — quote accuracy]` Verbatim from the transcript (S-03):

> "the first thing that I try and do in there's a chap in there what I call is people have a self
> opinion and what I mean by that is **you Ryan have an idea of yourself right and that idea of
> yourself is I'm very self-reliant I'm very disciplined I'm a good person I'm conscientious I don't
> I don't know what else is I'm a good father** they're mostly positive things but there'll be
> negative things in that opinion of itself you know I could be better at that it's how you think of
> yourself"

**Three context facts the draft does not surface, all material to how much weight this quote bears:**

1. `[VERIFIED FACT]` Greene is **illustrating a chapter concept** ("there's a chap in there what I
   call is people have a self opinion"), using the person across from him as a live example. Whether
   this is testimony _about Holiday specifically_ or a generic illustrative list is genuinely
   arguable. The draft calls it "the most useful thing anyone has said about him" and says Greene
   "seems not to have realized he was doing it" — both `[INTERPRETATION]`.

2. `[VERIFIED FACT]` **"I'm a good father" arrives immediately after Greene's filler "I don't I don't
   know what else is"** — i.e. after he audibly runs out of items. The draft's father section builds
   on the opposite reading: _"He put it last, which is where people tend to put the thing they are
   least willing to be wrong about."_ The transcript undercuts that inference. This is the packet's
   single sharpest challenge to a specific draft argument.

3. `[VERIFIED FACT]` Greene continues immediately with a contrast that the draft's "no achievement
   word" argument should be tested against: _"if you think of yourself as being extremely good with
   money and successful you'll buy a Mercedes but if you think of yourself as very conscientious and
   saintly you'll buy a Prius."_ Greene is drawing a generic taxonomy, and the conscientious/saintly
   pole is one of two stock options he had ready — which weakens, though does not destroy, the
   inference that _conscientious_ was uniquely reached for on Holiday's behalf.

The draft's claim "Greene has known him since he was nineteen" is `[VERIFIED FACT]` (S-01, S-04).
