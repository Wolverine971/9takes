## Bottom-line verdict

The profile survives the skeptical case that Sinek is primarily a gifted marketer who narrates ordinary business events through emotionally resonant frameworks.

## What landed

Survivorship bias, weak neuroscience, military criticism, and the millennials generalization are reader-visible. Success and eloquence are never used as proof of type.

## What missed

The article sometimes interprets care protocols as evidence of difficulty receiving care when they may simply demonstrate deliberate friendship.

## What I expected

Commercial incentives, contrary evidence, alternate types, and falsifiability. All are present.

## What surprised me

The 2005 crisis is stronger than the leadership slogans because it occurred when the external business story still looked successful.

## Red flags

No blocker. The article names the speculative boundary and includes critics by name or outlet.

## Specific improvements

Future revisions should distinguish transcript-verbatim claims from paraphrase and avoid treating the absence of a new book as permanent.

## Follow-on questions
