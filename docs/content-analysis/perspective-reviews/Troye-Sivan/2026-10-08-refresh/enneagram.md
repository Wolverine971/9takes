## Bottom-line verdict

Pass, with two non-blocking concerns. This is a responsible, readable Four hypothesis whose evidence is stronger for a recurring artistic practice than for a dominant personality motivation. The draft repeatedly acknowledges that difference. It does not treat sexuality, minority stress, introversion, artistry, loneliness or career success as sufficient typing evidence, and it gives professional craft and Type 3 real standing as alternatives. Type 9 remains open because the necessary ordinary-conflict evidence is missing.

The strongest achievement is the explanation of how imagined privacy makes disclosure possible. That insight remains useful even if Sivan is ultimately another type. Confidence in the artistic mechanism is appreciably higher than confidence in Four; the report does not independently confirm his type.

## What missed

The Three comparison mostly offers ambition, presentation and successful collaboration, while Four receives the more inward, sympathetic account of meaning. A Three can also value emotionally honest art. This modest asymmetry can make the preferred interpretation feel better evidenced than it is.
- **ENNEAGRAM-C2, concern:** The H3 “Troye Sivan is an Enneagram Type 4” has greater certainty than the paragraph immediately below it, which calls this an unconfirmed best-fit hypothesis. A scanning reader can encounter the categorical claim without its correction. The repeated qualifications elsewhere keep this from being a trust-breaking totalization, but the local mismatch is easy to remove.
