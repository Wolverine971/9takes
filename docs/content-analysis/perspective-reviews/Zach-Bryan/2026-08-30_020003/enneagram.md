## Bottom-line verdict

This is one of the stronger typing arguments this pipeline has produced. The Type 6 case is built
motivation-first, rests almost entirely on verified first-person evidence (the packet confirms the
load-bearing quotes verbatim), treats the strongest alternative seriously, names its own
falsifier, and keeps theory density low outside the Rabbit Hole. The 6w7 / social-with-
counterphobic-streak call is proportionate and — verified this pass — consistent with the site's
own published Type 6 teaching that phobic and counterphobic are coping strategies within one Six,
not separate types. No blocker. Four concerns, every one repairable with a sentence or two:
one certainty overclaim in the diagnosis section, an unaddressed alcohol confound at the causal-
engine claim, a stress-arrow paragraph that retrofits industry milestones, and the absence of any
reader-visible "what the type does not explain" beat. Recommendation: pass, with the four repairs
strongly urged before publish.

## What landed

**ENNEAGRAM-H1 — the paradox resolved at motivation level.** "Craving the structure and defying
the structure are usually treated as opposite personalities. In the Loyalist pattern they are the
same reflex under different levels of trust." This is the sentence that makes the piece an
Enneagram argument instead of a biography with a type sticker. It is accurate theory (the Six's
authority ambivalence) rendered without a single jargon term, and it resolves the intro's
"most anti-machine figure runs the biggest machine" hook rather than leaving it as color. Must
survive revision untouched.

**ENNEAGRAM-H2 — "It's hard to tell" as discriminating evidence.** The draft takes a verified
four-word answer (packet item 7: Rogan asks whether fans were happy or angry; Bryan cannot say)
and correctly identifies it as the highest-value first-person datum in the record: costly
altruism followed by inability to read the beneficiaries' intent. No rival reading covers this —
a One would know they were right, a Three would count the applause, an Eight wouldn't check. This
is the Six's intolerance of unreadable intent, illustrated without ever naming projection. The
best single evidentiary move in the draft.

**ENNEAGRAM-H3 — the machines-section close.** "He didn't leave the Navy's world of devotion. He
rebuilt it at stadium scale, with himself as the anxious master chief of the whole fleet."
Converts a catalog of fights into a claim of motivational continuity — the non-obvious insight
the lens exists to produce. It also earns the two-bottle ending.

**ENNEAGRAM-H4 — the alarm sentence.** "An alarm that never stops ringing will find a way to get
quiet." This one sentence takes the strongest piece of Type 4 counterevidence in the whole record
— "a consistent black hole in me always needing its void filled," which reads natively as a
Four's chronic lack — and gives it a 6-consistent mechanism (anxiety-quieting, not
identity-hunger). Without it, the Rabbit Hole's 4-rebuttal would be incomplete. Preserve.

**ENNEAGRAM-H5 — honest subtype handling.** The Rabbit Hole flags its own tension instead of
forcing the stack: "The counterphobic energy usually associated with the sexual six shows up in
bursts instead... He charges at fear in public, then processes the aftermath socially, through
confession to the group." The aftermath-processed-socially observation is a genuine discriminator
(it is what separates his blowups from a sexual Six's), and the handling matches the internal
type-6 page's teaching that most Sixes shift between phobic and counterphobic strategies by
domain. Non-dogmatic exactly where dogma was easiest.

**ENNEAGRAM-H6 — the growth arrow found in his own vocabulary.** The 6→9 integration claim is
anchored to his verbatim self-report: "I feel great. I feel content. I feel whole" — content and
whole being precisely the 9-direction language, arrived at after a slow solo ride and sobriety.
This is evidence meeting theory rather than theory being imposed on evidence, and it is the
quiet proof that the arrows section isn't decoration.

## What missed

**ENNEAGRAM-M1 — the 4-rebuttal answers only half the Four.** The Rabbit Hole counterargument
argues the affiliation-over-differentiation axis well ("Fours mine feeling to be singular, and
Bryan spends his interviews insisting on the opposite"). But the strongest Four datum is not
differentiation-seeking — it is the chronic-lack interior ("black hole... needing its void
filled," "perpetual discontent"). That datum is answered only implicitly, by the alarm sentence
(H4) two sections away in the sobriety section, and the Rabbit Hole never points at it. A
knowledgeable Four-advocate finishes the counterargument section still holding their best card.

**ENNEAGRAM-M2 — "what the type does not explain" never reaches the reader.** The draft's own
working notes concede "output pace unexplained by any type," and the packet lists three open
items (output volume, the talent itself, why the counterphobia targets institutions rather than
people). None of this appears in reader-visible text; the falsifier covers only
confessions-as-brand-strategy. As written, the 6 lens is permitted to explain everything it
touches, which is the monocausal shape the role contract tests for. Promoted to concern C4.

**ENNEAGRAM-M3 — a limit, not a fault: the doubt-process evidence isn't there.** The classic
Six discriminator — the inner committee, consultation before decisions, visible second-guessing —
is thin in the record. My own transcript grep (see Research log) found no material the draft
failed to use; "I called my dad" is essentially the whole file. The draft correctly built on
fear-narration, authority ambivalence, and the confession pattern instead. Worth recording so
the certainty ceiling stays understood: the case is strong on those three legs and cannot be
strengthened on this fourth one with current evidence.

## Red flags

**ENNEAGRAM-C1 (concern) — diagnosis-section certainty overclaim.**

- Passage: "A person driven by dominance defends the blowup. A person driven by image buries it.
  ... That behavior has one home on the Enneagram, and it is Type 6" (end of "What is Zach
  Bryan's personality type?").
- Problem: "A person driven by image buries it" is a contestable heuristic — fast, fulsome public
  apology is standard modern reputation management, so the Three-elimination as stated is weak;
  and a One's inner critic can also produce next-morning self-indictment ("I was an idiot"). The
  evidence base (an unfalsified confession pattern) does not support an exclusivity claim.
- Reader effect: the knowledgeable reader hits an absolute claim resting on a shaky elimination
  and marks down the calibration of the entire diagnosis.
- Minimum viable repair: import the Rabbit Hole's real discriminator into this sentence — the
  confessions arrive against interest, within hours, before any press cycle forces them — or
  soften "one home" to "its most natural home."
- Expected benefit: the diagnosis paragraph's strongest claim becomes defensible to its most
  skeptical competent reader.
- Confidence: high.
- Acceptance test: the paragraph either qualifies the exclusivity or names the
  against-interest/timing discriminator that actually rules out engineered apology.

**ENNEAGRAM-C2 (concern) — alcohol confound unaddressed at the causal-engine claim.**

- Passage: the engine paragraph closing "Why Zach Bryan keeps apologizing" ("Bryan's fights all
  detonate at the same trigger... The Enneagram calls this the Six's fight response to fear").
- Problem: every blowup the paragraph generalizes from (2022 Ticketmaster war, 2023 arrest and
  roadside dare) sits inside the drinking years the draft itself documents one section later; the
  packet explicitly lists alcohol as an alternative reading of the cycle (CLM-22). The draft
  juxtaposes the two threads but never addresses the confound where the causal claim is made.
- Reader effect: a careful reader supplies the deflationary explanation themselves and the
  engine claim loses force at its climax.
- Minimum viable repair: one clause acknowledging the overlap plus the fact that breaks it — the
  October 2025 DHS cycle, confession beat included, ran during early sobriety (packet timeline:
  sober from ~late Sept 2025; "Bad News" Oct 7–8). Verify the sequencing per Q2 before leaning
  on the blowup half; the confession half is safe.
- Expected benefit: converts the strongest alternative explanation into supporting evidence for
  the pattern's independence from the bottle.
- Confidence: high on the gap; medium on the exact sobriety start date.
- Acceptance test: the engine paragraph or the sobriety section names the drinking-years overlap
  and the pattern's persistence after sobriety.

**ENNEAGRAM-C3 (concern) — stress arrow retrofits industry milestones as stress behavior.**

- Passage: Rabbit Hole, Stress and Growth Arrows: "during the 2024-2025 breakup-and-feud period
  he chased the biggest deal, the biggest crowd record, and the biggest tour in the industry's
  history... Achievement stood in for the security he'd lost."
- Problem: the $350M deal was by all reporting label-initiated, and the record show and stadium
  tour were long-planned productions — "chased" asserts stress-driven agency on timing evidence
  alone. The verified stress-3 material is his interior report: achieving everything while
  privately drinking to quiet a discontent achievement never touched.
- Reader effect: an informed reader sees arrow theory bent to fit a calendar, which taxes the
  credibility of the otherwise-excellent growth-arrow claim beside it.
- Minimum viable repair: reframe from "he chased X" to "X failed to substitute" — achievement
  arriving at maximum volume while his own account says the discontent never let up.
- Expected benefit: the arrows section makes only claims his own statements support.
- Confidence: medium.
- Acceptance test: the paragraph no longer asserts he sought the milestones as a stress response;
  it asserts achievement failed to buy security, citing his own discontent language.

**ENNEAGRAM-C4 (concern) — no reader-visible "what the type does not explain."**

- Location: Rabbit Hole, end of the Counterarguments section (the natural home); currently the
  section ends at the confessions-as-brand falsifier.
- Problem: the draft's private working notes concede the output pace is unexplained by any type,
  and the packet lists talent and institution-targeting as open. The reader never sees any of
  it, so the 6 lens appears to explain everything it touches — the monocausal shape this
  perspective is contracted to flag.
- Reader effect: sophisticated readers distrust a lens with no stated residue; the draft's
  actual honesty stays invisible.
- Minimum viable repair: one or two sentences, e.g. naming that the type explains the fights and
  the apologies but not the talent, the 34-track output pace, or why the counterphobic charges
  aim at institutions rather than individuals.
- Expected benefit: inoculates the piece against the "Enneagram explains everything" critique at
  the cost of ~40 words (note the 4,431/4,500 lint ceiling — a matching trim is needed).
- Confidence: high.
- Acceptance test: the Rabbit Hole names at least two things the Type 6 hypothesis does not
  explain.
