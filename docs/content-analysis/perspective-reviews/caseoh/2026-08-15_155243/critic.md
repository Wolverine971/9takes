## Bottom-line verdict

The article withstands the obvious skeptical case: a successful creator can engineer “authentic” quietness, monetize body jokes, and preserve an Arkansas brand because it is profitable. It states the clip formula and copying accusation before advancing the personality interpretation.

## What landed

Commercial incentives are visible, the LosPollosTV accusation is quoted, and record-chasing is treated as real counterevidence. The article does not use awards or audience size as proof of type.

## What missed

Silence after an accusation has many explanations besides Type 9. “A game doesn't sulk, doesn't leave” is elegant but inferred, and the article occasionally presents the nervous-system explanation with more certainty than public evidence allows.

## What I expected

I expected incentives, contrary evidence, a falsifiable alternative, and source limitations. All are present, especially in the Rabbit Hole and sourcing note.

## What surprised me

The best evidence is the self-described cost of producing energy, not the silence or the family-friendly brand by themselves.

## Red flags

No P0 blocker. The article labels the overall exercise speculative and keeps commercial explanations in the reader-visible body.

## Specific improvements
