"Everyone is like, 'You're stepping into Woody's shoes,'" Joseph Zada told [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-features/joseph-zada-east-of-eden-hunger-games-james-dean-1236716673/) (THR) in a profile published October 1, 2026, the day Netflix released _East of Eden_. "No, I'm not. These are my characters and my movies — not James', not Woody's. I'm stepping into my own shoes."

James is James Dean, who made Cal Trask famous in the 1955 film of _East of Eden_. Zada plays Cal as a teenager, in the last two of the Netflix series' seven episodes, with Florence Pugh in the lead. Woody is Woody Harrelson, and Zada plays young Haymitch Abernathy in _The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping_, the prequel about the District 12 tribute who grows into the mentor Harrelson played. As of October 4, 2026, _Sunrise on the Reaping_ is scheduled for US theaters on November 20, 2026 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping)).

Those two releases are why people are searching for an Australian actor born in 2005. About the roles, he sounds sure. About the people, he is not: he told interviewers in July 2025 and again in June 2026 that actors he admired made him nervous. What settles those nerves is the question this profile cares about, and his record cannot answer it yet.

Almost every substantial source on Zada is an interview published between February 2025 and October 2026, inside the promotional windows of four projects. Nervousness, gratitude and "the cast became a family" are the house style of those formats, and a newcomer sharing a cast list with Glenn Close and Ralph Fiennes would produce them whatever his type. Even an early account from the _Invisible Boys_ set in February 2024 came with conditions: the [Sydney Morning Herald](https://www.smh.com.au/culture/tv-and-radio/i-still-had-fire-in-my-blood-the-anger-that-fuels-this-excellent-aussie-drama-20250210-p5lazd.html) (SMH) reporter had been told "not to get too personal" with the young cast. So this profile is an open case: a leading read held loosely, two live alternatives, and the signals that would decide between them.

## What is Joseph Zada's personality type?

Leading read: Type 6, held loosely, at low confidence. Type 3 is the live alternative and Type 9 is close behind. The fears he names are about being judged, which fits a Three. What settles them is more often people and preparation than results, which fits a Six or a Nine. His young record cannot yet separate them.

Nerves about roles this size would fit [Type 6](/enneagram-corner/enneagram-type-6), [Type 3](/enneagram-corner/enneagram-type-3) or [Type 9](/enneagram-corner/enneagram-type-9). In this nine-type model of core motivation, the difference lies in what the alarm is about and what settles it. A Six fears the ground giving way and settles once trusted people and structure are in place. A Three fears failing and settles through results. A Nine fears disruption and settles by tuning the pressure out. On those terms, his fears lean Three, his remedies lean Six or Nine, and nothing yet splits the Six from the Nine. For comparison, as of October 2026, 9takes reads [Timothée Chalamet](/personality-analysis/timothee-chalamet) and [Jennifer Lawrence](/personality-analysis/jennifer-lawrence) as Sixes and [Austin Butler](/personality-analysis/austin-butler) as a Nine.

## A brother in the room

Acting was the household business. His father, Jeremy Cumpston, has worked as a director, actor and doctor, and his mother, Jessica Brentnall, as a film producer ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada)). "It was never strange for us to act, or to be chucked into a short film for someone's friend," he told Elle Fanning, who plays Effie Trinket in _Sunrise on the Reaping_, for [Interview Magazine](https://www.interviewmagazine.com/film/joseph-zada-elle-fanning-hunger-games) in a conversation recorded June 12, 2026. Before he turned 14 he had played Toby in _Bilched_, a 2019 teen comedy his older brother Hal wrote and starred in and their father directed ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hal_Cumpston)).

The stage was another matter. According to THR, he was skipping so much school that his father set a condition for letting it continue: help out on a family friend's stage production of _Romeo and Juliet_. "I went into it kicking and screaming," Zada said. "I was not going to get on stage and embarrass myself." Then: "I would go to rehearsals trying to convince myself that I wasn't enjoying it. But the truth was, I was very much enjoying myself." THR puts him at 14. In a November 2025 [Casting Guild of Australia interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CMh6pJn6tGI) he put it at about 15 and said he "was quite insecure I think in that space and expressing myself that way."

"It only properly appealed to me at about 15, when I started helping my older brother with auditions when he'd come back from America," he told Fanning. "We'd work up to eight hours on auditions," Zada said. "We'd drive each other to the proper limits of a scene and try to do it 100 different ways just for fun." In an [October 2026 junket](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e9CkKjn15nc) he described about four years of auditioning with Hal "every day together" and called his brother's encouragement "just everything to me." He also credited his coach, Miranda Harcourt, who "taught me everything that I know." The figures are loose, but the habit is clear: he prepares with someone beside him.

Accounts of how school ended differ. THR reports that he kept "blowing off classes as he pursued auditions and small roles," was expelled from a private school and re-enrolled at a public one. In his last four weeks, the principal told him to attend every day or the school couldn't legally finish him, and he had a final callback the next week: "I just looked at my mum and I was like, 'I'm leaving.' And she just said, 'Yeah.'" On the _Invisible Boys_ set in February 2024, at 18, he told the SMH: "I got kicked out of both of my schools. I was sort of defiant."

"My original name is Cumpston, which my older brother still goes by," he told Fanning. Zada is his grandmother's surname, and he says he was thinking about switching to it "on my last callback" before his big break. "My grandmother's Australian Indigenous and also Afghan. The name comes from my great-grandfather, who was an Afghan cameleer." Sources disagree on which generation the cameleer belonged to. To THR he described a plan: "I'm convincing my siblings to change too. We all want to stick together as a family and make films together. It'd be awesome for us to have a production company under the same name." Of the name itself: "there's a power to it." The plan is brand-building as much as heritage, and it pictures what the audition sessions already were: family as a working unit.

## The tape with a drone in it

The Haymitch request reached him while he was shooting _East of Eden_ in New Zealand. They asked for one improvised scene, "a monologue to the camera," as he put it to THR. He told them, "I'll have it to you in three weeks," because "I had a plan to shoot the scenes properly and edit them." By THR's account he spent two weeks writing four scenes, then flew home to Sydney to shoot them with his brother and father. "We found different locations, I got costumes, we even got a drone," he said. To Fanning he put it differently: "I flew back to Australia from New Zealand just so I could work with my older brother. He helped me, and so did my dad and a couple of friends." Producer Nina Jacobson said in the casting announcement, "Jo prepared like crazy and stole our hearts." ([RUSSH, November 21, 2025](https://www.russh.com/who-is-joseph-zada/))

The tape is the record's clearest act of performing to win, and it was built around the person he prepares with. He shaped the performance to win, too. "I moved myself into an impression, hoping that that was what was going to push me over the line in terms of securing the role," he told [Vanity Fair](https://www.vanityfair.com/story/joseph-zada-interview) in October 2026. In the magazine's paraphrase, director Francis Lawrence encouraged him to find his own voice. THR places a further study after he signed: "For about two straight months, I became obsessed with getting Woody down." When shooting started, by THR's account, he set himself the reverse: "The challenge I set for myself was to be the opposite of Woody's character — make him happy, optimistic, caring — and then find a way to get to where he would eventually wind up."

The call came at 7 a.m. in Sydney in April 2025, after callbacks in Los Angeles. "I just started swearing, like, 'Now I have to do this and it's going to be really hard,'" he told Fanning. She laughed and asked, "You were more scared after finding out?" He said "Yes."

## Afraid of the people he admires

In July 2025, in Berlin preparing for _Sunrise on the Reaping_, he told his friend and _We Were Liars_ co-star Esther McGregor what had scared him about _East of Eden_ ([Behind The Blinds](https://www.behindtheblinds.be/love/sharing-the-ride)): "it wasn't so much the legacy of the story that scared me; it was the cast and the filmmakers. These are people I really admire. I was just afraid of how they would perceive me." Eleven months later Fanning asked whether any actors were on his bucket list. "I love the actors I love, but I'm shit scared of working with them." Fanning laughed. "I don't know how excited I'd be as opposed to deathly nervous."

His other named fears point the same way. Before _Invisible Boys_ premiered he told [Star Observer](https://www.starobserver.com.au/news/invisible-boys-cast-talk-sex-scenes-sexuality-and-being-seen/235479), "I was terrified I'd be dreadful! But I think it's come out all right." In April 2026, [Star Observer](https://www.starobserver.com.au/news/invisible-boys-star-joseph-zada-makes-hilarious-blunder-at-gq-awards/241443) reported that at the GQ Australia awards, where he was named Breakthrough Actor of the Year, he apologised "for his nervousness with public speaking." From "embarrass myself" as a young teenager to "deathly nervous" about his idols, each fear is about being judged or doing badly. That is the Three's material, though it is also every newcomer's.

After _East of Eden_ he told [Flaunt](https://www.flaunt.com/post/joseph-zada) his confidence had grown: "I just learned that these people are actually normal people and they're doing the same thing that I'm doing. I have it in me to go toe to toe with Florence [Pugh], I think. I feel like I can go toe to toe with anyone now." His confidence arrived once the people he feared had been reclassified as peers. The idols he had not yet worked with still frightened him in June 2026.

The most vivid example is also the shakiest. THR's profile says he sat beside Harrelson at a dinner party after the shoot and blanked: "I couldn't think of any questions. I forgot everything I wanted to ask him." In August 2026 he had told [Entertainment Weekly](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/articles/sunrise-reaping-stars-got-flowers-150000628.html) that they "spoke after shooting" and "I asked him a bunch of questions." In September he told [Entertainment Tonight](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6IsMsWONUDY) about a call in which "I had so many questions for him... But he really wanted to hear about me." These may be different encounters, but none of the sources says so. Release-week anecdotes drift, which is one more reason this case stays open.

## What settles him

His remedies are where the reads should split, and mostly do not. Counting only statements in which he ties a fix to nerves or pressure, preparation and a trusted team come up about four times, tuning out three or four, other people's competence or company five, and the reception or the finished result three.

Preparation and a trusted team come first. In Berlin, having admitted he was "phenomenally nervous," he described his fix: "I've been in prep mode. I got here early to work with the stunt team, the director, check out the costumes... And like every other project I've done, I think once I step on set and meet everyone, I'll be fine." Of the demanding audition, he told [Vanity Fair](https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/story/joseph-zada-profile-we-were-liars-the-hunger-games-east-of-eden) in June 2025, "Now I'm just so excited to know that I'm going to be in safe hands." That same week, asked by [MTV UK](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nmx7QHDxzBM) about joining the franchise, he said he was "really nervous but it's all right," and cited "the best team in the world backing in, an awesome cast."

Tuning out is almost as common. Of Harrelson during the shoot, he told Fanning: "For my nerves and for me to really attack it, I had to not think about that at all." Of learning "on a pretty big stage where there is a lot of noise," he told Flaunt: "I try to block it all out and stay in my own lane." His girlfriend, he told THR in October 2026, "helped me relax and not think about work so much."

Results and reception show up too. "I'm so nervous for it to come out but I saw it recently and it is fantastic," he told [Entertainment Tonight](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UDG0WMv53Rc) of _Sunrise_ on September 30, 2026. Asked by [TV Fanatic](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hgBfV1oFiT8) in June 2025 whether the story's following put pressure on him, he said the response to their casting was "so lovely" that he "struggled so hard to find a single bad" comment. "And so going into it, there's that sense of just, you know, ease around that."

The largest group fits both a Six and a Nine. Twice, fifteen months apart, he said admired co-stars stopped frightening him once their skill carried the work: "As much as acting with someone like Florence or Chris is terrifying, it's also so easy because they're so good at what they're doing that you just don't have to work as hard," he told Vanity Fair in June 2025, and on [TODAY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c00NcV0Un6M) in September 2026, "I was so nervous to begin with, but it makes your work so much easier." On the _Sunrise_ shoot in Spain, Mckenna Grace, who plays Maysilee Donner, made him come to dinner with her mother every night when he was too tired from stunts, and he thanked her for "being forced to spend family time so I wasn't chilling in my own head and going crazy by myself" ([HERO, March 23, 2026](https://hero-magazine.com/article/294797/inside-heroine-24-mckenna-grace-in-conversation-with-joseph-zada)).

His father's rule, as he told Vanity Fair in October 2026, fits all three readings: "You work as hard as you can, and then you let it go because there's nothing else that you can worry about." A rule handed down by a trusted authority sounds like a Six, "let it go" like a Nine, and "work as hard as you can" like a Three. Against the Three, people and preparation outnumber results. Against the Nine, the count is close to even, and the most common category, other people, fits both.

## Three reads, side by side

### Leading read: Type 6

The case for Six is the remedy record above plus a few phrases with a Six flavor. Since the casting, he told Vanity Fair in June 2025, he has had to be "a lot more careful about what I'm doing and saying, which is always a good thing. But it's a little scary sometimes." He kept to a request from Zoe Kazan, who created and ran _East of Eden_ and whose grandfather Elia Kazan directed the 1955 film, not to watch that film until the series wrapped: "I was so tempted throughout the entire shoot, but I felt like it was a bad omen." For the intimate scenes in _Invisible Boys_, he said, "we had rehearsals to map out every movement and make sure we both felt comfortable and safe."

Each of these is a single phrase in release-week press with an ordinary explanation, from media training to standard practice. He does not visibly test whether people can be trusted, and none of the fears he names is about losing support or footing.

### The live alternative: Type 3

The best case for Type 3 starts with appetite. His _We Were Liars_ co-star Emily Alyn Lind told People that during the Los Angeles callbacks he confided by FaceTime that he was on a short list, and "he's naming names that are really big that he's up against." Her verdict: "He just gets what he wants." ([THR, relaying People, June 26, 2025](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/joseph-zada-against-big-names-hunger-games-prequel-1236300634/)) Kazan told THR: "He could be the biggest movie star in the world if he decided that's what he wants to be." He told Vanity Fair that as a teenager he "became really hungry and obsessive about trying to just better my craft." Add the impression built to win, the four-scene tape, the evaluative fears and three reception-shaped remedies, and one motive covers a lot.

Against it, he disclaims approval repeatedly. Asked by Vanity Fair about the pressure of inheriting Haymitch, he said: "I don't know if this is a dangerous thing to say, but I don't really care... I don't want to create for someone else's opinion." On TODAY, Harrelson's recorded message warned that "it's okay when people tell you you're great. The big problem is when you start believing [it]." Zada answered that "my happiness comes from just working really hard and putting my best foot forward all the time." As of October 2026, Vanity Fair counted "less than two dozen photos spread out over three years" on his Instagram. Kazan's line is conditional, too, placing the open question in what he decides he wants. Disclaimers are what artists are expected to say, so these weaken the Three without ruling it out.

### Close behind: Type 9

When Fanning asked, "Who are you when you're not a character, Joe?" he answered: "I'm chilling." Flaunt's writer said his joking self-description as "extremely chill" was "all said in jest, but it's not untrue." Visiting the _Invisible Boys_ set in Perth in early 2024, [Variety Australia](https://au.variety.com/2025/tv/features/invisible-boys-shines-light-on-queer-stories-20235)'s Julie Hosking found him "remarkably upbeat for someone who has spent much of the past week in tears." He told her, "I don't tend to wear my emotions on my sleeve like Charlie does." The tuning-out remedies fit a Nine, and his remedy record does not separate Nine from Six.

What resists the Nine is intensity he names himself. Contrasting himself with Pugh on the _East of Eden_ set, he told Entertainment Tonight on September 30, 2026: "I'm intense you know I walk around in circles and I keep very much to myself and I play music." Of playing Johnny in _We Were Liars_, he told Vanity Fair in 2025, "I'm a little more reserved, but it was really fun to just force myself out of my comfort zone." The opposite-of-Woody challenge was self-set. He also qualified his own calm to Fanning: "There hasn't been a whole lot of chill until very recently. But yeah, Australia is a safe place." Immersion is also craft, and 9takes's own Austin Butler profile treats disappearing into roles as compatible with a Nine, so this is a lean, not a split.

## What would settle it

The next tests are dated. In October 2026, THR reported that he planned to move to Los Angeles early in 2027, and _Sunrise on the Reaping_ will put him through his first franchise release cycle. Four signals would separate the reads.

1. **What he credits after the release.** If he says the reception, the reviews or the box office got him through, the Three gains. If he names people and preparation he trusted, such as Lawrence, Hal or Harcourt, the Six gains. If he says he didn't read anything and took it day by day, the Nine gains. He already talks that way about acclaim: asked on TODAY whether he could soak in a place on Variety's actors-to-watch list, he said, "Not really. I'm not trying to."
2. **How he takes mixed reviews.** In June 2025 he said he had struggled to find a single bad comment about a casting, so he does read the reaction. A Three would answer criticism by pointing to the win, a Six would return to trusted people and prepare harder, and a Nine would say he never looked.
3. **Who picks the next role and city.** "My reps are eager for me to move," he told THR, but L.A. is also his own wish: "It's got literally everything I could possibly want." The biggest visible step, framed as one, favors the Three. Choosing vetted collaborators, such as the dramedy he told Vanity Fair he is writing with his brother, favors the Six. Letting his reps decide favors the Nine. Leaving the place he called "a safe place" in June 2026 already complicates the simplest Six picture.
4. **Testimony from outside the junket.** No collaborator has yet described what he did in one specific rattled moment. An account from Harcourt, Lawrence or Hal, for instance of the note to drop the Harrelson impression, would outweigh another round of press.

Whichever read wins, what the record already shows should stand: he claims the work as his own, admired people still rattle him, and when a big test comes he prepares for it with someone beside him.
