# Active blog queue portraits — 7 October 2026

Completed 17 portraits from the active queue, starting with Greg Brockman. All final subject pixels derive from the real photographs listed below. Processing uses Apple Vision foreground segmentation, measured and optically adjusted face placement, limited rotation where needed, grayscale/contrast correction, alpha-edge cleanup, and the unchanged repository eye-mark template. No generative reconstruction or synthetic upscaling is present in the final assets.

A Greg Brockman image-generation background-removal trial was rejected because it changed skin texture and introduced a halo. Its prompt and intermediate output are retained outside the production asset directory.

Each portrait has a 1080 × 1080 transparent PNG master, a 1080 × 1080 WebP, and a 480 × 480 WebP thumbnail. Greg's pair is installed in `static/types/3s/` for the existing unpublished draft; the other 16 pairs are staged in `output/portrait-preflight/2026-10-07-queue/` until their drafts provide an Enneagram type. Editorial queue statuses were not changed.

The image files contain a grayscale subject and violet mark on transparency. Theme backgrounds and muted tint are applied by the existing personality portrait CSS. No amber, type color, or theme background is baked into the files.
