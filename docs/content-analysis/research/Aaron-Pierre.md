## Timeline: facts relevant to the thesis

- **1994** — born in Brixton, London. Raised on a council estate in West Croydon, the eldest of three
  siblings. Pierre has described Jamaican, Curaçaoan, and Sierra Leonean family heritage.
- **Childhood** — wanted to become the fastest man in the world and idolized sprinter Maurice Greene.
  He responded to losing races by examining the experience and training harder.
- **About age 13 or 14** — joined a youth theater group and chose a _Moby-Dick_ production even though
  he worried theater might make adolescent social life harder. He later said he chose it because it
  gave him joy.
- **2016** — graduated from the London Academy of Music and Dramatic Art.
- **2017** — first television role in _Prime Suspect: Tennison_ and early professional screen work.
- **2018** — played Cassio in _Othello_ at Shakespeare's Globe opposite Mark Rylance. Barry Jenkins
  saw the production and later cast him in _The Underground Railroad_. Pierre had chosen the stage
  role over advice to take more visible television work.
- **2018-2020 period** — experienced serious financial hardship between jobs. He described being in
  debt, living on potatoes prepared in different ways, and preparing to apply for courier work before
  several acting offers arrived.
- **2021** — appeared as Caesar in Barry Jenkins's _The Underground Railroad_ and as Mid-Sized Sedan
  in M. Night Shyamalan's _Old_.
- **2022** — played Francis in _Brother_, earning Canadian Screen Award recognition; departed the
  planned _Blade_ project after its creative direction changed.
- **2023** — won gold in a Brazilian jiu-jitsu tournament after years of boxing and BJJ training.
- **2024** — starred as Terry Richmond in Jeremy Saulnier's _Rebel Ridge_; voiced Mufasa in Barry
  Jenkins's _Mufasa: The Lion King_; played Malcolm X in _Genius: MLK/X_. AP named him among its 2024
  breakthrough entertainers. _Rebel Ridge_ passed 30 million streams in its first three days,
  according to GQ's 2026 profile.
- **2025** — appeared in _The Morning Show_; his Jennifer Hudson Show Spirit Tunnel clip became a
  major viral moment. Public attention began centering his appearance as heavily as his work.
- **2026** — completed an eight-week _One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest_ run at the Old Vic. _Lanterns_,
  in which he plays John Stewart, premiered on HBO and HBO Max on 16 August. The current GQ cover
  profile appeared 3 August.
- **2027 announced work** — _Star Wars: Starfighter_ and _Man of Tomorrow_. Treat dates and titles as
  provisional until publication because release calendars can move.

## First-person evidence

### The work standard

**Backstage, 12 June 2025**

- Pierre said that when he boards the plane after wrapping a project, he wants to know he did his
  best and “earned my own respect.”
- His next sentence is the cleanest motive marker in the record: “Anything short of that doesn't sit
  well with me.”
- He chooses projects in three stages: filmmaker, story, role. The emphasis is on substance and the
  possibility of making a contribution rather than visibility alone.

Source: `https://www.backstage.com/magazine/article/aaron-pierre-rebel-ridge-interview-78846/`

**Square Mile, 2021**

- He said one audience member and one thousand deserve the same energy because each person committed
  time to attend.
- He described a high standard oriented toward truth, authenticity, and constant learning.
- While writing, he could produce one page and then spend the next day laboring over it. He called
  himself hypercritical and said he was trying to become more fluid.
- On his first professional set he began acting before the director called action. The director
  corrected him. Pierre said the error never happened again.
- He chose Langston Hughes's “April Rain Song” partly because it felt exact: no word too long or too
  short.

Source: `https://squaremile.com/culture/film-tv/aaron-pierre-actor-interview/`

### Size, voice, and impact

**Men's Health, November 2025**

- Pierre said young Black boys can be treated as men once they grow to adult size. He described
  becoming attentive to how his size and baritone might register with other people.
- He did not want to intimidate people and learned to gauge their response. This is strong evidence
  for the central contradiction, though it is not proof of an Enneagram type.
- He described the burden of taking everything onto himself and later learning that sharing the load
  is fundamentally human.

Source: `https://www.menshealth.com/entertainment/a69124795/aaron-pierre-lanterns-interview-2025/`

**GQ, 3 August 2026**

- When asked a casual “How's life?” question, he paused for several seconds to find an accurate word.
  He said he believes in the power of words and therefore speaks slowly.
- After an eight-week theater run, he was still testing the intonation and emphasis of one monologue.
  He described theater as a constant excavation whose bottom cannot be reached.
- After _Rebel Ridge_ made him recognizable, his first response was to stop going out. He later used
  a cap, sunglasses, and mask, then asked his younger sister for help building a way to navigate fame.
- His late grandmother's rule was “Nothing to lose, everything to gain.” He connected it to taking
  the Globe role when more visible television work had been recommended.
- He remembers the exact bus home after seeing _The Phantom Menace_ at age five. On the set of
  _Star Wars: Starfighter_, the fact that the mud was real made him speak faster with excitement.
- His son changed the stakes of his career. He said that on first holding him, acting “has to work”
  and that the roles requiring time away should build a legacy that can inspire him.
- Asked to identify himself, Pierre placed father, son, brother, grandson, and friend before actor.

Source: `https://www.gq.com/story/lanterns-aaron-pierre-gq-hype`

### Hardship and asking for help

**Men's Health, November 2025**

- During a period without acting work, he described himself as an actor who was unemployed and
  wondered whether he was delusional or committed.
- He was in debt and lived on potatoes cooked in seven different ways.
- Because people around him were also facing hardship, he did not want to be a burden. He isolated
  and disclosed the crisis to a trusted person only shortly before the situation changed.
- He applied for courier work. Multiple acting offers then arrived within a short window.
- He now speaks about learning that he does not need to put everything on his own back.
- He also said perfection is unobtainable while continuing to stress learning, improvement, and
  fine-tuning. This is important counterevidence to a simplistic perfectionist portrait.

### Training and discipline

**Men's Health, `Gym & Fridge`, 2025 video transcript pulled 2026-08-26**

- Pierre jokes that at 2 a.m. he opens the refrigerator, takes out self-discipline, and returns to
  bed. He immediately allows that discipline is not always tangible and tells viewers to give
  themselves grace because they are human.
- If he finds an exercise he is weak at, he can become absorbed in it until he improves.
- He works out in silence.
- His motivation list begins with his son and extends to family, partner, and friends. He wants to be
  the best version of himself for them.
- He said that he now communicates challenges with trusted loved ones and wishes he had learned to do
  that earlier.
- He discussed rehabilitating a distal-biceps repair before _Lanterns_. The injury is factual context,
  not an invitation to give fitness or medical advice.

Source: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eOKYTvMPuP4`

## Interpretation: Enneagram hypothesis

### Preliminary conclusion

**Type 1w9, probably self-preservation first. Confidence: medium-high.** This is a public-evidence
interpretation, not Pierre's confirmed type.

### Emotional thesis in Pierre's vocabulary

- **Fear:** giving less than the moment, role, or person deserves; allowing his weight, voice, need, or
  error to land carelessly on somebody else.
- **Desire:** to finish with work he can respect, make an authentic contribution, and honor the time
  people have trusted him with.
- **Protective strategy:** deliberate words, private preparation, physical control, correction after
  mistakes, and carrying difficulty without asking others to absorb it.
- **Growth task already present in his language:** accept that perfection cannot be reached, extend
  grace, share the load, and permit joy and help to coexist with standards.

### Evidence for Type 1

- “Earned my own respect” and “Anything short … doesn't sit well” name an internal quality test more
  directly than an image or applause test.
- The one-person audience receives the full effort because their time creates an obligation.
- Truth, authenticity, contribution, and precise language recur across years and interviewers.
- A first-set error becomes a permanent correction.
- He can spend a day revising one page and remains inside a monologue after the run ends.
- When weak at an exercise, he repeatedly works the weakness until it improves.
- He chose the Globe role over the more visible television path because it felt aligned with his
  deeper judgment.

### Why the 9 wing

- Low-volume speech, careful impact, preference for his own company, and reluctance to burden others
  soften the reforming edge.
- His most successful action role, Terry Richmond, made restraint visible, but the role itself is not
  evidence. The corroborating evidence is how collaborators describe Pierre off camera.
- A 9 wing fits the measured, contained presentation better than a more confrontational 2 wing.

### Likely instinct

Self-preservation is the best tentative reading because worry becomes preparation, disciplined
maintenance, privacy, and self-improvement. Social responsibility is also visible in how he talks
about community, representation, audiences, and legacy. There is too little intimate material to rank
the full stack with confidence.

### Connecting lines

- Under pressure, Type 1 can take on Type 4's sense of isolation and personal deficiency. Pierre's
  hidden financial crisis and his initial retreat after sudden fame resemble that pattern, though
  money stress and celebrity can produce the same behavior in any type.
- In growth, Type 1 can access Type 7's lightness and permission to enjoy what is here. His fast,
  delighted description of real _Star Wars_ mud and his increasing willingness to receive help are
  suggestive, not diagnostic.

### Strong alternatives

**Type 9:** The quiet delivery, self-minimizing, private life, gentle presence, and avoidance of being
a burden all fit. The tiebreaker is what happens after discomfort or error. Pierre repeatedly returns
to correction, exactness, and effort rather than disengagement. His stated objective is respect for
the quality of the contribution, not peace itself.

**Type 3:** The model-to-leading-man trajectory, training, franchise choices, and polished presence
can read as achievement and image management. The counterevidence is his willingness to choose stage
over higher visibility, the equal standard for one audience member, his discomfort with fame, and the
language of his own respect rather than recognition.

**Type 6:** Preparation, risk awareness, community loyalty, and asking trusted people for a toolkit
after fame fit. It remains a credible secondary reading. The available material more consistently
organizes preparation around correctness and contribution than security or reliable allegiance.

### What this type does not explain

- Racialized adultification and Pierre's learned management of size and voice have cultural and
  interpersonal causes that must not be collapsed into personality type.
- Financial secrecy can come from class pressure, masculinity, privacy, or circumstance.
- An actor's roles reveal what filmmakers cast and shape, not necessarily his core motivation.
- Love for family, privacy around a child, and disciplined exercise are not unique to any type.

### What would change the conclusion

- First-person evidence that his main aim is approval, winning, or visible status would strengthen
  Type 3.
- Evidence that he habitually lets preferences disappear to preserve connection would strengthen
  Type 9.
- Evidence that preparation centers on danger, trust, and certainty rather than quality would
  strengthen Type 6.
- A public self-typing should be reported but still evaluated against how Pierre defines the type.
