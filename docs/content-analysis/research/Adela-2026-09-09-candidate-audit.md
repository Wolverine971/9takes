[Vogue CS](https://www.vogue.cz/clanek/vogue-cs-in-english/tereza-patockova/the-baddest-bitches-ain-t-in-la-they-re-in-vogue-cover-star-of-the-september-issue-adela-by-michal-pudelka) supports accepting failed songs and describing the album as varied, without a grand manifesto. Use that narrower current account. Suggested replacement: **“She says the album had no grand manifesto; she wanted room for different sides of being a young woman.”** Alternatively, cut the sentence—the preceding sourced sentence already does useful work.

3. **Line 255 — “the costly protection of her own expression keeps Four close.”** The candidate demonstrates protection against disagreement, but does not identify a concrete cost paid for the relevant creative decision. Remove **“costly.”** Her defense of “Ain't in LA” is stronger evidence when the article does not convert eventual success into proof of sacrifice. [V Magazine](https://vmagazine.com/article/adela-checks-in/) supports the initial disagreement, live test, subsequent agreement, and Sennott's observation.

The later process retained executive decisions outside the immune top six; it was not an automatic pure-vote system. This is a procedural precision fix, not a reversal of the valid point about the late disclosure of fan voting. [Episode 5 transcript](https://www.springfieldspringfield.co.uk/view_episode_scripts.php?episode=s01e05&tv-show=pop-star-academy-katseye-2024).
