## Independent type challenge and writing recommendations

**Working conclusion:** Type 3 remains plausible, with Type 4 a serious alternative. Confidence should reflect the unusually strong career self-report but narrow public sample. Wing and instinct claims deserve more restraint than the core hypothesis.

| Hypothesis | What deserves weight | What would distinguish it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Type 3 | Repeated emotional investment in competence, progress, and managing reception | Does professional success function as the preferred remedy when value or identity feels threatened? The work-attention answer and moving-arrival problem are more useful than fame alone. |
| Type 4 | Authorship, unwanted feelings, cultural specificity, and resistance to an externally prescribed self | Does protecting a personally truthful expression outrank accomplishment? Choosing alienating material could support this; it cannot automatically be explained away as a clever route to distinction. |
| Type 1 | Correction, rigor, and an effort-based conception of fairness | Is the primary distress about being wrong or irresponsible even when unseen, rather than not measuring up? Ballet discipline by itself cannot decide. Present evidence does not establish an independent moral-perfection pattern. |
| Type 8 | Self-direction, bluntness, anger, and resistance to control | Is vulnerability itself intolerable, and does autonomy organize ordinary relationships? Professionally staged aggression cannot establish this. Cultural directness is an alternative explanation. |

Avoid treating a capable artist’s ordinary preparation as automatic Type 3 evidence. A document, deadlines, and a stage identity are common occupational tools. Their value here comes from her stated emotional relationship to them.

Do not turn the claim into an unfalsifiable loop: wanting approval cannot prove 3 while rejecting approval also automatically proves 3. Give the strongest counterexample its own weight, then explain why the repeated work-related language still tilts the reading.

Treat the album as a development that changes the argument, not a news append. The current draft’s repeated rank, proof, scoreboard, and applause vocabulary risks squeezing divergent evidence into one metaphor. Keep the distinctive bodily training and bedroom imagery, while replacing repetition with an observed decision or a concrete cost.

The invented interior narration about an empty Los Angeles day and knowing which future she was inside should either be clearly signaled as interpretation or replaced by reported language. Similarly, shame, the inability to receive applause, and automatic growth along an Enneagram connecting line are hypotheses, not observed facts.

Character construction deserves a nuanced boundary: performing an amplified self does not establish deceit, dissociation, or absence of a private identity. Use both the private/public distinction and her assertion of continuity.
