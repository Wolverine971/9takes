## Enneagram argument: recommended pressure test

- **Type 3:** Strongest available case is her repeated emphasis on doing the job well, achievement, scale, and effort directed toward a desired professional identity. Say “we read this as” or “appears consistent with.” Public ambition alone is not a core-fear test.
- **Type 4:** The current counterargument is a straw man: a Four can make plans, execute deadlines, and organize a visual world. The issue is whether authorship/identity or achievement/recognition is primary. Her descriptions of imposed group identity and satisfying self-expression should count against the preferred explanation, not automatically become proof of it. Do not write that a Four would wait passively for a true self to appear.
- **Type 8:** A rehearsed persona does not rule out an Eight; artists of any type design a performance. Her concern with power and autonomy is relevant. The available evidence is insufficient to determine whether self-protection/control is her underlying driver. Explain the relative weight of evidence instead of declaring “surface” and “underlying need” as known facts.
- **Wing:** Aesthetic specificity, sexuality, anger, outsiderhood, and sensitivity are not exclusive to a Four wing. A 3w4 call can remain tentative without claiming it explains every artistic choice.
- **Instinct:** Social-first is especially vulnerable to occupation bias. Rank, audience, and reputation are inherently present in a rising entertainer's interviews. Prefer unresolved subtype unless evidence extends meaningfully beyond career presentation.
- **Stress/growth:** The research still mentions depression as Type 9 stress evidence even though the old editor notes say it was removed from the article. Update the durable research so later stages do not restore it. Low mood after a difficult transition is not specific connecting-line evidence. Collaboration, friendship, and family support can illustrate useful practices; they do not demonstrate movement to Type 6. If retained, this should be an optional theoretical lens, not a firmer conclusion.
