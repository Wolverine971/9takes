## Preliminary type hypothesis (HYPOTHESIS, not a verdict)

**Leading: Type 6, 6w5.** Core motivation reads as security through non-revocability rather than competence through understanding.

Strongest supporting evidence:

- Open source framed explicitly as protection from a vendor "pausing your ability to do what you do" — the fear is having support withdrawn, not being incompetent.
- Sovereignty argued in literal defense terms ("independent defense systems").
- Alliance density: founds with two friends of a decade, hires friends first, wants "low ego" people, assembles France, the EU, Nvidia, ASML, Samsung. Sixes build coalitions.
- Systematic hype-puncturing: AGI, technosolutionism, competitor capex, and his own origin myth all get deflated. Doubt as a working method.
- Loyalty and stated debt to the French public system.
- Phobic presentation ("sage, poli, presque timide," "ne faisait jamais le malin") with counterphobic action in the market (raising €3B to take on the US labs directly).

**Strongest alternative: Type 5.** Academic lineage, capital-efficiency minimalism, privacy, deflationary self-concept, and the "electrician" competence frame all read Five. Three of the four other frontier-builders in the 9takes corpus (Amodei, Hassabis, Liang Wenfeng) are typed Five, and the pull toward that reading is strong.

**Named tiebreaker:** confronted with commoditization, he did not withdraw into a defensible niche of superior understanding and minimize capital exposure (Five). He raised the largest round in European history to **own** the physical layer and simultaneously gave the weights away so no one could revoke them. Ownership plus non-revocability is a security architecture, not a competence strategy.

**Disconfirming evidence (kept in view):** his scientific openness ("il partageait toujours publiquement le résultat de ses expériences") predates any strategic reason for it and may be inherited from the Inria/scikit-learn open-science culture rather than driven by type. And his willingness to hold a contrarian market position alone looks Five-ish; the Six reading requires the observation that he is not actually alone — he is the most alliance-dense founder in frontier AI.

**Confidence: medium-high.** Living subject, guarded private life, no long-form personal-history interview in English. The public record is deep on strategy and thin on interior life.

## Emotional thesis (hypothesis)

- **Core fear, his vocabulary:** that the thing you depend on can be switched off by someone whose terms you do not control — "a vendor that is from a foreign entity," "pausing your ability to do what you do," "two or three American companies control completely the way we access information."
- **Core desire:** ground that cannot be revoked. Weights you can run "on your own terms." Wires you own.
- **Formative message (verified, institutional rather than familial):** the French public system educated him end to end, he says so out loud and says he owes it — and then that same system turned him down for a permanent research post before offering it a year too late. The institution both raised him and failed to hold him.

## Unresolved question (candidate ending material)

He built the most valuable private company in European technology on the argument that what it makes is not special. If he turns out to be right about commoditization, Mistral wins. If he turns out to be wrong, the labs he warned about own the future outright. Either way the one position he has never taken is the one where the technology is a miracle — and that is the only position that would require him to trust someone else's word for it.
