## Contrary evidence (kept on the page)

- She told Wilson "no one's unique" and "there's a million more of you" and found it freeing. This cuts against a textbook Four. Named in the Rabbit Hole.
- Type 6 case: social anxiety, scanning reactions, catastrophic what-ifs about her daughters, testing powerful guests. Named in the Rabbit Hole as the strongest rival.
- Eric Schiffer (Insider, 2023) thought a talent booker was likelier than hustle. Kept, with the note that no booker has surfaced.
- Jenkins calls her "anti-religion"; her own account is church attendance after the divorce.
- Soul Boom says her parents shared the house until she was 14; on Not This Again she said they stayed in an unhappy home until she was 16. The page uses 14 (the more detailed account).
