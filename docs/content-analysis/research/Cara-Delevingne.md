## First-person quotations

### Call Her Daddy, 3 June 2026 (transcript via Singju Post)

Long-form podcast, 1h45m. Highest-value source in the record; nearly all of the below are unavailable
elsewhere.

**Childhood and her mother**

- "I didn't feel like I had a voice as a kid… there was a lot going on with my mum, who especially was very sick when I was growing up."
- "I thought she was dead and I didn't understand… I just would stop eating at 7 and just be on a hunger strike because I didn't know where my mother was."
- "I didn't have the tools to talk… I just held everything in to the point where I had a mental breakdown at 15."

**Not belonging**

- "I knew I was a little queer kid running around. I didn't feel like I belonged in my body, in my family, in any of it."
- "I really wanted to do the opposite… I preferred going to raves and breaking into festivals."

**The modelling image**

- "When I'd see a billboard of myself, it wasn't me… I'm a girl who wears a hoodie, smoking a joint… I'm not this."
- "It wasn't me. Like, it was me, but… what? It didn't make sense."
- "I don't believe what I'm selling, and what I'm selling is a lie."

**Injury and making pain visible**

- "I broke… I don't know how many bones in like a year. Because I was in pain inside, but I didn't know how to express it."
- "I broke my elbow… and being like, 'Oh, yay.' Like, I can finally have a cast and say, 'Ow, I'm hurting.'"
- "I didn't deserve to then do what I did to myself."

**Drugs**

- "I loved what drugs made me feel. I loved not having to think about my mum… all of that, it went away."
- "What drugs really gave me at that point was connection."
- "I was so good at hiding it… The damage was really being done alone because no one could stop me."
- "I knew it was a problem, and that was young… when I started doing them alone and how much I liked that."

**The turn**

- "I was so close to ending my life… A song came on… and it was like — that's the thing, when you're in that position where you can choose to try and end your life or not, it is such a quick flip." (A song that had played at the funeral of a friend who died of an overdose came on shuffle while she was alone in a hotel room.)
- "I threw all the drugs down the toilet."

**Early sobriety**

- "I was just so fragile… You feel like a newborn child, but who can technically talk and walk."
- "You are just a raw nerve."
- "It takes a strain on my relationship… the pressure I can put on myself is sometimes unmanageable."

**Love, sexuality, anger**

- "I couldn't love, I couldn't receive love, and I didn't love myself enough."
- "I didn't come out because… I didn't feel like I owed that to anyone either."
- "Harvey Weinstein called me and was like, 'You can't be gay.'"
- "I have a pit of anger in me because I never expressed it… you would die to save them and there's no reason for it."
- "There is this f***ing anger that you have that you're just angry against the world."

**Music**

- "I prayed to create something with this pain… I couldn't do it until I had gotten to this place of belief and self-love."
- "Every emotion that I was scared of, I now see as an amazing gift. And I never had that."

Also reported from this episode: drug use beginning around age 14, including buying and selling; ketamine,
GHB and Xanax named; first drinking recalled at age 8. (NZ Herald; Sportskeeda; Just Jared, Jun 2026.)

### Vogue, April 2023 cover story (reported 8 March 2023)

Interview conducted late January 2023; four months sober at publication.

- "I hadn't slept. I was not OK." (on the September 2022 airport images)
- "The way that I was living was not sustainable."
- "The opposite of addiction is connection, and I really found that in 12-step."
- "It was my first sober Christmas, my first sober New Year. And honestly it was the best I've ever had."
- "You don't recover, and that's okay… I prefer 'healing,' because I'm constantly doing it."
- "All I knew is if I was continuing to go down the road I was, I would either end up dead… or do something really, really stupid."
- "Treatment was the best thing. It was always something I was very scared of, but I think I needed that community. I needed that support group."
- "You can't run from the things in your life that happen, and that's all I ever wanted to do: just pretend they don't exist."
- "Your life can change, if you give yourself a chance to really be who you are and sit in that uncomfortability, because my god, it's uncomfortable!"

## Accomplishments, failures, controversies, counterevidence

**Accomplishments.** British Fashion Awards Model of the Year 2012 and 2014. Burberry, Chanel, and
a decade at the centre of the Instagram-model era. SAG nomination for _Only Murders in the Building_.
West End lead (Sally Bowles, _Cabaret_, 2024). Signed to Warner Records; debut album _Not Normal_,
25 Sep 2026; first headline tour, 11 cities, opening 1 Jun 2026, several dates sold out.

**Failures and controversies.**

- _Valerian_ and _Suicide Squad_ were critically punished; her Enchantress performance was widely panned.
- The September 2022 airport images drew global mockery before they drew concern.
- 2015 _Good Day Sacramento_ interview, in which she was accused on air of being ungrateful and told to
  take a nap; widely relitigated.
- June 2026: documented backlash to the music pivot. Fashion Times (9 Jun 2026) reports Reddit reactions
  including "She has no talents" and "I feel bad for her [because] it seems like music is her actual
  passion but her songs suck." Professional reviews (Atwood, Nylon, When The Horn Blows) were markedly
  more positive than the social reaction.

**Serious counterevidence to the Type 4 hypothesis.**

- Her own stated motive for drug use is escape, which is the Type 7 mechanism verbatim: "I loved not
  having to think about my mum… all of that, it went away," and "you can't run from the things in your
  life that happen, and that's all I ever wanted to do."
- Extremely high stimulation appetite: raves, festivals, Burning Man, breaking into events.
- The public affect for a decade was comic, loud, restless and gregarious, which is why crowd databases
  read 7w6.
- Career breadth (modelling, film, TV, theatre, music) reads as Type 7 option-keeping if you only look
  at the résumé.

## Hypotheses (not findings)

### Preliminary type hypothesis: Enneagram Type 4, wing 3, sexual-dominant instinct

**Case for.** The record is organised around identity and legibility rather than around appetite.
"I didn't feel like I belonged in my body, in my family, in any of it." "When I'd see a billboard of
myself, it wasn't me." "I don't believe what I'm selling, and what I'm selling is a lie." The injuries
episode is the strongest single item: breaking bones and welcoming the cast because the cast let her say
"Ow, I'm hurting" out loud. That is a bid to have an interior state witnessed, which is Type 4 territory.
The 3 wing accounts for the commercial fluency, the image literacy and the finished, shipped output.
The sexual-instinct dominance accounts for the anger ("a pit of anger… angry against the world"), the
combativeness, and the intensity focus that makes her read as louder and more extroverted than the
melancholic textbook Four. The 9takes pillar names 4w3 "The Aristocrat," whose shadow is _performing_
authenticity rather than embodying it. She is literally an aristocrat and that shadow is her stated
account of the modelling decade.

**Strongest alternative: Type 7 (crowd consensus 7w6).** See counterevidence above. The 7 case rests on
the escape motive, the stimulation appetite, and a decade of gregarious public affect.

**Named tiebreaker.** The broken bones. A Seven's relationship to pain is exit. She sought a cast — a
visible, socially legible token of injury she could point at. Alongside it, the psoriasis history: her
skin erupted specifically at Fashion Week, and she said she wanted work that did not make her skin
erupt. Both are the same move, involuntary or otherwise: interior distress forced into external form.

**One behaviour the type does not fully explain.** The sheer volume and duration of the substance use,
and the appetite for raves and festivals, is genuinely gluttonous in the Type 7 sense. The Four lens
explains why she needed the pain witnessed; it does not by itself explain a decade of chasing
stimulation. Honest reading: the escape layer is real and should be named on the page, not argued away.

**Confidence: high.**

**Disconfirming evidence that would change the call.** Sustained on-record framing of her own motive as
avoiding limitation or boredom rather than as absence of voice or identity; or a music/creative output
that turns out to be optimistic and options-focused rather than identity-focused. _Not Normal_ as a title
cuts the other way.

### Core tension (hypothesis)

Selling uninhibitedness as a product versus having no access to her own voice.

### Emotional thesis (hypothesis, in her vocabulary)

- **Core fear:** that the version of her people can see is a lie, and the one underneath has no way to be
  heard. "I don't believe what I'm selling, and what I'm selling is a lie."
- **Core desire:** to be witnessed accurately. "I can finally have a cast and say, 'Ow, I'm hurting.'"
- **Childhood message:** your distress does not get a voice, so hold it in. "I didn't feel like I had a
  voice as a kid." "I didn't have the tools to talk."
