## 6. Type hypothesis (labeled as hypothesis, not finding)

### Leading hypothesis: **Type 8, self-preservation dominant, 9 wing.** Confidence: high.

**Strongest case for 8**

1. The formative event is the literal 8 formation story: a drunk man forcing a door with a gun, and a 15-year-old holding the door from the other side. Her stated damage is not the gunshot; it is "**living at their whim**" — the exact language of the 8 core fear (being at the mercy of another's control).
2. Her response to every subsequent loss of control is to seize the controls. A director made her do fittings to see "how fuckable" she could be → she founded a production company. Financiers wanted "a hot lesbian movie" → she became a producer on her own Oscar vehicle. She could not be healthy in a relationship → she built a family without one.
3. She refuses to be pitied, which is the 8's sharpest tell: "people feel sorry for you… I'm good." And: "I don't want to have shame about it anymore."
4. Protection of the vulnerable, expressed institutionally: CTAOP's mandate explicitly includes gender-based violence.
5. Intensity registered as bodily cost: cracked molars from clenching, 50 lbs, torn intercostals at 51.

**Strongest case against 8 (the Type 3 read)**

The alter ego. A child who invents a "happy house" persona and maintains it through boarding school is doing image work, and image-as-survival is Type 3's home turf. Add three physical transformations, three Oscar nominations, and a modeling career, and 3 is a serious reading.

**Named tiebreaker**

A 3 refines the image; Theron detonates it. Her own words on the deglam accusation are not a 3's defense of craft, they are an 8's irritation at being handled: "It really irks me that people think I choose these roles because if I'm not looking attractive, they will take me seriously." And the origin story is anti-3 in kind: she was discovered **making a scene** at a bank teller, not being decorative in a room. A 3 would have charmed the teller.

**One behavior the type does not explain**

Staying in a relationship with a man who told her she was "the problem," and entering therapy to save _him_. Textbook 8s exit contempt early. Best available reading: this is the 8-to-5 stress line plus the childhood placating script running in the one arena — intimate partnership — where her armor never installed. She names the script herself: "walking on eggshells… I was doing the same thing that I did at home."

**Subtype:** self-preservation dominant. Naranjo's sp/8 is the survivor-provider: stamina, material self-sufficiency, low expressiveness relative to other 8s. Farm childhood, one-way ticket and $300, her own company, her own family, her own house that no man moves into. Social instinct second (CTAOP, UN, industry advocacy). Sexual instinct visibly last-ish — she has organized her life to not need it structurally.

**Wing: 9.** Evidence is the _stillness_, not the noise. Miller's "always the first one on set" and dancer's precision; her stated contentment in solitude; her explicit non-crusade about her mother ("she is who she is and I'm not here to change her"). An 8w7 would be seeking stimulation. Theron is seeking non-interference.

**Stress line (8 → 5):** withdrawal, secrecy, going into the fortress. Evidence: the closed-off high schooler; decades of not speaking about her father ("I couldn't quite honestly speak about it"); the _Tully_ depression where she could not exit the character.

**Growth line (8 → 2):** genuine openness and care. Evidence: therapy in her late 30s; the 2019 decision to tell the story publicly; two adoptions; CTAOP; and her willingness to say she was "emotionally and physically broken" on _Apex_ and stop.

### Emotional thesis (in her vocabulary)

- **Core fear:** waking up "not knowing what I was walking into." Being at someone else's whim.
- **Core desire:** "to live exactly how I want to live" — and never to run "every fucking thing by a guy."
- **Childhood message:** be the cute girl who is fine. Placate, hide, defend, shield, and smile, or the house turns.
