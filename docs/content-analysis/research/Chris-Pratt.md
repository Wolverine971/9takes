### The discriminator to test (3 vs 7)

What does he do with a bad feeling when no audience needs managing? A Three hides the failure and works harder on the image, because the threat is being seen as worthless. A Seven turns the pain into a good story or escapes into pleasure, because the threat is being trapped in pain. Positive reframing of a failure is the Seven defense. "Hid a failure behind a success story" is not Three evidence on its own: check what he did next.

He grew up with "total dreamers" and "no safety net." He followed a stranger to church after "Jesus told me to talk to you" (VF 2017).
