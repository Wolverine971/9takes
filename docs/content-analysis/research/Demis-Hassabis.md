## Dated timeline (relevant to thesis)

- 1976-07-27 — born North London. Father Costas: Greek Cypriot, aspiring singer-songwriter, at one point sold toys; mother Angela: Chinese Singaporean, worked retail (Wikipedia; brief).
- ~1980 (age 4) — learns chess watching father and uncle; child prodigy from age four (Wikipedia; Lex #475 line 503: "I've been doing that since I was four").
- ~1984 (age 8) — buys first computer (ZX Spectrum) with chess winnings; teaches himself programming (widely reported; BBC Science Focus, officechai).
- ~1987-88 (age 11-12) — Liechtenstein international tournament: ~8-10 hour marathon vs veteran Danish master; resigns; opponent shows the drawing/saving move; walks out and has the "are we wasting our minds?" epiphany (told on BBC Desert Island Discs w/ Kirsty Young, 2016; recap at qebarnet.co.uk).
- age 13 — chess master standard, Elo 2300; captained England junior teams (Wikipedia).
- ~1992 (age 15-16) — completes A-levels two years early; hired at Bullfrog after winning a Games programming competition; meets Peter Molyneux (Wikipedia; Time 2023).
- 1994 (age 17) — co-designs/lead-programs Theme Park; sold several million copies (Wikipedia).
- 1997 — graduates Queens' College Cambridge, double first, Computer Science (Wikipedia).
- 1998 — founds Elixir Studios. Republic: The Revolution (2003) underdelivers vs its grand simulation ambitions; Evil Genius (2004).
- 1998-2003 — Mind Sports Olympiad: World Pentamind Champion five times (1998, 1999, 2000, 2001, 2003); Decamentathlon champion twice; Diplomacy World Team Champion 2004, 4th at 2006 World Championship (Wikipedia).
- 2005-04 — Elixir closes. Statement: "It seems that today's games industry no longer has room for small independent developers wanting to work on innovative and original ideas." (PCGamesN 2016 retrospective.)
- 2009 — UCL PhD, cognitive neuroscience (hippocampus, imagination/memory). His PNAS paper (hippocampal-damage patients can't imagine future experiences) listed in Science's Top 10 Breakthroughs of 2007 while he was a PhD student (Wikipedia).
- 2010-08 — Singularity Summit, Thiel's house: one-minute chess gambit pitch (see quotes). Thiel invests £1.4M ($1.85M); DeepMind founded with Shane Legg + Mustafa Suleyman. Founding slogan: "Step one, solve intelligence. Step two, use it to solve everything else."
- 2014-01 — Google acquires DeepMind ~$650M. Conditions pushed by founders: ethics board, weapons ban. Mallaby's book: Hassabis was unenthusiastic about acquisition but accepted it to escape fundraising; rejected Facebook's higher offer believing Zuckerberg undervalued AI (LessWrong review of The Infinity Machine, 2026).
- 2015-12 — OpenAI founded; Mallaby book reports Hassabis felt betrayed that Musk/Hoffman drew on a DeepMind safety meeting in launching it (LessWrong review).
- 2016-03 — AlphaGo beats Lee Sedol 4-1 in Seoul; Move 37.
- 2019 — cofounder Mustafa Suleyman placed on leave amid complaints about his management style; later leaves for Google, then Inflection (2022), then Microsoft AI CEO (2024). Mallaby describes him as Hassabis's "estranged cofounder."
- 2020/2021 — AlphaFold2 wins CASP14; AlphaFold DB eventually releases 200M+ predicted protein structures free.
- 2023-04 — Google Brain + DeepMind merged under Hassabis; Gemini era; "wartime" (see quotes).
- 2024 — Nobel Prize in Chemistry (half shared with John Jumper; other half David Baker); knighthood; The Thinking Game documentary.
- 2026-03 — Sebastian Mallaby, _The Infinity Machine: Demis Hassabis, DeepMind, and the Quest for Superintelligence_ (Penguin Random House). NYT bestseller; 30+ hours of interviews.
- 2026-08-05 — steps down as Google DeepMind CEO → chair of Google DeepMind + chief scientist of Alphabet; still leads Isomorphic Labs. Koray Kavukcuoglu (CTO) runs daily ops as SVP reporting to Pichai. Context: Jeff Dean left Alphabet (founded Discovery Loop); Nobel co-laureate John Jumper joined Anthropic; Gemini flagship delays; morale reporting. Semafor: not pushed out, but "struggled to gain satisfaction" with business-focused duties (via Time 2026-08-06).
