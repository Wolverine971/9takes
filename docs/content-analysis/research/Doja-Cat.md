## Owner type decision (DJ, 2026-10-11): read first

**Doja Cat is Enneagram Type 9.** DJ reread her and decided the published Type 7 is wrong, then approved a retype and a full rebuild after the 2026-10-10 evidence pass agreed. Treat Type 9 as the type under test.

- Two alternatives need a serious answer on the page. **Type 6** is the strongest rival on this record (her 2025-26 interviews are full of fear, threat and safety language). **Type 7** is the old published type, so readers arriving from "Doja Cat type 7" need to see why it loses. Pick `alternative_type` on the evidence; the one not chosen still gets a paragraph.
- DJ's stated reasons, and how the evidence pass graded them:
  - "Very in tune with her body, good dancer": the weakest leg. The dance history is real (Q10), but her own words about her body are about disconnection: lifelong body dysmorphia, ignoring discomfort ("I will wear knives all over my body if it looks good"). Do not build the argument on it.
  - "Kind of lazy, a sloth": the wrong word and easy to attack (three finished songs a day, 10-12 hour streams, a rehearsal ethic Baz Luhrmann compared to Madonna's). She calls herself lazy once, about tutorials. What the record does show is sloth as the site's own Type 9 page defines it: self-forgetting, inertia toward her own agenda. Argue that, never "lazy."
  - "Not an intellectual booky reader": true ("I don't read and I wish I read"), but it does not separate a 7 from a 9.
  - "Very vibey in a way 7s are not": holds ("I like to doodle and dance and just get through my day").
- What carried the 9 reading in the evidence pass (each is sourced below):
  1. She goes along, then erupts (Q4, Q5, Q11). A 7 leaves when it stops being fun; she stayed through two albums she later called cash grabs.
  2. Her escape is stillness and comfort, not novelty (Q1). This is the site's own Nine-vs-Seven test.
  3. Little sign of a 7's future engine (Q3): things happen to her, and the future is "anxiety in a nutshell."
  4. Third-party childhood testimony that there "may not have been space for [her] feelings" at home (Q8). Use as testimony with limits, never as a causal wound.
  5. Under stress she gets afraid, not critical (Q6), which fits the 9's line to 6. The old page's reading of Paraguay as a 7 going to 1 does not hold.
- What cuts against 9 and must be confronted on the page: "I get bored very easily. It might be due to my ADHD"; "I love to go to fucking war with trolls… It's fun for me"; the Type 6 vocabulary; and the era-hopping the old page was built on.
- Site canon to stay consistent with (`src/blog/enneagram/enneagram-type-9.md`): sloth "doesn't mean lazy. It means self-forgetting"; "Sevens distract themselves with stimulation, new plans, positivity. They're restless. Nines numb out and withdraw. They're still."; arrows are 9 to 6 under stress and 9 to 3 in growth. A wing is optional. 9w8 is plausible; do not force it.
- If the evidence cannot support Type 9 at the release bar, hold with `insufficient_evidence` and say why. Do not keep 7 or switch to 6 on your own.
- Set `enneagram: 9` and rebuild title, `meta_title`, `persona_title`, description, FAQs and keywords around the new argument. The database type, portrait folder and the other posts that call her a Seven are handled outside the pipeline after an eligible release.
