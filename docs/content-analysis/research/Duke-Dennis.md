## Timeline (dated, thesis-relevant)

- **1994-02-26** — Born Denzel Shaquille Dennis, Greenville, South Carolina (Wikipedia). Childhood also in Georgia and Alabama. Raised by a single mother with two younger brothers. Neighborhood he calls "DeeBlock" / "D-Block" (Sans Souci / City Heights area per his own story time).
- **2010–2012** — Varsity football at Travelers Rest High School; received small-college offers and declined them ("I was done with football and I was tired of school" — army story time).
- **2013-12-31** — Ships out to Army basic training ("the day before New Year's... one of the saddest moments of my life"). Pact with a childhood friend: no plan after high school → enlist. "We shook on it."
- **2013–2017** — U.S. Army, four-year contract, stationed in Germany after basic/AIT.
- **2016-03-03** — Starts Twitch account while still enlisted (Wikipedia).
- **2016-10-29** — First YouTube upload while still enlisted. Channel was a gift from his friend Deo (had 3K subs vs. Duke's 100). The channel still uses Deo's email (360 With Speedy, 2024).
- **2017** — Discharged. Works 29 days at a plastics factory ("Berry's plastic"), 12-hour graveyard shifts; nearly falls asleep into the roller; walks out mid-shift and walks home (360 With Speedy, 2024). Goes full-time on NBA 2K YouTube.
- **2018** — Passes 100K subscribers (Wikipedia).
- **2018-02-06** — Army story-time video posted (upload_date in repo transcript metadata; earlier "~2019" estimate was wrong — second-pass correction 2026-08-07).
- **~2019** — Co-founds AMP (Any Means Possible) in October 2019 with Kai Cenat, Agent 00, Fanum, ImDavisss; Chrisnxtdoor makes six.
- **2023-08-04** — Kai Cenat's Union Square giveaway collapses into a riot; Duke and Agent 00 charged alongside Kai. **2024-05** — Manhattan DA drops charges in exchange for public apologies and restitution (Duke paid $1,049.50; Kai $55K) (Rolling Stone/AP/Tubefilter, May 2024).
- **2024-12** — Fan mails Duke a shirt honoring his late cousin Dee; Duke tears up opening it on stream, clip goes viral (TikTok/YouTube clips, Dec 2024). NOTE (verified 2026-08-07): cousin **Dee** (deceased, mourned on stream) and **Deo** (friend who gave him the YouTube channel, shouted out warmly in the Feb 2024 Complex interview with no mention of a death) appear to be DIFFERENT people — do not conflate.
- **2021 (late)** — Buys his mother a house — before buying his first chain ("I just wouldn't feel right" — 360 With Speedy, 2024).
- **2023** — AMP wins "Lifestyle Creators of the Year" (Streamy Awards). Duke nominated in Sports category, 13th Streamys.
- **2024-02-01** — First sit-down interview ever: 360 With Speedy (Complex). Speedy: "you don't have many interviews out there... I couldn't really find anything like that." Duke: "I think this is my first one."
- **2024-08** — Rolling Stone ranks him **#5 most influential creator of 2024**. On stream, Duke's reaction is to protest IShowSpeed's exclusion: "Where is Speed bruh? Like why was Speed not one, two, three, four, or five?" (Sportskeeda/Dexerto, Aug 2024).
- **2024** — Complex ranks him 4th of the 25 best streamers. AMP wins "Best Content Organization" (Streamer Awards). AMP Freshman Cypher.
- **2024-11-11** — Kai Cenat calls Duke live on stream to thank him for his service on Veterans Day (viral clip, Mafiathon 2 era).
- **2025-02-25** — Streams his 31st birthday; Kai Cenat gifts him rare Freddy Krueger Nike Dunks (The Shade Room).
- **2025 (May)** — Part of Kai Cenat's inaugural Streamer University.
- **2025 (Nov)** — India Love publicly discusses their flirty on-stream dynamic on 360 With Speedy: "We're friends but we can get flirty and playful... it's for the entertainment of Twitch. They love it, they eat it up."
- **2026-07-06** — Kai Cenat announces Streamer University 2026 professors during comeback stream; Duke returns as a professor (Wikipedia: teaching personal branding; staff page: "the art of interacting with an audience during unscripted and live broadcasts").
- **Current scale** — ~3.5M YouTube subscribers across channels, ~232M views; ~3.3M Twitch followers (Wikipedia, 2026).

## Hypotheses (interpretation — clearly labeled)

**Preliminary type: Enneagram 8, wing 9, self-preservation-leaning with a strong social/protector streak.**

Core tension: **command vs. being claimed** — the most self-directed man in streaming is famous for things other people named (rizz, Unc, aura, even "Duke").

Emotional thesis (his vocabulary): fear = the bus, the clock, the machine — anything that decides for him ("told when to eat, sleep, when to go to work"; the factory roller). Desire = "slept-on freedoms" — waking up owing no one obedience. Childhood message: "Stop being a [expletive]. Man up, do what you gotta do" — feelings get you nothing; handling it does.

Strongest alternative: **Type 3** (brand mastery, physique, blueprint). Disconfirming: seven years refusing sit-down interviews; shelving finished videos; deflecting the rizz brand instead of monetizing it harder; celebrating #5 by protesting a friend's exclusion; "respected over liked" pattern throughout.
Second alternative: **Type 6** (loyal sealed circle, military, punctuality). Disconfirming: no anxiety signature; distrust is of authority itself, not of abandonment; decisions are instant and unhedged (walked out of the factory mid-shift; told the first sergeant to his face).

Named tiebreaker: "I just don't like people telling me what to do" — stated three separate ways in one story-time video, echoed five years later in the 360 interview ("be your own boss"; "when you put somebody above you, you going to stay there forever").

What the type does NOT explain: staying the full four-year contract after openly asking to leave (read here as the mother's "man up" message + completion stubbornness, not type-typical submission). Flag for perspective review.

Confidence: **high** (behavior + self-report + third-party converge).
