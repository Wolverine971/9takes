## Findings for the refresh

- **Correct the event date to September 6, 2026.** The surprise Telluride screening was Sunday night, September 6. September 7 is the date on much of the subsequent coverage; it should not replace the screening date. TheWrap's advance announcement is itself dated September 6 at 5:40 p.m. Its eyewitness follow-up, dated September 7, describes the preceding Sunday screening. See sources D3–D4.
- **October is officially announced; an exact day is not verified.** A24's verified YouTube teaser description says the film is coming to theaters in October. Its dedicated film page still displays **2026 (TBD)**. Describe it as an announced October release, checked September 9. Do not claim that A24 has supplied no window; do not promote the October 16 date from one secondary report to a settled official date. D1–D4.
- **Direct-name search interest is independently verified.** Google Trends' U.S. Trending Now page, with “Past 7 days” selected and the direct name filtered, displayed an active Elizabeth Holmes cluster with documentary-related queries. Exact scope and observation below. This is editorial timing evidence, not personality evidence. D5.
- **The new material complicates a confident Type 3 reading.** The teaser provides evidence of how Holmes presents herself in a selected exchange. It does not establish why she behaves that way, whether she believes her account, or whether filmmakers' access reveals an unmediated “real Holmes.” The useful question concerns her repeated insistence on authenticity and the uncertainty surrounding it; the film should not serve as a fresh diagnosis or proof of deception. D1–D2, D6–D8.
