## Fix these first

### 1. Distinguish evidence against a caricature from evidence against Type 3

Lines 230–234 say uncertain exposure through a documentary complicates the Type 3 case; lines 238–242 give useful prison work a similar job; lines 269–274 propose accepting obscurity or admitting wrongdoing as evidence against the interpretation. These passages blur three different questions: whether Holmes is Type 3, whether her conduct is unhealthy, and whether everything she does is calculated to win admiration.

An invitation to filmmakers can undermine certainty that someone controls every presentation. Ordinary useful work can undermine a claim that someone has no motives beyond admiration. Neither, on its own, is a strong reason to prefer another Enneagram type. A healthier Three could accept correction and share credit. Otherwise, the article makes wrongdoing evidence for Three and improvement evidence against it.

Concrete fix: change the conclusion of the documentary paragraph to say it complicates a portrait of total calculation. Change the prison paragraph to say useful work leaves room for ordinary contribution. In the rabbit hole, state that those behaviors would weaken the account of defensive image protection; reserve actual type alternatives for different recurring motivations supported by evidence. Preserve the stated moderate confidence rather than manufacturing a stronger alternate typing from the same ambiguous record.

### 2. Stop relying on the 2023 profile as an uncontested admission

Lines 156–158, 218 and FAQ 79 depend on Amy Chozick's profile, with Uproxx standing in for the original. The independent documentary memo flags reporting that Holmes and Evans dispute aspects of how she was quoted, while The Times stands by the piece. The draft omits that material qualification even though it spends substantial space on less concrete cautions.

Concrete fix: use “Chozick reported that Holmes described…” rather than presenting an unqualified admission. Link the original profile if accessible and accurately describe any secondary route. If including the newer dispute, attribute it to the reporting and say what is actually known: aspects of the profile are disputed; the research does not identify a retraction or establish that this particular sentence is false. Apply the same treatment to the FAQ. Do not turn the dispute into another long meditation on unknowable authenticity.

Research limit: the documentary memo's New York Times access was a search extract, not a full canonical-page read. That qualification belongs in the research record. This review does not independently certify the exact disputed wording.

### 3. Put a concrete instance of deception near the thesis

The first third describes identity, shame, costume, attention and greatness before the reader sees a precise misrepresentation. The sentence “Its claims outran its technology” can sound like an ordinary failed startup until much later. The investor verdict helps, but the article needs an example of the conduct that distinguishes this case.

The logo episode already fits the spine and has stronger available sourcing than the draft uses. The amended Ninth Circuit opinion, printed page 15, recounts Holmes's admission that she personally added pharmaceutical-company logos to favorable reports. It also recounts company representatives' testimony that they had neither independently validated the technology nor authorized their logos. That is a direct instance of borrowing credibility, not just a general observation about impressive boards. [Amended appeals opinion](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2025/12/22/23-1167.pdf)

Concrete fix: expand the logo example by one or two factual sentences and cut the surrounding duplicate explanations at lines 166, 170–172 and 176. Consider briefly previewing it in the opening. Attribute the legal record precisely; do not add a speculative motive to make it sound more dramatic.

The current link labeled “SEC announcement and complaint” leads only to the announcement, which does not contain the logo detail. The actual complaint's paragraphs 50–51 supply that allegation and its investor-binder context. Add the direct link if retaining the civil-complaint formulation. [SEC complaint](https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/complaints/2018/comp-pr2018-41-theranos-holmes.pdf)

### 4. Restore the complication within the prison interview itself

The prison passage gives a useful account of modest work, then asks what responsibility might look like in the future. The same interview contains a more immediate tension: Holmes continued to maintain her innocence while describing changes in her life. Include that as her position, without treating a refusal to confess as proof of personality type or using it to invalidate any helpful work. The juxtaposition is more informative than the draft's sequence of hypothetical possibilities. [PEOPLE interview, February 12, 2025](https://people.com/elizabeth-holmes-breaks-her-silence-in-first-interview-from-prison-it-s-been-hell-and-torture-exclusive-8789737)

Also keep the evidence limit: a reported role in a prison profile is not independent proof of sustained rehabilitation. The existing conditional wording is appropriate; it does not need several further disclaimers.
