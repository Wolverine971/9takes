## THE KEY FIND (chronology the old draft missed)

- **Oct 4, 2022** — Gigi publicly calls Kanye West "a bully and a joke" defending Vogue editor Gabriella Karefa-Johnson.
- **Oct 14, 2022** — Gigi sits across from _that same woman_ at Vogue's Forces of Fashion and says "I have imposter syndrome all the time."

Ten days apart. Total protection available for someone else; none for herself. This is the signature
detail the rebuild is built around.
