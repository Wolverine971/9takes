## EVIDENCE (facts, quotes, testimony — no interpretation)

### Timeline

- 1988-09-20 — born in Sildi, Dagestan (Avar family). Father Abdulmanap: military veteran, USSR-era champion grappler, master coach who trained village kids in the converted ground floor / basement gym of the family home.
- 1997-09-23 — bear-wrestling footage filmed, three days after Khabib's 9th birthday (timestamp on video; Bleacher Report, Deadspin). Real footage; the "trained by wrestling bears" framing is mythologized — treat as one afternoon, not a program.
- 2001 — family moves to Makhachkala.
- Teens — competes across freestyle wrestling, judo, combat sambo; twice combat sambo world champion. Street fights ages ~13-20 (his account, PBD).
- Late 2011 — signs with UFC, among the first Russians on the roster ("it was almost impossible to sign with the UFC, someone from Russia" — Hotboxin').
- 2013-05-25 — UFC 160: sets still-standing UFC single-fight takedown record vs Abel Trujillo — 21 takedowns landed on 27 attempts (FightMetric via Bleacher Report/Yahoo).
- 2018-04 — McGregor attacks the fighter bus at UFC 223 media day (dolly through window).
- 2018-10-06 — UFC 229: submits McGregor R4 (neck crank); immediately vaults the cage at Dillon Danis (McGregor's cornerman); brawl; NSAC fines/suspends.
- 2020-07-03 — Abdulmanap dies at 57, heart condition with COVID-19 complications (BBC).
- 2020-10-24 — UFC 254 (Abu Dhabi): submits Justin Gaethje (triangle, R2), fighting on a broken foot/toes injured ~3 weeks out and concealed; weeps in the octagon; announces retirement at 29-0, age 32.
- 2023-01 — announces full exit from MMA including coaching, "to focus on family" (date/wording to verify — see unresolved).
- 2024 (reported ~UFC 302, June) — back cornering Islam Makhachev (verify exact return event before asserting).
- 2025-01 — removed from a Frontier Airlines flight in an exit-row dispute (widely covered; verify details before using).
- 2026-08-02 — Bloody Elbow: nephew Usman Nurmagomedov (22-0, Bellator/PFL champion) is MMA's hottest free agent; Khabib publicly fronting negotiations.
- 2026-08-08 — Miftaah Institute event (New Jersey): says he, Islam Makhachev, and Usman Nurmagomedov lost sponsorships/contracts over pro-Palestine statements (Al Jazeera clip; Free Press Kashmir Aug 11).

## INTERPRETATION (hypotheses — not facts)

### Preliminary core tension

**Total control vs. total obedience.** The most controlling fighter of his era — a man whose style was the systematic removal of another man's choices — never held, and never wanted, final authority over his own life. Father, faith, word, mother: every big decision was surrendered upward, on purpose, and he calls that strength.

### Emotional thesis (his vocabulary)

- **Core fear, his shape:** softness as leak — "love make your heart weak." A life without the rules that hold him ("America — too much freedom. It have to be always rules"). Deepest form: doing it alone — "without father."
- **Core desire, his shape:** to be strong inside a structure someone he trusts built — "when I see how father believe me... this is giving me crazy energy."
- **Childhood message:** strength is how we say love here. The father never said "I love you" after 15; he said "bite him back."

### Preliminary type hypothesis: Enneagram 8, 9 wing (8w9) — confidence: high

For: territory control, testing reflex ("not good idea to joke with me"), war-framing, justice trigger (religion/country/father → fence jump), contempt for hidden games vs respect for honest force (despised "it's only business"), gut-body processing, the 8w9 signature (quiet, heavy, few words landing like verdicts; "do not mistake calm for permission").

Strongest alternative: **Type 6** (loyalty, duty, rules-seeking, tradition, promise-keeping, tight in-group). Tiebreaker against 6: no anxiety signal anywhere on record — he walks into hostile arenas without a tremor, negotiates with Dana White as an equal, and holds a five-year retirement against $100M pulls without wobble. His relationship to authority is allegiance given from strength, not reassurance sought from fear. A 6 keeps the promise to quiet the dread; Khabib keeps it because a broken word would make him smaller than any opponent ever could ("if I give my word, I have to follow this").

Behavior the type does NOT explain: voluntary, comfortable, total dependence on his father into adulthood (shy to ask for money at 18-19; "okay — because I don't have choice"). Eights typically claw for autonomy early. Candidate explanations (rabbit-hole material): 9-wing merging with a trusted strong other; social-subtype group-first wiring; Dagestani father-son hierarchy as the cultural container. Flag honestly, don't paper over.

### Type-challenge memo (pre-write, required)
