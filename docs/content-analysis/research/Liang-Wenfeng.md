## First-person quotations

### 36Kr Waves, 2023-05-24 ("The Crazy High-Flyer"), translation Zihan Wang for ChinaTalk (2024-12-09), cross-checked against Recode China AI (2025-01-27)

URL: https://www.chinatalk.media/p/deepseek-from-hedge-fund-to-frontier

- "AGI may be one of the next most challenging frontiers, so for us, the question is not 'why' but 'how'."
- "It's driven by curiosity. ... What you think of as 'thinking' might actually be your brain weaving language."
- On the 10,000-GPU buildup: "It was a gradual process, from a single card in the early days to 100 cards in 2015, 1,000 cards in 2019, and then 10,000 cards. ... People may think there's some hidden business logic behind this, but it's mainly driven by curiosity."
- "Researchers have an insatiable hunger for computational resources."
- "An exciting endeavor perhaps cannot be measured purely in monetary terms. It's like someone buying a piano for a home: first, they can afford it, and second, such a group of people are eager to play beautiful music on it."
- "NVIDIA GPUs hold their value well, and older cards still find buyers."
- "It might be hard if we must find a commercial justification, because it's not cost-effective."
- "Many VCs seem hesitant about investing in research. They have exit requirements and prioritize rapid product commercialization."
- "Experienced people will tell you how something should be done without hesitation, while those without experience will explore repeatedly, think carefully, and find a solution that fits the current situation."
- "We don't have KPIs or so-called quotas."
- "Assign them important tasks and avoid interfering."
- "Innovation is inherently expensive and inefficient, often accompanied by waste."
- What excites him most: "Verifying whether our hypotheses are correct. If they are, that's immensely satisfying."
- "It's like walking 50 kilometers: your body is completely exhausted, but your spirit feels deeply fulfilled."
- Preamble facts (interviewer, not Liang): Chengdu cheap rental and failures; the DJI friend; "reclusive 'tech nerd' founder"; first public interview after a decade.

### 36Kr Waves, July 2024, translation ChinaTalk (2024-11-27); Jie Xu Substack (2025-01-28) as cross-check

URL: https://www.chinatalk.media/p/deepseek-ceo-interview-with-chinas

- "We didn't mean to become a catfish. We just accidentally became a catfish." (em-dash in translation normalized)
- "Very surprising. We didn't expect pricing to be so sensitive to everyone. We were just doing things at our own pace and then accounted for and set the price. Our principle is that we don't subsidize nor make exorbitant profits."

- "For many years, Chinese companies are used to others doing technological innovation, while we focused on application monetization."
- "We're used to Moore's Law falling out of the sky, lying at home waiting 18 months for better hardware and software to emerge."
- "The real gap is the difference between originality and imitation. If this doesn't change, China will always be only a follower."
- "Money has never been the problem for us; bans on shipments of advanced chips are the problem."
- "Our hiring standard has always been passion and curiosity."
- "Anyone on the team can access GPUs or people at any time. If someone has an idea, they can access the training cluster cards anytime without approval."
- "Because we're tackling the hardest problems. Top talents are most drawn to solving the world's toughest challenges."
- "I believe innovation starts with believing. Why is Silicon Valley so innovative? Because they dare to do things."
- "OpenAI is not a god."
- "My main energy is focused on researching the next generation of large models."

## Type hypothesis (hypothesis, not evidence)

- **Preliminary type:** Enneagram Type 5. Stated prime motive is curiosity and hypothesis-testing; decade of near-invisibility; minimization of needs elevated to doctrine ("restraint is a strategy," "I only want to eat one piece"); hoards the scarce resource (compute) while disclaiming money; autonomy over dependence (no VC for years, retained control); autodidact who skipped class because teachers were slow; long silences then precise questions (Bloomberg); "not domesticated" socially (Luo).
- **Strongest alternative:** Type 9 (consensus authority, "no organization," non-adversarial posture, "own pace," easygoing culture). Secondary alternative: Type 1 (right-and-wrong before profit-and-loss, contributor-not-free-rider morality, teacher household, never late).
