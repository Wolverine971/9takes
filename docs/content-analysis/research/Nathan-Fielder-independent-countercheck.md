[Original interview](https://www.thewrap.com/benny-safdie-the-curse-showtime-interview/). Full article opened and read. Relevant passages: opening intent discussion and closing discussion of community perspectives.

## Interpretation tests and exclusions

These are editorial tests, not conclusions about Fielder:

- **Knowledge, uncertainty, precision, and audience management are different possible motivations.** The same elaborate production can fit several explanations. Require repeated first-person patterns before choosing which explanation does the most work.
- **Select evidence outside a performed scene.** A writer's explanation of a creative decision and a collaborator's concrete work account carry different evidentiary weight from a character's voice-over.
- **Do not flatten the contradiction.** Purposeful professional boldness can coexist with difficult unstructured interaction. Do not assume one invalidates the other or explains all of it.
- **Separate intent, technique, and effect.** A creator can intend empathy, use manipulation, and produce a participant's distress. Neither praise nor criticism alone settles the whole account.
- **Do not infer diagnosis.** Deadpan speech, rehearsals, social awkwardness, an episode's scan, or an interview's discussion of autism do not establish a clinical diagnosis. No diagnosis is verified here.
- **Do not infer private causes.** Do not invent a childhood wound, cause of a divorce, present relationship status, parenting wishes, sexuality, or a permanent inability to connect. Fictional Asher is not biographical evidence about Fielder.
- **Do not launder disputed claims.** Attribute professional criticism and responses; do not erase either because it complicates a flattering reading. No broad claim about all participants being happy or fully informed is established here.
