## Timeline (relevant to thesis)

- 1971-06-04 — Born in Hollywood, Los Angeles. Mother Marjorie Speer, orthopedic nurse. Father Stephen Wyle, entrepreneur/electrical engineer. Parents divorced when he was young; six siblings and half-siblings. Stepfather James C. Katz, film preservationist. Grandmother Edith R. Wyle founded the LA Craft and Folk Art Museum; grandfather Frank Wyle founded aerospace firm Wyle Laboratories. (Britannica; Wikipedia)
- 1985–1989 — The Thacher School, Ojai (CdeP 1989). School requires every ninth grader to care for and ride a horse for a year. His son later attends the same school. (thacher.org "At Home With Horses: Noah Wyle CdeP 1989")
- 1994 — Cast at 23 as med student John Carter on ER; youngest of the original doctors. Show becomes a phenomenon.
- 1994–2005 — Series regular for 11 seasons; returns as guest in later seasons. Last pre-Pitt Emmy nomination: 1999 (SlashFilm: win came "2.5 decades after his last nomination").
- 2009 — ER ends. Wyle enters what THR's Awards Chatter later frames as his 16 "wilderness years" (Falling Skies 2011–2015, The Librarians 2014–2018, Leverage: Redemption).
- 2012-04 — Arrested with ~100 ADAPT disability-rights activists in the Cannon House Office Building rotunda protesting proposed Medicaid cuts; ~12 hours in holding cells under the congressional office building. Said afterward the arrest felt "really good." (CBS News; THR; Disability Scoop)
- 2020–2021 — During COVID, receives letters/DMs from healthcare workers who went into medicine because of Carter. Emails John Wells. (NPR; Television Academy; multiple)
- 2024-08 — Michael Crichton's estate (widow Sherri Crichton) sues Warner Bros., John Wells, R. Scott Gemmill, and Wyle, alleging The Pitt is an unauthorized ER reboot: "The Pitt is ER. It's not like ER, it's not kind of ER, it's not sort of ER. It is ER complete with the same executive producer, writer, star, production companies, studio and network as the planned ER reboot." WB calls the suit "baseless." (TVLine; CBS)
- 2025-01 — The Pitt premieres on Max. Wyle stars as Dr. Michael "Robby" Robinavitch, also executive producer and writer.
- ~2025 (spring) — Judge rules the Crichton estate's case can move ahead. (Rolling Stone)
- 2025-03 (approx.) — Capitol Hill nurse-advocacy trip; tells nurses "You deserve to be seen and heard." Brings his mother; she marches with nurses. He calls it a "core memory." (nurse.org; Great Chat podcast)
- 2025-09-14 — Wins first Emmy (Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama), 26 years after his last nomination. Wears FIGS' first-ever "black-tie scrubs" tuxedo. Speech: "To anybody who's going on shift or coming off shift tonight, thank you for being in that job. This is for you." (ABC News; THR; FIGS press release; nurse.org)
- 2026-01 — The Pitt season 2 airs (Robby's mental-health arc deepens; he tells biker friend Duke he doesn't know if he wants to be here anymore; finale is Robby-centric). (Time; SoapCentral; Gold Derby recaps)
- 2026-07 — The Pitt leads all shows with 25 Emmy nominations (nearly double season 1's 13); Wyle nominated again for Lead Actor; he submitted for both acting and directing. (TribLive; Variety; Deadline)
- 2026-09-14 — Emmy ceremony (resurgence narrative runs through it; backlog note).

## Type hypothesis (labeled as hypothesis)

- **Leading: Enneagram 6 (w7).** Loyalty loop (11 seasons; returns to the same producers 30 years later; "old, old, old friends" everywhere; marries into his crew world). Vigilance/imposter theme (competency mask, therapy reliance, universalizing on-set fear). Duty-shaped courage: rehearsed the doctor role for 15 years, and when a real crisis arrived, wrote "I volunteer." Community-first deflection (trophies < anecdotes; Emmy speech dedicates the award to people on shift). Builds safety for the group (phone ban to force bonding; de-fearing his sets; "keeps it calm" per McCormack).
- **Strongest alternative: 2 (Helper).** "Love letter" framing, who-helps-the-helper, mother-identification, advocacy. Weakness of the 2 case: his giving is cause-and-institution shaped, not be-needed shaped; the emotional register is duty and reassurance, not seduction/indispensability.
- Also considered: 9 (keeps-it-calm mediator; but the anxiety/imposter/advocacy-arrest data cuts against), 3 (career resurrection narrative; but he detached from image-metrics, gave up on awards, values anecdotes over trophies, self-describes worry not winning).
- **Tiebreaker behavior:** the on-set fear speech. He treats fear as the ambient medium of his workplace and organizes the entire environment to neutralize it preemptively. That is management of the Six's own weather, projected outward as leadership.
- **Disconfirming evidence to watch:** three decades of comfort as the face of giant productions; playful swagger (joking he's "the better doctor" than Clooney). Read here as w7 + integrated-6 steadiness, but a 3 reading could claim it.
- Confidence: medium-high.
