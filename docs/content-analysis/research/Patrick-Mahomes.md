## 6. Hypotheses (labeled)

**Preliminary type hypothesis:** Enneagram 6 (Loyalist), counterphobic expression, 7 wing. Evidence: copies trusted senior figures wholesale (father's tongue habit, A-Rod's tee, Alex Smith's film schedule, Reid's system); needs authority sign-off to trust himself (Brady's one sentence "validated everything"; "once he cleared me...
