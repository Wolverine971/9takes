## Timeline (relevant to thesis)

- 1981-04-13 — Born Washington, D.C. Military family: both parents career Army officers (retired
  lieutenant colonels per entity-gap packet sourcing); grandfather a general; youngest of four.
  Grew up "everywhere... base to base and post to post" (Library of Congress, 2024).
- ~1992-93 — Aunt's basement library in Colorado; handed her first Mercedes Lackey (Heralds of
  Valdemar). Read mom's 1980s romances (Jude Deveraux, Catherine Coulter) at 11-12; never censored.
- High school — Wrote a novel about a fictitious Ebola outbreak engineered by the US government as
  population control. "I was either going to be an author or an epidemiologist" (LoC, 2024).
- 2000 — Meets Jason Yarros at a Colorado karaoke bar at 19. Married 2002-05-18. "He's the only guy
  I'm going to fall in love with" (LoC, 2024).
- 2002+ — Jason (Army Chief Warrant Officer 3, Apache pilot, 22 years of service): seriously wounded
  on his first deployment ("They called me and told me that half of his face had been blown off" —
  militaryfamilies.com). Oldest son was 5.5 weeks old at first deployment (Task & Purpose, 2019).
- ~2009-2012 — Third deployment (Afghanistan): she reads a book a night, then writes a page a night
  after the kids sleep; finishes her first novel before he returns.
- 2014 — Full Measures published (Entangled). ~20 contemporary novels over the following decade.
- 2019-02 — The Last Letter releases ("supposed to be the book that broke me out and it didn't and
  it took me to my knees" — WaPo Live, 2025). Same month: her open letter rejecting Jason's $105,000
  three-year re-enlistment bonus ("His life is worth more than $105,000"; "In a lifetime of war...
  This man deserves peace" — Task & Purpose, 2019). "That was the first time I got canceled on the
  internet" (LoC, 2024).
- 2019-10 — Co-founds One October with Jason after fostering-then-adopting their youngest daughter
  (adopted in October); provides first-week essentials (duffel bags, clothing, gear) to kids
  entering foster care.
- ~2020-21 — Diagnosed with hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome after two years of POTS flares and
  vestibular migraines ("I thought I was going to die... please, you know, autopsy my body kind of
  things" — LoC, 2024). All four sons also have EDS (her words, LoC + Book Hook).
- 2021 — Entangled asks for five complete fantasy pitches for the new Red Tower line; she paces the
  floor "listening to Taylor Swift at 2 a.m."; is "shocked" they pick the dragon pitch. Editor
  suggests the college setting. Writes an 8-page synopsis overnight-ish; had "almost quit writing a
  few months beforehand" (LoC, 2024).
- 2022 — Writes Fourth Wing in ~2.5 months ("it fell out of me"; "I get to go play with dragons
  today"). The year she wrote it: ~5 books, ~800,000 words, 17-hour days (Book Hook, 2025).
- 2023-05-02 — Fourth Wing publishes (Red Tower); daughter's wedding the weekend before. Amazon TV
  deal in place since before release. 67 consecutive weeks on the NYT hardcover list as of Sept 2024
  (stated at LoC event); sold in 30+ languages.
- 2023-10 — NYC Comic Con: Scottish Gaelic speaker criticizes her mispronunciation/appropriation of
  Gaelic character names ("Minority languages... deserve respect, especially from fantasy American
  authors who co-opt them to add a little bit of spice"). She tells Variety she was "aware" she
  erred and expressed regret. NOTE: distinct from the romance-to-romantasy "cash grab" backlash
  (Goodreads-borne: "the most blatant cash grab I've seen"). Do not conflate (per packet).
- 2023-11 — Iron Flame. Era of death threats: "People tracked down our house... scaring my kids and
  we had to move... my husband just has like a personal contact with the FBI at this point"
  (Book Hook, 2025; first said in Australia per LoC, 2024). Grieved the garden; planned a two-story
  library at the new house ("we're gonna get the lemonade out of that").
- 2024-09 — Library of Congress National Book Festival closing event; first romance writer given a
  main-stage event in festival history (per NPR's Emily Kwong on stage).
- 2025-01-21 — Onyx Storm: 2.7M North American copies week one; fastest-selling adult novel in 20
  years (Circana BookScan, via Kirkus/PW, Jan 2025). She describes the writing period as "the most
  dangerous time in my entire life"; husband and sister "didn't leave me alone for five minutes";
  sobbed when it went to the printer; Jason: "You can't do this to yourself again" (Book Hook,
  2025 — recounted by her). Announces a break; takes one month off social media, four months off
  writing, then writes two graphic novels.
- 2025-11 — Washington Post Global Women's Summit ("Writing Her Story"). Cynthia Erivo Simply More
  NYC event: Erivo calls her "one of my favorite authors"; on stage tells her "You wrote a book and
  you didn't stop. And then you wrote a book that the world over wanted and you didn't stop," and
  "The way you write... the world is not going to let you hide."
- 2026-05 — Prime Video series order at upfronts: Michael B. Jordan's Outlier Society producing,
  Meredith Averill showrunning, Lisa Joy directing ep 1; Yarros EP. Averill (via trade coverage):
  "Rebecca's books have become one of the fastest-selling franchises of the 21st century."
- 2026-04-08 — Denver Q&A: writes at least 4,000 words a day.
- 2026-07-15 — Threshing Day announced: Empyrean anthology, 13 character stories, releases
  2026-09-29. NOT Book 4 (high-value disambiguation).
- 2026-11-17 — Peculiar Stars (contemporary romance, Montlake) upcoming; cover revealed on TODAY.
- Book 4: unannounced; "not before 2027" is secondary reporting only.

## Hypotheses (labeled as hypotheses)

- PRELIMINARY TYPE: Enneagram 6 (6w7; sp-dominant, social second; counterphobic streak).
  Confidence: high. Core evidence: threat-simulation as creative engine ("worst thing I could make
  happen to you"); safety-first stated value hierarchy; doorbell hypervigilance; loyalty extremes
  (one man since 19; guaranteed-HEA "contract" as the point of romance); ambivalence toward the
  military institution she loves ("swore I would never marry a soldier and did"; letter questioning
  leadership "pride"; Fourth Wing's institutional-lie plot); preparedness rituals (five pitches,
  color-coded plot boards); documented anxiety (attacks, fight-or-flight amnesia); avoidance of her
  own image; teenage government-conspiracy plague novel.
- STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE: Type 3 (records, 800K words/year, brand empire, adaptive warmth at
  signings). Weakened by: aversion to image ("I avoid myself at all costs"), no-memory dissociation
  during peak visibility, hiding preference, truth-blurting over media polish. The 3-ish grind
  reads as the 6's stress arrow (6→3) during the Iron Flame/Onyx period.
- SECONDARY ALTERNATIVES: Type 2 (six kids, fostering, One October, serving readers in line) —
  giving is framed as provisioning/duty, not being-needed identity. Type 4 (emotional depth) — no
  specialness/longing identity pattern.
- TIEBREAKER BEHAVIOR: the $105K letter — reward vs. attachment-threat calculus resolved in one
  line ("what is the point of life-changing money if he's not alive").
- WHAT THE TYPE DOES NOT EXPLAIN: the velocity (Fourth Wing in 2.5 months; a book in 3 weeks; 800K
  words/year). Better explained by self-disclosed ADHD hyperfocus + 7 wing appetite. Name honestly.
