## Contrary / complicating evidence (preserve for the jury)

- Sept 2026 safety turn vs. Oct 2025 restructuring. The man who removed "safely" from the mission statement (Oct 2025) invoked safety to delay the IPO and endorsed embedded independent evaluators (Sept 2026). The draft names two readings: 4-to-1 integration (accepting outside rules) vs. a Type 3 reading (timing an IPO around a bad safety news cycle). Do not resolve it without new evidence, such as whether evaluators are actually embedded at OpenAI.
- The Dec 2025 "Zero percent" interest in being a public-company CEO (Benzinga) fits the IPO delay. But the June 2026 confidential S-1 shows the company moving toward a listing anyway. Both are true.
- Wikipedia lists Cooper Hoffman's Brockman as "board chairman of OpenAI." At the firing Brockman was president and chair and was removed from the board the same day. The draft calls him "co-founder and president, removed from the board alongside the firing."
