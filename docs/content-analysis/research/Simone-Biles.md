## Accomplishments, failures, controversies, counterevidence

**Accomplishments:** 11 Olympic medals (7 gold), 30 World medals (23 gold), 5 named skills, first
gymnast to compete a Yurchenko double pike in competition (2021 US Classic), 2024 Paris vault gold,
2024 Paris team and all-around gold.

**Failures and low points, in her own framing:**

- Rio 2016 beam bronze, publicly shamed ("throw that medal away").
- Tokyo 2021 team final withdrawal after the vault; withdrew from all-around, vault, bars, floor finals;
  returned for beam with a changed dismount.
- Her named lowest point is _not_ Tokyo. It is the return to training: "one step forward, five steps back."

**Controversies and named critics:**

- Charlie Kirk, July 2021 podcast: "We are raising a generation of weak people like Simone Biles"; called
  her "very selfish… immature and she's a shame to the country"; "She's totally a sociopath"; "What kind
  of person skips the gold medal match?"
- Piers Morgan, July 2021: questioned whether "mental health issues" are now "the go-to excuse" for poor
  performance in elite sport. "What a joke."
- 2016 Fancy Bears WADA leak, weaponized as a doping insinuation. Approved TUE, no rules broken.
- 2024 Jordan Chiles bronze/CAS appeal — adjacent, complicated, and not Biles' story. **Leave out.**

**Serious counterevidence to the Type 6 reading:** her fluency and evident enjoyment inside the celebrity
machine (Dancing with the Stars, endorsement portfolio, the GOAT branding), her record-chasing, and the
self-description "I've always been an open and honest book." All better explained by a Three reading,
or by the 6→3 stress line plus a 7 wing.

## Hypotheses (not findings)

**Preliminary type hypothesis: Enneagram Type 6, 6w7, social-dominant with a strong counterphobic charge.**

Support: the foster-home vigilance account and the physical checking behavior; "Did I do it?" to Boorman
after a third consecutive world all-around title; "Are we sure? Are we sure?" to her therapist eight years
after Tokyo; staying inside USA Gymnastics explicitly to keep the failures attached to the competition;
the standing 0.5 spotter deduction; deferring instantly to her coaches' "no"; "my love blanky"; "Mom, come
pick me up, I'm scared"; suppressing her own alarm to steady the team; "if you said I couldn't do something,
I'm doing it."

**Strongest alternative: Type 3.** The medal record, the GOAT branding, the commercial fluency, the
identity question "who I am without gymnastics." The 9takes house reading of 6→3 under stress ("fear puts
on a competent face") absorbs most of this, and the pre-Tokyo period fits that description closely.

**Named tiebreaker:** "Did I do it?" (Boorman, 2015) and "Are we sure? Are we sure?" (therapist, ~2023).
A Three reads the scoreboard and the room. Biles asks a trusted authority to confirm a result that has
already been objectively measured, and does not fully accept the answer. Second tiebreaker: paying a
standing half-point in a world final for a spotter she does not need to land the vault.

**Disconfirming evidence, kept in view:** her ease and appetite inside celebrity; the "open book"
self-description; her humor, which reads more Seven than Six. Reconciliation offered: 7 wing plus the
stress line. This is the softest seam in the typing and should be flagged in the draft's Rabbit Hole.

**Arrows (9takes house reading, `enneagram-connecting-lines`):** 6 under stress → 3 ("fear puts on a
competent face"); 6 in growth → 9 ("vigilance settles into grounded trust"). The 2018–2021 run maps to
the stress line; therapy, the podium bow, and the 50-50 map to the growth line.

**Confidence: high.**
