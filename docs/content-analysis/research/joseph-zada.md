## Honest read (the human researcher's, a hypothesis)

The new material strengthens Six and adds what the record lacked: a security-shaped remedy in his
own words ("safe hands"), vigilance language, and a worry rule from a trusted authority. It also
weakens both alternatives. Against Nine there is "I'm intense... keep very much to myself" and
"force myself out of my comfort zone". Against Three there is "I don't want to create for someone
else's opinion" and his observed Instagram reticence.

It does not remove the confound. Almost all of it is press inside release windows. The Casting Guild
interview (A) and the GQ speech (J) are the only non-promotional items, and A is more about craft
than motive. The fear content still leans Three, and the impression-to-win episode (B) is a real
Three datum.

**Read: Type 6, low-to-medium confidence.** Three is the nearer alternative now and Nine is
weaker. If the research stage judges that "safe hands" plus vigilance is still routine press
language, the honest outcome is a low-confidence Six with Three named as a live second reading in
the type answer, rather than another medium claim. Do not type him from Cal or Haymitch.
