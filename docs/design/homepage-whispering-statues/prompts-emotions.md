# Emotion statues: shame, fear, anger (2026-11-04)

These are three portraits for a row between "What's under every take" and "Before you post" on the v4 homepage (https://claude.ai/artifact/8GYieRBEDYbPu9XTW7dq3K). DJ generates them in ChatGPT; agents never generate images. The prompts match the whispering-statues hero and `docs/design/image-refresh-2026-11-04/README.md`. The indigo duotone is added afterwards with sharp, so the prompts ask for neutral grey stone.

**How to run them:** use one ChatGPT chat, in order. Attach `02-he-whispers-shes-scandalized-v2.webp` to the first prompt as the material reference. Send prompt 1, then 2, then 3 in the same chat, so the framing and light carry over.

**Save as:** `docs/design/homepage-whispering-statues/emotion-shame.png`, `emotion-fear.png`, `emotion-anger.png`.

## 1. Shame (heart types)

```
Editorial photograph of a life-sized ancient Greek marble bust of a young man, head and shoulders, feeling shame. His head is bowed and turned away toward the viewer's left, chin dropped toward his chest, as if he can't stand to be looked at. One hand is raised with the fingertips resting against his brow, half-shielding his eyes. Shoulders drawn slightly inward. The emotion is quiet and restrained, carried by posture and the angle of the head, not a grimace.

Match the attached image's stone exactly: dry matte weathered grey marble, fine irregular pores, worn chisel marks, a few small edge chips, a little charcoal grime in recesses. Solid carved stone eyes with no irises, pupils or wet highlights. Classical carved curls. Natural five-fingered hand.

One believable raking light from the upper left, natural falloff, detail kept in the charcoal shadows. Ash, chalk and charcoal palette only. Plain near-black background with no scenery, no pedestal, no rim glow.

Framing (this is the first of a matching set of three): vertical 4:5 portrait. The head sits centered horizontally with the top of the hair about 12% below the top edge; the bust is cut at mid-chest. Leave black space on both sides. No text, logos or watermark.
```

## 3. Anger (gut types)

Same series, same camera, same light, same stone, same framing and scale as the previous two images. Only the subject and emotion change.

Editorial photograph of a life-sized ancient Greek marble bust of a bearded man in his thirties, head and shoulders, feeling anger he is holding in. ## If one comes back off

- **Too theatrical:** "Make the expression more restrained and sculptural, closer to a real Hellenistic marble. Keep everything else."
- **Doesn't match the others:** "Match image 1's framing, head size, eye-line height and light direction exactly."
- **Looks plastic or CGI:** "More weathering: dry matte stone, visible pores and chisel marks, no gloss."
