## Where all four agree

1. **The product is hidden.** The example card, the one thing that shows what 9takes is, sits about five screens down on a phone, and its answers are blurred. Every reviewer's "one change" is a version of "show a real question and at least one real answer up top, and let me type before signing up."
2. **No proof real people are here.** No counts, no recency, no visible human takes.
3. **Anonymity and safety are unexplained.** "Anonymously" sits next to JOIN FREE and LOG IN, with no word on what's anonymous, who sees your type, or who moderates. (Reddit, Linda and Okafor.)

## Where they split

- **Statues:** strong yes from the Gen Z teen; no from the other three (AI slop, risqué, pop-psych).
- **The anger/shame/fear section:** the Enneagram expert's favorite part; the teen's exit point.

## AI-generated feel: the specific tells

- **Imagery:** purple-glow duotone marble with blank eyes and plastic-smooth skin.
- **Layout:** the same headline treatment five times: a mint tracked eyebrow, a condensed caps headline, then a lavender italic serif tail. Also outline type, a mint pill button, and a numbered 1-2-3 list.
- **Copy:** rule-of-three lines ("Say what you really think. Anonymously. Before you see anyone else's.", "find out / find out / understand"), "Then watch how it shapes the way they see everything," and two "really think"s on one screen.
- Okafor's read: the Enneagram lines sound human; the frame around them reads as template.

## Enneagram accuracy (from Okafor)

- ✅ Correct: centers (2·3·4 heart / 5·6·7 head / 8·9·1 gut), core emotions (shame/fear/anger), the head and gut type lines, and the 8/4/9 example takes.
- ❌ Wrong: Four "to be seen as different" is the stereotype. Use "to be seen for who they really are."
- ⚠️ Fix cheaply:
  - "Perfectionist" (Palmer's name) next to eight Riso-Hudson names → "Reformer."
  - Shame ≠ insecure → "What they're ashamed of."
  - Anger misfits 9 and 1 → "What they're angry about, even when it doesn't show."
  - Two "need to be liked" → "to be needed."
  - The heart line lists wants, not strategies → "win people over, polish the image, or turn inward to find what's missing."
  - "shapes the way they see everything" → "shapes what they notice first."
  - Add "everyone picks their own type."

## Funnel insights (out of character)

- **TikTok teen:** continue the clip's question; the framework belongs after the first keystroke, as the payoff.
- **Reddit:** they land on question pages, not the homepage, so proof of real people and a plain anonymity explanation matter most there.
- **Facebook mom:** a sharer, not an activator. Serve her on article pages and family-question landing pages, not this homepage.
- **Practitioner:** a referrer. Give-first blocks her from vetting, so give referrers one permanently unlocked showcase question.
