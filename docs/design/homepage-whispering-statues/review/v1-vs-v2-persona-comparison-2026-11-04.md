# v1 vs v2: persona comparison (2026-11-04)

- v1: https://claude.ai/artifact/Rif2yTPiXRhcWq1nsSBXsA
- v2: https://claude.ai/artifact/QmrWtzWpjQ89ZeCTdEmCAh
- Personas: `../review-personas.md`

## How the test was run

The test was blind. Reviewers saw "Version A" and "Version B" in neutrally named folders, and nothing told them which one was newer. Marcus and Linda saw v2 as A. Reddit and Okafor saw v1 as A. Reviewers could not read the earlier review notes.

## Verdict: all four reviewers picked v2

| Reviewer                    | Pick | Confidence        | Why, in one line                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------- | ---- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Marcus, 17 (phone)          | v2   | 70/30             | "v1 looks cooler, but v2 actually has stuff in it." He'd answer the kid question.                                                     |
| u/throwaway_ennui (desktop) | v2   | 85/15             | He stayed about 15s on v1 and 2–3 min on v2. Real takes and the FAQ won him over; the empty slots read as a "ghost town."             |
| Linda, 56 (iPad)            | v2   | 70/30             | The FAQ, the named human, and the gentle kid question. She'd send v2 to her daughter, not to her Facebook group.                      |
| Dr. Okafor (desktop)        | v2   | 80% to colleagues | Riso-Hudson names, corrected triad lines, and the self-typing caveat. Not sendable to clients yet (about 55%); it needs a care layer. |

## Category winners (all four reviewers)

- **v2 wins:**
  - clarity of the product
  - desire to answer
  - trust and safety
  - proof that real people use it
  - Enneagram accuracy
  - less AI feel
  - readability
- **v1 wins:** visual appeal, unanimously. It's bolder, more poster-like and more cohesive. v2's lower half reads as "SaaS cards and FAQ."

## What v2 lost from v1 (consensus: steal these back)

1. **A plain one-line explainer in the hero** (all 4). v2's first screen never says what 9takes is. Bring back "Say what you really think. Anonymously. Before you see anyone else's." or a plainer line. The word "anonymous" should sit near the input (Marcus).
2. **v1's poster-level visual confidence** (all 4). Two blocks were singled out: the "Answer before the crowd does" section and the "NINE PERSPECTIVES" type.
3. **"Find out what people really think / Understand the other side"** (Linda). It was the best copy for her.
4. **The blurred tease** (Marcus). It beat the gray bars. It must be fake or image-based, though: v1's real-text blur leaked the takes in the page source.

## New problems in v2

1. **Empty type slots read as dead** (Marcus, Reddit, Linda). Collapse the four "No Type X has answered yet" cards into one line, e.g., "Types 1, 5, 6, 7: be the first."
2. **The hero question is too heavy** (Marcus, Linda, Okafor). "Seem fine" plus "slowly killing me inside" is the first thing people read. Lead with the 3-word kid question instead.
3. **No care layer** (Okafor, the blocker for client referrals). Add a crisis-resource line near the composer and in the FAQ, plus how fast takes are reviewed.
4. **The statues still trigger "AI slop" for Reddit.** He'd shrink them on desktop so the question card opens the page. Marcus still loves them.
5. **Small accuracy and honesty fixes** (Okafor):
   - Drop "Live" when the newest take is 6 days old.
   - Label type tags "self-typed."
   - Change "Find your type" to "Explore your type."
   - The highlighted 8 in the number row looks like the page favors one type.
6. **"Take" is never defined** (Linda).
7. **There's only one moderator** (Reddit, Okafor). The honesty helps, but it also reads as small.

## AI-feel tells still left in v2

- triplet rhythm in the triad blurbs
- the repeated three-card triad template
- the decorative 1–9 circle row with the 8 lit
- section titles like "Plain answers" and "See what's behind the lock"
- the closing manifesto block

What saves v2 is its real, messy user text ("rambunctious rowdy and a flirt," "Grandma likes to say that I was 'never a child'").
