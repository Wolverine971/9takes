I don’t react to things anymore. If someone yells at me, I’ll just nod then walk away. I wanna yell back so badly, but I know it’ll only make things worse. So instead, I hold my tongue and remove myself from the situation. If someone hurts my feelings, I’ll say everything is fine until they believe me. It’s slowly killing me inside, but it’s still easier than trying to explain myself or my feelings.
