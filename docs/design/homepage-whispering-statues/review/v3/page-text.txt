What's something you do every day to seem fine that nobody knows is costing you effort?

I don’t react to things anymore. If someone yells at me, I’ll just nod then walk away. I wanna yell back so badly, but I know it’ll only make things worse. So instead, I hold my tongue and remove myself from the situation. If someone hurts my feelings, I’ll say everything is fine until they believe me. It’s slowly killing me inside, but it’s still easier than trying to explain myself or my feelings.

What's a take?

Your honest answer to the question, in your own words. Three words counts.

Do I need an account?

No. Most takes on 9takes are posted without one. Make one later if you want to keep track of yours.

What's anonymous?

Your name and email are never shown. Your take appears with the type you picked, or with no type at all.

Who reads what I post?

DJ, who built 9takes, reads new takes and removes spam, attacks, and anything that identifies someone.

Why answer first?

So the first take you read can't steer yours. You get the honest version from everyone, not the loudest one repeated.

Having a hard time?

9takes isn't a crisis line. In the US, call or text 988. Anywhere else, findahelpline.com.
