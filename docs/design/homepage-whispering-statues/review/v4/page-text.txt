BEFORE
you post
What's a take?

Your honest answer to the question, in your own words. Three words counts.

Do I need an account?

No. Most takes on 9takes are posted without one. Make one later if you want to keep track of yours.

What's anonymous?

Your name and email are never shown. Your take appears with the type you picked, or with no type at all.

Who reads what I post?

DJ, who built 9takes, reads new takes and removes spam, attacks, and anything that identifies someone.

Why answer first?

So the first take you read can't steer yours. You get the honest version from everyone, not the loudest one repeated.

Having a hard time?

9takes isn't a crisis line. In the US, call or text 988. Anywhere else, findahelpline.com lists free, confidential lines.
