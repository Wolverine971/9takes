# Conversation circles: four approved variations

Generated using the built-in image generation tool and the approved cel-shaded neo-noir community scene as a reference. All four final candidates have been visually counted: **nine participants each**. These are imagined illustrations, including the circa-1910 scene, rather than depictions of a documented meeting. DJ approved the four variations and requested adding them to V2 with a subtle transition. They now rotate alongside the original college-age scene in the community section.
