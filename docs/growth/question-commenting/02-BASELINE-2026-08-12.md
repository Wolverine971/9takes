## Corrected email finding

The first PostHog pass reported 10 Gmail-referrer comments from three PostHog people. Database reconciliation changes the interpretation:

- The 10 comments came from two humans, not three.
- Both humans received Welcome Sequence Email 1 immediately before commenting.
- The subject was “What were you like as a kid? Three words.”
- The email’s main CTA deep-links to `/questions/what-were-you-like-as-a-kid-in-3-words`.
- One recipient posted two comments within five minutes of the send on 2026-07-28.
- The other recipient clicked the tracked question link 52 seconds after send on 2026-08-10, posted anonymously 41 seconds later, then became identified and posted seven more comments over the next 25 minutes.
- The anonymous fingerprint matches the later signed-in profile for that second journey. PostHog did not merge it.

Conclusion: the useful acquisition pattern is Welcome Email 1, not the reactivation sequence. Its strongest features are one concrete question, a direct CTA, a clear two-minute expectation, and an immediate reveal promise.

## Reactivation sequence reality

As of 2026-08-12:

| Sequence             | Recipients sent | Sends | Clicked sends |
| -------------------- | --------------: | ----: | ------------: |
| Reactivation dormant |              61 |   184 |             1 |
| Reactivation zombies |              29 |    69 |             1 |
| Reactivation cold    |               0 |     0 |             0 |

One zombies recipient commented twelve days after clicking a step-one question link, but that person had already commented before the reactivation send. This is a weak possible assist, not evidence of a newly activated commenter. The dormant step-three click did not produce a confirmed comment in the reviewed path.

The list is finite and nearly consumed. Do not use further reactivation sends as the default way to grow question participation.

## Current tracking gaps

- No canonical `question_created` event.
- No universal `question_impression` event.
- No reliable unanswered-question or time-to-first-response reporting in PostHog.
- Featured-question impressions are anonymous and do not reliably merge into later identified behavior.
- Welcome sequence links do not currently receive the structured UTM attribution already applied to reactivation links.
- The email template's brand link can bypass content-link click attribution.
- `is_first_contribution` currently means an answer-shaped event, not necessarily the person's first comment ever.
- No event records the gentle reply-email ask, consent, delivery, notification click, or exact-thread arrival.
