## The diagnosis in one paragraph

The account is **not** underperforming on engagement — it's a **cold-start problem**. As of 2026-07-24: **11 posts · 29 followers · 101 following**, real 9takes posts landing ~2 likes (Chappell 2, Lana 1, Steven 3). At 29 followers, Instagram shows each post to ~10–20% of them = **3–6 people**, so ~2 likes is arithmetic on a tiny base, not weak content. The copy is genuinely good. What's missing is **reach to non-followers** — and the only surface that delivers that is the one we're not using: **Reels**. (Carousels are a retention tool: high engagement _per viewer_, low reach.)

## Why Reels, given the constraints

DJ's constraints (2026-07-24): **under 1 hr/week**, **Reels-reluctant but has a script**, goal = **traffic to 9takes.com + follower base** (not coaching leads).

The fastest cold-start lever — daily manual engagement — costs 3+ hrs/week we don't have. So we can't grind; we play **leverage**. One Reel scripted from a carousel we already wrote (~10 min) that reaches 2,000 strangers ≈ the discovery of ~200 manual comments, with near-zero ongoing labor. **Being time-poor is exactly why Reels stop being optional.**

## 2026 reach mechanics (what we're optimizing for)

- **Reels reach non-followers (~36% more reach); carousels retain existing ones.**
- **Sends/DM-shares are weighted 3–5× likes.** Saves rule carousels, watch-time rules Reels. Likes barely count.
- **First 20–30 min decides a post's reach for life.** → post, then reply to every comment fast.
- Organic reach = 10–20% of followers, so growth must come from reach, not the followers we have.
