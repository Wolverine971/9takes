## The four experiments

### E1 — Does changing the surface (Reels) change reach? _(the big one)_

- **Do:** Batch 6 Reels (once, ~40 min) from existing carousel scripts; ship over ~3 weeks.
- **Measure:** reach / plays per Reel vs. carousels' reach (post insights).
- **Keep if:** any Reel clears ~300 plays (≈10× a carousel). → Reels become the spine.

### E2 — Do shares beat likes? _(the currency)_

- **Do:** End every post with one specific send-CTA ("send this to the Type-8 in your life").
- **Measure:** sends + saves (post insights) — not likes.
- **Keep if:** sends/saves rise even while likes stay flat.

### E3 — Does a clean topic lift reach? _(the signal)_

- **Do:** Move BuildOS content off @9takesdotcom; account goes 100% Enneagram for 3 weeks.
- **Measure:** average reach per post, before vs. after.
- **Keep if:** reach drifts up once the account reads as one clear topic.

### E4 — Does 15 min of real engagement move it? _(if minutes allow)_

- **Do:** 2×/week, 10 min — genuine comments on 5 posts in one type's pond.
- **Measure:** profile visits + follows that week.
- **Keep if:** follows tick up on engagement weeks vs. quiet ones.

### 2026-08-03 — E1 answered by accident (pulled live from Professional Dashboard → Insights)

**No batch shipped. But the account already contained the experiment.** The off-brand BuildOS "MORNING LOG / 001" Reel (energy drink → coffee, **0:02 long**, posted Jul 15) is a Reel, and its numbers settle E1 outright.

| Post                                   | Format   | Views | Accounts reached | Likes | Comments | Saves | Shares |
| -------------------------------------- | -------- | ----- | ---------------- | ----- | -------- | ----- | ------ |
| Morning log / energy drink (7/15)      | **Reel** | 549   | **375**          | 21    | 0        | 0     | 1      |
| "One mask, nine inner tensions" (7/22) | Carousel | 21    | —                | 2     | —        | —     | —      |
| Robert Greene (8/2)                    | Carousel | 8     | —                | 1     | —        | —     | —      |

**Account, last 30 days:** 636 views · **98.4% of all views came from Reels, 1.6% from Posts** · 32 interactions · 7 profile visits · 1 external link tap · 30 followers (+1 since 7/24).

- **Reach/plays:** 549 (Reel) vs 8–21 (carousels) → **~26–45× the reach**, clearing E1's ~300-play threshold on the first data point.
- **Non-followers:** **94.3%** of the Reel's views. Carousels are follower-only by construction.
- **Read:** E1 is **CONFIRMED, and stronger than hypothesized.** The reach gap is not caused by content quality — it is caused by surface. A 2-second, off-topic, zero-production founder clip out-reached every well-designed on-brand Enneagram carousel combined, by more than an order of magnitude. Engagement _rate_ is fine everywhere (Reel: 21 likes / 375 reached = 5.6%; carousel: 2 / 21 = ~10%). The account does not have an engagement problem. It has a **distribution** problem, and Reels are the only surface that solves it.
- **Second finding — reach did not convert:** that same Reel produced **0 saves, 0 comments, 0 follows** off 375 accounts reached. Right format, no payload. Reels buy the audience; they do not by themselves earn the follow. A native 9takes Reel (hook → nine beats → question close) has still **never been posted** — E1's actual intended test remains un-run.
- **Decision:** **KEEP — promote Reels to the spine.** Invert the content-ops queue, which is currently 16 carousels / 6 Reels with Reels scheduled as carousel "companions." Next real test is payload, not format.
