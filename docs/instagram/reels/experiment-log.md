## The four experiments

### E1 — Does changing the surface (Reels) change reach? _(the big one)_

- **Do:** Batch 6 Reels (once, ~40 min) from existing carousel scripts; ship over ~3 weeks.
- **Measure:** reach / plays per Reel vs. carousels' reach (post insights).
- **Keep if:** any Reel clears ~300 plays (≈10× a carousel). → Reels become the spine.

### E2 — Do shares beat likes? _(the currency)_

- **Do:** End every post with one specific send-CTA ("send this to the Type-8 in your life").
- **Measure:** sends + saves (post insights) — not likes.
- **Keep if:** sends/saves rise even while likes stay flat.

### E3 — Does a clean topic lift reach? _(the signal)_

- **Do:** Move BuildOS content off @9takesdotcom; account goes 100% Enneagram for 3 weeks.
- **Measure:** average reach per post, before vs. after.
- **Keep if:** reach drifts up once the account reads as one clear topic.

### E4 — Does 15 min of real engagement move it? _(if minutes allow)_

- **Do:** 2×/week, 10 min — genuine comments on 5 posts in one type's pond.
- **Measure:** profile visits + follows that week.
- **Keep if:** follows tick up on engagement weeks vs. quiet ones.
