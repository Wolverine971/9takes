# Saved Post: @ingri.skai — the "dopamine menu," a five-tier skeleton for novelty-seeking

## Original Context

- **Caption:** Opens on the stuck state, not the solution: _"whenever we feel stuck, bored or overwhelmed, our brains can struggle to start on things that need to get done. Sometimes doing a seemingly 'irrelevant' thing can actually help us to get out of that funk."_ Credits Jessica McCabe (@howtoadhd) for the term. Then five menu tiers:

  > 🫒 **starter** — "something small that doesn't require much effort but usually is enough to build momentum. Just sending a funny meme to a friend and having a little chuckle can be enough to get going"
  > 🍔 **main** — "requires more effort or preparation but gives back BIG results! These activities usually help us relax, boost mental, physical or emotional energy, so we're more capable to tackle what needs to get done later"
  > 🍟 **sides** — "also something small to help with the main tasks… Like grabbing a nice tea, lighting a candle and wrapping yourself in a blanket can make you weirdly more productive…or help you take a nap (50/50 risk and reward)"
  > 🍰 **dessert** — "gives a high rush of dopamine - good for joy and relaxation but also has a tendency to completely suck us in… that rush eventually will turn into a crash. Yet, without dessert life can become a bit… sad."
  > ✨ **specials** — "my favourite category! having something to look forward to can provide so much motivation and energy to keep going with daily tasks (especially when the present moment doesn't seem as exciting)."

  Closes with an open question: _"💬 what would be in your dopamine menu?"_ Hashtags: #adhd #add #adhdawareness #adhdproblems

## 9takes Opportunity

- **Pattern observed:** Type 7 novelty-seeking is usually framed as a _flaw to manage_ (distraction, avoidance, shiny-object syndrome). The insight underneath: what makes a "starter" work is not the dopamine, it's that it converts an undefined task into a defined one. Undefined is the thing the 7 brain flees; the meme isn't the point, the finishing is.
