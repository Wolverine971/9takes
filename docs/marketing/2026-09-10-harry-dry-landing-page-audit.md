# Harry Dry → 9takes: landing page and core offer

September 10, 2026. Research and proposed copy; no product changes made.

**Recommendation:** Make the value of one completed answer immediately understandable and easy to experience. Keep “One question, nine perspectives,” put a real question in the opening screen, explain the reward plainly, and show what understanding another perspective actually looks like.

Harry makes expertise easy to sample. The opportunity for 9takes is to make understanding people equally easy to sample.

**What this review covers.** I read Marketing Examples’ landing-page collection and selected articles on headlines, rewrites, CTAs, signup, email conversion, and keyword research. I inspected the current 9takes homepage, question index, starter-question page, and coaching waitlist in a browser, plus the canonical messaging hierarchy, recent growth reports, and limited server code that explains the question/reveal contract. The browser showed newer versions of the questions and coaching pages than the web text extractor; observations below use the browser versions. I did not submit a public answer, test the personalized reveal end to end, query current analytics, or audit either site's search rankings. Recommendations are hypotheses, not measured conversion lifts.

**What to learn from Harry's own site.**

The linked `/landing-page` URL is a topic collection, not a conventional product sales page. Visitors can choose a specific lesson immediately. Article cards advertise small reading commitments; the site lets the reader experience the format before asking for an ongoing relationship. Its newsletter describes a Monday delivery containing three examples, two copywriting tips, and a favorite tweet. The page displays a 130k readership claim and reader endorsements. That combination makes the content, commitment, and credibility legible. [Marketing Examples landing-page collection](https://marketingexamples.com/landing-page)

For 9takes, the analogous unit of value is one question, one honest answer, and one useful contrast with someone else's perspective. A visitor should be able to describe that exchange before deciding whether to participate. The ongoing relationship can follow a satisfying first experience.

**The core offer I would communicate.**

9takes gives people a way to practice understanding reactions that differ from their own: answer a question, compare perspectives, and notice the emotional logic underneath them.

This is supporting explanation within the [existing messaging hierarchy](/Users/djwayne/9takes/docs/brand/messaging-hierarchy.md), not a proposed new master tagline. The homepage's immediate offer is free participation in one question. The larger benefit is seeing more than your first interpretation. The Enneagram supplies the framework, and the answer-first rule protects the initial response from exposure to the displayed answers. Neither rule guarantees unbiased answers or accurate judgments of people.

For this first pass, I would address a curious visitor who sometimes struggles to understand other people's reactions and may know nothing about the Enneagram. Validate that audience assumption with visitor interviews and entry-page data before narrowing the whole business around it.

| Layer                   | What the visitor receives                         | Appropriate next action                       |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| First visit             | A concrete question and a revealing comparison    | Answer the question                           |
| Continued participation | Further conversation or another relevant question | Optional reply notification or another answer |
| Educational content     | More detailed explanations and examples           | Read the guide relevant to the question       |
| Personal help           | Potential 1:1 application to a real situation     | Join the existing coaching waitlist           |

The coaching page is currently a waitlist. It does not take payment or offer immediate booking. Keep that secondary to the homepage participation goal. Its existing focus on relationship loops and work dynamics provides more recognizable use cases than an abstract promise of greater understanding. [Current coaching page](https://9takes.com/book-session)

**The current homepage has several assets worth keeping.**

The central line is short and memorable. There is already an inline answer form. Anonymous participation is already supported. The page moves from participation to explanation and offers further learning. The visual identity is distinctive. These are useful foundations for a conversion pass. [9takes homepage](https://9takes.com/)

The main weakness is that a new visitor still has to imagine the reward. A scene of people talking establishes atmosphere but does not show the result of using 9takes. The question asks for personal disclosure before there is much visible evidence that the answer will lead somewhere worthwhile.

The lighter prompt is a recommendation to test, not proof that the current prompt is bad. The questions index already has a five-question starter section. Reuse that work. A reasonable first candidate is the existing question about describing yourself as a child in three words. A second candidate is the existing question about what makes someone a friend. The first reduces effort; the second may demonstrate divergent priorities more naturally. Choose after reviewing their answer sets. [Questions and starters](https://9takes.com/questions)

Do not assume either starter can simply replace the homepage question: the homepage has a distribution mechanism, and the Chorus lookup requires a complete set of nine takes. The fallback is currently the deeper question. Verify eligibility and the fallback before changing the featured selection. **Proposed opening screen, using the current brand line.**

> **One question. Nine perspectives.**
>
> Answer one question about your life. Then explore different ways of seeing it—and the fears, priorities, and assumptions behind each take.
>
> **[Selected starter question appears here]**
>
> Your answer: [short answer field]
>
> **Post my answer and reveal the perspectives**
>
> Anonymous · No account required · No Enneagram knowledge needed

This is a proposed layout and copy treatment. The active question and form should supply the proof; the streetlamp imagery can support them lower on the page or in a smaller role. Keep sufficient explanation for visitors who have never encountered answer-first Q&A.

Place a plain disclosure beside the form explaining that the answer is posted to the question. Distinguish AI-written Enneagram examples from participant responses wherever those are used. Suggested wording, if the featured set is AI-written: “Includes AI-written examples based on the nine Enneagram types.” The server data model supports both AI and human sources, so the production label should follow the actual data. I have not verified the source composition or all existing labels in the current post-answer view. [Source contract](/Users/djwayne/9takes/src/lib/server/nineTakes.ts)

Avoid making the stronger claim that every question has been answered by all nine real personality types. The current questions index makes that implication alongside its real-people framing. Also replace its claim of unbiased takes with the observable fact that answers are initially hidden. [Questions index](https://9takes.com/questions)

**A concrete example of the payoff.**

An ungated sample could use a different question from the live one, so it does not supply answers to the question the visitor is about to answer. The following is original illustrative copy, not actual user testimony or a claim about specific types:

> A friend cancels dinner at the last minute.
>
> One person feels disrespected: they arranged their day around it.
>
> Another worries: they wonder whether their friend is okay.
>
> A third feels relieved: they needed an evening alone.
>
> Same message. Different things at stake.

Follow with: “9takes helps you explore what is behind those different reactions.” This demonstrates the kind of understanding the product aims to build. It does not imply the platform can identify a stranger's actual motives from a single answer. Prefer a real, permissioned example once one is available and comparably clear.

After the live reveal, a prompt such as “Which answer made sense to you only after you read its explanation?” could test whether the insight landed. Treat that as a proposed addition, not an existing capability.

**Specific copy edits to consider.**

| Current element                              | Proposed treatment                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hero reassurance about type knowledge        | Name the Enneagram, or simply lead with the more immediate reassurance that no account is required.                                 |
| “Reveal through response”                    | “Answer first. Then see the other perspectives.”                                                                                    |
| Hero CTA                                     | If it scrolls to the form: “Answer the question.” If the form is already visible, use the explicit submit-and-reveal button above.  |
| Abstract paragraph introducing the framework | Lead with a short situation and contrasting reactions, then explain how the framework organizes recurring patterns.                 |
| Final invitation to browse                   | Repeat the invitation to answer the featured question; keep browsing as a secondary option.                                         |
| General follow-up signup                     | Tie it to an actual next benefit: “Email me when someone replies to my answer.” Use the existing notification flow where available. |

Retain distinct button text for navigation versus submission. A button that posts a public answer must communicate that action; an alluring reward alone would be incomplete.

Add a compact FAQ answering the concrete hesitations: Do I need an account? Do I need to know my type? Why are answers hidden? Who wrote the perspectives? Is my answer posted publicly? A short founder note can then explain why DJ built the answer-first experience in his own voice. Draft it from an actual founder story rather than inventing one.

**Proof to build before more promotional claims.**

The best early evidence would be a real example of someone encountering an answer they initially disagreed with, understanding the reasoning, and describing what changed in their own words. Ask permission before turning that into a testimonial. Specific demonstrations of insight are more relevant here than a raw article or visitor count.

Harry makes the signup invitation personal and explains why someone should subscribe. For 9takes, apply that after value has been experienced: make the follow-up about the answer just posted. Do not copy an exit popup merely because his historical email-conversion article reports it working. [Email conversion article](https://marketingexamples.com/landing-page/email-subscribers)

A weekly question offer is a reasonable later extension, but only promise a schedule once there is an owner and reliable delivery. It should add a reason to revisit the conversation, not become another unrelated newsletter production obligation.

**Why this deserves priority over a broad SEO expansion.**

The September 7 growth audit reports 4,489 new site visitors and four comments for the August 31–September 6 cohort; its tracked gate funnel is 29 visitors to three contributors. It also reports zero returning contributors across six matured cohorts totaling 28 contributors. Those are different denominators and historical snapshots, not a homepage conversion rate or a measurement I reproduced today. [Growth audit](/Users/djwayne/9takes/docs/growth/growth-log.md)

The same audit says reply/host infrastructure and starter improvements shipped after the measured week closed. It also records a welcome-email delivery failure. Verify the current delivery status before running a return experiment; do not attribute an infrastructure failure to copy. Avoid recreating features the audit says already shipped.

Harry's keyword-research case connects a search need to a relevant on-page offer. For 9takes, the first SEO application is to connect an existing article's subject to a well-chosen question after the article has met the reader's original need. [Keyword-research case study](https://marketingexamples.com/seo/keyword-research)

For example, on a guide about conflict, invite a reader to answer a relevant disagreement question. On a public-figure profile about ambition or approval, use a question that naturally follows that theme. These are editorial mappings to test; I have not established demand or conversion intent for new target keywords. Select candidate pages using fresh landing-page and Search Console data, not traffic assumptions.

Do not divert every visitor to a generic homepage or insert the same sensitive prompt everywhere. Preserve the article's primary purpose. Measure the bridge from article visit to completed answer and subsequent return, not just CTA clicks. A later search audit can cover rankings, intent, internal linking, and topic opportunities; this pass does not establish that either site's SEO is performing well.

**A practical two-stage test.**

1. **This week: comprehension and first value.** Prepare the opening-screen treatment and a reviewed starter/reveal pair. Show it to five to eight people unfamiliar with 9takes. Ask them to describe what it offers, what clicking does, and what they expect to see after posting. In a safe preview, observe whether they complete an answer and can describe one useful difference in perspective. This is directional usability research, not statistical proof.
2. **Then: a measured live pilot.** Run one clearly labeled treatment against a recorded baseline and keep the question/source mix controlled where possible. Track unique new visitors who actually see the question, answer starts, successful submissions, successful reveals, optional reply opt-ins, and a second contribution more than 24 hours and within seven days later. Report absolute counts and the relevant denominators. Separate homepage entry, article entry, and question-page entry.

Agree on the comparison period and decision rule before launch. With this contribution volume, use the pilot to find large failures and gather feedback; do not announce a winner from a handful of conversions. If layout, copy, and question all change together, describe the result as the effect of the package rather than assigning it to the headline.

Judge answer quality by whether the answer sincerely addresses its question. A three-word childhood prompt should not fail a generic 40-character minimum. Guard against improving raw submission numbers by encouraging filler that adds no value to the reveal.

**The first implementation should be narrow:** make the question and its reward visible immediately, reduce the first answer's emotional cost, preserve an honest explanation of the nine perspectives, and measure whether the person wants a second experience.
