# 9takes Communications Unification Overview

**Status:** Start-here brief for review and loose-ends audit  
**Created:** 2026-08-13  
**Owner:** DJ Wayne  
**Primary source:** [Lulu Meservey communications playbook applied to 9takes](./lulu-meservey-comms-playbook-2026-08-13.md)  
**Canonical authority:** [9takes messaging hierarchy](../brand/messaging-hierarchy.md)

## Why this document exists

This document gives a reviewing agent the shortest complete account of what was learned, what was
decided, what was implemented, and what may still need to be unified.

The next task is **not to create a better umbrella idea**. It is to find places where the existing
idea has not yet been carried through the product, brand, content system, or distribution system.

## The conclusion in one paragraph

9takes does not have an idea shortage. Its communications problem was that several good supporting
concepts were competing to be the main concept while the strongest founder belief remained buried.
The opportunity is to build recognition around one stable story: people experience their own
emotional alarm as reality; another person’s alarm can look like a defect; the internet intensifies
that mistake by allowing one interpretation to win the room; 9takes protects an independent first
read, reveals other coherent perspectives, and helps people understand what they could not see
alone. The work now is compression, embodiment, proof, and repetition.

## The unified story

```text
One situation creates different emotional emergencies
                         ↓
Our own alarm feels real; another person’s can look defective
                         ↓
The internet rewards one interpretation and buries the rest
                         ↓
9takes asks each person to answer before seeing the crowd
                         ↓
One question opens into nine perspectives
                         ↓
People see the emotions behind the takes and discover blind spots
```

The Enneagram explains why recurring differences exist, but it is the map in the background. The
human conflict, choice, fear, contradiction, or misunderstanding is the foreground.

## What we learned from Lulu Meservey

| Learning                                         | Meaning for 9takes                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Build a movement, not a publicity layer**      | Recruit people into a different practice of disagreement, not merely another personality product.                   |
| **Go direct**                                    | DJ’s voice should carry the origin belief. A brand account and automation can distribute it but cannot replace it.  |
| **Conviction compounds when language is stable** | Stop restarting recognition with new umbrella concepts. Campaigns may change; the five-level hierarchy should not.  |
| **Keep the human as protagonist**                | Begin with what someone fears, wants, protects, or misunderstands. Introduce the personality framework later.       |
| **Make the unfamiliar system ambient**           | Tell familiar stories about marriage, work, family, public conflict, and identity; let the Enneagram explain them.  |
| **Do what cannot be faked**                      | Real independent answers, relationships, participant reflections, and changed minds are stronger proof than output. |
| **Templates erase the valuable details**         | Use AI for research, transcription, editing, and operations; preserve DJ’s judgment, stories, and wording.          |
| **Beauty signals care**                          | Make public artifacts deliberate, but do not confuse more polish with the current strategic bottleneck.             |
| **Supply the connective story**                  | Q&A, personality profiles, coaching, founder content, and research should feel like parts of one mission.           |

The central operational learning is:

> **The bottleneck is not content supply. It is founder embodiment, real product proof, and direct
> delivery of a stable belief.**

## What is now settled

### The frozen hierarchy

| Level                   | Canonical language                                                  |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Movement belief**     | **The internet collapses nine perspectives into one winning take.** |
| **Brand promise**       | **See the emotions behind every take.**                             |
| **Product ritual**      | **Answer before the crowd.**                                        |
| **Product explanation** | **One question, nine perspectives.**                                |
| **Personal payoff**     | **Stop mistaking someone else’s alarm for a defect.**               |

These are five levels of one message, not five interchangeable taglines. New language must clarify
one of these jobs rather than compete for a sixth job.

### The founder story

The core human story is the anger/fear feedback loop in DJ’s marriage:

- DJ saw fear and tried to override it with urgency and force.
- His wife became more fearful as his anger increased.
- Her fear made him more frustrated and forceful.
- She wanted evidence that her concerns and safety mattered.
- He wanted trust in his judgment and ability to move forward.
- They were responding to the same situation as though it contained two different emergencies.

The hinge line is:

> **She was afraid of my anger. I was angry at her fear.**

The bridge to the movement belief is not “marriage is like social media.” It is that this same human
error happens everywhere: our own internal alarm feels like reality, and a different alarm can look
irrational or defective. Online crowd mechanics harden one interpretation before the other
perspectives can be understood.

### The product doctrine

- **Self-discovery over diagnosis.** People should recognize themselves rather than be told what they are.
- **Questions over verdicts.** Inquiry is the product posture and the content posture.
- **Understanding over judgment.** Understanding emotional logic does not require agreement.
- **Independent expression before social influence.** The answer-first mechanic is the product’s most distinctive behavior.
- **Differences are the value.** The purpose of nine perspectives is not to manufacture consensus or declare one type correct.

### The communication doctrine

- Lead with a human tension rather than Enneagram terminology.
- Use the framework as an explanatory map, not the protagonist.
- Carry one hierarchy level as the primary job of each piece.
- Give every distributed piece a specific live destination.
- Prefer real participants and real consequences to synthetic proof.
- Do not make “the crowd,” Reddit users, or people with popular opinions into villains. The problem is the architecture of anchoring and flattening.

## Supporting concepts: keep, but subordinate

These ideas still have value when they perform a defined supporting job:

- **The Chorus:** the reveal of other perspectives.
- **Give-first unlock:** internal/product-strategy name for the answer-first mechanism.
- **The Mirror Moment:** a content technique that turns analysis toward the reader.
- **The Nine:** the nine recurring perspective patterns.
- **One Take:** an existing founder-video format.
- **Streetlamp Symposium:** visual-world language.
- **Crowd gravity:** explanatory shorthand for anchoring and accumulated social proof.
- **Personality-maxing:** optional campaign language, never the umbrella positioning.
- **Open-source conflict resolution:** internal strategic metaphor, not the public master message.

The reviewing agent should identify any surface where one of these has escaped its supporting role
and now competes with the hierarchy.

## Loose ends worth auditing

### 1. Product truth

The strongest brand promises must be literally true in the experience.

- Does every promoted question actually hide the room until the visitor answers?
- Does the reveal consistently provide meaningful alternative perspectives?
- Does “one question, nine perspectives” describe what users reliably receive, or only the aspiration?
- Are enough questions populated to make the promised reveal satisfying?
- Is the transition from answering to seeing the room obvious?
- Does the product give users a reason to share, invite someone, or return after the reveal?
- Is public use of “The Chorus” explained clearly, or does it add avoidable vocabulary?

Flag product/message gaps rather than solving them with copy.

### 2. Surface consistency

Audit the actual language and CTA across:

- Homepage and navigation
- About and manifesto
- Question list, question detail, answer composer, and reveal state
- Registration and onboarding
- Footer, social bios, metadata, and link previews
- Email onboarding and lifecycle messages
- Coaching and book-session pages
- Blog introductions, famous-person analyses, and category pages
- Internal content prompts, agent instructions, and marketing automation

Look for obsolete umbrella phrases, competing taglines, unexplained internal vocabulary, generic
Enneagram-first openings, and CTAs that lead somewhere other than a real question.

### 3. Founder voice and privacy

- Does the manifesto sound like DJ rather than a polished brand narrator?
- Is the disclosure about DJ’s marriage accurate and within the privacy boundary he intends?
- Are the four video scripts natural when read aloud in one take?
- Are any phrases too clinical, absolute, or polished for DJ’s speaking voice?
- Is the distinction between understanding someone and excusing behavior consistently preserved?

### 4. Proof and claims

- Run the nine-person pilot with one self-identified participant per type.
- Preserve independent answers before any reveal.
- Obtain granular publication permission.
- Do not present nine qualitative answers as statistical validation.
- Audit broad claims about social platforms, anchoring, consensus, and emotional alarms for the right level of qualification and sourcing.
- If the pilot produces a strong signal, consider the controlled 90-person follow-on described in the protocol.

### 5. Distribution

- Deploy the manifesto after final review.
- Give each founder video one specific, populated question or analysis as its destination.
- Film and ship before creating more founder-content infrastructure.
- Build a small direct-delivery list of relationship, therapy, personality, workplace-conflict, and communication creators.
- Bring those people a tailored question or artifact rather than asking them to “check out 9takes.”
- Decide where the manifesto is discoverable beyond the About page.

### 6. Measurement

Create one view of the full behavior chain:

```text
Founder story / direct outreach
              ↓
Manifesto or question visit
              ↓
Answer start → completed answer
              ↓
Perspective reveal
              ↓
Share / invitation → seven-day return
```

Views, likes, and non-follower reach are distribution diagnostics. The stronger measures are
completed answers, meaningful reveals, invitations, direct replies, and return behavior.

### 7. Documentation cleanup

- Find older brand documents that still present a different umbrella positioning.
- Mark superseded concepts as historical, campaign-level, or internal instead of silently deleting useful thinking.
- Confirm every agent-facing instruction points to the canonical hierarchy first.
- Identify duplicate founder-story versions and decide which should be editorial mirrors versus historical drafts.
- Check that sitemap, navigation, and metadata treat `/manifesto` as a real public surface.

## Decisions not to reopen without new evidence

- Do not propose a sixth master concept.
- Do not replace the hierarchy merely because another phrase sounds cleverer in isolation.
- Do not make an Enneagram type label the opening of every story.
- Do not turn the manifesto into an exhaustive explanation of the framework.
- Do not begin a visual rebrand when the issue is product proof or distribution.
- Do not automate the founder format before enough real examples have been shipped to learn from.
- Do not interpret qualitative variation as proof that personality type caused the variation.

## Brief for the reviewing agent

Review the repository and return:

1. **An inconsistency inventory** with surface, current language, conflict, and proposed resolution.
2. **A product-truth audit** showing where the message matches or exceeds the actual experience.
3. **A concept cleanup map** classifying old concepts as canonical, supporting, campaign-level, internal, historical, or retire.
4. **A prioritized loose-ends list** using P0 (blocks the promise), P1 (weakens coherence), and P2 (polish).
5. **A minimal integration plan** that reuses the existing hierarchy and artifacts.
6. **Specific proposed edits**, but no changes to the frozen hierarchy without DJ’s explicit approval.

The best result will probably remove ambiguity and connect existing pieces. It should not increase the
number of ideas 9takes asks people to remember.
