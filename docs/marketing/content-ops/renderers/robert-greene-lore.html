Five moments. Robert Greene’s own words. One open question.

1960s · LA Diary of a CEO + Tetragrammaton · 2023

“My parents kind of left me alone a lot.” His sister, he said, “almost kind of raised me.” Elsewhere: “My mother’s a very anxious person”—and “you absorb the energy patterns” around you.

Before 1995 · Hollywood Recounted at Yale · 2010

Greene says his ideas made a superior insecure. After he reacted emotionally, he vowed: “I am just going to become a master observer of the game of power.”

1995 → 1998 · Italy Fabrica to Viking

At Fabrica, he pitched Joost Elffers a book about power. In 1998, The 48 Laws was published. His rule: “It’s all material.”

Aug. 17, 2018 · Stroke Revisited on Tetragrammaton · 2023

His left side was paralyzed. Five years later: “I was a very independent person, and now I’m a dependent person.”

Nov. 10, 2026 · New book Tetragrammaton + Viking

The idea predated his stroke by decades; the stroke changed its terms: “The sublime has to be something you can access from your chair, from your bed, in your daily life.”

What makes someone study power this closely—then spend his next chapter studying wonder?
