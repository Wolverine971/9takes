## 3. Evaluate competitors and evidence

Compare the pages that satisfy the query, but do not copy their structure or claims. For every proposed fact, distinguish:

- established Enneagram-system description;
- 9takes editorial interpretation;
- observed site or user data with a reproducible source;
- external research with a valid primary source; and
- unsupported material that must not be published.

The brief should focus on helping a reader understand pair dynamics and use the chart responsibly, not manufacturing precision.

## 4. Implementation boundary

Create the brief first. If it reveals a small, evidence-safe improvement that does not conflict with T-05, implement a bounded change such as clarifying chart terminology, making the chart easier to reach, improving a jump link, or aligning visible copy with verified query intent.
