# Seed Takes: The "Kid in 3 Words" Question (v1 draft, T-41 item G)

Question: "What were you like as a kid in 3 words?"
Backing question: id 118, `/questions/what-were-you-like-as-a-kid-in-3-words`

## What these are

Nine anonymous, type-labeled takes for the post-answer reveal on celebrity pages (and anywhere else the chorus for this question renders). Same rules as wave 1 (`docs/content-research/2026-07-15-wave1-seed-takes.md`): no usernames or avatars, never posted as comments or fake accounts, and seeds are scaffolding that the best real take per type replaces over time (DJ curates). Each take is written from a hidden persona (the italic note on the `Type N` line, never displayed; the seed script drops it).

## Grounding (read-only production data, 2026-10-03)

42 live human answers on q118. Almost all are literally three words ("Curious Shy Resistant", "Quiet, absorbed, creative", "Unabashed, outspoken, competitive"), and a few add one sentence of scene. So every seed below leads with its three words and then adds only as much texture as that person would. Light, nostalgic question: the types that go quiet on vulnerability (3, 5, 8) still answer, just short.

Positioning check: these describe the kid, never explain the adult. No "that's why I became...", no origin story for a present-day pattern.

**Type 1** _(persona: hospital pharmacist, early 40s, chose the three words carefully)_

Earnest, bossy, careful. I read the rules on the back of every board game box out loud before anyone could touch a piece. My classmates tolerated it, which seemed fair at the time. I would pick the same three words today, though I like to think "bossy" has been edited down to "organized."

**Type 2** _(persona: elementary school counselor, early 50s, the one who brings extra snacks)_

Ha, my sister would answer this better than me, she says I was "the little mayor." Okay, three words: chatty, sweet, watchful. I always knew which grown-up at a party was getting ignored, and I'd go sit with them and ask about their dog. Looking back I spent a lot of birthday parties checking on everybody else's fun. Pretty sure I still do.

**Type 3** _(persona: regional sales director, mid-30s, answered in an elevator)_

Fast, loud, first. First hand up, first one done with the test, first to ask if there was a prize. My dad still has a drawer of my ribbons and I could tell you what's in it without looking.

**Type 4** _(persona: museum exhibit designer, early 30s, keeps a box of childhood drawings)_

Dreamy, dramatic, elsewhere. I spent most of second grade convinced I was a changeling, and the family I lived with was very kind about it. I invented a language for my stuffed animals, with grammar, and was genuinely wounded when my cousins wouldn't learn it. Recess was me walking the fence line narrating my own life in the third person. I still have the notebook from that year, the purple one with the lock that never worked, and reading it now is strange because the handwriting belongs to a child and the feelings are completely mine. Same weather, smaller umbrella.

**Type 5** _(persona: data engineer, late 30s, answered and closed the tab)_

Quiet, curious, unsupervised. Took the toaster apart at eight to see where the heat came from. Got most of it back together. We used the other toaster after that.

**Type 6** _(persona: insurance claims adjuster, late 30s, still texts their kindergarten best friend)_

Careful, loyal, worried? (Is worried allowed as a kid word? I'm keeping it.) I had one best friend from kindergarten through eighth grade and I would have walked into traffic for her, after looking both ways. She still does an impression of me checking the car doors twice at the gas station. Honestly it's a pretty accurate impression.

**Type 7** _(persona: event producer, late 20s, typing on the train)_

loud, sticky, everywhere. wait can I get a fourth. I once put on a backyard circus with zero animals and charged a quarter, and when nobody came I turned it into a parade and marched it around the block collecting neighbors. my aunt says she never once saw me walk anywhere, it was running or skipping. honestly still the plan

**Type 8** _(persona: union electrician, early 50s, answered under mild protest)_

Stubborn, loud, protective. Nobody picked on my little brother twice.

**Type 9** _(persona: library aide, mid-40s, started this answer twice)_

hmm. three words... easygoing, dreamy, content? that feels right. my mom says I could sit in the backyard with one stick and a bucket of water for a whole afternoon and be totally happy, and I believe her, because honestly I'd still take that afternoon. I was the kid nobody really had to worry about. which was nice. I think it was nice. if my brother wanted the front seat I'd just... not want it, and it didn't feel like giving anything up at the time. anyway. we had a really good tire swing.
