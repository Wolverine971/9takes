**Caption:**
Personality language can help you notice the story you attach to a hard moment. It cannot tell you whether that moment comes from ADHD, autism, dyslexia, DCD, sensory needs, stress, or something else.

The updated guide separates motivation, processing, environment, and support. It also explains which popular terms are formal diagnoses and which are community or education language.

Educational only. It does not diagnose, prescribe treatment, or provide legal advice.
