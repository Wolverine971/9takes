## The scene

- 30-something. Every sibling and cousin paired off; some with kids
- the kids' table has more dates than you
- nobody asks. That's worse
- [DJ: who you know who's lived this]

## What they'll say → what they're really saying

- "your time will come" → I've been worrying about you
- "enjoy the freedom!" → I wonder what it'd be like
- the extra place setting removed without a word → we planned around you

## What's going on for them

- pity, but also envy they won't admit
- the couples are comparing too

## What's going on for you

- grief about a timeline, not a person
- the tally: weddings attended, babies held, years
- you start performing "fine"

## The nine at this table (carousel)

1. holds itself to a standard nobody asked for
2. over-helps in the kitchen so nobody notices
3. arrives with good news to balance the ledger
4. feels it hardest; keeps it to themselves
5. leaves early, calls it tired
6. scans for who's judging
7. books the whole weekend so there's no time to feel it
8. doesn't let anyone pity them
9. goes along; feels it later in the car

## Defuse it

- stop performing fine: one honest sentence to one safe person
- bring something that's yours: a dish, a story, a plan
- set your own exit time before you arrive
- say: [DJ: line]
- don't: spend the night proving you're happy

"Have you been the only single one at a family holiday? What did you actually feel?"
